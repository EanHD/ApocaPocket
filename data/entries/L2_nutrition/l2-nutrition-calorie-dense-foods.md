# Calorie-Dense Foods

## Calorie Density

In a crisis, storage and carrying capacity are limited.
Prioritize foods that deliver the most calories per pound.

Daily adult calorie needs:
- Sedentary: 1800-2000 kcal
- Light activity: 2000-2500 kcal
- Heavy labor or cold exposure: 3000-4000 kcal
- Pregnant or nursing: add 300-500 kcal

## Top 15 Survival Foods

All values approximate per 100g dry weight.

Fats and oils:
- Coconut oil or lard: 900 kcal, indefinite shelf life sealed
- Olive oil: 884 kcal, 2 years sealed
- Peanut butter: 590 kcal, 1-2 years sealed

Nuts and seeds:
- Macadamia nuts: 718 kcal, 1 year sealed
- Walnuts: 654 kcal, 1 year, high omega-3
- Sunflower seeds: 584 kcal, 1-2 years
- Pumpkin seeds: 559 kcal, 1-2 years, high iron and zinc

Grains and starches:
- Rolled oats: 389 kcal, 30 years in mylar
- White rice: 365 kcal, 25-30 years sealed
- Cornmeal: 370 kcal, 5 years sealed
- All-purpose flour: 364 kcal, 5-10 years sealed

Legumes:
- Lentils: 353 kcal, 25+ years sealed, high protein and iron
- Dry black beans: 341 kcal, 25+ years sealed

Dried meat:
- Beef jerky: 410 kcal, 1-2 years sealed, high protein
- Pemmican (fat and dried meat): 600 kcal, 2-5 years

## Storage Life

Heat and humidity degrade all stored food.
Store in cool, dark, dry location.

Short term (1-2 years): oils, nuts, jerky, peanut butter
Medium term (5-10 years): flour, cornmeal, oats
Long term (25+ years): white rice, dry beans, lentils

Mylar bags with oxygen absorbers in food-grade buckets
extend grain and legume life to the upper end of these ranges.

## Nutrient Density

Calorie density alone is not enough for long-term survival.
Micronutrient deficiencies appear within weeks on poor diets.

Best combined value (calories and nutrients):
- Lentils: protein, iron, folate, fiber
- Pumpkin seeds: protein, zinc, magnesium
- Oats: fiber, B vitamins, sustained energy
- Pemmican: protein, fat, iron, used for months historically

Supplement calorie-dense stores with vitamin C sources.
Scurvy appears in 4-8 weeks on pure starch and dried meat.

## Calorie Calculation

Quick cache estimate for planning:
- 1 person for 30 days at 2000 kcal per day = 60,000 kcal needed

Roughly:
- 16 kg white rice provides about 58,000 kcal
- Add 8 kg dry beans, 2 liters oil, nuts, and jerky
  to reach target and add protein and fat balance
