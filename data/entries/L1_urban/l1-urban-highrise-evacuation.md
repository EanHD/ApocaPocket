# High-Rise Evacuation

## Overview

- High-rise = 7+ stories; NEVER use elevators during fire/earthquake (power fails, elevator recalled)
- Evacuate EARLY  -  once 300+ people fill stairwells, descent slows to 10-15 min per floor
- Evacuate for: fire alarm, visible smoke, felt earthquake (M5+), explosion, official order
- Shelter in place for: active shooter outside floor, external hazmat cloud, tornado/severe wind

## Pre-Evac Assessment

1. Touch door (not handle): hot = fire on floor  -  seal room, call 911, signal from window
2. Cool door  -  open slowly; heavy smoke in hall  -  low-crawl with wet cloth; clear  -  proceed
3. Grab go-bag only if pre-staged at door (15 sec max); otherwise leave it
4. Alert 2-3 neighbors (knock, shout "evacuate!"); don't wait for response
5. Shoes on, close door behind you, head to stairwell

## Stairwell Descent

- Right hand on railing; face forward; small controlled steps; don't rush (falls cause pile-ups)
- Keep left lane open for emergency personnel ascending
- Estimated time: 10 floors = 2-3 min, 20 floors = 5-7 min, 30 floors = 8-12 min
- Add 50-100% time if 100+ people in stairwell; rest on landings only (never mid-flight)

## Stairwell Hazards

- **Smoke:** drop to knees (air cleaner 12-18 in up); wet cloth; if smoke worsens, retreat up and call 911
- **Crowd crush:** cross arms in front of chest; shuffle toward wall; yell "KEEP MOVING!"
- **Locked doors:** try next floor; use panic bar; break door glass; use stairwell emergency phone
- **Falls:** yell "STOP!", move injured to landing, designate one person to stay, continue descent

## Alternate Exits

- **Roof (fire below):** break lock if needed; stay away from edges; signal rescuers with bright cloth or flashlight; wait for helicopter
- **Window/rope:** tie rope to radiator or pipe; test full weight; rappel; survivable drops: 1 floor ~95%, 2 floors 50-70%, 3+ floors <20%
- **Makeshift rope:** bedsheets tied with square knots; 6-8 ft/sheet; holds ~200 lbs; test each knot

## Special Populations

- **Wheelchair/mobility:** use Area of Refuge (fire-rated, pressurized room on each floor); call 911; wait for firefighters
- **Children under 5:** carry; ages 5-12: hold hand; age 13+: treat as adult with clear instructions
- **Elderly:** offer arm (don't pull); rest every 5-10 floors; keep talking for reassurance

## Fire Scenarios

- **Fire above you:** evacuate normally (smoke rises; descend away from it)
- **Fire below you:** descend until heavy smoke, then retreat up; go to roof or Area of Refuge; call 911
- **Fire on your floor:** seal door gaps with wet towels; go to window; signal rescuers; wait for rescue

## Ground Floor Exit

- Check lobby before exiting: smoke, crowd crush, debris
- If one exit has 100+ people: find side door or service entrance; yell "USE OTHER DOORS!"
- Once outside: move 200+ ft from building; never re-enter for any reason

## Common Mistakes

- Waiting for elevator  -  always use stairs during fire/earthquake
- Grabbing belongings  -  go-bag only, 15 sec max
- Opening a hot door  -  touch first; if hot, seal room and signal from window
- Sprinting  -  controlled pace prevents pile-ups
- Ignoring alarm  -  every alarm is real until proven otherwise
- Re-entering building  -  never, not even for pets or documents
