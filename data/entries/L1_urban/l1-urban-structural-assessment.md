# Building Structural

## Overview

- Entering a compromised building is the #1 cause of post-disaster urban death
- 60-70% of post-earthquake deaths occur AFTER the initial event from secondary collapse
- Three categories: GREEN (safe to occupy), YELLOW (<15 min entry only), RED (do not enter)
- When in doubt, mark RED — no resource is worth your life

## Rating System

- **GREEN:** Minor cosmetic damage only; safe to occupy normally
- **YELLOW:** Moderate damage; enter briefly (<15 min), ground floor only, buddy system
- **RED:** Any critical failure indicator present — DO NOT ENTER

## Lean Assessment

- Stand 50-100 ft away; hold a vertical stick as plumb-line reference
- Lean <1 deg: safe | 1-2 deg: caution | 3-5 deg: YELLOW | >5 deg: RED (may collapse in minutes)
- Visual signs: doors/windows no longer square; visible gap between wall and door frame

## Foundation Check

- Walk full perimeter; look for cracks and water pooling
- Hairline (<1/16 in): safe | 1/16-1/4 in: caution | 1/4-1/2 in: YELLOW | >1/2 in: RED
- Horizontal cracks or diagonal (45 deg) cracks = RED at any width >1/8 in
- Water pooling against foundation = undermining risk

## Wall Assessment

- **X-pattern (shear) cracks:** diagonal lines crossing = RED
- **Horizontal cracks** mid-height = RED (any width >1/8 in)
- **Stair-step cracks** in masonry: <1/4 in = YELLOW; >1/4 in = RED
- **Bulging or bowing** visible along wall length = RED

## Roof Assessment

- Straight roofline = safe; sag <2 in = caution; sag >6 in = RED
- Missing sections, standing water >3 in on flat roof, or shifted HVAC units = RED
- Porches, balconies, fire escapes fail first — check for rust, rot, cracks at attachment points

## Interior Hazards

- **Before stepping inside:** tap floor with crowbar; soft/bouncy/hollow = don't enter
- **Charred floors:** if beam >50% charred through = RED; probe with knife to test depth
- **Ceilings:** sagging or wet = RED; avoid walking directly underneath; stay near walls
- **Stairs:** stomp first step from landing; cracking stringer or visible sag = use ladder or abandon

## Earthquake Damage

- **Shear cracks** (45 deg diagonal): <1/4 in = YELLOW; >1/4 in = RED
- **Soft-story collapse** (first floor compressed, upper floors intact) = DO NOT ENTER
- **Foundation offset** >1 in: RED — building no longer on stable base
- Damaged buildings are MORE vulnerable to aftershocks; minimize time inside YELLOW buildings

## Fire Damage

- **Wood:** surface char only = safe; char >50% of beam depth = RED
- **Steel:** visible warping or drooping beams = RED (indicates 1200 deg F+ exposure)
- **Concrete:** spalling (chunks missing) plus exposed rebar = RED

## Tagging

- GREEN box + "INSPECTED": safe to occupy
- YELLOW box + "LIMITED ENTRY": critical items only, <15 min, 2-3 people, buddy system
- RED box + "UNSAFE": add specific hazard ("LEANING", "GAS", "FIRE DAMAGE")
- Include date and assessor initials; re-inspect after any aftershock

## Decision Flowchart

1. Lean >3 deg? -> RED
2. Cracks >1/4 in in foundation or walls? -> RED
3. Roof sagging significantly or collapsed? -> RED
4. Gas, chemical smell, or sparking electrical? -> RED
5. X-cracks, bulging walls, or soft-story collapse? -> RED
6. Moderate cracks (1/8-1/4 in), minor sag, water damage? -> YELLOW
7. Minor cosmetic damage only? -> GREEN (stay alert; re-inspect after aftershocks)
