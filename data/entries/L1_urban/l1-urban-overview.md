# Urban Survival: Start

## Use this when:
- Urban infrastructure has failed (power, water, transit)
- You need to evacuate or shelter-in-place in a city environment
- You are navigating crowds, highrises, or grid-down conditions

## Grid-Down Flow
- Power out under 4 hours? Monitor, conserve, do not open fridge
- Power out 4-24 hours? Activate food/water reserves; check on neighbors
- Power out 24+ hours? Shift to full grid-down protocol; consider evacuation
- Water service failed? Use stored water first; locate secondary sources
- Evacuation ordered? Use pre-planned route; avoid highways if gridlocked

## Scenarios Compared:
- Power Outage: most common urban crisis; heat/cold risk is primary threat
- Evacuation: timing is critical; early departure avoids gridlock and crowds
- Crowd Safety: identify exits first; move against walls, not with crowd crush
- Highrise Survival: stairwell awareness, floor supplies, roof signaling option
- Urban Scavenging: dumpsters, abandoned buildings; legal and safety awareness

## Key Rules:
- Know your building's emergency exits before you need them
- Store 1 gallon water/person/day for at least 3 days minimum
- A candle provides light and morale; never leave unattended
