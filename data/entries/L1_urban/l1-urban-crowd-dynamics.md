# Crowd Dynamics and Crush

## Key Facts

- Crowd crushes kill more in urban disasters than fire, violence, or structural collapse
- 5,000+ deaths worldwide since 2000; death from compressive asphyxiation in 3-6 min
- Cause is physics/density, NOT panic  -  fatal threshold: 7+ people per square meter

## Density Zones

- Zone 1-2 (1-3/m2): normal flow  -  SAFE
- Zone 3 (3-4/m2): bodies touching; begin moving toward crowd edge
- Zone 4 (4-5/m2): HIGH RISK  -  move to exit now
- Zone 5 (5-6/m2): CRITICAL  -  escape immediately; use boxing stance
- Zone 6-7 (6-7+/m2): FATAL  -  protect chest, fight for breathing room

## Pre-Entry Steps

- Find 3 exits (primary, secondary, emergency/window) before the event starts
- Best positions: near exits but not directly in front; at crowd edge; elevated ground
- Worst positions: crowd center, directly at exit door, between two solid barriers

## Warning Signs

- People standing motionless, arms pinned at sides, faces pressed together
- Distressed screaming (not cheering)  -  "I can't breathe!" or "Stop pushing!"
- Feet lifted off ground; crowd is choosing your direction
- Stop-and-go shockwaves rippling backward through crowd

## Boxing Stance

1. Bend elbows 90 degrees, fists at chest (boxer's guard)
2. Feet shoulder-width, knees slightly bent
3. Creates 6-8 inches of space for lung expansion
4. Hold until density drops or you reach crowd edge

## Escape Techniques

- Move **diagonally** (45 deg angle) toward edge  -  never fight flow head-on
- Use small shuffle steps; fill each gap as it opens (can take 10-20 min for 20 ft)
- Do NOT push  -  causes backlash and worsens crush
- **If you fall:** yell "I'M DOWN!", curl into ball protecting head, get up immediately

## Bottleneck Survival

- Stop 20-30 ft before bottleneck; find alternate exit or wait for density to ease
- If already inside: boxing stance, shuffle to doorframe edge, grip frame and pull through
- Yell "STOP PUSHING FROM BEHIND!"  -  may reduce rear pressure
- Drop bags; don't bend over; don't push forward

## Group Tactics

- Wrist grip (not fingers); strongest adult leads; children/elderly in middle
- Agree on a code word in advance that means "exit now, no questions"
- Pre-arrange rally point 200+ ft from building in case of separation

## Alternate Exits

- Side doors and fire exits first (least crowded)
- Ground-floor windows: break glass, clear frame, lay cloth over edge, go feet-first
- Safe drop from height: hang from edge first, land on balls of feet, roll sideways

## When to Leave

- Crowd density reaches Zone 4 (4+ people/m2)
- You hear distressed screaming or see people falling
- You feel chest compression or cannot choose your direction
- Exit is blocked or alarm sounds  -  don't wait for official evacuation order

## After a Crush

- Check for breathing difficulty, injuries, dizziness; seek medical help if symptomatic
- Report to responders: approximate count down, locations, any hazards
- PTSD and crowd anxiety are common after crush events  -  seek trauma support
