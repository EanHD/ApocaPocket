# Urban Navigation

## Overview

- Without GPS, average person gets lost within 1-2 miles in 30-45 min
- Walking speed: 3 mph open, 2-2.5 mph urban, 1.5-2 mph with heavy load
- Core skills: cardinal direction, distance estimation, route planning

## Pre-Move Plan

- Know exact address/intersection AND a backup landmark description
- Best maps (in order): paper road map, printed screenshot, hand-drawn, mental map
- Plan 3 routes: primary (main roads), alternate 1 (parallel street), alternate 2 (different approach)
- Mark routes: primary = blue, alternates = green/orange, avoid zones = red X

## Finding Direction

**Sun (most reliable, daytime):**
- Morning: sun in East (shadow points West)
- Noon: sun in South, shadow points North
- Afternoon: sun in West (shadow points East)
- Shadow stick: plant stick, mark shadow tip, wait 15 min, mark again — line between marks = W to E

**Stars (night):**
- Big Dipper pointer stars x 5 = Polaris (North Star, true North within 1 degree)
- Southern Hemisphere: extend Southern Cross long axis 4.5x to find South

**Urban shortcuts:**
- Street grids: numbered streets often N-S or E-W; increasing numbers = heading N or E
- Satellite dishes: point South in Northern Hemisphere
- Building shadows: opposite the sun (morning = West shadow, noon = North shadow)

## Distance Estimation

- **Pace count:** count every left-foot step; calibrate on a known 100 m (typically ~100 paces)
- **Time-distance:** urban = 2 mph, 1 mile in 30 min; heavy load = 1.5 mph, 1 mile in 40 min
- **Blocks:** US city blocks approx 100-150 m; 10 blocks approx 1 mile

## Navigation Methods

- **Major street following:** stick to main roads (landmarks, signs, fewer turns)
- **Landmark chaining:** identify visible target 0.5-1 mi ahead, walk to it, pick next landmark
- **Dead reckoning:** maintain heading using sun/shadows; recheck every 0.25 mi (humans drift 15-20 degrees)
- **Backtrack markers:** chalk arrows on buildings (single arrow = direction, X = wrong way)

## Navigation Hazards

- **Disoriented:** STOP, sit 5-10 min, re-orient with sun/street signs, backtrack to last known point
- **Smoke/fog:** wet cloth over mouth; follow building face with hand on wall; listen for water/traffic
- **Night:** use Polaris; walk 1-1.5 mph; use headlamp sparingly; stay on major streets
- **All routes blocked:** shelter in place, reassess after 1-2 hours

## Group Navigation

- Roles: Navigator (map), Point (5-10 ft ahead), Rear Guard (watches behind), Civilians (middle)
- Hand signals: fist up = STOP, point = direction, tap head = look/listen
- Rest every 60-90 min; group moves at speed of slowest member

## Tools

- **Magnetic compass:** red needle = North; align bezel N with red needle
- **Watch method (analog):** point hour hand at sun; midpoint between hour hand and 12 = South
- **Improvised map:** paper + pencil; sketch streets/landmarks; mark North arrow; update as you walk

## Stop and Shelter If

- Injured, severe weather, all 3 routes blocked, or night falls with no safe destination
- Temporary shelter: abandoned building (safety check first), vehicle, green space off main streets
