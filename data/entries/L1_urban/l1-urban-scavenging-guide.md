# Urban Scavenging Guide

## Safety First

- No resource is worth your life  -  if a building feels unsafe, leave immediately
- 60% of scavenging deaths = structural collapse; do 5-point check every 15 min inside
- Recon full perimeter before entering; identify 2+ exits first

## Target Selection

- **High value/lower risk:** convenience stores, pharmacies, hardware stores, gas stations
- **Medium risk:** homes, schools, gyms
- **Avoid:** warehouses, multi-story parking garages, industrial sites

## Minimum Gear

- Sturdy boots, heavy gloves, N95 mask, headlamp, whistle, crowbar, first aid kit

## Entry Checklist

- **Red flags (don't enter):** lean >5 deg, gas/chemical smell, FEMA Orange/Red X, cracking sounds
- Stand to the side when opening door; push with foot; wait 10 sec and assess
- Identify 2+ exits before stepping fully inside

## Structural Check

- **Foundation:** cracks >1/4 inch wide or horizontal -> leave
- **Walls:** bulging, horizontal cracks, or X-pattern cracks -> leave
- **Roof:** sagging, missing sections, cracking/groaning sounds -> leave
- **Floors:** soft spots, bouncing, hollow sound when tapped -> don't step on
- **Hazards:** gas smell -> walk out (don't run), 200 ft upwind, no phone near building

## Room Search Order

1. Entry room -> Kitchen -> Bathrooms -> Bedrooms -> Garage (if safe)
- Mark each searched room with chalk X on door
- 5 min/small room, 10 min/medium, 15 min/large; **30-60 min total max**
- Set alarm before entering; leave immediately when it sounds

## Priority Items

- **Tier 1:** bottled water, canned food, medicines, first aid supplies, batteries
- **Tier 2:** clothing, blankets, fire-starting tools, multi-tool, hygiene items
- **Tier 3:** fuel, hardware, maps, radios
- **Skip:** powerless electronics, perishable food, bulky furniture

## Carry Limits

- <1 mi: 50-70 lbs | 1-5 mi: 30-40 lbs | 5+ mi: 20-30 lbs
- Best weight-to-value: antibiotics -> canned food -> tools (water heaviest, lowest priority)

## Hazard Response

- **Gas leak:** walk (not run) out; 200 ft upwind; no spark sources near building
- **Floor collapse warning:** spread weight, roll toward wall, crawl to exit
- **Cracking/popping sounds:** drop items, run to nearest exit immediately
- **Chemical smell/burning eyes:** exit, 15-30 min fresh air, don't re-enter

## Threat Response

- Observe before approaching: 5-10 min from cover; count people; assess weapons
- Hands visible; calm voice: "Looking for food/water for my family"
- De-escalate: "I'll leave, no problem" -- back away slowly facing them
- Self-defense only if cornered and actively attacked (last resort)

## Specialty Targets

- **Pharmacies:** antibiotics, insulin, EpiPens -- go within 24-48 hrs; in/out 10-15 min max
- **Gas stations:** maps, lighters, snacks, small tools; pumps don't work without power
- **Homes:** garage (tools/fuel), pantry (canned goods), bathroom (meds); leave a note

## Post-Scavenge

- Strip outer layer outside shelter; wash hands/face with soap and water
- Quarantine damaged packaging; purify all unknown water before drinking
- Log: location, date, items taken, hazards, threats encountered
