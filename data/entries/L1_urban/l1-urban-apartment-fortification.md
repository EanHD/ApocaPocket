# Apartment Fortification

**Goal**: Create layered security that DELAYS intruders. Every 60 seconds of delay = higher chance they move on.
**Timeline**: Basic setup in 2-4 hours. Advanced reinforcement over 24-48 hours.

---

## Security Layers

```
LAYER 1: Building perimeter (limited control)
LAYER 2: Apartment door (PRIMARY FOCUS)
LAYER 3: Safe room (interior fallback)
LAYER 4: Escape routes (if breach occurs)
```

---

## Door Reinforcement

**1. Door wedge (0-5 min, $5)**
- Jam rubber wedge under door from inside. Stack 2-3 for more resistance.
- Transfers force to floor. Upgrade: kickproof door brace ($30-50).

**2. Deadbolt reinforcement (10-20 min, $20-40)**
- Replace 1" screws with 3" screws (reach wall studs).
- Add metal wrap-around strike plate ($25).
- No deadbolt: install portable door lock ($15-30) or security bar.

**3. Hinge protection (15-30 min)**
- Hinges on outside? Attacker can remove pins and lift door off. Install setscrew hinges.
- Hinges on inside: already protected.

**4. Door frame reinforcement (30-60 min, $50-100)**
- Problem: kicks break the FRAME, not the lock.
- Metal sleeve around door frame, secured with 3" screws into studs.
- DIY: 2x4 lumber screwed to inside of frame.

**5. Floor-to-ceiling brace (1-2 hours, $50-200)**
- 2x4 angled 45 deg from floor to top of door frame. Most effective.
- Adjustable car jack or extendable pole works.
- Cannot be defeated by kicks.

---

## Window Security

**Ground floor or fire escape access  -  high risk.**

- **Dowel rods ($10)**: Cut to exact track width, drop in lower channel. Window cannot open.
- Keep one window without dowel  -  emergency exit.
- **Security film ($30-60/window)**: Holds glass together when broken. Apply with squeegee.
- **Blackout curtains**: Block light and block view of supplies from outside.

---

## Safe Room

**Best room**: no windows, solid door, away from exterior walls, bathroom preferred (water access).

**Stock it**:
- Water (1 gal/person minimum), energy bars.
- First aid kit, flashlight, charged phone.
- Crowbar or tool (force exit if trapped).

**Reinforce the door**: same wedge and deadbolt techniques as entry door.

**Goal**: Hold for 15-30 minutes. Most intruders leave within 5-10 minutes.

---

## Noise & Light

**Noise**: Whisper or write notes. Muffle sounds with blankets over windows and towels under doors. No TV, music, or radio audible from outside.

**Light**: No light visible from outside, especially at night. Blackout all windows. Use red-filtered flashlight (less visible, preserves night vision). Candles only in interior rooms.

---

## Escape Routes

**Fire escape**: Keep clear of storage. Practice descent in daylight.

**Balcony upper floors (2-4 stories)**:
- 25-50 ft rope ($30-50), tied to railing.
- Tie figure-8 follow-through knot, use carabiner.

**Ground floor / 2nd floor**: Hang from edge, drop (reduces fall by 6 ft). Land on feet, roll sideways.

---

## When to Leave

Evacuate if:
- Fire in building (smoke, flames).
- Structural damage (cracks, explosion).
- Out of water and situation is worsening.
- No help coming after extended time.

**Bug-out steps**: Grab pre-packed bag. Check hallway through peephole. Use stairs not elevator. Avoid main entrance if compromised.

---

## Fortify Checklist

**Basic (2-4 hours)**:
- [ ] Door wedge installed
- [ ] Deadbolt reinforced with 3" screws
- [ ] Window dowels in place
- [ ] Blackout curtains up
- [ ] Safe room identified and stocked
- [ ] Escape routes identified

**Advanced (24-48 hours)**:
- [ ] Door frame reinforcement installed
- [ ] Hinge pins secured
- [ ] Security film applied to vulnerable windows
- [ ] Communication system with trusted neighbors
- [ ] Bug-out bag packed and staged
