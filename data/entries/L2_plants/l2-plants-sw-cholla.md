# Cholla Cactus Buds

## Identification

- Cylindrical jointed spiny stems (not flat)
- Joints detach and attach to skin/fur easily
- Small oval pink-purple flowers on tips
- Cylindropuntia bigelovii (teddy bear cholla)
- Green tight buds at stem tips in spring

## Spine Warning

- Barbed spines detach joint on contact
- Removal requires two sticks or combs
- DO NOT brush against or touch bare
- Dropped joints on ground attach to feet

## When to Harvest

- Spring only: March-April before buds open
- Buds must be tight and green, pre-flower
- After opening: tough, less edible
- 1-2 week harvest window per year

## How to Harvest

- Two long sticks or two combs required
- Knock bud from stem into container
- Never reach in with hands
- Cook on site or transport in covered container

## Preparation

- Place buds directly on hot coals
- Burn off spines: 2-3 min until char removed
- Boil buds 10-15 min until soft
- Eat like artichoke hearts; mild flavor

## Nutritional Value

- Calcium: 500mg per 100g dry weight
- Higher calcium than milk per calorie
- Iron, magnesium also present
- Carbohydrates for desert energy

## Lookalikes  -  DANGER

- No dangerous lookalikes in Sonoran Desert
- Cylindrical joints = cholla; flat pads = prickly pear
- Both edible when properly prepared
- Confirm cylindrical jointed stems
