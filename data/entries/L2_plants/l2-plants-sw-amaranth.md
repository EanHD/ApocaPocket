# Amaranth Wild

## Identification

- Erect annual weed 2-6 ft tall
- Oval to diamond leaves, alternate
- Dense bristly flower/seed spikes at top
- Tiny red-green seeds in dense clusters
- Stems may be reddish; rough texture

## Where to Find

- Disturbed soil, roadsides, fields
- Gardens and agricultural margins
- Thrives in heat; common in SW summer
- Often grows alongside lamb's quarters

## How to Harvest

- Young spring leaves from top of plant
- Seeds: thresh mature heads into bag
- Hit seed head into bag in August-Sept
- Separate chaff by blowing gently

## Preparation

- Young leaves: raw or boiled like spinach
- Boil mature leaves to reduce nitrates
- Seeds: rinse, boil as porridge 20 min
- Seeds: dry roast then grind to flour

## Nutritional Value

- Seeds: 13-14% protein; complete amino acids
- Lysine-rich: rare in grain-type seeds
- Seeds: ~370 cal per 100g dried
- Greens: iron, vitamins A and C

## Seed Processing

- Thresh: rub heads between hands over bag
- Winnow: pour between containers in wind
- Rinse seeds in water; float chaff off
- Boil 20 min in 3:1 water ratio

## Lookalikes — DANGER

- No dangerous lookalikes when mature
- Young plants: confirm rough texture, no smell
- Lamb's quarters (Chenopodium): also safe
- Both common in same habitat; both edible
