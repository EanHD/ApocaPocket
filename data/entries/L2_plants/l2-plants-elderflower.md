# Elderflower

## Identification

- Compound leaves: 5-9 serrated leaflets
- Large flat-topped white flower clusters
- Flowers: tiny 5-petal creamy white
- Strong muscat-floral fragrance
- On same shrub that produces berries later

## Flower Harvest Window

- Spring: flowers appear before berries
- Harvest when freshly opened; fragrant
- 2-4 week window in most areas
- Do NOT wait until flowers age and smell musty

## How to Harvest

- Snip entire flower head (corymb) off
- Bring fresh to use same day
- Shake to dislodge insects first
- Avoid washing; removes fragrant pollen

## Elderflower Tea

- Steep 2-3 flower heads per cup
- Hot water; steep 5-10 minutes
- Floral, light taste; slightly sweet
- Traditional flu and cold fever remedy

## Edible Uses

- Eat flowers raw in small amounts
- Dip entire flower head in batter and fry
- Steep in water for floral drink
- Dry flowers for later use as tea

## Antiviral Properties

- Flavonoids in flowers active against flu virus
- Traditional remedy predating modern medicine
- Diaphoretic: induces sweating for fever
- Anti-inflammatory for respiratory infection

## Lookalikes — DANGER

- Water hemlock (Cicuta): similar white clusters
- Hemlock: NO compound alternate leaves
- Elder: compound leaves + pithy hollow stem
- Poison hemlock: purple spotted hollow stems
