# Serviceberry

## Identification

- Shrub to small tree 10-25 ft
- Smooth gray bark; alternate leaves
- White 5-petal flowers in early spring
- Round berries: deep red-purple when ripe
- Berry has crown at tip (remnant calyx)

## Where to Find

- Forest edges, stream banks, open woods
- Throughout eastern US and Canada
- Flowers early spring (before leaves often)
- Berries ripe: June-July (first summer berry)

## How to Harvest

- Pick ripe berries by hand; dark red-purple
- Ripe = sweet, pulls off easily
- Harvest in quantity; prolific producer
- Strip comb technique for efficiency

## Preparation

- Eat raw; excellent fresh berry
- Very similar to blueberry in taste
- Dry for winter storage
- Boil briefly for trail jam

## Nutritional Value

- Vitamin C, iron, copper, manganese
- ~85 cal per 100g fresh berries
- Anthocyanins: high antioxidant content
- Seeds contain some iron

## Timing Note

- One of earliest summer berries available
- Often ripe before other berries in region
- After fruit set, plant becomes inconspicuous
- Mark trees in flower so you can find in fruit

## Lookalikes  -  DANGER

- No deadly lookalikes
- Crown tip on berry is distinctive
- Chokecherry: similar but more cluster-arranged
- Chokecherry also edible when ripe; no danger
