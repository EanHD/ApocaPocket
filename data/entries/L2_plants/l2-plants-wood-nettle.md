# Wood Nettle

## Identification

- Alternate leaves (not opposite like stinging nettle)
- Oval to egg-shaped serrated leaves
- Stinging hairs on stems and leaves
- Moist forest; not open disturbed areas
- Laportea canadensis: eastern US species

## Species Comparison

- Stinging nettle: OPPOSITE leaves
- Wood nettle: ALTERNATE leaves
- Both have stinging hairs; both edible
- Wood nettle: moist forest understory
- Stinging nettle: open disturbed areas

## Where to Find

- Rich moist deciduous forest
- Floodplains and stream banks
- Eastern US; widespread
- Spring harvest: April-June best

## How to Harvest

- Wear gloves; sting similar intensity
- Pinch top 4-6 leaves when young
- Harvest before plant flowers
- Bag immediately; sting neutralizes when wilted

## Preparation

- Boil 2-3 minutes: completely neutralizes sting
- Use exactly like stinging nettle
- Excellent in soups, stews
- High-nutrition spring green

## Nutritional Value

- Iron, calcium; vitamins A, C, K
- Protein: ~3g per 100g cooked
- ~40 cal per 100g cooked
- One of best spring wild greens in East

## Lookalikes  -  DANGER

- False nettle (Boehmeria): no stinging hairs
- False nettle is edible; just not stinging
- All stinging nettles are safe to eat cooked
- Confirm sting before harvesting (confirms ID)
