# Chickweed Universal

## Year-Round Season

- Cool-season plant; thrives in fall and spring
- Survives light frost; first spring green
- Dies back in summer heat; returns in fall
- Mild winter areas: available all year

## Identification Review

- Small oval opposite leaves
- Single line of hairs along one stem side
- Tiny white star flowers (5 deeply cleft petals)
- Juicy stems snap cleanly; no milky sap
- Low growing mat 4-8 inches tall

## Global Distribution

- Introduced to North America; now universal
- Found on every continent including Antarctica area
- Disturbed soil, gardens, lawns worldwide
- If there is disturbed soil, check for chickweed

## Nutritional Value

- Vitamin C: significant anti-scurvy value
- Vitamin A, calcium, iron
- ~20 cal per 100g; eat in large quantities
- Dried chickweed retains most vitamins

## Emergency Primary Use

- Find large patch; harvest armloads
- Boil into green soup for mineral boost
- Mix with bark or seed flour for balanced meal
- Priority green in cool-season survival

## The Sap Test

- Break stem: clear watery sap = safe
- Milky white sap = spurge (Euphorbia)
- Spurge causes GI upset and skin irritation
- Do sap test every time with new patch

## Drying for Storage

- Lay loosely on flat surface out of rain
- Dries in 1-2 days in sun or warmth
- Crumble dried chickweed into container
- Rehydrates in soup; use within weeks
