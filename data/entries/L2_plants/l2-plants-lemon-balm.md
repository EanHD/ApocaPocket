# Lemon Balm

## Identification

- Opposite toothed oval-heart-shaped leaves
- Square stem (mint family)
- White to pale yellow small flowers
- Crush leaf: immediate strong lemon smell
- Hairy leaves; wrinkled surface texture

## The Lemon Smell Test

- Crush any leaf between fingers
- Strong clean lemon scent = confirmed
- Melissa officinalis: strongest smell
- No other mint-family plant smells exactly like this

## Where to Find

- Gardens, roadsides, disturbed areas
- Escaped cultivation; widely naturalized
- Moist soil in partial shade
- Spring through fall availability

## Making Lemon Balm Tea

- Steep fresh or dry leaves in hot water
- 5-10 minutes; mild pleasant flavor
- More leaves = stronger effect
- Drink 2-3 cups for anxiety relief

## Antiviral Properties

- Active against herpes simplex virus
- Apply strong tea topically to cold sores
- Reduces healing time significantly
- Apply cool used teabag to lip/skin lesion

## Stress Sleep Support

- Mild anxiolytic effects in healthy adults
- Works well combined with valerian
- Calming without causing heavy drowsiness
- Good daytime stress-reduction option

## Edible Uses

- Leaves edible raw as flavoring
- Add to any water for flavor improvement
- High vitamin C in fresh leaves
- Morale-improving; important in survival stress
