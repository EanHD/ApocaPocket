# Basic Chemistry

## Overview

- Elements: pure substances that cannot
  be broken down by ordinary means
- 92 naturally occurring elements
- Compounds: elements bonded together
- Mixtures: elements or compounds combined
  but not bonded; separable
- Reactions: bonds broken and reformed
- Energy released or absorbed in reactions
- Chemistry underlies all craft processes

## States of Matter

- Solid: definite shape and volume
- Liquid: definite volume; no fixed shape
- Gas: no fixed shape or volume
- Plasma: ionized gas; rare in daily life
- Changes between states: melt, freeze,
  boil, condense, sublime (solid to gas)
- Temperature and pressure control state

## Acids and Bases

- Acid: donates hydrogen ions; tastes sour
- Examples: vinegar (acetic), lemon (citric)
- Base: accepts hydrogen ions; feels slippery
- Examples: lye (KOH), ash water, lime
- Neutral: pH 7 (pure water)
- Acid + base = salt + water (neutralize)
- pH affects: fermentation, soil, tanning

## Oxidation and

- Oxidation: element loses electrons
  (combines with oxygen; iron rusting)
- Reduction: element gains electrons
  (removes oxygen; iron smelting)
- Fire is rapid oxidation
- Smelting is reduction: ore to metal
- Rusting is slow oxidation
- Oil prevents oxidation by excluding air

## Key Reactions to Know

- Lime + water: exothermic; make limewater
- Vinegar + baking soda: CO2 production
- Lye + fat: saponification (soap)
- Charcoal + oxygen: combustion
- Acid + metal: hydrogen gas produced
- Fermentation: sugars to alcohol + CO2

## Chemical Safety

- Unknown reaction: start very small
- Lye, strong acids: corrosive; wear
  leather gloves, protect eyes
- Chlorine bleach + ammonia: toxic gas
- Carbon monoxide: no smell; CO kills
- Ventilate all chemical work spaces
- Neutralize spills with opposite pH

## Long-Term Value

- Chemistry underpins soap, dye, medicine,
  metallurgy, and food preservation
- Understanding beats guessing every time
- Teachable framework for all craft work
- Record successful formulas precisely
