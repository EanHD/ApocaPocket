# Weather Science

## Overview

- Weather: state of atmosphere at a place
- Driven by: solar heat, earth rotation,
  ocean, and terrain differences
- Pressure: weight of air above a point
- High pressure: descending air; fair weather
- Low pressure: rising air; clouds and rain
- Fronts: boundaries between air masses
- Forecasting: pattern recognition + science

## Barometric Pressure

- Rising pressure: improving weather
- Falling pressure: deteriorating weather
- Steady rapid fall: storm approaching
- Make barometer: sealed tube, liquid,
  calibrate against known readings
- Aneroid barometer: spring-based; portable
- Record pressure twice daily; note trend

## Cloud Types and

- Cirrus (high, wispy): fair; may mean
  weather change in 24-48 hours
- Cumulus (puffy, low): fair weather
- Cumulonimbus (tall, anvil): storm now
- Stratus (flat, layer): drizzle or fog
- Nimbostratus (dark, flat): steady rain
- Alto (mid-level prefix): moderate height
- Cloud sequence: cumulus to cumulonimbus
  in hours means thunderstorm developing

## Wind Indicators

- Wind shifts: counterclockwise (backing)
  often means deteriorating weather
- Clockwise (veering): often improving
- Strong wind from south/SW: storm often
- Northern Hemisphere: storm rotation
  counterclockwise; high clockwise
- South Hemisphere: opposite
- Calm before sudden storm: dangerous

## Temp and Humidity

- Dew point: temperature at which air
  reaches saturation; clouds, dew, fog form
- High humidity + high temp: heat danger
- Frost warning: clear sky, calm, cold
  (radiation frost: heat escapes to sky)
- Cloud cover insulates: no cloud, colder
- Fog: surface radiation cooling at night;
  usually clears by mid-morning

## Local Signs

- Red sky at night: sailors delight
  (high pressure to west, moving toward you)
- Red sky at morning: sailors warning
  (moisture-laden air approaching)
- Insects flying high: good weather
- Cattle lying down: rain often follows
- Smoke descending: low pressure; rain
- Swallows flying low: insects low; wet air

## Long-Term Value

- Saves crops from late frost with warning
- Keeps travelers from being caught in
  severe weather
- Enables planting calendar optimization
- Forecast skill grows with record keeping
