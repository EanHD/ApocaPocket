# Field Latrine Design

## Overview

- Cat hole: individual, short-duration use
- Straddle trench: small group, 1-3 days
- Deep trench: group, 1-4 weeks
- Bored hole: individual, semi-permanent
- Distance from water: 200 ft minimum
- Fecal-oral disease kills more in disaster
  than any other single sanitation factor
- Enforce rules from day one; habits set

## Cat Hole Rules

- Single-use personal hole
- Dig 6-8 in deep; not deeper
- Squat; use hole; cover immediately
- Cover with soil; tamp down firmly
- Mark used holes; or record GPS point
- Space holes at least 200 ft from water
- Do not reuse a cat hole
- Decomposition: 4-6 weeks in warm soil

## Straddle Trench

- 1 ft wide, 2.5 ft deep, 4 ft long
- Serves 8 people for 1-3 days
- Mark with stakes and privacy screen
- Sprinkle soil after each use
- Cover completely and mark when full
- Dig replacement trench before needed
- Locate latrine area downwind if possible

## Deep Trench Latrine

- 2 ft wide, 5-6 ft deep, 6-8 ft long
- Install seat boards over trench with holes
- Fly-proof cover between uses; essential
- Ash or lime added: reduces flies, odor
- Collect fly attractors and burn separately
- Serves 25 people for 2-4 weeks
- Abandon and fill when 2/3 full

## Sanitation Discipline

- Assign latrine area clearly; enforce
- No open defecation in any other area
- Hand-washing station at latrine exit:
  water, ash, or soap
- Children require supervision and teaching
- Post rules with pictures if needed
- Appoint sanitation officer in groups

## Closing a Latrine

- Fill with soil in 6 in layers; tamp each
- Mound 6 in above grade for settlement
- Mark location with post and date
- Do not excavate for 2 years
- Do not plant root crops for 5 years
- Map all latrine locations for future use

## Long-Term Value

- Cat hole enforcement alone reduces
  diarrheal disease deaths by 50%+
- Cheapest possible sanitation technology
- No materials required; just behavior
- Foundation for more permanent systems
