# Community Waste Mgmt

## Overview

- Waste categories: organic, human, ash,
  hazardous, dead animals
- Each needs separate handling protocol
- Unmanaged waste = vectors = disease
- Appoint waste management roles early
- Zone your settlement: clean vs waste areas
- Prevailing wind matters; waste downwind
- Distance from water: 200 ft minimum

## Waste Zones

- Latrine zone: designated, marked, managed
- Ash pit: for hearth and fire ash
- Organic pit: food scraps, composted
- Burn pit: combustible non-food waste
- Animal disposal zone: away from all else
- Clearly mark with physical barriers
- Enforce; no exceptions for community members

## Organic Waste

- Food scraps: bury or compost daily
- Do not leave exposed; attracts rodents
- Composting turns waste into resource
- Three-bin system: active, resting, done
- Cover each addition with carbon material
- Keep compost moist but not wet
- Ready in 6-12 months; pathogen-free if hot

## Ash and Incineration

- Burn ash: sprinkle in latrine for fly
  control and odor reduction
- Ash from wood: alkaline; kills pathogens
- Incinerator: simple brick or stone box
  with air gap at bottom for draft
- Burn medical waste, contaminated cloth
- Temperature must reach 600F+ for safety
- Burn only in calm wind conditions

## Dead Animal Disposal

- Burial: 4 ft deep, away from all water
- Lime layer above carcass speeds decay
- Never leave carcass exposed; disease risk
- Mark burial site; do not excavate
- Separate from any food burial caches
- Burning large animals: time and fuel
  intensive; burial preferred

## Vector Control

- Rats: remove food sources; seal storage
- Flies: cover latrines and food; fly strips
- Mosquitoes: eliminate standing water
- Lice: hygiene, clean bedding, combing
- Rodent traps placed around food stores
- Ash repels insects from storage areas

## Long-Term Value

- Disease prevention is infrastructure
- Waste management protects water supply
- Composting recycles nutrients to soil
- Establishes habits for sustainable living
- Sphere standards codify minimum norms
