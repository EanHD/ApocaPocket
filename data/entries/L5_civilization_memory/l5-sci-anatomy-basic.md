# Human Anatomy Basics

## Overview

- Anatomy: structure of the human body
- Body organized: cells, tissues, organs,
  systems, whole organism
- 11 major body systems
- Understanding enables better assessment
  and basic medical intervention
- Symmetry: most structures bilateral
- Planes: sagittal, frontal, transverse
  divide body for description

## Skeletal System

- 206 bones in adult; 270 in infant
- Skull protects brain; ribs protect chest
- Spine: 33 vertebrae; transmits load
- Long bones: femur, tibia, humerus, radius
- Joints: hinge (elbow), ball (hip),
  pivot (neck), gliding (wrist)
- Fracture sites: think about nearby vessels

## Muscular System

- 600+ muscles; move, stabilize, pump
- Skeletal: voluntary; attached to bones
- Cardiac: involuntary; heart only
- Smooth: involuntary; gut, vessels
- Tendons: connect muscle to bone
- Ligaments: connect bone to bone
- Strain = muscle; sprain = ligament

## Circulatory System

- Heart: 4 chambers; 2 sides
- Right side: blood to lungs for oxygen
- Left side: oxygenated blood to body
- Arteries: away from heart (high pressure)
- Veins: toward heart (low pressure)
- Capillaries: gas and nutrient exchange
- Femoral, brachial, carotid: major bleed sites

## Respiratory System

- Airways: nose, throat, trachea, bronchi
- Lungs: 5 lobes; left 2, right 3
- Diaphragm: main breathing muscle
- Inhalation: diaphragm contracts; lung fills
- Oxygen in; carbon dioxide out per breath
- Normal rate: 12-20 breaths per minute
- Labored breathing: assess airway first

## Digestive System

- Mouth, esophagus, stomach, small
  intestine, large intestine, rectum
- Small intestine: 20 ft; main absorption
- Liver: detoxification and bile production
- Kidneys: filter blood; produce urine
- Appendix: right lower quadrant; pain
  there may indicate appendicitis

## Long-Term Value

- Anatomy guides wound assessment
- Knowing vessels reduces fatal mistakes
- Fracture vs sprain identification
- Guide to examining and describing
  symptoms for remote consultation
- Teach as part of basic health literacy
