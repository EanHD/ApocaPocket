# Bloomery Iron Smelting

## Overview

- Bloomery: stone or clay shaft furnace
- Ore + charcoal + air = iron bloom
- Bloom: sponge iron with slag inclusions
- Not pig iron; direct reduced iron
- Wrought iron after working the bloom
- Temperature needed: 2200F (1200C)
- Bellows provide forced air (blast)
- One smelt: 2-6 hours for 5-15 lb bloom

## Ore Selection

- Best: magnetite (black, magnetic)
- Good: hematite (red-brown), limonite
- Bog iron: widely available; easy smelt
- Roast ore first: break down hydrates
- Crush to walnut size before charging
- Remove obvious waste rock (gangue)
- Test ore: hold magnet; grade by pull

## Charcoal Preparation

- Hardwood charcoal only; not soft wood
- Must be dry and hard; not crumbled
- Walnut-to-fist size pieces
- Break overly large pieces; no dust
- Charcoal is both fuel and reductant
- Production: separate charcoal entry
- Need 5-10 lb charcoal per lb of bloom

## Bloomery Construction

- Clay and stone shaft, 12-18 in diameter
- Height: 3-5 ft for good draft
- Tuyere: air pipe through wall near base
- Tuyere angle: 20-30 degrees downward
- Slag tap hole at base; below tuyere
- Clay interior; fire to harden before use
- Drain hole important; slag pool kills smelt

## Smelting Process

- Light charcoal base; allow to ignite fully
- Begin bellows: steady rhythmic blast
- Charge alternating ore and charcoal
- Keep top charged as material drops
- Slag tapping: every 30-40 min
- Watch tuyere color: yellow-white = correct
- Bloom forms at tuyere level; 3-5 hr in

## Extracting the Bloom

- Stop bellows; allow brief cooling
- Knock out tuyere; extract bloom with tongs
- Bloom glows orange; hammer immediately
- Initial consolidation hammering: removes
  slag, welds iron, compacts structure
- This is shingling: 10-15 heat cycles
- Resulting bar: wrought iron billet

## Long-Term Value

- Unlocks iron tools from local materials
- No mining industry needed; bog iron works
- One bloomery serves a village
- Skills learnable; Roman-era technology
- Iron tools multiply agricultural output
