# Fly and Vector Control

## Overview

- Flies transmit cholera, typhoid, dysentery
- Breed in feces, food waste, dead animals
- Life cycle: 7-10 days in warm weather
- Interrupt breeding to reduce population
- Mechanical traps and physical barriers
  more reliable than chemical sprays
- Ash is the simplest multi-purpose tool
- Combined: latrine cover + ash + fly strip

## Fly Breeding Sites

- Human feces: primary site; cover always
- Food waste: bury or compost daily
- Animal manure: manage and compost
- Dead animals: bury within 24 hours
- Stagnant water: mosquito breeding
- Rotting vegetation: remove or turn
- Eliminate all exposed organic waste

## Latrine Fly Control

- Tight lid on latrine seat: most important
- Ventilation pipe with fly screen at top
- Dark interior: flies attracted to light
  in pipe, caught at screen, die
- Add 2 tbsp ash or lime after each use
- Ash raises pH; discourages larvae
- Never leave latrine seat uncovered

## Sticky Fly Strips

- Coat paper strips with pine resin,
  castor oil, or sugar-resin mix
- Hang near food prep and latrines
- Replace when covered with flies
- Boil pine resin; cool; paint on paper
- Brown paper or birch bark both work
- Effective, zero-cost supplement

## Mosquito Control

- Eliminate all standing water weekly
- Cover water storage with tight lids
- Drain low spots or fill with gravel
- Mosquito net over sleeping area
- Smoke from smudge fires repels at dusk
- Screen windows with fine woven cloth
- Plant aromatic herbs: mint, basil nearby

## Rodent Control

- Rats carry leptospirosis, plague, typhus
- Remove food access: covered storage
  in metal, ceramic, or stone containers
- Snap traps along walls; rats follow walls
- Check and reset traps daily
- Cats are effective passive control
- Eliminate harborage: brush piles, debris

## Long-Term Value

- Vector control is disease prevention
- No medicine required; behavior-based
- Community enforcement needed to work
- Ash and lime are free in all settlements
- Combined measures cut fly burden 90%+
