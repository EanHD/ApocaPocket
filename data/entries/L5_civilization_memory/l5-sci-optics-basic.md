# Basic Optics

## Overview

- Optics: behavior of light
- Reflection: light bounces off surface
- Refraction: light bends at medium boundary
- Lens: curved transparent object bends
  light to converge or diverge
- Mirror: curved reflective surface focuses
- Applications: magnification, fire-starting,
  signaling, instruments, eyeglasses

## Reflection

- Angle of incidence = angle of reflection
- Flat mirror: virtual image; reversed L-R
- Concave mirror: focuses light at focal pt
  (like satellite dish or searchlight)
- Convex mirror: wide field, smaller image
  (rear-view mirror, security mirror)
- Polish metal mirror: bronze or silver
- Mirror grid: searchlight or fire-starter

## Refraction

- Light bends when crossing media boundary
- More dense medium: light bends toward
  normal (toward perpendicular line)
- Water looks shallower than it is
- Prism: separates white light to spectrum
- Total internal reflection: fiber optic
  principle; signal glass rods
- Eyeglass correction: refraction applied

## Converging Lens

- Convex lens: thicker in center
- Parallel rays converge at focal point
- Focal length: distance from lens to focus
- Objects beyond focal length: inverted image
- Objects inside focal length: magnified image
- Magnifying glass, projector, telescope
  objective all converging lenses

## Diverging Lens

- Concave lens: thinner in center
- Parallel rays spread apart
- Used for correction of nearsightedness
- Combined with converging lens in
  compound optical instruments
- Eyeglasses made from polished glass
  or clear curved mineral surfaces

## Fire-Starting with

- Clear convex lens focused on tinder
- Magnifying glass, eyeglass, bottle
  filled with clear water also work
- Move lens until smallest bright dot
  forms on tinder surface
- Tinder: char cloth, dry moss, punk fungus
- Takes 30 seconds to 2 minutes in sun
- Requires clear sky and direct sunlight

## Long-Term Value

- Magnification enables quality inspection
- Fire-starting without flint or steel
- Signaling mirror: visible 20+ miles
- Corrective lenses: restore productivity
  to vision-impaired community members
- Telescope: navigation and astronomy
