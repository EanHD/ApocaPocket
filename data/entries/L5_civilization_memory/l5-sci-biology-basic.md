# Basic Biology

## Overview

- Biology: study of living organisms
- All life shares: cells, metabolism,
  reproduction, response to environment
- Classification: kingdom to species
- Understanding biology improves farming,
  medicine, and environmental management
- Evolution: organisms change over
  generations; drives all adaptation

## The Cell

- Smallest unit of life; all organisms made
  of one or more cells
- Cell membrane: controls what enters/exits
- Cytoplasm: fluid interior of cell
- Nucleus: contains genetic information
- Prokaryotes (bacteria): no nucleus
- Eukaryotes: nucleus present
- Plants have cell wall; animals do not

## Classification System

- Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family,
  Genus, Species
- Mnemonic: King Philip Came Over For
  Good Soup
- Genus + Species = scientific name
- Two species that interbreed = same species
- Classification predicts traits and
  relationships between organisms

## Reproduction

- Asexual: one parent; clone offspring
  (bacteria, some plants, fungi)
- Sexual: two parents; mixed offspring
- Seeds: plant embryo with food store
- Spores: fungi and fern reproduction
- Vegetative: cuttings, tubers, division
- All farming relies on reproduction biology

## Metabolism

- Metabolism: all chemical reactions in
  an organism that maintain life
- Photosynthesis: plants convert light
  to sugar using CO2 and water
- Respiration: all cells burn sugar for
  energy; produces CO2 and water
- Both processes cycle carbon and oxygen
  through the ecosystem

## Ecology Basics

- Organism: one living thing
- Population: same species in area
- Community: all species in area
- Ecosystem: community + environment
- Food chain: who eats whom
- Decomposers: recycle nutrients to soil
- Balance: remove top predator = cascade

## Long-Term Value

- Biology explains disease, soil health,
  plant growth, animal breeding
- Classification helps identify edibles,
  medicinals, and dangers
- Understanding ecology guides land use
- Teaches stewardship; prevents mistakes
