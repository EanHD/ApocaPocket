# Outhouse Construction

## Overview

- Pit privy: simplest permanent sanitation
- Pit depth: 5-6 ft minimum
- Seat height: 18 in off floor
- Enclosure: privacy and fly control
- Vent pipe: reduces odor and flies
- Move when pit is 2/3 full; fill old pit
- One outhouse per 10 people minimum

## Site Selection

- 100 ft minimum from any water source
- Downhill from wells and springs
- Downhill from garden areas
- Away from flood zones; flooding spreads
  waste over large area and contaminates
- Mark used locations to prevent reuse
- Upwind from living areas if possible

## Pit Digging

- 4x4 ft footprint; 5-6 ft deep
- Line walls with dry-stone or boards
  if soil is unstable or sandy
- Leave bottom and lower walls open
  so liquid percolates into soil
- Do not line bottom; percolation needed
- Mark pit corners with stakes during dig

## Enclosure Construction

- Simple post-and-board or cob walls
- Roof required; rain fills pit fast
- Door on the downwind side
- No lock; simple latch for privacy
- Floor joists bridge pit; support seat
- Floor boards close except seat hole

## Seat and Lid

- Seat hole: oval 10x14 in at comfortable
  height; smooth all edges to prevent
  splinters (important for daily use)
- Tight-fitting wooden lid: keeps flies out
- Lid must be replaced after each use
- Teach this practice explicitly

## Vent Pipe System

- 4 in pipe from pit through roof peak
- Fly screen on top of pipe
- Dark pipe absorbs heat; creates updraft
- Updraft draws odor out of pit
- Flies enter pipe, cannot escape screen
- Works best when outhouse interior dark

## Long-Term Value

- Prevents fecal-oral disease transmission
- Cholera, typhoid, dysentery all reduced
- Ash or lime added: reduces odor and flies
- Simple enough any community can build
- UNESCO estimates 50% of preventable
  deaths avoided with basic sanitation
