# Strategy: Start Here

## Use this when:
- A crisis has just started and you need a prioritized action order
- You are pre-planning household or community resilience
- You face vehicle breakdown, extended outage, or neighborhood disruption

## Priority Order
- Hour 0-1: Confirm immediate safety; account for all people in your group
- Hour 1-3: Secure water supply (72-hour minimum); locate food reserves
- Hour 3-6: Assess communications; notify contacts of your status/location
- Hour 6-12: Document situation (photos, notes); inventory critical supplies
- Hour 12-24: Establish shelter-in-place or bug-out decision; connect with neighbors

## Entries Compared:
- Vehicle Breakdown Remote: isolation risk, signaling, shelter-in-vehicle protocol
- Urban Outage Strategy: utility failure, heat/cold management, community grid
- Neighborhood Mutual Aid: resource pooling, skill mapping, trust-building fast
- Documentation: paper records, photo evidence, priority docs to protect

## Key Rules:
- 72-hour rule: assume no outside help for 72 hours; plan to be self-sufficient
- Communication first: people who know your plan can help; silence is dangerous
- Don't go it alone: neighborhood coordination multiplies survival odds
