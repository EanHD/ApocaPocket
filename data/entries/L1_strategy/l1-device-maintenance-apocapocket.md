# ApocaPocket Device

## Battery Charging

Keep charge between 20-80% for maximum lifespan.
- Do not hold at 100% for extended periods
- Do not drain below 10%
- Charge in shade or cool air — heat degrades cells
- Before long-term storage: charge to 50%
- Expected field lifespan: 3-5 years

## SD Card Care

- Remove only when device is powered off or safely ejected
- Keep away from moisture, dirt, and magnetic sources
- Do not bend. Store in case when not inserted.
- Format only with the device's built-in format tool
- Replace if files fail to load or corruption appears

## Screen Cleaning

- Dry soft cloth only. No paper towels.
- No liquid directly on screen.
- Slightly damp cloth (water only) for stubborn dirt.
- Never press hard on display. Field cracks do not self-repair.

## Firmware Updates

1. Download update file to SD card from trusted source.
2. Power off device fully.
3. Insert SD card. Power on while holding volume-down.
4. Select Update from menu. Do not remove SD during update.
5. Device reboots automatically. Confirm version in Settings.

## Troubleshooting

Won't boot: hold power 10 sec, release, hold again to restart.
- No response: charge 30 min first — dead battery will not boot.
Display blank (device on): adjust brightness in Settings > Display.
- Horizontal lines or cracks: hardware damage, not field-repairable.
SD not reading: remove card, wipe contacts with dry cloth, reinsert.
- Still failing: test card in another device to confirm damage.
