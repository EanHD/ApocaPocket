# Elderly Emergency Prep

## Medications
- Full medication list with dosages
- 30-day supply in go bag
- Refrigerated meds: cold pack plan
- Pill organizer: labeled by day and time
- Pharmacy emergency transfer plan

## Mobility Planning
- Walker or cane: in go-bag or vehicle
- Ramp access: know route from home
- Practice evacuation route monthly
- Assistance arrangement: pre-agreed
- Medical alert device: wear always

## Heat Management
- Elderly: heat illness at lower temps
- Air conditioning: arrange early in heat
- Check-in: every 2 hours in heat wave
- Hydration: 8 oz water hourly in heat
- Cooling centers: locate 2 options nearby

## Cold Management
- Hypothermia risk higher in elderly
- Internal temp falls fast without heat
- Layer early: before feeling cold
- Heated interior room: maintain 68F+
- Hot beverages: warming and hydrating

## Cognitive Planning
- Simple plan: fewer steps is better
- Written checklist: large print
- Practice until steps are automatic
- Caregiver present if cognitive impairment
- ID bracelet: name and emergency contact

## Support Network
- Identify 2 neighbors as check-in persons
- Daily phone check-in system
- Register with local emergency management
- Doctor informed of living situation
- Social worker contact if isolated
