# Group Survival Dynamics

## Why It Matters

- Groups often survive when individuals
  would not, if organized correctly
- Conflict in crisis has caused death
- Clear roles prevent paralysis
- Morale is a group responsibility
- Leadership should be fluid by skill

## Role Assignment

- Leader: decision-making authority
- Medic: any with training takes this
- Navigator: map/compass competent
- Shelter/fire: physical workers
- Morale: keeps spirits constructive
- Assign within first 30 minutes

## Stress Decisions

- Small group, 1-2 person consensus
- Leader has final say in deadlock
- No prolonged debate in emergency
- Revisit decisions when safe to do so
- Explain decisions briefly to group
- Prevent group decision paralysis

## Avoid Group Failure

- No exclusion or scapegoating
- Give everyone meaningful tasks
- Acknowledge fear and difficulty
- Hold brief group check-ins daily
- Rotate hardest tasks
- Celebrate every small success

## Managing Conflict

- Address early, not when it explodes
- Separate parties if needed briefly
- Redirect energy to shared tasks
- Shared enemy (the situation) unites
- Avoid personal blame language
- Focus on what needs to happen next

## What Can Go Wrong

- Passive members who do not engage
- Dominant personality causes poor decisions
- Conflict left unresolved festers
- Hierarchy that ignores best skills

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Assign all 5 roles in
  a group within 5 minutes
- Sign of mastery: Group reaches
  consensus on stay/go in 10 min
