# Survival Priority Order

## Why It Matters

- Wrong order wastes limited energy
- Foraging for food while cold = death
- Priorities change by environment
- This order saves lives consistently
- Know it before you need it
- Recite it when confused

## Priority 1: Protection

- From immediate environmental threats
- Extreme cold, heat, rain, wind
- Dangerous terrain or hostiles
- Injury treatment comes first too
- Stabilize situation before anything
- Shelter before water in cold

## Priority 2: Location

- Rescuers must be able to find you
- Signal immediately if rescue expected
- Stay near last known point if possible
- Establish your position on map
- Leave trail markers if moving
- This priority is time-sensitive

## Priority 3: Water

- Death from dehydration in 3 days
- Urgency depends on climate
- Hot desert: hours before critical
- Cool climate: days before critical
- Secure water source before rest
- All water needs purification

## Priority 4: Food

- Starvation takes 3+ weeks typically
- Food seeking burns energy fast
- Do not forage instead of shelter
- Secure other priorities first
- High-calorie passive foraging best
- Snares and fishing over hunting

## Adjust by Environment

- Desert: water may beat shelter
- Arctic: shelter is immediate and first
- Injured: medical care beats all
- Group: location/signal is first act
- Urban: concealment may beat location

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: State priority order from
  memory in correct sequence
- Sign of mastery: Apply correct
  order to 5 different scenarios
