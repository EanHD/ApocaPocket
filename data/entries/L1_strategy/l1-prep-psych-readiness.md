# Psych Readiness

## Stress Inoculation
- Expose yourself to controlled stress
- Practice skills under mild discomfort
- Cold shower: tolerance training
- Practice fire-starting in wind and rain
- Time-pressure drills build calm action

## The OODA Loop
- Observe: what is actually happening
- Orient: what do I know about this
- Decide: choose the best action
- Act: commit and execute fully
- Speed up loop with practice

## Freeze Response
- Freeze is normal: 85% of people do it
- Pre-planning bypasses freeze
- Simple checklists reduce freeze time
- Practice activates muscle memory
- Verbal cue: say action out loud first

## Managing Fear
- Fear is information, not the enemy
- Identify the specific threat
- Focus on what you can control
- Action reduces fear: start something
- Breathing: 4 count in, 4 count hold, 4 out

## Decision Under
- Binary decisions are easier: go or stay
- Pre-commit trigger points in advance
- Avoid decision fatigue: simplify choices
- Speed beats perfection in survival
- Commit and adjust rather than hesitate

## Family Emotional
- Normalize preparedness: not fear-based
- Practice calm tone during drills
- Children: routine reduces fear response
- Debrief after drills: feelings matter
- Model confident calm for others
