# Bug-Out Bag Essentials

## Water and Purification
- 2 liters water in durable containers
- Water filter (LifeStraw or Sawyer)
- Iodine tablets: backup purification
- Collapsible water bladder: 2L capacity
- Stainless steel bottle: boil water use

## Food (72 hours)
- High-calorie compact bars: 2000 cal/day
- No-cook options: bars, jerky, nuts
- Electrolyte packets: 6-10 servings
- Emergency ration bar: 3600 cal block
- Small stove and fuel: optional warmth

## Shelter and Warmth
- Emergency Mylar bivy: 2 oz, reflects heat
- Compact tarp 8x10 ft minimum
- 50 ft paracord
- Fire starter: ferro rod plus lighter
- Wool or synthetic hat and gloves

## First Aid
- IFAK (individual first aid kit)
- Prescription medications: 7-day supply
- Extra eyeglasses or contacts
- N95 masks: 5 pack minimum
- Nitrile gloves: 2 pairs

## Documents and Comms
- Copies of ID and insurance cards
- $200 cash in small bills
- Hand-crank or battery radio
- Phone charger and power bank
- Local map printed on paper

## Weight Guidelines
- Target under 30 lb total weight
- Eliminate luxury and duplicate items
- Heaviest items: closest to your back
- Practice carry: build fitness
- Reassess contents every 6 months
