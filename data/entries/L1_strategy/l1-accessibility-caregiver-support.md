# Caregiver Support

## Fatigue Warning Signs

Caregiver breakdown puts BOTH people at risk. Stop if you notice:
- Dropping things, stumbling, or trembling hands
- Cannot think clearly or make basic decisions
- Crying without knowing why
- Resentment or rage toward the care recipient
- Forgetting medications or tasks
- Skipping your own food, water, or sleep

## Sustainable Rotation

Never let one person carry all care.
- Assign shifts: max 4-6 hrs continuous caregiving
- Schedule mandatory rest before you hit the wall
- Wake your relief partner on time  -  don't push through
- Keep a simple log: who's on, who's resting, what was done
- Eating and sleeping are non-negotiable for the caregiver

## When to Ask for Help

Ask for help immediately if:
- You have not slept in 24+ hours
- You are in physical pain from lifting or transfers
- You feel you cannot keep the person safe
- Medical needs exceed your skill level
Say it out loud to the group. Silence risks both of you.

## Delegatable Tasks

These do NOT require the primary caregiver:
- Fetching water, food prep, and supply runs
- Sitting watch (presence only, not skilled care)
- Medication reminders (if doses are pre-organized)
- Laundry, cleaning the care area, changing bedding
- Relaying communications to the rest of the group
Ask others clearly and specifically. Vague requests get ignored.
