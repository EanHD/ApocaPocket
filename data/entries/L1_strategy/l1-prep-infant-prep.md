# Infant Emergency Prep

## Feeding
- Formula: 2-week supply minimum
- Ready-to-feed formula: no water needed
- Extra bottles and sterilization supplies
- Nursing mothers: extra calorie stores
- No honey: under 12 months, dangerous

## Water Safety
- Never use contaminated water for formula
- Boil water even during boil order
- Bottled water: safest emergency option
- Formula dosing requires accurate measure
- Electrolyte solutions: for diarrhea only

## Warmth and Clothing
- Infants lose heat 4x faster than adults
- Layer clothing: hat is critical in cold
- Swaddling retains body heat well
- Skin-to-skin: best warmth method
- Keep feet and hands covered at all times

## Diapering
- 2-week supply of diapers
- Waterless wipes: conserves water
- Diaper rash cream: prevent in stress
- Hand hygiene before and after diapering
- Waterproof pad for changing surface

## Medical
- Infant acetaminophen: per weight dose
- Pediatric thermometer
- Pediatric first aid reference card
- Nasal aspirator for illness
- Know infant CPR before needed

## Evacuation with Infant
- Front carrier: hands free and warm
- Car seat in go vehicle always
- Infant medical records in bag
- Extra clothes: 4 full outfit changes
- Comfort item: familiar smell reduces stress
