# Urban Grid-Down

## Why It Matters

- Grids fail for storms, cyber, disaster
- 72 hours is critical decision window
- Water supply fails within hours
- Medical equipment depends on power
- Stores empty in hours not days
- Preparation done now, not during

## Hours 0-6 Actions

- Fill bathtubs with water immediately
- Fill all containers with tap water
- Check and note all food on hand
- Charge all devices immediately
- Locate candles and flashlights
- Check on neighbors in buildings

## Water Security

- Bathtub = 80-100 gallons
- Average need: 1 gallon per person per day
- Toilet tank water is drinkable
- Hot water tank: 40-80 gallons
- Swimming pool water: filter and purify
- Rain barrel if you have one

## Heat and Cold

- Congregate in one room to share heat
- South-facing rooms warmer in winter
- Block door drafts with rolled towels
- Sleeping bags and layers in bedroom
- Do not run generator indoors
- Candles produce carbon monoxide too

## Food Management

- Eat fridge food first within 4 hrs
- Freezer food safe 24-48 hrs closed
- Shelf stable: canned, dry goods next
- No cooking fuel? Eat no-cook foods
- Prioritize calorie density items
- Ration proportionally from day 1

## What Can Go Wrong

- Water contamination after pipe loss
- Generator CO poisoning indoors
- Panic purchases before assessment
- Leaving home unnecessarily early

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: 72-hr plan written for
  your specific household
- Sign of mastery: Execution of
  plan in first 6 hours from memory
