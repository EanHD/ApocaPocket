# 3-Day Survival Plan

## Why It Matters

- First 24 hours: instinct and reaction
- Days 2-3 require deliberate planning
- Energy is finite and must be managed
- Resources used now reduce options
- Day 3 decisions determine week 1
- Planning is as important as action

## Day 1: Stabilize

- STOP and assess before acting
- Treat injuries first
- Secure immediate shelter and water
- Create signal if rescue likely
- Inventory all resources on hand
- Do not exhaust yourself yet

## Day 2: Assess

- Stay or move decision required
- Evaluate rescue likelihood honestly
- Map resources: water, food, fuel
- Improve shelter if staying
- Ration food to 3-day supply
- Make this decision before hunger sets in

## Day 3: Act

- If no rescue contact: commit to move
- If rescue active: improve signals
- Conserve energy for movement day
- Carry only essentials if moving
- Leave note at camp with direction
- Daylight hours only for travel

## Resource Budget

- Water: 2 L minimum per day
- Calories: 1,500 minimum to function
- Sleep: 6 hrs minimum per night
- Fire fuel: 4 hrs ahead at all times
- Energy: 50% physical capacity max
- Reserve 20% for emergencies always

## Mental Check Schedule

- Morning: assess overnight and plan
- Midday: are you on plan?
- Evening: what worked? What changes?
- Document decisions if possible
- Partner debrief if group survival
- Adjust without shame

## What Can Go Wrong

- Moving too early, before assessing
- Consuming all water day 1
- Making day 3 decision on day 1
- Ignoring injury because of urgency

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Write a complete 3-day plan
  for a simulated scenario
- Sign of mastery: Adapt plan on
  day 2 based on changed conditions
