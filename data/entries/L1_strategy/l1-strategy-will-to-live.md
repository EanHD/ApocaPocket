# Will to Live

## Why It Matters

- Survivors and victims often had
  the same skills and gear
- Mental state is the key variable
- US Air Force SERE data: attitude
  separated survivors from victims
- Fear is normal; panic is a choice
- Task focus overrides despair

## The Survival Mindset

- Acknowledge fear, then act anyway
- Emotions inform but do not decide
- Focus on immediate next task only
- Do not think about full situation
- One task at a time: water, shelter
- Break survival into 10-min goals

## Reasons to Keep Going

- Think of specific people waiting
- Name reasons out loud if needed
- Write them if you have paper
- Anger works as well as hope
- Curiosity is a survival emotion
- Pride: you will tell this story

## Managing Hopelessness

- Dark thoughts are normal, not fact
- Distinguish emotion from reality
- Ask: what can I do right now?
- Physical action shifts mental state
- Improving your situation = hope
- Do not make decisions in despair

## Group Psychology

- One person hopeful helps others
- Assign tasks to prevent withdrawal
- Celebrate small wins together
- Prevent isolation within group
- Rotate leadership to share burden
- Morale is a survival resource

## What Can Go Wrong

- Inaction due to overwhelm
- Waiting to feel ready to act
- Catastrophizing one setback
- Isolation from group

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Identify 3 personal reasons
  to persevere in difficult moments
- Sign of mastery: Maintain task
  focus through a 30-min adversity drill
