# Get Home Bag

## Purpose and Scope
- Designed for 24-hour return trip home
- Store at work desk or in vehicle
- Lightweight: max 10-15 lb total
- Assumes walking is required
- Urban and suburban focus

## Core Contents
- 1 liter water minimum
- Water filter or purification tabs
- 1 day food: 1500-2000 cal compact
- Comfortable walking shoes or boots
- Phone battery bank: 10,000 mAh min

## Navigation
- Printed map of route home
- Compass: small and compact
- Backup route marked on map
- Emergency contact list on paper
- Neighborhood map 5-mile radius

## Safety Items
- Small first aid kit
- Cash: $50 in small bills
- Flashlight: compact AA or USB charged
- Whistle
- Gloves and rain poncho
- N95 mask (dust and smoke)

## Communications
- Phone charger cable
- Personal FM radio
- Written contact list: no phone needed
- List of check-in points along route

## Seasonal Additions
- Summer: extra water and sunscreen
- Winter: hand warmers and balaclava
- Rain: waterproof bag liner
- Review and restock every 3 months
- Adjust for current medications
