# Water Storage at Home

## Minimum Requirements
- 1 gallon per person per day
- 3-day minimum: 3 gal x people
- 2-week target: 14 gal x people
- Include pets in calculation
- Account for hot weather increase

## Container Types
- Blue 55-gal barrel: best long-term
- 5-gal food-grade jugs: portable
- Commercial bottled water: convenient
- WaterBOB bathtub liner: emergency fill
- Never use milk jugs or juice containers

## Water Treatment Before
- Tap water: already treated, store as-is
- Well water: add 8 drops bleach per gal
- Use unscented household bleach only
- Label container with fill date
- Store away from direct sunlight

## Rotation Schedule
- Commercial bottles: replace at expiration
- Stored tap water: replace every 6 months
- Mark dates on all containers clearly
- Use FIFO: oldest water goes first
- Test stored water before drinking

## Storage Location
- Cool dark area preferred
- Not directly on concrete: leach risk
- Not near chemicals, fuel, or pesticides
- Elevated off floor in flood risk area
- Accessible without moving heavy items

## Emergency Quick Fill
- Fill bathtub immediately when warning
- Fill all pots and pans with water
- WaterBOB holds 100 gal in bathtub
- Fill before municipal pressure drops
- Freeze water for cold storage bonus
