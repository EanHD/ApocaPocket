# Music & Instrument Making

## Why Music Matters

- Every human culture makes music  -  biological imperative, not coincidence
- Music reduces anxiety 65% (British Journal of Psychiatry, 2013)
- Group singing releases oxytocin equal to physical touch
- PTSD symptoms reduced 43% with music therapy (Journal of Traumatic Stress, 2015)
- When a community stops making music, it is in crisis

## Bamboo Flute

**Materials:** bamboo section 30-40 cm x 2-2.5 cm diameter, drill or hot rod, sandpaper, cork plug

**Steps:**
1. Cut between nodes (hollow chamber); sand smooth
2. Plug one end with cork or wood
3. Blow hole: 2 cm from stopped end, 1.2 cm diameter, chamfered at 45 degrees
4. Six finger holes spaced 1.8-2.2 cm apart; start small, enlarge to tune
5. Practice 5-10 min daily; clear tone develops in 1-2 weeks

- Pentatonic scale (5 holes): no wrong-note combinations; universally musical
- Tune by enlarging holes slightly; larger hole = higher pitch

## Pan Pipes & Whistle

**Pan pipes:** cut tubes at ~20/18/16/14.5/13 cm; stop ends with wax; bind side-by-side; adjust wax depth to tune

**Willow whistle (15 min):** cut 10-15 cm thumb-thick branch; tap bark loose (spring sap); slide bark off; cut notch + air channel in wood; slide bark back on

## Drums & Percussion

**Frame drum:**
1. Bend green wood into 30-50 cm ring; lash ends
2. Soak animal skin (or thick fabric) 2-4 hours
3. Stretch over frame while wet; lace around perimeter
4. Dry in warm place; skin shrinks tight

- Drumming induces theta brain waves; reduces stress hormones; creates group synchrony

**Shakers/rattles (5 min):** gourd or bamboo tube + seeds/pebbles inside; plug ends

**Clave sticks:** two hardwood sticks 20 cm x 2.5 cm; cut and sand; universal rhythm foundation

## String Instruments

**Diddley bow (30-60 min):**
1. 60-90 cm wood plank
2. String wire between two raised bridges (bolts/nails at each end)
3. Place tin can under string for resonance
4. Pluck or slide with bottle neck; tune by adjusting wire tension

**Mouth bow:** bend flexible branch; string taut between ends; hold near mouth; pluck; shape mouth to change pitch

## Group Music

**Singing circles:** sit in circle; leader starts simple phrase; others join in unison or harmony; repeat until meditative  -  no instruments needed

**Drumming circles:** start simple 4/4 beat; layer complexity; 20+ min sustained rhythm induces theta state

**Call-and-response:** leader sings a line, group repeats; low-pressure participation; encodes knowledge in memory

**Work songs:** coordinate effort and reduce perceived exertion; historical use in harvesting, rowing, construction

## Music Therapy

- **Anxiety:** slow 60 BPM rhythm, predictable pattern, 20-30 min group session
- **Depression:** upbeat 100-120 BPM, major key, movement (dance/clap), social setting critical
- **PTSD:** rhythmic/grounding, never force participation, allow emotional release
- **Grief:** minor key validates emotional state; collective mourning; permission to cry

## Warning Signs

- Community music stopping completely = depression indicator or collective trauma
- Intervention: start with humming/simple rhythm; gentle invitation; honor silence but keep offering

## Instrument Care

- Oil wood regularly (linseed or tung oil); repair cracks immediately
- Skin drumheads: moderate humidity; re-tension as needed; replace every 2-5 years
- Strings: keep backups; clean after use (sweat degrades); know tension limits

## Mission

1. Build 1 wind, 1 percussion, 1 string instrument
2. Memorize 20 songs: work, celebration, mourning, children's
3. Establish weekly music night; teach children now
4. 30 min daily music = 40% cortisol reduction; weekly group = 3x stronger social bonds
