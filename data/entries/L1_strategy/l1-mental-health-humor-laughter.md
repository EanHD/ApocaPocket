# Humor & Laughter

## Why Laughter Works

- Laughter is a human universal — biological survival adaptation, not cultural preference
- Cortisol (stress hormone) reduced 39% within 20 min of laughter
- Endorphin release: same pathways as exercise; immune NK cells +40%
- Shared laughter releases oxytocin (trust/bonding); stronger than conversation alone
- Trauma resilience: survivors who maintain humor have 3x better outcomes
- Loss of sense of humor = early warning sign of depression or despair

## Warning Signs

- No spontaneous laughter at meals, meetings, or work
- Everything feels serious; humor perceived as offensive or threatening
- People isolating, avoiding social contact
- Intervention: start small, model laughter yourself, create low-pressure opportunities

## Types of Humor

- **Jokes:** setup (expectation) + punchline (violation) = surprise = laughter; shareable, portable
- **Pranks:** surprise not danger; laughing WITH not AT; dignity preserved; never endanger or humiliate
- **Funny stories:** true/embellished mishaps; self-deprecation = trust + relatability
- **Improv games:** "Yes, And..." (build absurd scenario together); Fortunately/Unfortunately alternation
- **Physical comedy:** exaggerated falling, silly walks, face-pulling; universal across languages
- **Wordplay/puns:** groan = engagement; cognitive stimulation; builds insider group identity

## Group Activities

**Bad Joke Night (weekly):**
- Everyone brings worst joke they can find
- Groan = success; intentionally bad = low-pressure participation
- Ritual rhythm: same time weekly; gives people something to look forward to

**Funny Story Competition:**
- Theme: "Worst day ever," "Most embarrassing moment"
- 3-5 min per person; builds storytelling skill and vulnerability (bonding)

**Laughter Yoga:**
- Circle; start with "ho-ho, ha-ha"; make eye contact; contagion takes over
- Body can't distinguish fake from real laughter — same physiological benefits
- Reduces depression 35% over 8 weeks

**Comedy Roasts (high-trust groups only):**
- Volunteer in "hot seat"; gentle roasting; ends with genuine appreciation
- Punch up not down; 80% roast + 20% affirmation; skip if trust isn't solid

## Humor as Coping

- **Gallows humor:** acknowledge threat without being paralyzed; maintains agency; bonding
- Holocaust prisoners, POWs, and disaster workers all use it — described as "tiny freedom"
- Viktor Frankl prescribed humor as survival tool; organized secret comedy nights in Auschwitz
- Trauma survivors with humor = 2x better at complex problem-solving (cognitive reframing)
- Caution: if ONLY joking and never processing feelings, humor becomes avoidance

## Ethical Guidelines

- Punch up, not down (mock power/absurdity, not the vulnerable)
- Laugh WITH not AT (everyone laughing, including the target)
- Know your audience; fresh grief = inappropriate timing
- "I'm just kidding!" after a hurtful comment is not humor — address it directly

## Toxic Humor

- Constant mockery that deflects every serious topic (intimacy blocker)
- Repeated "jokes" targeting the same person (bullying disguised as humor)
- Manic forced laughter to avoid feelings (exhausting; people withdraw)
- Intervention: "That crossed a line"; private check-in with target; community accountability

## Trickster Figures

- Every culture has a trickster (Coyote, Anansi, Loki, Monkey King, Br'er Rabbit)
- Function: social critique through humor; permission to question authority; weak defeating strong
- Value your community's natural class clowns — they serve a vital social function
- Allow some controlled chaos: rigid = brittle; flexible = resilient

## Mission

1. Create weekly comedy events (bad joke night, funny story rotation)
2. Model humor: be first to laugh at yourself
3. Monitor: if laughter stops, intervene immediately
4. Balance humor + seriousness (humor is not a substitute for processing)
5. Teach children: silliness and play = resilience training

**Groups that laugh together survive together. A community without laughter is in crisis.**
