# Emergency Documents

## Critical Documents to
- Passport and birth certificate
- Social security cards for all family
- Insurance policies: home, health, auto
- Property deed or lease agreement
- Vehicle titles and registration
- Medical records and prescriptions

## Storage Options
- Waterproof pouch: keep with go-bag
- Fireproof safe: home storage
- Cloud backup: encrypted folder
- Trusted family member: off-site copy
- Safe deposit box: limited access speed

## Financial Documents
- Bank account numbers on paper
- Credit card company numbers
- Small cash reserve: $200-500
- Investment account contacts
- Employer contact and HR info

## Medical Information
- Blood type for all family members
- Allergy list per person
- Prescription list with dosages
- Doctor names and phone numbers
- Medical device serial numbers

## Digital Backup
- Scan all documents to encrypted PDF
- Store on encrypted USB drive
- Cloud backup: not only option
- Practice retrieving them before needed
- Update after any life change

## Grab List
- 5-minute grab: passport, cash, meds
- 15-minute grab: add insurance docs
- 30-minute grab: add full document kit
- Priority list posted inside go-bag
- Laminate copies for water resistance
