# Financial Preparedness

## Cash Reserve
- $500 minimum in small bills at home
- Mix: $1, $5, $10, $20 denominations
- Store in fireproof container
- Separate stash in vehicle
- Replenish after any use immediately

## Banking and Records
- Account numbers on paper: off-site copy
- Credit union vs bank: local may be better
- Multiple institutions: reduce single point
- Automatic bill pay: continues in disruption
- Account access without internet: call number

## Barter Goods Inventory
- High-value barter: fuel, medications
- Commodity barter: food, water, ammo
- Skill barter: medical, mechanical, trade
- Never expose full inventory to strangers
- Small quantities only for safety

## Insurance Review
- Home inventory: photos and video stored
- Insurance policies: copies off-site
- Know your deductibles and limits
- Flood insurance: separate from home policy
- Renter's insurance: often overlooked

## Income Disruption
- 3-month emergency fund minimum
- Know government assistance options
- Neighbor network: informal mutual aid
- Freelance skills identified in advance
- Reduced expense plan: pre-written

## Financial Records
- Shred all financial documents
- Freeze credit if ID theft risk present
- Monitor accounts during disruption
- Report fraud: have hotline numbers
- Cloud backup for tax and legal records
