# Food Storage Basics

## Caloric Targets
- 2000 cal per person per day minimum
- 3-day supply: minimum starting point
- 2-week target: realistic disruption
- 3-month goal: serious preparedness
- Note special diet needs per person

## High Density Staples
- White rice: 1680 cal/lb, 25 yr shelf life
- Hard wheat: long shelf, grind to flour
- Dried beans and lentils: protein dense
- Rolled oats: fast prep, good nutrition
- Pasta: 1600 cal/lb, 5-8 yr shelf life

## Ready-to-Eat Options
- Canned goods: 2-5 year shelf life
- Freeze-dried meals: 25 year shelf life
- Peanut butter: calorie dense, no cook
- Granola bars and jerky: short term
- MREs: 3-5 year, complete and portable

## Rotation System
- Label every item with purchase date
- FIFO: first in, first out practice
- Eat and replace monthly
- Full inventory every 6 months
- Dedicated storage space: not mixed

## Storage Conditions
- Cool dry dark location preferred
- Mylar bags with oxygen absorbers
- 5-gal buckets with gamma seal lids
- Off floor: moisture and pest control
- Away from chemicals and fuel

## Cooking Without Power
- Propane camp stove and extra fuel
- Rocket stove: wood fuel option
- Solar oven: free energy, slow cook
- Wood fire grill: dual use
- No-cook meals: reduce fuel needs
