# Information Kit

## Offline Maps
- State road atlas: full state coverage
- County map: local road detail
- Topographic map: region around home
- City street map: printed and laminated
- Trail maps: areas you may evacuate into

## Reference Manuals
- First aid manual: printed hard copy
- FEMA family preparedness guide
- Wildfire home protection guide
- Local emergency contact numbers
- Red Cross disaster handbook

## Contact Lists
- Family: all numbers printed on card
- Neighbors: 5 nearest with cell numbers
- Doctor, dentist, pharmacy numbers
- Utility companies emergency lines
- Local emergency management number

## Technical References
- Water purification methods quick card
- Generator start procedure printed
- Utility shutoff locations mapped
- Local shelter locations list
- Amateur radio frequencies by region

## Storage and Access
- Waterproof binder or bag for all docs
- Laminate highest-priority cards
- Copy in go-bag, copy in vehicle, copy home
- USB drive backup: encrypted
- Annual update: replace outdated maps

## Device Offline Info
- Download offline maps on phone
- ApocaPocket: field reference device
- Tablet with offline survival guides
- USB with PDF library of references
- Keep charged or hand-crank available
