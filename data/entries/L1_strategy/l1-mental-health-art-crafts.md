# Art & Crafts

## Why Art Matters

Creating beauty is psychological survival. Not optional. Not decoration.

- Flow state (45+ min absorbed) reduces cortisol 25%
- Art therapy as effective as CBT for mild-moderate depression
- Creating = asserting control in chaos
- Art predates agriculture. Every culture makes it. Stop making it = dying inside.

## Drawing

**Materials to make:**
- Charcoal: burn hardwood in low-oxygen fire 4–6 hours, cool, break into sticks
- Pigments: ochre (red/yellow), charcoal powder (black), kaolin clay (white)
- Binders: animal fat, egg yolk, or tree sap — mix with pigment
- Surfaces: smooth bark, leather, stone, cloth

**Techniques:**
- Basic shapes: everything = circles, squares, triangles. Combine them.
- Contour drawing: trace outline without looking at paper. Trains observation.
- Shading: smudge charcoal from light side toward dark with finger or cloth.

**Why drawing is a survival skill:**
- Maps, plant IDs, construction diagrams — visual beats written every time
- Language-free communication
- Anxiety relief: focused attention breaks rumination

## Carving

**Wood selection:** basswood, willow, pine (soft = easier). Avoid rotten or brittle wood.
**Tools:** knife (primary), gouge (bowls), chisel (detail), sandstone (finish).
**Safety:** cut away from body. Sharp knife = controlled cut = safer.

**Beginner project — spoon:**
1. Split blank 15 × 5 × 2 cm
2. Sketch outline: side view and top view
3. Carve profile, gouge bowl shallow and smooth
4. Sand to finish, oil with linseed or mineral oil

Time: 2–4 hours. Use it daily — ongoing reminder of what you made.

**Next level:** figures (identity, ancestors), bowls (functional heirlooms), tool handles.

## Weaving

**Materials:** willow (best), cattail leaves dried and soaked, bark strips, long grass.

**Basket — over-under:**
1. Lay spokes in star pattern, odd number
2. Weave over-under in spirals going up
3. Bend spokes upward as sides rise
4. Fold and tuck spokes at rim

**Sleeping mat:** 2 m × 1 m, cattail leaves. Insulation from ground + dignity.
**Rhythm:** weaving is meditative. Rhythmic = flow state = anxiety treatment.

## Dyeing

**Dye sources:**
- Yellow: onion skins (easiest), goldenrod
- Brown: walnut hulls (most available)
- Red: pokeberry — handle carefully, plant is poisonous
- Black: oak galls + iron-rich water
- Blue: indigo or woad — rare and valuable

**Process:**
1. Mordant fabric in alum solution, simmer 1–2 hours
2. Simmer dye plant in water 1–2 hours
3. Add mordanted fabric to dye bath, simmer 1–2 hours
4. Rinse until water runs clear, dry in shade

Gray world = depression. Color in environment = 15% reduction in depression symptoms.

## Group Art

- **Quilt:** each person makes one 30 cm square, sew together. Heirloom + shared identity.
- **Mural:** charcoal sketch on wall, paint with ochre and plant pigments. Permanent community statement.
- **Pottery decoration:** carve or paint patterns before firing. Display together.

Group art = individual contribution → collective whole → belonging.

## Art Therapy

- **Trauma:** draw or sculpt it. Externalize without words. Destroy the object if needed (catharsis).
- **Depression:** 15-min small project. Share it. One accomplishment = momentum.
- **Anxiety:** repetitive technique — weaving, sanding, pattern drawing. Breaks rumination.

No judgment. Process over product. Low barrier = more access.

## Teaching

- Art ≠ realism. It is expression, pattern, beauty.
- Never criticize a child's work. One bad comment kills creativity for years.
- Display what children make. Signal: your work has value.
- Adults create too. Model it or it won't happen.
- Provide materials. Permission is implicit when supplies are available.

## Mission

1. Learn charcoal drawing and at least one natural pigment
2. Learn one craft: carving, weaving, or dyeing
3. Create weekly — build the habit before crisis hits
4. Teach children — next generation carries it forward
5. Run group projects — community bonding through shared making

**Art is medicine for the soul. Dose daily.**
