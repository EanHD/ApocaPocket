# Emergency Communications

## Family Plan Basics
- Out-of-area contact: single point person
- All family members memorize one number
- Meeting point: near home and far from home
- Code word: signals something is wrong
- Establish plan before you need it

## Radio Options
- NOAA weather radio: all-hazard alerts
- FRS/GMRS: family radios, no license
- GMRS license: $35 for family, 5 yr
- Ham radio: best range and reliability
- Shortwave: receive international info

## Text vs Call
- Text: routes through congested network
- Calls: use more bandwidth, often fail
- Text first: more likely to deliver
- Wi-Fi calling: may work when cell fails
- Email over data: last resort option

## Check-In Protocol
- Scheduled check-in times: 8am and 8pm
- Missed check-in: go to meeting point
- Two missed check-ins: assume emergency
- Keep check-in brief: short network use
- Report location, status, and next move

## Offline Communication
- Physical message board at rally point
- Leave notes inside mailbox
- Chalk on sidewalk: visible and public
- Predetermined symbols: pre-agreed meanings
- Paper and pencil kit in every bag

## Power for Commss
- Hand-crank radio: no battery needed
- Solar charger: 10W panel minimum
- Power bank 20,000 mAh: 5+ phone charges
- Car charger: use engine sparingly
- Battery cache: lithium AA and AAA
