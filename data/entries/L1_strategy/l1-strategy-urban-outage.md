# Urban Power Outage

## What This Covers
Actions to take when city power goes out for hours to days.

## Do First (0-2 hrs)
- Charge all devices NOW before battery backup dies.
- Fill bathtubs and large containers with water.
- Check on elderly or disabled neighbors immediately.
- Report outage to utility if not already widespread.
- Locate flashlights, candles, and battery supply.

## Food Safety
- Keep refrigerator closed — safe for 4 hours.
- Freezer safe 48 hours full, 24 hours half-full.
- Eat refrigerator items first, then freezer.
- When in doubt, throw it out.
- Never store food in oven for warmth.

## Water
- Fill tubs immediately — pressure may drop.
- Store in clean containers with lids.
- One gallon per person per day minimum.
- Water heater tank holds 40-80 gallons — usable.

## Lighting Safety
- LED flashlights first choice. Long battery life.
- Battery lanterns second. No fire risk.
- Candles last resort. Never leave unattended.
- Do not use gas stoves or grills for heat indoors.

## Temperature Management
- Heat: layer clothing, close off rooms, sleep together.
- Cold: use battery fans, wet towels, basement is cooler.
- Find community cooling/warming centers if needed.
- Check local emergency management for shelter locations.

## Medical Priorities
- Insulin: stays good unrefrigerated 28 days (open vial).
- Other medications: cooler with ice extends viability.
- Call pharmacy for emergency supplies if needed.
- Note any power-dependent medical equipment now.

## When to Leave
- Extreme heat or cold lasting more than 4 hours.
- Medical emergency requiring powered equipment.
- Building structurally unsafe.
- No water after 24 hours.

## Power Restored
- Check appliances for damage before running.
- Reset GFCI outlets (push button on outlet).
- Restock water, food, and battery supplies.
- Check freezer — refreeze only if still has ice crystals.
