# Community Calendar

## Overview

- Calendar: agreed system for tracking
  and coordinating time
- Agricultural timing is life-or-death:
  plant too early or late = crop failure
- Social coordination: when to meet,
  trade, celebrate, mourn
- Astronomical basis: most reliable
  and reproducible system
- Solar year: 365.25 days; moon 29.5 days
- Begin from solstice or equinox observation

## Solar Calendar Basis

- Observe winter solstice: shortest day
- Mark with gnomon shadow: longest shadow
- Year begins at solstice or equinox
- 365 days with extra quarter day correction
- Leap year every 4 years: add 1 day
- Verify annually with gnomon observation
- Any deviation: correct by adding or
  removing days before planting season

## Month Structure

- 12 months; unequal lengths (28-31 days)
- Or: 13 months of 28 days = 364 days
  plus 1-2 additional days; simpler
- Name months for seasons or events
- New moon as month marker: easy to observe
- 4 weeks of 7 days: 28-day month
- Consistent structure aids coordination

## Agricultural Events

- Last frost date: historical average;
  plant tender crops after this
- Planting windows: each crop has range
- First frost date: harvest before this
- Rainy season start and end dates
- Dry season management
- All recorded from historical observation
  over multiple years; refine each year

## Social Calendar

- Weekly market day: predictable
- Monthly council meeting: fixed day
- Seasonal celebrations: solstice, equinox
- Annual census: fixed date
- Planting ceremony: community event
- Harvest festival: share and celebrate
- Mourning period: agreed duration

## Publishing the

- Written and posted publicly
- Block-printed copies: one per household
- Oral announcement of upcoming events
- Weekly reminder at community gathering
- Update with actual observed dates when
  astronomical events are confirmed
- Archive each year's calendar for records

## Long-Term Value

- Agricultural timing alone justifies
  the entire calendar system
- Coordination reduces conflict over
  shared resources and market timing
- Ceremony calendar: social cohesion
- Historical comparison: track climate
  drift over decades from planting records
