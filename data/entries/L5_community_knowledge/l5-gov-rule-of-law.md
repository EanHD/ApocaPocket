# Rule of Law

## Overview

- Rule of law: same rules apply to all
  including those who make the rules
- Opposite: rule of man (arbitrary power)
- Requirements: known, stable, public,
  equally enforced, fair process
- Without rule of law: might-makes-right
- Ostrom: written rules known to all is
  the first design principle of resilient
  commons governance
- Short-form: nobody is above the law

## Traits of Good Law

- Written: cannot be changed in memory
- Public: no secret rules; known to all
- Stable: does not change without process
- Clear: not ambiguous; people can comply
- Proportional: penalty fits the offense
- Fair process: hear both sides always
- Equal: same punishment for same act

## Codifying Rules

- Assembly proposes and votes on rules
- Written in plain language; simple terms
- Posted publicly; read aloud quarterly
- New members receive orientation
- Conflicting rules: resolve explicitly
- Sunset clauses: some rules expire; review
- Keep list short; prioritize enforcement

## Enforcement

- Consistent: same rule enforced every time
- Impartial: enforcer has no personal stake
- Proportional: graduated consequences
- First offense: warning in many cases
- Records kept: pattern of behavior noted
- Enforcement by designated role; not mob
- Community accountability of enforcers

## Common Failure Modes

- Powerful members exempt from rules
- Rules applied to political opponents only
- Vague rules: allow arbitrary application
- Too many rules: selective enforcement
  becomes unavoidable and corrupting
- Private rules: rumors and fear spread
- No appeal process: resentment builds
- Punitive rather than restorative focus

## Process for Accused

- Right to know the accusation
- Right to respond and present evidence
- Decision by neutral party or group
- Written record of proceedings and outcome
- Right to appeal to higher body
- Punishment only after process complete
- No double jeopardy for same act

## Long-Term Value

- Rule of law is what separates
  community from mob rule
- Reduces violence by providing
  legitimate alternative to revenge
- Attracts people who value fairness
- Critical to any functioning society
