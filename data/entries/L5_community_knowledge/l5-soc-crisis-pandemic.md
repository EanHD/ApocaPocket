# Pandemic Protocol

## Overview

- Pandemic: infectious disease spreading
  across community or region
- Key metrics: infection rate (R0),
  case fatality rate, incubation period
- R0 > 1: spreading; R0 < 1: declining
- Reduce transmission: goal of all measures
- Community response must be fast;
  exponential growth is unforgiving
- Declare early; act hard; review weekly

## Trigger for Action

- Two or more cases of same presentation
  within one incubation period
- Case definition: write it down; be precise
- Report to community health officer
- Do not wait for confirmation; start
  precautions while investigating
- False alarm cost: low
- Missed outbreak cost: potentially fatal

## Isolation and

- Isolation: known sick; keep separate
- Quarantine: exposed but not yet sick;
  keep separate from healthy
- Isolation space: separate building;
  own ventilation; marked clearly
- Caregiver: designated; PPE; limit to few
- Duration: longest known incubation plus
  two more days after symptom end
- Communicate: family knows; not secret

## Contact Tracing

- For each case: who did they contact?
- Timeframe: 2 days before symptoms
- List all contacts: name, date, proximity
- Notify all contacts: quarantine begins
- Track quarantine compliance
- Monitor for symptom development
- Break chain: each traced contact who
  quarantines prevents further spread

## Supplies to

- Soap and water: most important
- Masks: cloth or N95 if available
- Gloves: reusable if necessary
- Disinfectant: diluted bleach (0.5%)
- Oral rehydration: salt-sugar solution
- Fever management: willow bark, rest, water
- Isolation food supply: 2-week minimum
- Written protocols posted at isolation site

## Community Behavior

- Reduce gatherings: temporarily suspend
- Hand washing: enforce at all key points
- Sick stay home: enforce; no stigma
- Compensate: isolated members need
  food and care from community
- Information: daily accurate update
  prevents panic and rumor
- Children: school closure if high risk

## Long-Term Value

- 1918 influenza: communities that isolated
  fast had 10x lower mortality rates
- Isolation is the primary tool when
  no pharmaceutical response exists
- Infrastructure (sanitation, hygiene)
  reduces baseline transmission continuously
- Practice protocol before outbreak
