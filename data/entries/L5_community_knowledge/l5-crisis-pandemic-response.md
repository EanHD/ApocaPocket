# Pandemic Response

Priorities: (1) Avoid infection. (2) Care for sick while protecting caregivers. (3) Maintain essential services.

## Recognize a Pandemic

- Unusual severity -- healthy people dying
- Cases doubling every 2-7 days
- Hospitals overwhelmed, healthcare workers ill
- Incubation 2-14 days (spread before symptoms appear)
- Mortality rate >1% (seasonal flu = 0.1%)

## Immediate Actions

Assess: isolate sick now | quarantine exposed separately | identify vulnerable (elderly, chronic illness, children) | inventory supplies.
Stop contact: cancel social plans, avoid public spaces, pull children from school.
Stockpile: 2-4 weeks shelf-stable food | 1 gal/person/day water for 14 days | fever reducers + electrolytes + prescriptions | soap, bleach, sanitizer | N95 masks, gloves, eye protection.
Sick room: one room with closeable door and dedicated bathroom if possible; stock tissues, trash bags, thermometer, fluids, medications.

## Isolation

Sick person: stay in designated room | wear mask when leaving | dedicated or disposable dishes | trash double-bagged.
Exposed (not yet sick): separate room if possible | monitor temperature twice daily | full 14-day quarantine.

## PPE Use

Donning: wash hands 20+ sec -> gown/apron -> N95 mask (mold to nose, check seal) -> eye protection -> gloves over gown cuffs.
Doffing: gloves (peel inside-out, no outside contact) -> sanitize hands -> gown (roll inside-out) -> sanitize hands, exit room -> eye protection (touch straps only) -> mask (touch ties only, not
front) -> wash hands 20+ sec.
CRITICAL: do not touch face at any point during doffing sequence.

Improvised PPE: mask = multi-layer tight-woven cotton + HEPA layer | goggles = clear plastic or swim goggles | gown = large trash bag with holes | sanitizer = 2 parts 91% isopropyl + 1 part aloe.

## Home Care

Symptom management:
- Fever: acetaminophen or ibuprofen; cooling cloths
- Dehydration: ORS -- 1 L water + 6 tsp sugar + half tsp salt; or broth
- Breathing difficulty: sit at 45-degree angle; fresh air

Monitor twice daily: temperature (log it) | breathing rate (normal 12-20/min) | O2 saturation (<92% = urgent) | mental status.

Emergency signs -- get medical help if available:
- Gasping or cannot complete sentences
- Persistent chest pain
- Confusion or cannot be woken
- Blue lips or face
- No urination for 12+ hours

## Disinfection

- High-touch surfaces (door handles, switches): twice daily minimum
- Sick room: disinfect after every care visit
- Bleach solution: 1 part bleach to 9 parts water; make fresh daily
- Leave disinfectant wet on surface 1-5 min before wiping

## Fatal Mistakes

- Caregiver skips PPE or doffs improperly -- caregiver infected, now two patients
- Breaking quarantine early -- still contagious at day 5; complete full 14 days
- Panic hoarding visibly -- attracts theft and violence
- Abandoning sick without comfort care -- preventable deaths, trauma for survivors
- Internet cures -- bleach ingestion and overdoses harm and can be fatal

## 2-Week Minimum Supply

- Food: 28,000 calories (rice, beans, canned goods, peanut butter)
- Water: 14 gallons per person
- Medicine: 30-60 doses fever reducer; electrolytes; all prescriptions
- PPE: 20-50 masks; 50-100 glove pairs; 1-2 eye protection pieces
- Hygiene: 2 bars soap; 8 oz hand sanitizer; 1 qt bleach
