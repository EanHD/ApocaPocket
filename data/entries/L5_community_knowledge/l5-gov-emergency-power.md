# Emergency Authority

## Overview

- Emergencies require fast decisions; normal
  deliberative process too slow
- Emergency power: limited authority
  granted to individuals or small group
- Must be: defined triggers, limited scope,
  automatic sunset, council oversight
- History: emergency powers that persist
  become permanent dictatorship
- Design safeguards into the system;
  do not trust good intentions alone

## Trigger Conditions

- Define in advance; not during crisis
- Examples of valid triggers:
  - Attack on community
  - Disease epidemic
  - Critical infrastructure failure
  - Natural disaster (flood, fire, quake)
- Requires: council declaration; majority
- Duration: 7-30 days; not open-ended
- Renewal requires new vote

## Scope of Emerg Powers

- Specify exactly what may be done
- Examples of valid powers:
  - Mandatory evacuation orders
  - Commandeer stored resources
  - Suspend normal work assignments
  - Restrict movement temporarily
- Invalid even in emergency:
  - Permanent property seizure
  - Criminal punishment without process
  - Suspension of constitutional rights
  - Dissolution of governing bodies

## Safeguards

- Sunset: power expires automatically
- No self-declaration: leader cannot
  declare own emergency
- Oversight: council monitors daily
- Record: all decisions documented
- Review: assembly reviews within 14 days
  of emergency end; ratify or reverse
- Accountability: post-emergency audit
  of all actions taken under authority

## Returning to Normal

- Emergency ends at sunset or by vote
- Commandeered resources returned or
  compensated from community funds
- All emergency orders expire; review
  each for permanent adoption if needed
- Leaders account for all actions
- No-fault review: learn, not punish
  (except clear abuses)
- Resume normal governance immediately

## Signs of Abuse

- Emergency extended without new vote
- Scope expanded beyond original
- Personal benefit to authority holder
- Opposition silenced during emergency
- Normal bodies not re-convened
- If these occur: full community assembly
  has right to revoke authority

## Long-Term Value

- Structured emergency response saves lives
- Prevents decision paralysis in crisis
- Sunset clause prevents power capture
- Rome: dictatorship tradition; works
  only with genuine sunset enforced
- Design assumes leaders will be tempted
