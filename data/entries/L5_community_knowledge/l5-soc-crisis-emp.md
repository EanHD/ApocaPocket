# EMP Survival

## Overview

- EMP: electromagnetic pulse disables
  electronic circuits in range
- Sources: nuclear high-altitude burst,
  solar Carrington event, directed weapon
- Effect: modern electronics fail;
  older mechanical systems survive
- Grid-down: no electricity at grid scale
- Duration: weeks to years; repair depends
  on industrial supply chain availability
- 90-day survival window is critical

## Immediate 72 Hours

- Water: grid pumps fail; have 3-day supply
- Food: refrigeration fails; preserve now
- Medical: insulin and other cold storage
  medication: protect immediately
- Fuel: vehicles post-1980 may not run;
  older mechanical vehicles may work
- Communication: radio (older tube type
  or shielded) for broadcast reception
- Security: infrastructure failure causes
  opportunistic crime within 48 hours

## Water Priority

- All grid water supply fails without power
- Hand pump wells: functional (see entry)
- Rainwater harvest: functional if set up
- Slow sand filter: functional; build now
- Treat all water; infrastructure that
  normally treated it is offline
- Community water point: centralize supply
- Track consumption vs supply carefully

## Food and Agriculture

- Refrigeration failed: process and preserve
- Canning, smoking, drying: immediate
- Winter: root cellar for stored produce
- Spring: planting is critical priority
- Seed saving: identify and protect seeds
- Animals: butcher non-essential; preserve
- Hunting and foraging: immediate supplement
- Agriculture transition: all-hands task

## Medical

- Hospital backup power fails in days
- Medications that require cold storage:
  identify users; plan alternatives
- Surgical capability: without electricity
  extremely limited; prevent injury
- Infection risk rises dramatically
- Water sanitation: highest medical priority
- Pre-position manual medical equipment
- Document community medical needs

## 90-Day Governance

- Community census immediately: who, what
- Triage: who needs help; who can contribute
- Labor mobilization: all adults contribute
- Food rationing: fair allocation system
- Security rotation: 24-hour watch schedule
- Communication: runners and signal fires
- Leadership clarity: prevent power vacuum

## Long-Term Value

- EMP is highest-impact single grid-down
  scenario; industrial civilization dependent
  on electronics at every layer
- All entries in this device relevant;
  electricity not required for any of them
- This device itself: no grid required
