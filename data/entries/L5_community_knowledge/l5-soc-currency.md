# Community Currency

## Overview

- Currency: widely accepted medium
  of exchange; solves barter limitations
- Three forms: commodity, token, ledger
- Commodity: intrinsic value (grain, salt)
- Token: stamped metal or marked object
  with agreed community value
- Ledger: credit system; virtual currency
- All require: community agreement and trust
- Trust is the true backing of any currency

## Commodity Money

- Best commodities: durable, divisible,
  scarce, widely desired
- Salt: historically valuable; essential
- Grain: widely valued but bulky; spoils
- Metal (copper, iron): durable, divisible
- Cloth lengths: standardized weight
- Store commodity in community granary
  or vault; issue receipts against it
- Physical currency must be weighed; fraud

## Token Currency

- Stamped metal disk: community seal
- Issue limited supply: council controls
- Match supply to goods and services
  available; excess = inflation
- Denomination: 1, 5, 10 units
- Difficult to counterfeit: complex stamp
  or unusual alloy
- Accept in payment of all community debts
  and taxes: creates demand for it

## Ledger System

- Community ledger: central record of
  credits and debits per member
- Member earns credits for labor
- Member spends credits for goods
- Balance visible to community or private
  depending on system design
- Dual-signature entries: prevents fraud
- Periodic reconciliation: clear debts
- No physical money needed

## Preventing Inflation

- Do not issue more currency than goods
  and services available
- Track: money supply vs production
- Retire old notes or tokens periodically
- Commodity backing: print receipt only
  for commodity actually in storage
- Council vote required for money supply
  increase; prevents arbitrary expansion

## Currency and Justice

- Fines and restitution: payable in currency
- Taxes: payable in currency or commodity
- Wages: currency or ledger credit
- Debt: recorded; interest regulated
- Default: legal remedy via council
- Usury (excessive interest): regulated
  or prohibited to prevent exploitation

## Long-Term Value

- Currency enables specialization and
  division of labor
- Without currency: only barter possible;
  very limited economic complexity
- Graeber: debt and credit precede coinage
  by millennia; ledger is first money
- Start with ledger; add tokens as needed
