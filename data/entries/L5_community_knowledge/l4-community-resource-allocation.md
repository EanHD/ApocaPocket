# Community Resources

## Inventory System

Count everything before distributing anything.
Track each resource:
- Item name, quantity, unit, storage location
- Date counted and who counted it
- Expiration or estimated shelf life

Update weekly. One designated keeper per category.
Never distribute from memory.

## Rationing Principles

Trigger rationing when supply falls below 30 days.

Calculate the daily ration:
- Total supply times 0.85 (reserve 15% emergency buffer)
- Divide result by people, then by days to stretch

Show the math publicly. People accept cuts when they
understand the numbers. Unexplained cuts breed resentment.

Ration reduction levels:
- Level 1: cut all non-essentials
- Level 2: 80 percent of staple ration
- Critical: 70 percent, absolute minimum for survival

Do not go below 1400 calories per adult per day.

## Equitable Distribution

Split all resources into three tiers:

Tier 1, survival basics: water, staple food, critical
medicine. Equal or need-based. No one goes without.

Tier 2, standard living: better food, clothing, tools.
Tied to contribution, with a guaranteed floor.

Tier 3, extras: luxuries. Earned only.

Children and sick members receive Tier 1 automatically.
Pregnant women need 300-500 extra calories daily.

## Conflict Prevention

Perceived unfairness destroys communities faster than
actual scarcity. Act before resentment hardens.

Keep these visible to all:
- Total current inventory
- The allocation formula in use
- Who approved each distribution decision

Resolve disputes within one week. They fester fast.

Dispute process:
- Verify facts first, re-measure, check logs
- Confirm whether policy was actually followed
- If followed and still feels unfair: bring to group
- Vote on policy change if problem is systematic

Unauthorized reserve access is a serious violation.
Reserve is collective survival insurance. Treat it as such.

## Stockpile Priority

Build reserve to 30 days minimum before distributing surplus.
Target 90 days. Ideal is 180.

Priority order:
- Water purification supplies
- Calorie-dense staples: grains, legumes, fats
- Salt and food preservation materials
- Medical essentials
- Fuel and lighting
- Seeds for next planting season

Label all reserves with date and quantity.
Rotate using FIFO: oldest stock out first.
