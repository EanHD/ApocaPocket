# Signal Fire Codes

## Overview

- Signal fire: pre-agreed meaning for flame
  or smoke at specific location
- Beacon chain: relay message faster than
  any runner over long distances
- Simple codes only; not full text
- Pre-agreement essential; no code = noise
- Day use: white smoke; night: flame visible
- Roman signal chains reached 500 miles
- Simple two-signal system adequate

## Signal Meanings

- One fire: all clear; routine check-in
- Two fires: alert; stand by for runner
- Three fires: attack or serious threat
- Fire plus smoke puff pattern: direction
- No fire (no response): station may be
  compromised; send runner to investigate
- Complex codes: too many misread; keep simple

## Fire Types

- Smoke (day): green/damp wood on good fire
  white smoke: birch bark or dry grass
  black smoke: rubber, oil if available
- Flame (night): dry hardwood; bright
- Height: fire on hilltop visible 10-15 miles
- Elevation: gain altitude for longer range
- Line of sight: pre-survey your chain

## Station Setup

- Station: hilltop with line of sight
  to adjacent stations both directions
- Pre-stacked fuel: ready to light fast
- Two types of fuel stored separately:
  dry for flame, wet/green for smoke
- Cover fuel from rain; critical that it
  lights reliably on demand
- Manning: minimum two people per station;
  one sleeps while other watches

## Operating Procedures

- Watch schedule: continuous at elevated
  threat levels
- Light fire immediately on receiving signal;
  do not wait for confirmation
- Send runner simultaneously for significant
  signals; relay fire is backup not substitute
- Acknowledge: fire lit = message received
  and passed on
- Log all signals: time, type, direction

## Relay Chain Design

- Survey line of sight before committing
- Gaps require relay towers or towers on
  raised platforms
- Distance: 10-15 miles per station
  for reliable naked-eye observation
- Binoculars: extend to 20+ miles
- Test system monthly during daylight

## Long-Term Value

- Fastest possible long-range warning
- Warns entire community in hours
  versus days by runner
- Roman beacon system defended empire
- Build before crisis; test thoroughly
- Pair with runner system for detail
