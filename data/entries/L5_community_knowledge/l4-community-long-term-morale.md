# Community Morale

## Warning Signs

Low morale kills communities before starvation does.
Watch for two or more of these together:
- No laughter for 2+ days
- Conflict escalating in frequency or intensity
- Work slowdowns or outright refusals
- People isolating, sleeping excessively
- No future talk, only grieving the past
- Self-neglect: stopped washing or eating

Act when you see two or more. Do not wait.

## Activities That Work

Keep people busy and together. These survive hardship:
- Physical competition (releases stress, builds pride)
- Skill teaching, every person teaches what they know
- Storytelling after dark
- Group singing or music, however rough
- Shared meals with assigned seating (forces mixing)
- Projects with visible daily progress

Busy hands and shared purpose hold groups together.

## Leader Communication

Tell the truth. Lies are discovered and destroy trust.

When things are bad:
- Acknowledge it directly: This week was hard.
- State what you know and what you do not know
- Name one concrete action being taken
- Thank people by name for specific contributions

Silence from leaders reads as catastrophe. Say something weekly.

## Celebrations

Mark progress. Humans need narrative arcs.
- First harvest: a real event, not a passing mention
- Births, birthdays, anniversaries: acknowledge them
- Completion of major builds: gather everyone
- Monthly meeting: name contributions publicly

Rituals need no resources. They need intention.
Even a brief gathering and spoken acknowledgment works.

## Avoiding Collapse

Collapse begins with one person who stops caring.
Protect these before problems start:
- Private time for individuals, even 10 minutes alone
- Conflict resolution before resentment hardens
- Perceived workload fairness, unfairness spreads fast
- Visible care for children, elderly, and sick

Assign one person per week to watch for morale signs.
Rotate the role. Make it official. Report weekly.
A group that monitors its own health survives longer.
