# Community Constitution

## Overview

- Constitution: foundational legal document
- Defines: who has power, how it is limited,
  what rights all members hold
- Higher law: overrides all other rules
- Amendment: process for changing document
- Ratification: community approval of draft
- Without constitution: rule by strongest
- Ostrom: good governance requires rules
  known and agreed to by all members

## Core Elements

- Preamble: purpose and values statement
- Rights: what members are guaranteed
- Duties: what members owe community
- Governing structure: who decides what
- Amendment process: how to change rules
- Dispute process: how conflicts resolved
- Ratification requirement: supermajority

## Rights to Include

- Right to life and physical safety
- Right to fair process before punishment
- Right to speak and dissent publicly
- Right to hold and transfer property
- Right to refuse compelled labor
- Right to religious or personal practice
- Equal application of rules to all

## Duties to Include

- Contribute to community labor pool
- Follow agreed-upon rules
- Pay fair share of common costs
- Participate in governance processes
- Defend community when called upon
- Report threats and crimes honestly
- Maintain shared infrastructure

## Amendment Process

- Propose: any member or council
- Discussion period: minimum 30 days
- Vote: supermajority required (2/3 or 3/4)
- Record: written, signed, witnessed
- No retroactive punishment for past acts
- Emergency amendments: same process,
  shorter timeline with sunset clause

## Ratification

- Draft by committee with diverse members
- Public reading and comment period
- Line-by-line discussion at assembly
- Vote: 2/3 of adult members to ratify
- Ceremony: signing or mark by all
- Post publicly in multiple locations
- Review every 5-10 years; revise as needed

## Long-Term Value

- Prevents tyranny from within
- Legitimate authority reduces violence
- Written document survives leaders
- Binding: power-holders cannot ignore
- Communities with constitutions survive
  power transitions more safely
