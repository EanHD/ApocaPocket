# Runner Relay System

## Overview

- Relay: each runner carries message a
  short distance; fresh runner takes over
- Speed: 150-200 miles per day vs 40 miles
  for single runner
- Inca chasqui: covered 1500 miles in 5 days
- Roman cursus publicus: imperial relay system
- Simple version: stations 5-10 miles apart
- Station: shelter with food and runner on
  standby ready to go immediately

## Station Design

- Spaced at easy fast-run distance apart
- Location: hilltop or visible landmark
- Shelter for runner; food and water
- Two runners per station: alternate rest
- Signal system: see station from previous
- Record log: time in, time out, message
  summary (not content if encrypted)

## Message Format

- Standard header: From, To, Date, Time
- Message body: brief and clear
- Routing note: via which stations
- Priority level: urgent or routine
- Return receipt required: yes/no
- Signature: originating authority
- Encrypted if sensitive; see encryption entry

## Runner Selection

- Fit and healthy; regular route training
- Know route: landmarks, hazards, shelters
- Paired: two runners on sensitive messages
- At least two routes to every destination
- Code words: distress signal if coerced
- Runners carry minimal gear; light and fast
- Weapons only on dangerous routes

## Message Handling

- Do not open sealed messages; deliver sealed
- Count and confirm: receiver signs receipt
- Cannot deliver: document why and return
- Damaged message: report condition
- Verbal supplement: allowed for urgent
  additions but note it was verbal
- No copies made without authority

## Network Management

- Map: all stations and routes
- Maintenance: assign accountability
  per route segment
- Backup routes: at minimum two per segment
- Test: monthly routine message circuit
- New station integration: supervised first run
- Decommission: when route no longer secure

## Long-Term Value

- Fastest pre-electronic communications
- Inca empire administered 5 million people
  entirely by runner relay
- Scales from village to region
- No infrastructure beyond trained people
- Redundant; survives individual failures
