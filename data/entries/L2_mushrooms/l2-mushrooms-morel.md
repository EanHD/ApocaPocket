## Identification
- Honeycomb pitted cap attached at base
- Hollow inside when sliced lengthwise
- Cap and stem form one hollow chamber
- Colors: tan, gray, black by species
- Spring only; 2-4 week season

## Slice Test
- Cut mushroom in half top to bottom
- True morel: single continuous hollow cavity
- Cap and stem walls thin; fully open
- No cotton or fiber inside = true morel
- False morel: chambered or cottony = STOP

## True vs False Morel
- True morel cap: attached at stem base
- False morel (Gyromitra): cap hangs free like skirt
- True morel: honeycomb pits AND ridges
- False morel: brain-like wrinkled, not pitted
- Gyromitra contains gyromitrin toxin — deadly

## Where to Find
- Near dead elms, ash, old orchards
- Burned areas from prior year
- Spring when apple trees bloom
- Morel season: 2-4 week window only

## Harvest and Prep
- Cut at base; do not pull
- Brush clean; pits hold dirt
- ALWAYS cook; never eat raw
- Saute in fat: nutty, earthy flavor
- Soak briefly in salt water to remove bugs
- Dry for preservation; rehydrates excellently
