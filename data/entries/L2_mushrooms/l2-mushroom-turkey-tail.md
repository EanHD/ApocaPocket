# Turkey Tail

## Identification

- Thin flexible bracket on dead logs
- Concentric color bands: brown, gray, tan
- Velvety upper surface
- Underside: tiny pores (need close look)
- Edges wavy; fan to kidney-shaped

## The Pore Test

- Look at underside with naked eye
- True turkey tail: visible tiny white pores
- Count: 3-8 pores per mm
- Stereum (false): smooth underside, no pores

## Where to Find

- Dead hardwood logs and branches
- Nearly universal; every forest type
- Year-round; perennial shelf mushroom
- Most common medicinal mushroom globally

## How to Harvest

- Pull or cut from log
- Harvest young portions near edges
- Dry specimens also usable for tea
- Collect in quantity; very abundant

## Making Medicinal Tea

- Boil 2-3 brackets in 2 cups water
- Simmer 20-30 min; liquid turns amber
- Strain and drink the liquid
- Drink 1-2 cups per day when available

## Immune Properties

- PSK and PSP polysaccharides
- Clinically studied immune support
- Used in cancer supportive care in Japan
- Anti-viral properties documented

## Nutritional Value

- Not caloric; primarily medicinal
- Important morale and health support
- Boil longer for stronger extract
- Can reboil same pieces 2-3 times

## Lookalikes

- False turkey tail (Stereum): no pores
- Stereum not toxic; just no benefit
- Confirm pore surface every time
- Pores = turkey tail; smooth = stereum
