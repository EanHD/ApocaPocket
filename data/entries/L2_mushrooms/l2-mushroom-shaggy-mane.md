# Shaggy Mane

## Identification

- Tall white cylindrical cap when young
- Shaggy white scales on cap surface
- White gills turning black from bottom up
- Hollow stem; ring visible on young
- Grows in disturbed soil and grass

## Auto-Digestion

- Gills dissolve into black ink from edges
- This process begins after picking or when old
- Black inky base = past prime; still edible if white at top
- Fully dissolved = decomposed; discard

## Where to Find

- Disturbed soil, lawns, roadsides
- Often appears after fall rains
- Groups of several to dozens
- City parks, campgrounds, field edges

## Harvest Window

- Harvest when white and cylindrical
- Gills still white to pink: prime quality
- Cook and eat SAME DAY as harvest
- Do not store overnight; will liquefy

## Preparation

- Cook within 1-2 hours of harvest
- Saute quickly in hot fat
- Add to any soup for excellent flavor
- Do not overcook; becomes slimy

## Alcohol Warning

- Contains coprine compound
- Eating with alcohol causes flushing, nausea
- Symptoms: hot flashes, racing heart
- Not life-threatening but very unpleasant

## Nutritional Value

- Protein: ~3g per 100g fresh
- Iron, potassium, phosphorus
- ~20 cal per 100g
- Best fresh; no preservation value

## Lookalikes  -  DANGER

- No dangerous lookalikes when young
- Older specimens with black ink: still the same
- Ink can be used as writing ink
- Confirm: shaggy white scales + auto-digests
