# Cattail Full Use

## Harvest Calendar

- Early spring: young shoots from base
- Late spring: green pollen spike (spear)
- Spring-summer: pollen harvest
- Summer: green seed heads boiled
- Fall-winter: rhizome starch

## Spring Shoots

- Pull young green shoots from mud/water
- Peel to white core: mild, crispy
- Green sheathed spear before pollen: boil
- Best wild vegetable of early spring

## Pollen Harvest

- Yellow pollen spike above brown head
- Shake into bag when bright yellow
- Mix into batter, sprinkle on food
- Pollen: 3x protein of most grain flours

## Summer: Green Heads

- Before brown; when fully green
- Boil corn-cob stage 5-10 min
- Eat like corn on the cob
- Brief window; don't miss it

## Rhizome Starch

- Dig roots from mud in shallow water
- Pound roots in water, let starch settle
- Pour off water; collect white starch
- Dry and use as flour or porridge

## Nutritional Summary

- Shoots: vitamins A, B, C; potassium
- Pollen: high protein (~18%)
- Rhizome starch: 266 cal/100g dry
- One of highest-yield wild starch plants
