# Valerian Root

## Identification

- Tall plant 2-5 ft; compound leaves
- Leaves: pairs of leaflets along stem
- White to pale pink small flower clusters
- Root: distinctive strong musty unpleasant smell
- Roots: fibrous, tan-brown, smell like dirty socks

## The Root Smell Test

- Fresh valerian root smells musty/cat-urine
- Strong unpleasant odor is definitive ID
- This smell is due to isovaleric acid
- Dried root smells even more intense

## Where to Find

- Moist meadows and stream banks
- Forest edges and woodland margins
- Throughout eastern US and Pacific NW
- Introduced; naturalized widely

## How to Harvest

- Dig roots in fall (most potent)
- Wash and scrub thoroughly
- Roots can be thick; look like parsnip
- Dry at low heat or use fresh

## Making Valerian Tea

- Steep 1 tsp dried root in hot water
- Do NOT boil; heat degrades compounds
- Steep 15-20 minutes covered
- Unpleasant taste; mix with other herbs

## Mental Health Value

- Sleep deprivation impairs judgment severely
- Anxiety makes decisions life-threatening
- Valerian addresses both in emergency
- 1-2 cups before sleep: significant improvement

## Dosage Caution

- Take 30-60 minutes before sleep
- Mild drowsiness; not incapacitating
- Do not use before first watch or navigation
- Safe herb; no dependency reported
