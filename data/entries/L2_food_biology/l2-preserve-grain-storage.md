# Grain Storage

## Overview

- Dry grain stores for months to years
- Main threats: moisture, insects,
  rodents, mold
- Wild grains: cattail seed, grass
  seeds, acorn, wild rice
- Fully dry before storage: critical

## Drying Grain

- Spread in thin layer on flat surface
- Sun dry 2-5 days in dry weather
- Over fire: spread on bark or hide
  at 3-4 ft height, slow heat
- Done: hard, no flex, snap when
  bitten
- Moisture = mold in days

## Container Selection

- Ceramic or clay with lid: best
- Tightly woven bark basket with
  clay coating: good
- Metal container: excellent
- Avoid cloth bags alone: rodents
  chew through, moisture enters

## Anti-Insect Measures

- Dry bay leaves in storage container
- Wood ash mixed with grain: deters
  insects (see Ash Salt entry)
- Bay, cedar, and lavender repel pests
- Inspect monthly and remove insects

## Anti-Rodent Measures

- Suspend container off ground
- Metal container: rodents cannot
  chew through
- Hang from cordage in cache
- Bury metal container in ground

## Monitoring

- Check every 2 weeks minimum
- Signs of problem: musty smell,
  webs, chewed areas, clumping
- Moldy grain: distinctive off-white
  or green fuzz -- discard completely
