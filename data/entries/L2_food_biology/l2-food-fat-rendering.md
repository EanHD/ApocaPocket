# Fat Rendering

## Overview

- Rendered fat: cooking medium,
  preservation base, calorie source
- Lard = pig fat, tallow = beef/venison
- Shelf stable for weeks when
  rendered completely
- Two methods: wet and dry

## Fat Sources

- Leaf fat (around kidneys): best quality
- Gut fat: good, slightly stronger
- Trim fat from meat before jerking
- Bear fat: excellent, very stable
- Waterfowl fat: good for cooking

## Wet Rendering

- Add fat and equal volume water to pot
- Simmer on low heat
- Water prevents burning
- Fat liquefies and rises to top
- Water boils off last
- Strain through cloth, cool

## Dry Rendering

- Place diced fat in pot, no water
- Heat on very low heat
- Fat melts slowly: cracklings form
- Cracklings (fried solid bits): edible
- Strain fat from cracklings
- More flavorful but burns easily

## Doneness

- Fat is clear golden liquid
- No water spattering remains
- Cracklings are golden brown
- Strain while warm (thickens when cool)
- Set in container to harden

## Storage

- Pour into clean dry container
- Cover when cool to prevent dust/bugs
- Shelf life: 1-2 weeks at room temp
- Longer in cool conditions
- Salt added extends shelf life
