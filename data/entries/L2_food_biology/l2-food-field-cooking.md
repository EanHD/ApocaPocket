# Field Cooking Methods

## Overview

- No pot needed: many cooking options
- Match method to food type
- Priority: always fully cook meat
  and poultry
- Direct fire fastest, driest method
- Wet methods (steam, boil) preserve
  more nutrients

## Direct Fire Roasting

- Skewer on green stick: 1-1.5 in diam
- Hold or prop over coals (not flames)
- Rotate continuously for even cooking
- Distance from coals: 4-6 inches
- Internal temp: meat 165F, fish 145F
- White throughout = cooked

## Plank or Rock Cooking

- Heat flat rock beside fire (not in)
  until hot (water drops dance on it)
- Lay fish or thin meat directly on rock
- Cook 3-5 min each side
- No oil needed if rock is hot enough
- Stone must be dry or use only dry

## Leaf Wrapping (Steam)

- Wrap food in large fresh leaves
  (not toxic species)
- Tie with grass or flexible stem
- Bury in coals with rock on top
- Steam cooks from moisture in leaves
- Cook time: 20-30 min for small fish

## Coal Cooking

- Push coals aside, create flat bed
- Lay food directly on hot ground
- Cover with coals on top
- Tubers, corn: 20-30 min
- Fish: 15-20 min per inch thickness

## Suspended Cooking

- Twist green rope/vine for cordage
- Hang container over fire on tripod
- Twist-and-release: rotates naturally
- Good for bark vessels and water
