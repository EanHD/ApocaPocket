# Groundhog

## Overview

- Also called woodchuck or marmot
- Active April through September
- Hibernates Oct-Mar in burrow
- 4-14 lbs -- significant meat source
- Common in fields, meadows, wood edges

## Finding Groundhogs

- Burrow entrance: 8-12 in diameter
  mound of fresh dirt outside
- Primary burrow: in open field or edge
- Look for clipped vegetation nearby
- Active mid-morning and late afternoon
- Watch from distance with elevation

## Hunting Method

- Spot from 100+ yards before approach
- Stalk upwind along field edge
- Groundhog stands upright when alarmed
- This pause is harvest opportunity
- Head or chest shot for clean harvest

## Burrow Trapping

- Live trap at burrow entrance
- Bait: apple, carrot, peanut butter
- Cover trap with grass or branches
- Check morning and evening

## Field Dressing

- Same as rabbit: skin from legs down
- Remove musk glands on back/underarm
  (small yellow lumps, bitter taste)
- Gut, rinse with cold water
- Young of year most tender

## Cooking Notes

- Parboil 30-45 min with wild onion
- Roast over coals after parboiling
- Stew with roots and greens
- Fat reserves rich in calories
