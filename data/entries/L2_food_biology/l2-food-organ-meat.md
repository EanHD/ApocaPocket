# Organ Meat Nutrition

## Overview

- Organs are the most nutrient-dense
  part of any animal
- Muscle meat is calorie dense but
  lacks full micronutrients
- In starvation: organs prevent
  deficiency diseases
- Cook and eat organs first: spoil fast

## Priority Order

- Liver: highest priority
  (iron, B12, Vitamin A, folate)
- Heart: protein, CoQ10, B vitamins
- Kidneys: protein, minerals
- Lungs: lower priority but edible
- Stomach/intestines: cleaned, edible

## Liver

- Dark reddish-brown, firm texture
- Smell: rich metallic, not sour
- Cook within 4 hours of harvest
- Signs of disease: pale, spotted,
  bumpy, or greenish = discard
- Slice thin, fry or roast quickly
- Do not overcook: becomes rubbery

## Heart

- Dense muscle, stores well
- Rinse interior chambers of blood
- Slice, pound to tenderize
- Roast, grill, or add to stew
- Excellent texture and mild flavor

## Kidneys

- Rinse thoroughly in cold water
- Remove white tubular center (hilus)
- Soak 30-60 min in water if strong
  ammonia smell: reduces odor
- Dice and fry or add to stew

## Quick Cooking Method

- Slice liver thin, kidneys small
- Heat rendered fat to hot
- Cook 2-3 min per side for liver
- Eat with any available starch
  or vegetable -- complete meal
