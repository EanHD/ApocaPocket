# Nut Oil Extraction

## Overview

- Walnuts: 60-65% fat -- excellent yield
- Hickory nuts: 70%+ fat
- Butternuts: high fat content
- Oil = critical calories in lean times
- Two extraction methods: boil or press

## Boil (Water) Method

- Shell and lightly crush nuts
- Boil in large amount of water
  30-45 minutes
- Oil rises to surface as cream layer
- Cool completely: oil layer solidifies
- Skim off hardened fat/oil layer
- Most accessible field method

## Press Method

- Shell and grind nuts to paste with
  two stones
- Wrap paste in cloth, twist tight
- Press against flat rock or use
  improvised lever press
- Collect oil that runs out
- Higher yield than boiling method

## Oil Uses

- Cooking: fry or saute in oil
- Calorie dense: carry for energy
- Mix with dried meat as spread
- Skin protection in cold weather
- Lamp fuel: hickory nut oil burns

## Storage

- Store in sealed container, cool, dark
- Use within 1-2 weeks before rancid
- Rancid oil: sour or paint-like smell
  -- do not consume
- Salt added helps slow rancidity

## Yield Estimates

- 1 lb hickory nuts = 2-3 oz oil
- 1 lb walnuts = 3-4 oz oil
- Labor intensive but calorie-critical
  when carbohydrates are scarce
