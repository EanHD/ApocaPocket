# Natural Bait Selection

## Overview

- Best bait: what fish already eat
  in that water body
- Flip rocks: find what's underneath
- Check stomach contents of first fish
- Worms are universal -- nearly always
  work in freshwater

## Best Universal Baits

- Earthworms: found under logs, rocks,
  moist soil after rain
- Crawfish/crayfish: under rocks in
  stream -- deadly for bass
- Crickets and grasshoppers: catch
  in grass at dawn
- Larvae/grubs: found in rotting wood

## Finding Worms

- Dig in moist, dark soil
- Under logs and rocks near water
- After rain: come to surface at night
- Leaf litter areas in forest
- Near compost or decaying vegetation

## Insect Baits

- Grasshoppers: excellent surface bait
- Crickets: good general bait
- Caterpillars: effective in summer
- Beetles and grubs: bottom fishing
- Stone fly nymphs (under rocks):
  excellent trout bait

## Dough and Paste Baits

- Mix flour with water to stiff paste
- Add garlic, cheese, or fish oil
- Form small balls on hook
- Change every 30 min -- softens
- Effective for carp, catfish, panfish

## Fishing with Bait

- Hook worm through body 2-3 times
  leaving ends free to wiggle
- Crayfish: hook through tail
- Insects: through thorax or body
- Dough: mold around hook bend
