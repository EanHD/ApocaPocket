# Destroying Angel

## Identification

- Entire mushroom pure white
- Classic mushroom shape; medium to large
- White gills; white cap; white stem
- Ring (skirt) on stem visible
- Bulb (volva) at base buried in soil

## Why It's So Dangerous

- Looks like a perfect white mushroom
- Tastes mild; no bitterness or warning
- Symptoms delayed 6-24 hours
- Liver failure occurs 3-5 days later
- High mortality even with treatment

## Amanita East Species

- Most common destroying angel in eastern US
- Pure white; grows in deciduous forest
- Late summer through fall
- Often near oak and beech

## Amanita West Species

- Western destroying angel; Pacific coast
- White to off-white; under oaks
- Spring (earlier than eastern species)
- Equally deadly

## The Dig Test

- Always dig up any white mushroom base
- Destroying angel: white bulb in membrane cup
- Cup is often underground; must dig
- Bulb in membrane sack = Amanita = STOP

## Button Stage Danger

- Young buttons look like puffballs
- This is why puffball slice test is critical
- Cut any white ball: angel inside = mushroom outline
- Solid white interior only = puffball

## If Accidentally Eaten

- Seek medical help IMMEDIATELY
- Even if feeling fine: go now
- Treatment window is narrow
- Do not wait for symptoms to appear

## Safe vs Dangerous

- Puffball: NO gills, NO stem, solid white inside
- Oyster mushroom: on wood, NOT in soil
- Destroying angel: ALWAYS in soil with volva
