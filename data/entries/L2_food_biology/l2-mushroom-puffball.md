# Giant Puffball

## Identification

- White round to oval ball on ground
- Size: baseball to soccer ball
- Smooth white outer skin
- Interior must be pure white when sliced
- No stem, cap, or gills visible

## Mandatory Slice Test

- ALWAYS cut in half vertically
- Interior: pure solid white = SAFE
- Any yellow, purple, or outlines = STOP
- Yellow/discolored = old; do not eat
- Outline of a small mushroom inside = AMANITA EGG (deadly)

## Amanita Egg Test

- Young Amanita buttons look like puffballs
- Death cap starts as white egg underground
- Slice reveals outline of cap/stem/gills inside
- SOLID white throughout = true puffball only

## Where to Find

- Meadows, open fields, forest edges
- Often in fairy rings or scattered groups
- Late summer through fall
- Calvatia gigantea: largest, most common

## How to Harvest

- Kick gently to check if attached to ground
- Cut at base; take whole ball
- Slice immediately to verify interior
- Fresh: firm, dry, pure white

## Preparation

- Peel outer skin if tough
- Slice like bread loaf; 1/2-1 in slices
- Saute slices in fat: mild flavor
- Protein-rich and filling

## Nutritional Value

- Protein: ~5g per 100g fresh
- Vitamins B, C, D; calcium, potassium
- ~40 cal per 100g
- One of best-yield edible mushrooms by weight

## Lookalikes — DANGER

- Amanita egg (all Amanita spp in button stage)
- Death cap: inside has cap/gill outline
- Earthball (Scleroderma): dark interior when sliced
- RULE: solid white interior only = safe
