# Death Cap Warning

## Identification

- Cap: pale green to yellowish-green
- White gills; white to pale yellow cap
- SKIRT (ring) on upper stem
- CUP (volva) at stem base in soil
- White stem; bulbous base in membrane

## Amanita Checkpoints

- Always dig up base; check for cup (volva)
- Cup: membrane sack at soil level
- Skirt: floppy ring around upper stem
- Both cup AND skirt = Amanita genus
- Amanita = assume toxic until proven

## Deceptive Properties

- Tastes good; no warning signs when eaten
- Symptoms delayed 6-24+ hours
- By the time symptoms appear: liver failing
- No antidote; treatment is supportive only

## Symptoms Timeline

- 0-6 hours: often no symptoms
- 6-24 hours: severe GI pain, vomiting
- 24-72 hours: apparent improvement
- 72+ hours: liver/kidney failure begins
- Mortality 15-30% even with hospital care

## Species to Know

- A. phalloides: green cap; most common
- A. ocreata: white cap; west coast
- A. bisporigera: destroying angel; white
- All cause identical delayed liver failure

## Never Eat These

- No preparation removes the toxin
- Cooking, drying, boiling: all ineffective
- The alpha-amanitin toxin is heat-stable
- One cap contains lethal dose for adult

## What It Looks Like

- Often mistaken for edible paddy straw mushroom
- Asian immigrants most common victims (straw mush)
- Also mistaken for button mushrooms
- RULE: any mushroom with cup at base = STOP
