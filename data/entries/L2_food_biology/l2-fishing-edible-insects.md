# Edible Insects

## Overview

- Insects: 60-80% protein by dry weight
- More protein per pound than beef
- Available year-round in most climates
- Best when other food scarce
- Psychological barrier: biggest issue

## Best Target Species

- Grubs (beetle larvae): rotting wood,
  soil -- fat and protein rich
- Crickets: under rocks, logs, grass
- Grasshoppers: grass at dawn
- Ants: large black or carpenter ants
  (avoid fire ants)
- Termites: in dead wood, mounds

## Grub Collection

- Split rotting logs along grain
- Dig in soft soil near roots
- White/cream colored = edible
- Bright colors = potentially toxic
- Handle gently: bite if large species

## Grasshopper Harvest

- Sweep long grass with shirt/bag
- Collect at dawn: cold, cannot fly
- Check under rocks and logs
- Build small brush pile, set fire
  at one end: grasshoppers flee
  to other end -- collect there

## Preparation

- Remove wings, legs, and hard parts
- Gut large grubs (squeeze abdomen)
- Toast, fry, or boil all insects
- Do not eat raw if avoidable
- Roast in hot pan or on flat rock
  until crispy (2-5 min)

## Nutrition Facts

- Large grub: 10-15 calories each
- 100g grasshoppers: ~100 calories
- Collect large quantities for effect
- Add to stews or pound into paste
  to hide texture
