# Shore Fishing Technique

## Overview

- Shore fishing requires no boat
- Effective presentation beats distance
- Position matters most -- read water
  before casting
- Still fishing: bait on bottom or
  suspended at correct depth

## Position and Approach

- Stay low: fish spook easily
- Approach water slowly, crouch
- Do not stand directly at bank edge
- Cast parallel to shore when possible
- Sun behind you reduces shadow on water

## Cast and Drift

- Upstream cast: let bait drift down
  through productive water naturally
- Swing cast: cast across current,
  let line swing to bank on downstream
- Dead drift: no retrieve, natural float
  through current seam

## Still Fishing

- Find current break or structure
- Set bait at depth fish are holding
- Weight to hold bottom in current
- Bobber: set at 1.5x water depth
- Check bait every 15-20 min

## Retrieve Techniques

- Slow: bottom bouncing for catfish
- Medium: steady retrieve near structure
- Fast: surface lure or spinner
  (improvised with foil and hook)
- Varied: pause then pull, pause, pull

## Maximizing Effort

- Fish two or more lines at once
- Use passive set line while active
  fishing with another
- Move if no activity in 30 min
- Dawn and dusk are peak times
- After rain: fish shallow and active
