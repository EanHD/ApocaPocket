# Improvised Vinegar

## Overview

- Vinegar = dilute acetic acid
  made by acetobacter bacteria
- Made from fruit wine left open to air
- Takes 2-4 weeks for full conversion
- Uses: food preservation, flavoring,
  cleaning wounds, brine pickling

## Starting the Process

- Begin with wild fruit wine or
  fermented grain liquid
- Must have some alcohol (fermented)
  before vinegar can form
- Pour into wide-mouth container
- Leave open to air -- acetobacter
  needs oxygen to convert alcohol

## Vinegar Formation

- Week 1-2: sharp alcohol smell
- Week 2-3: vinegar smell develops
- "Mother" forms: jellylike film on top
  (acetobacter colony) -- this is good
- Week 3-4: strong sour vinegar smell
  and sharp taste

## Testing Readiness

- Taste small amount: should be
  distinctly sharp and sour
- No alcohol taste should remain
- Strain through cloth to remove mother
  (can use mother to start next batch)

## Uses in Survival

- Brine pickling: see Vinegar Brining
- Preserve cooked meat for 1-2 days
- Flavoring for bland foraged foods
- Diluted: clean wounds (not ideal
  but better than nothing)

## Storage

- Store in sealed container
- Lasts indefinitely if sealed
- Stronger = better preservation
- Label clearly
