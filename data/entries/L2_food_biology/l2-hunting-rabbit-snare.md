# Rabbit Snaring

## Overview

- Rabbits follow same runs repeatedly
- Most reliable snare target in
  temperate North America
- Best wire: 22-24 gauge stiff wire
- Paracord inner strand also works
- Set 10+ snares for consistent yield

## Finding Rabbit Runs

- Look for worn paths through grass
  or under brush tangles
- Brown fur snagged on branches
- Round pea-sized droppings on trail
- Runs connect brush piles to fields
- Edges of fields and thickets

## Snare Construction

- Cut 12 inches of wire
- Form loop 4-5 inches diameter
- Twist end back through itself 2-3x
  to form sliding noose
- Leave 6-8 inches for attachment
- Noose should slide freely

## Placement

- Bottom of noose: 3-4 in above ground
  (rabbit neck height)
- Center snare in the run opening
- Support with forked stick or branch
- Tie off to anchor: stake or limb
- Guide rabbit through -- not block

## Natural Guides

- Pile brush on sides to funnel rabbit
  through the snare opening
- Use natural constrictions: under logs,
  between bushes
- Avoid disturbing run with boots
- Wear gloves to reduce scent

## Checking and Resetting

- Check every 4-8 hours minimum
- Dispatch rabbit: twist neck quickly
- Reset snare after every catch
- Move snare if no catch in 2 days
