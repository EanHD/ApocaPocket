# Clay Baking

## Overview

- Ancient cooking method requiring
  only clay and fire
- Works for: fish, birds, frogs, crab
  shell-on vegetables, tubers
- Steam inside clay cooks food evenly
- Feathers/scales pull off with clay
  when cracked open
- No seasonings needed but can include
  herbs inside clay wrap

## Finding Suitable Clay

- Look near riverbanks and stream cuts
- Bluish-gray or red-brown clay
- Test: press in hand, hold shape,
  does not crumble when pushed
- Too sandy: crumbles -- add more clay
- Work clay until smooth, pliable

## Preparing Food

- Do not scale fish or pluck bird
- Gut fish and bird: remove organs
- Add herbs or wild onion inside cavity
- Wrap in fresh green leaves first
  (optional, prevents clay contact)

## Clay Wrapping

- Work clay into flat sheets 1/2 in
- Wrap all sides of food completely
- Seal all seams firmly
- Smooth over to remove air pockets
- Ball shape cooks most evenly

## Cooking

- Rake coals aside, place clay ball
- Push coals around and over clay ball
- Maintain fire for 30-45 min for
  medium fish, 45-60 min for large
- Clay turns gray, hard, and may crack

## Cracking Open

- Pull from coals with sticks
- Let cool 5 min
- Strike with rock to crack open
- Feathers, scales, skin stick to clay
  and pull away cleanly
- Steam releases: food inside is cooked
