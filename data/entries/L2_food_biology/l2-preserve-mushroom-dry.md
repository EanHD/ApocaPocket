# Drying Mushrooms

## Overview

- Drying preserves mushrooms 6-12 months
- Intensifies flavor and concentrates
  nutritional value
- Works for: morels, chanterelles,
  oysters, porcini, turkey tail
- Rehydrates easily in water or broth

## ID and Cleaning

- Only dry positively identified species
- Toxic mushrooms remain toxic dried
- Clean: brush dirt off with cloth
  or dry brush -- do not wash if
  possible (retains moisture)
- Trim stem bases if dirty

## Slicing for Drying

- Slice through cap and stem: 1/4 in
- Thin, uniform slices dry evenly
- Very small mushrooms: dry whole
  or halved
- Remove all damaged/soft areas
  before drying

## Drying Methods

- Thread on cordage: string through
  center of slices, hang in sun/wind
- Rack dry: lay on stick rack in
  sun and breeze
- Over fire: hang at 2-3 ft height
  in warm smoke zone (not hot)
- Time: 2-5 days in sun, 12-24 hrs
  over gentle heat

## Dryness Test

- Completely hard and crisp when done
- Snap cleanly with no flex
- No soft or pliable areas remain
- Color: darker, concentrated
- If stored before fully dry: molds

## Rehydration

- Cover with boiling water
- Soak 15-30 minutes minimum
- Mushrooms plump and soften
- Use soaking liquid in cooking:
  full of flavor and nutrients
- Squeeze out and add to any dish
