# Field Jerky Making

## Overview

- Simplest meat preservation method
- Any lean meat: venison, rabbit,
  turkey, beef, fish
- Fat causes rancidity: remove all fat
- Salt optional but improves safety
  and shelf life significantly
- Shelf life: 1-2 weeks without salt,
  2-4 weeks with salt

## Slicing

- Cut with grain for chewiness
- Cut against grain for tender jerky
- Thickness: 1/4 inch uniform slices
- Remove all visible fat and connective
  tissue before slicing
- Partially freeze meat for easier
  uniform slicing

## Salting and Seasoning

- Salt both sides generously
- Rub in and allow 1-2 hours to cure
- Optional: wild garlic, dried herbs,
  black pepper
- Rinse excess salt before drying
  (if large amount of salt used)

## Drying Methods

- Sun dry: hang on stick rack, hot sun
  with good airflow, 2-3 days
- Fire dry: hang at 4-6 ft above fire
  in smoke (not flames), 12-24 hrs
- Hot rock: lay thin strips on sun-
  heated flat rocks, flip frequently

## Done Test

- Dry throughout -- no moist center
- Bend test: cracks but holds together
- Dark, leathery appearance
- No translucency remaining in meat
- Should snap cleanly when cold

## Storage

- Breathable container: cloth bag,
  woven basket
- Do not seal in plastic while warm
- Cool and dry location
- Check daily -- remove any soft pieces
