# Field Food Safety

## Overview

- Food poisoning kills in wilderness
  where medical care is unavailable
- When in doubt: throw it out
- Prioritize temperature control
  over everything else
- Learn the danger zone: 40-140F

## Temperature Rules

- Below 40F: bacteria slow -- safe 5-7d
- 40-140F: DANGER ZONE
- Above 140F: bacteria stop growing
- Above 160F: kills most pathogens
- Above 212F (boiling): kills almost all

## Spoilage Signs in Meat

- Sour or sweet-sour smell
- Slimy or tacky surface
- Gray, green, or unusual color
- Any mold growth
- Soft texture when it should be firm

## Spoilage Signs in Fish

- Strong ammonia or sour smell
- Dull or sunken eyes (fresh fish)
- Flesh separates from bones easily
- Gills are gray or brown not red
- Slime is discolored or absent

## Safe Food Handling

- Wash hands with soap or scrub with
  sand before handling food
- Keep raw and cooked food separate
- Use clean cutting surfaces
- Cook food the day it is killed
  when above 60F

## High-Risk Situations

- Swollen preserved containers: botulism
  risk -- do not open, do not eat
- Roadkill or old kill: only eat if
  core of meat smells completely clean
- Shellfish from unknown waters: PSP
  risk in marine species
