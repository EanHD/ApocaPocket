# Small Game Snares

## Overview

- Snares are the most calorie-efficient
  hunting method in survival
- Set many snares (10+) to ensure yield
- Wire best: 22-24 gauge for rabbit
  28-32 gauge for squirrel or birds
- Paracord inner strand for small targets

## Loop Sizes by Species

- Mouse/vole: 1-2 inch diameter
- Squirrel: 3-4 inch diameter
- Rabbit: 4-5 inch diameter
- Raccoon/fox: 5-7 inch diameter
- Coyote: 8-10 inch diameter
- Beaver: 8-10 inch submerged

## Height from Ground

- Mouse/vole: 1 inch
- Squirrel: 4-6 in on pole or branch
- Rabbit: 3-4 inches (fist height)
- Fox/raccoon: 6-8 inches

## Placement Rules

- On an active trail with fresh sign
- Support noose so it hangs open
- Anchor to stake driven in ground
- Use natural vegetation as guides
- Check/reset every 4-8 hours

## Improving Catch Rate

- Funnel with brush beside trail
- Wear gloves to reduce scent
- Boil wire to remove oil before use
- Set snares on dark or moonless nights
  for nocturnal species

## Dispatch and Reset

- Approach calmly from downwind
- Dispatch quickly: neck dislocation
- Reset snare immediately after catch
- Relocate after 3 days of no catch
