# Root Cellar Basics

## Overview

- Ground below frost line maintains
  near-constant 32-50F year round
- Higher humidity than air: good for
  root vegetables and some fruits
- Passive refrigeration: no energy
- Can store roots, tubers, cabbages,
  apples, carrots for months

## Site Selection

- North-facing slope: less solar heat
- Well-drained soil: prevent flooding
- Away from trees: roots intrude
- Near camp but not in shelter area
- Accessible in all seasons

## Construction

- Dig down 4-6 ft or into hillside
- At least 6 ft deep in cold climates
- Dimensions: 4x4 ft minimum
- Log or timber roof structure
- 18 in of soil over roof minimum
- Leave ventilation: small stick pipe
  through roof (insect-screened)

## Shelving

- Build simple shelves from sticks
- Keep food off ground: air circulation
- Separate different foods: some
  produce ethylene (apples) which
  ripens others faster

## Temp and Humidity

- Target: 32-40F for most produce
- 40-50F: root vegetables acceptable
- High humidity: line walls with damp
  moss or leave bucket of water
- Low humidity (grains): keep dry,
  line floor with bark or gravel

## What to Store

- Roots: carrots, turnips, parsnips
- Tubers: potatoes, Jerusalem artichoke
- Apples/pears: separate from roots
- Cured squash: warmer section
- Avoid: berries, lettuce, soft fruit
