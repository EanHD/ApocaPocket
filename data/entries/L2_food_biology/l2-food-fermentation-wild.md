# Wild Fermentation

## Overview

- Wild yeast exists on grain, fruit
  skin, and in air
- Fermentation: preserves food, adds
  nutrition, makes bread rise
- Simplest wild ferment: flour + water
  sourdough starter
- Sour flavor = healthy lactobacillus
  bacteria -- safe

## Wild Sourdough Starter

- Mix 2 tbsp ground grain or flour
  with enough water to make paste
- Leave uncovered in warm location
- Wild yeast lands from air
- Stir twice daily
- Bubbles appear in 2-3 days = active
- Small sour smell = healthy

## Feeding the Starter

- Discard half, add fresh flour/water
- Feed every 12-24 hours
- Smell: tangy/sour = good
- Pink or orange color = discard all
- After 5-7 days: reliably active

## Bread with Starter

- Mix 1 cup starter with 2 cups flour
- Add water until shaggy dough forms
- Knead 5-10 min until smooth
- Rest 4-8 hours in warm location
- Shape, rest 1-2 hours more
- Bake on hot rock until hollow sound

## Fruit Fermentation

- Crush ripe fruit (wild yeast on skin)
- Place in container at room temp
- Bubbling in 2-3 days = fermenting
- Consume within 1 week or distill
- Low alcohol, high nutrition

## Grain Fermentation

- Soak grain 24-48 hours until soft
- Drain, allow to sprout slightly
  (24 hrs) -- increases nutrition
- Grind to mash for porridge/beer
