# Fruit Drying

## Overview

- Simple and effective preservation
- Works for: berries, apples, plums,
  rose hips, crabapples, wild grapes
- Sun drying requires: sun, low humidity,
  good airflow, insect protection
- Shelf life: 2-12 months when fully dry

## Preparing Fruit

- Wash in clean water
- Remove pits and cores
- Slice thin: 1/4 inch for large fruit
- Small berries: use whole or halve
- Optional pretreating: dip in dilute
  lemon juice to reduce browning

## Drying Rack Setup

- Weave sticks to create flat rack
- Space gaps 1/2 inch for airflow
- Lay fruit in single layer: no overlap
- Elevate rack for airflow under
- Shade cloth or thin fabric over
  top keeps insects off

## Drying Methods

- Sun: 2-5 days in hot, dry conditions
- Fire: hang above or beside low fire
  in smoke zone, 12-48 hours
- Avoid: direct flame, temps over 150F
  (cooks fruit rather than dries)

## Testing Dryness

- Small berries: hard and leathery
- Sliced fruit: pliable, no moist center
- Bend test: cracks slightly but holds
- Squeeze test: no juice releases
- Color: intensified, darker

## Storage

- Store in breathable container
- Cool, dry, dark location
- Check weekly: remove any moldy
  pieces immediately to save batch
