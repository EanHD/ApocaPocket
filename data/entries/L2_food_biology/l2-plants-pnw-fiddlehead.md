# Fiddlehead Fern

## Identification

- Tightly coiled spiral shoot emerging from ground
- Papery brown scales on the crozier
- Deep U-shaped groove on inside of stalk
- Ostrich fern: large vase shape when mature
- Harvest when coil is tight, 2-6 inches tall

## The Groove Test

- Ostrich fern: deep U or C groove inside stalk
- Bracken fern: round stalk, NO groove
- This is the critical safety distinction
- Only harvest if groove is clearly present

## Where to Find

- Moist forest stream banks and floodplains
- PNW: April-May in lowland areas
- Forms colonies; multiple plants in area
- Shaded moist soil near running water

## How to Harvest

- Snap off tightly coiled head at base
- Take only 2-3 from each plant max
- Harvest when coil is tight and firm
- Remove papery brown scales

## Preparation

- ALWAYS cook; never eat raw
- Boil or steam 10-15 minutes minimum
- Saute in fat after boiling for flavor
- Tastes like asparagus-green bean cross

## Nutritional Value

- High in vitamins A, C, and niacin
- Iron and potassium content notable
- ~34 cal per 100g cooked
- Brief seasonal window; prioritize harvest

## Lookalikes — DANGER

- Bracken fern (Pteridium aquilinum)
- Bracken: round stalk, no U groove
- Bracken contains carcinogens even cooked
- Ostrich fern: MUST have U-groove in stalk
