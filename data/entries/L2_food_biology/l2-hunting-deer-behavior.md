# Deer Behavior Patterns

## Overview

- Deer are crepuscular: peak at dawn/dusk
- Bed in thick cover during midday
- Travel along edges: field/forest,
  ridge lines, creek bottoms
- Follow same trails repeatedly
- Dominated by smell -- scent is key

## Daily Movement Cycle

- Pre-dawn: move from feeding to bed
- Dawn 1-2hr: active feeding, travel
- Midday: bedded in thick brush
- Late afternoon: rise, begin moving
- Dusk: active feeding in fields
- Night: feed in open areas

## Feeding Zones

- Mast crops: acorns, beech nuts
- Agricultural fields: corn, soybeans
- Browse: young shrubs, twigs, forbs
- Fruit: apples, persimmons
- Preferred food changes by season

## Seasonal Patterns

- Spring: follow green-up uphill
- Summer: near water sources, shade
- Fall pre-rut: scrapes and rubs appear
- Rut (Oct-Nov): bucks roam widely
- Winter: conserve energy, south slopes

## Ambush Strategy

- Find funnel between bedding/feeding
- Set up 100-150 yds from bedding
- Hunt feeding areas at dusk
- Hunt funnels during rut
- Wind in face, sun at your back

## Sign to Watch For

- Fresh tracks in soft soil or mud
- Droppings: size indicates deer size
- Trails worn into ground
- Rubs and scrapes during fall rut
