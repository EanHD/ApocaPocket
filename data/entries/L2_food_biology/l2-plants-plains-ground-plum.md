# Ground Plum

## Identification

- Low spreading plant 6-12 in on ground
- Pinnate compound leaves with many leaflets
- Purple pea-like flowers in May
- Inflated oval pod: green to red-purple
- Pod is fleshy and firm when young

## Safe Species Only

- Fat plum-shaped inflated pods
- Pods turn red-purple when mature
- Ground level plant in prairie grassland
- Locoweed species: pods elongated, not plum-fat

## Where to Find

- Dry to mesic prairie grassland
- Well-drained soil in Great Plains
- Kansas, Nebraska, Dakotas
- Pods ready May-June when still green

## How to Harvest

- Harvest only young green pods
- Pull entire pod from plant
- Eat raw or boil briefly
- Stop harvesting after pods purple/dry

## Preparation

- Young pods: eat raw; sweet pea flavor
- Raw pods: like crisp snap peas
- Boil older pods 5-10 min
- Seeds inside: edible when young, not dry

## Nutritional Value

- Protein and carbohydrate in pods
- Similar to garden peas nutritionally
- Vitamins C in fresh pods
- Limited harvest window; opportunistic

## Lookalikes — DANGER

- Locoweed (Astragalus/Oxytropis): TOXIC
- Toxic species: elongated pods, not plum-fat
- A. crassicarpus: distinctly plum-fat oval pods
- If pods not clearly fat and plum-like: skip
