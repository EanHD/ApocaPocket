# Deer Field Dressing

## Overview

- Start within 30 min of harvest
- Core temp must drop fast to prevent
  bacterial growth and spoilage
- All you need: sharp knife, gloves
- Work quickly and cleanly
- Cooling priority over everything

## Step-by-Step

- 1. Drag deer to flat, cool ground
- 2. Lay on back, spread hind legs
- 3. Pinch skin at sternum, cut to
  pelvis -- avoid cutting gut
- 4. Reach in, cut diaphragm free
- 5. Sever windpipe at base of neck
- 6. Roll deer to drain cavity fully

## Removing Organs

- Pull esophagus and trachea down
- All organs follow out together
- Save: heart, liver, kidneys
  (highest nutrition -- cook first)
- Tie off anus with cord before
  removing to prevent contamination

## After Gutting

- Wipe cavity with dry grass or cloth
- Prop cavity open with stick for
  maximum airflow and cooling
- Hang if possible: at least 6 ft up
- Remove skin quickly above 50F

## Meat Cooling Priority

- Above 50F: skin immediately
- 40-50F: skin within 4 hours
- Below 40F: can leave skin 12-24 hr
- Do not seal in airtight bag if warm

## Common Mistakes

- Cutting through gut: wipe off bile
  immediately with clean cloth
- Leaving liver too long: harvest first
- Not elevating carcass: ground heat
  ruins meat from underneath
