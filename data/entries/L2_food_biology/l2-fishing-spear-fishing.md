# Spear Fishing

## Overview

- Effective in clear, shallow water
  under 3 feet deep
- Best for large slow-moving fish
- Refraction: fish appear closer to
  surface than they are
- Always aim lower than you see the fish
- Move extremely slowly to avoid
  startling fish before thrust

## Spear Construction

- Straight pole 6-8 ft, 1-1.5 in diam
- Split end 4 ways for 6 inches
- Insert small crosspiece stick to
  spread tines apart
- Sharpen each tine to a point
- Fire-harden all points: hold in
  coals, rotate, do not char

## Refraction Correction

- In 1 ft water: aim 3-4 in below fish
- In 2 ft water: aim 6-8 in below fish
- Practice rule: aim at reflection
  of fish on bottom, not the fish
- Angle spear steeply to compensate
- Accuracy comes only with practice

## Hunting Strategy

- Move against current, facing fish
- Fish hold nose into current
- Wade extremely slowly -- lift feet
  clear before placing down
- Stop when fish faces away or holds
- Thrust fast, straight down

## Best Locations

- Pools below riffles and rapids
- Rocky shallows in clear streams
- Tidal flats at low tide
- Around logs and undercut banks
- Night: fish hold still in shallows

## Tips for Success

- Early morning and evening best
- Cloudy days: less shadow from you
- Night gigging with torch: fish
  are mesmerized by light, hold still
