# Pot Smoking

## Overview

- Small-scale smoking without full rig
- Best for: single fish, small game,
  portions of larger meat
- Works in clay pot, bark box, or
  any partially enclosed space
- Useful when weather prevents
  traditional open-air smoking

## Container Setup

- Clay pot: place smoking wood on
  bottom layer, food on rack above
- Bark box: birch bark folded into
  box shape, food suspended inside
- Rock enclosure: stack rocks to
  create 3-sided box, cover with bark
- Size: just larger than the food

## Wood Chip Method

- Handful of dry hardwood chips
- Or handful of green hardwood chips
  (more smoke, lower heat)
- Place in bottom of container
- Heat from small fire below container
- Or place chips on hot coals inside
  base of container

## Process

- Load container with food and chips
- Place on small fire
- Seal top loosely with bark or cloth
  (allow steam and smoke to escape
  through small gap)
- Smoke 30-60 min for small fish
- Check every 15 min

## Signs of Completion

- Fish: opaque, flakes easily
- Meat: surface dried, golden color
- Internal: hot to touch throughout
- Smoke smell permeated the food

## Limitations

- Small capacity -- one or two servings
- Less smoke penetration than full rig
- Good for immediate consumption
- Not for long-term preservation only
