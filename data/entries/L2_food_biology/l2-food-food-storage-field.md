# Field Food Storage

## Overview

- Two threats: temperature and animals
- Temperature: affects bacterial growth
- Animals: bears, raccoons, rodents
  can destroy entire food supply
- Cache well every single time

## Bear Protection

- Never store food inside shelter
  or within 200 ft of camp
- Hang method: throw rope over branch
  12 ft high, 6 ft from trunk
- Bag hangs 6-8 ft below branch
  and 8 ft below attachment point
- Bear canister: most reliable
  hard-sided option

## Rodent Protection

- Hang all food off ground
- Line inside of hang bag with
  mesh or stiff fabric (rodents chew)
- Do not leave crumbs in camp
- Store in metal container if possible
- Check cache each morning

## Temperature Management

- Use natural cold: streams, north
  slopes, caves, root cellar concept
- Shade is critical above 50F
- Evaporative cooling: wet cloth over
  container in breeze
- Desert: bury container 12 in deep
  in shade -- much cooler

## Dry Food Storage

- Dry food in airtight container
- Bury container in dry soil
- Mark location clearly
- Moisture ruins dried food fastest
- Check seal every few days

## Wet or Cooked Food

- Store in sealed container
- Keep in coolest location available
- Consume within 24 hours in summer
- Consume within 48 hours in cool
  weather (below 50F)
