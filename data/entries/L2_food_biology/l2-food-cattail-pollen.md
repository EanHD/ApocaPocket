# Cattail Pollen Flour

## Overview

- Cattail pollen: abundant, nutritious,
  free from common allergens
- Bright yellow, powdery -- June/July
  in most North America
- High protein, carbohydrate
- Can replace or augment other flours
- Easiest wild flour to collect quickly

## Harvest Timing

- Male flower spike (above) is bright
  yellow with heavy pollen coating
- Pollen shakes off freely when touched
- Window: 1-2 weeks per location
- Timing varies by elevation and region
- After pollen shed: green spike remains

## Collection Method

- Bend pollen head into bag or pot
- Shake vigorously -- pollen falls free
- Collect directly into container
- One head: 1-2 tablespoons pollen
- 20-30 heads = useful baking quantity
- Work quickly to beat wind/rain

## Processing

- Sift through cloth to remove insect
  or plant debris
- Ready to use as-is: no drying needed
- Mix 50/50 with other flour
- Using pure pollen: very dense batter
  -- needs more water

## Recipes

- Pollen pancakes: mix with water,
  salt if available, cook on hot rock
- Pollen bread: mix with acorn flour,
  form patty, bake on hot rock
- Pollen mush: stir into boiling water,
  simmer 10-15 min, eat as porridge

## Nutrition

- High in protein (approx 28%)
- Carbohydrate rich
- Contains vitamins and minerals
- Calorie dense relative to volume
