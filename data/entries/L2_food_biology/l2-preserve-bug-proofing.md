# Bug-Proof Food Storage

## Overview

- Insects contaminate and consume
  stored food rapidly in warm weather
- Two main threats: flying insects
  (flies, moths) and crawling
  (ants, beetles, weevils)
- Prevention: containment, elevation,
  and natural repellents

## Container Choices

- Hardest: metal, ceramic, clay pot
  with tight lid
- Good: tightly woven bark basket
  with clay coating
- Adequate: cloth bag inside sealed
  outer container
- Poor: open cloth bag alone

## Hanging Food

- Hang container from single cord
  so insects cannot walk to it
- Greasy cord: insects slide off
- Height: 5-6 ft off ground minimum
- Away from tree trunk: ants climb
  trees and drop onto food cache

## Natural Repellents

- Bay leaves: place 5-6 in container
  with grain -- repels weevils
- Cedar chips: repels moths
- Mint bundles near storage area
- Lavender: repels many insects
- Pine pitch: apply to container
  exterior as barrier

## Fly Protection on Meat

- Cheesecloth or thin cloth over
  hanging meat
- Smoke keeps flies off (active fire)
- Hanging in direct wind: reduces
  fly landing
- Process meat immediately after
  harvest: do not leave exposed

## Infested Storage

- If weevils in grain: spread thin
  and heat in sun -- weevils leave
- Sift weevils out: still edible grain
- Fly eggs on meat (white clusters):
  cut off and discard affected area
