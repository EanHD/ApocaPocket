# Rabbit Field Dressing

## Overview

- Fastest field dress of any game
- No special tools required
- Two-minute process when practiced
- Wear gloves -- tularemia is real risk
- Check: spots on liver or white nodules
  = discard entire animal

## Pinch-Pull Skinning

- Hold rabbit by hind legs
- Pinch skin at mid-back
- Pull skin apart in both directions
- Skin peels toward head and feet
- Pull over feet and head: one motion
- Entire skin off in 30 seconds

## Gutting

- Hold skinned rabbit upside down
- Pinch belly skin, make small cut
- Extend cut from chest to pelvis
- Organs slide out from cavity
- Cut esophagus at neck to free all
- Shake out or finger-sweep remaining

## Organ Evaluation

- Liver: smooth, dark red = healthy
- Spotted or bumpy liver = tularemia
  -- discard entire animal
- Off smell from cavity = discard

## Preparing for Cooking

- Rinse in cold water
- Joint into quarters: two legs,
  two back pieces
- Parboil older rabbits 20-30 min
  before other cooking
- Young rabbits under 3 lbs: roast

## Tularemia Note

- Infection from handling or eating
- Symptoms: fever, swollen glands
- Prevention: gloves, full cooking
- If in doubt, discard -- not worth risk
