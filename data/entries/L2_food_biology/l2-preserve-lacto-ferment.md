# Lacto-Fermentation

## Overview

- Lactobacillus bacteria (present on all
  vegetables) convert sugars to lactic
  acid in absence of oxygen
- Acid preserves food and adds
  probiotics for gut health
- No starter needed: wild bacteria do
  the work
- Simplest safe ferment for beginners

## Salt Percentage

- 2% salt by weight: standard
  (20g salt per kg of vegetables)
- Too little salt (under 1.5%): yeast
  and mold can dominate
- Too much salt (over 5%): fermentation
  stops, food too salty
- Measure by taste: noticeably salty
  but not overpoweringly so

## Vegetables That Work

- Cabbage (sauerkraut): classic
- Daikon radish, turnips
- Wild garlic scapes
- Green beans, cucumbers
- Most non-starchy vegetables

## Process

- Shred or slice vegetables thin
- Massage with salt until liquid
  releases (10-15 min)
- Pack tightly into container
- Liquid should cover vegetables fully
- If not: add 2% brine to cover
- Weight down to keep submerged
- Cover with cloth, not airtight lid

## Fermentation Timeline

- Day 1-2: bubbling begins (CO2)
- Day 3-5: tangy smell develops
- Day 5-7: sour taste established
- Week 2+: fully fermented, sour
- Ready when: distinctly sour, crisp
  texture maintained

## Storage

- Move to cool location when sour
- Cool slows fermentation further
- Shelf life: months in cool storage
  if kept submerged
