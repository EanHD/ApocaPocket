# Drying Wild Herbs

## Overview

- Dried herbs preserve culinary and
  medicinal value for 6-12 months
- Shade drying preserves essential oils
  better than direct sun
- Works for: mint, thyme, oregano,
  sage, yarrow, chamomile, rosemary
- Quick to harvest and easy to process

## Harvest Timing

- Harvest before flower fully opens:
  essential oil content highest
- Morning after dew dries: best time
- Take no more than 1/3 of plant
- Choose healthy, undamaged stems
- Dry weather days: less mold risk

## Bundle Method

- Group 5-10 stems together
- Tie at cut ends with cordage
- Hang upside down in shade
- Good airflow essential: spread
  bundles apart, not touching
- Takes 5-14 days

## Loose Herb Drying

- Strip leaves from stems
- Spread single layer on bark or cloth
- Keep in shade with airflow
- Turn once daily
- Faster than bundle method: 2-5 days

## Dryness Test

- Crumble between fingers: powders
  or breaks into pieces easily
- No green smell (dried = muted smell,
  stronger when crumbled)
- No moisture felt when squeezing

## Storage

- Strip dried leaves from stems
- Store in sealed container or
  cloth bag away from light
- Label with name and date
- Use within 12 months for best
  potency
- Whole dried herbs last longer
  than pre-crumbled
