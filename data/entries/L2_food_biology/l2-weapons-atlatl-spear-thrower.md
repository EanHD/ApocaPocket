# Atlatl (Spear Thrower)

**Function**: Extends arm by 18–24 inches, doubling dart velocity (2x speed = 4x kinetic energy). Equivalent stopping power to a .357 Magnum. Easier to learn than a bow (10 hrs vs 30+).

## Atlatl Build

- Length: 18–24 inches; hardwood (oak, ash, maple); flat stick with slight curve following grain; 4–8 oz
- Hook/spur at far end: 1/4–1/2 inch projection, angled slightly upward; carved from same wood, lashed antler tine, or simple notch
- Grip: proximal end wrapped with leather; optional stone/shell weight 2–4 oz at grip for balance
- Steps: select straight branch or board; sand smooth; carve 1/4 inch hook at far end; wrap grip; add balance weight; must be rigid — no flex during throw

## Dart Design

- Length: 4–6 feet; diameter 1/2 inch at midpoint tapering to 1/4 inch at ends; weight 1–3 oz
- Point: stone (knapped flint/obsidian, sharpest but fragile), bone (durable), metal (best), fire-hardened wood (easiest)
- Fletching: 3–4 feather vanes 6–8 inches from rear; slight helical twist for spin; lash with sinew, seal with pine pitch
- Nock: 1/2 inch deep hollow cup drilled or burned into dart rear, precisely centered (off-center = tumble)

## Throwing

- Stance: left foot forward (right-handed), sideways to target, weight on back foot
- Grip: atlatl like a hammer; dart rests on atlatl with nock engaging hook; support dart with free hand
- Motion: cock past head (dart horizontal); step forward shifting weight; snap atlatl forward (do not release it); wrist snap at finish; follow through pointing atlatl at target
- Dart releases automatically at vertical — no finger timing required
- Angle: 30–40° for range, 20–30° for accuracy

## Hunting Applications

- Large game (deer, elk): vitals zone heart/lungs, 40–60 yards; kinetic energy 50–70 ft-lbs vs bow 30–50 ft-lbs
- Spear fishing: shallow clear water 10–15 feet; use barbed point; aim low to compensate for refraction

## Legal

- Legal in 30+ US states where archery equipment is allowed; permitted during archery seasons
- States including Alabama, Arizona, Colorado, Idaho, Missouri, Nebraska, Wyoming; verify current regulations

## Troubleshooting

- Dart wobbles in flight: off-center nock or bent shaft — re-center nock, straighten shaft over steam
- Dart falls off atlatl: nock too large or hook too small — adjust fit
- Low velocity: weak wrist snap or atlatl too short — lengthen atlatl; drill wrist snap drills
