# Dehydration Basics

## Overview

- Dehydration: removing water that
  bacteria and mold need to grow
- Water activity below 0.6 = shelf
  stable (most foods: 85%+ removed)
- Applies to: meat, fish, vegetables,
  fruit, mushrooms, herbs
- Sun, wind, and gentle heat all work

## Temperature Guidelines

- Meat/fish: 145-160F (kills pathogens)
- Vegetables: 120-140F
- Fruit: 115-135F
- Herbs: 95-115F
- Too hot: case hardening (dry outside,
  wet inside -- looks done but isn't)

## Airflow is Critical

- Stagnant hot air traps moisture
- Elevate food on rack for under-airflow
- Space pieces: no touching or stacking
- Position rack in breeze direction
- Turn food at least once during drying

## Field Drying Setup

- Suspend bamboo, stick, or cordage
  racks between trees in sunny spot
- Thin strips for fastest drying
- Slice parallel to maximize surface
- Cover with light cloth at night
  or when insects are heavy

## Testing for Dryness

- Meat: hard, snap-brittle, dark
- Vegetables: crisp or leathery
- Fruit: pliable, leathery, no juice
- Universal test: squeeze firmly --
  no moisture released
- Fold test: dry surface, no wet core

## Storage After Drying

- Allow to cool completely before
  storing: condensation destroys work
- Breathable container preferred
- Airtight if completely dry
- Label date and type
