# Fat Preservation

## Overview

- Rendered fat = calorie-dense stable
  preservation medium and food
- Lard (pig), tallow (beef/venison),
  bear fat all work
- Shelf life: 1-6 months if fully
  rendered and stored correctly
- Temperature critical: cool and dark

## Fat Longevity

- Bear fat: most stable -- high in
  monounsaturated fats
- Tallow (beef/venison): very stable,
  solid at room temp
- Lard (pig): stable, slightly less
  so than tallow
- Duck/goose fat: less stable, use
  within 1 month

## Rendering Method

- Remove all meat, blood, connective
  tissue before rendering
- Render low and slow: all moisture
  must be driven off completely
- No water remaining = no spoilage
  from bacteria
- Strain through cloth: remove all
  solid particles

## Vessel Selection

- Clay pot with tight-fitting lid
- Bark container sealed with pine pitch
- Any clean, dry, non-reactive vessel
- Avoid: containers that absorbed
  previous odors or had water

## Signs of Rancidity

- Yellow or orange discoloration
- Sour or paint-like smell
- Bitter taste
- Oxidized smell (like old cooking oil)
- Rancid fat: do not consume

## Extending Shelf Life

- Add salt (1 tsp per cup)
- Keep cool (below 60F ideal)
- Seal airtight if fully rendered
- Keep away from light
