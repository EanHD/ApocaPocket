# Fruit Leather

## Overview

- Preserves wild fruit when abundant
- Works with: berries, plums, apples,
  rose hips, elderberries
- Shelf life: 1-4 weeks dry and cool
- High sugar = natural preservation
- Can be eaten as-is or rehydrated

## Preparing the Fruit

- Mash or pulp ripe fruit thoroughly
- Remove large seeds if possible
- Small seeds are OK in finished leather
- No need to cook first -- raw works
  but cooked spreads more evenly
- Add nothing: natural sugar sufficient

## Drying on Rock

- Find large, clean flat rock in sun
- Heat rock first: pour water on,
  if it beads and steams = hot enough
- Spread thin layer of pulp (1/4 inch)
- Allow to dry completely: 1-2 days
- Peel when edges lift from rock

## Drying on Bark or Wood

- Line wooden frame with fresh bark
- Spread pulp thin on smooth side
- Set in sun and wind
- Turn leather once partially dry
- Remove when fully dry and pliable

## Signs of Completion

- Leather peels cleanly from surface
- No wet or tacky spots remain
- Surface slightly sticky but not wet
- Fold test: bends without cracking
  and holds its shape

## Eating and Storage

- Roll up and wrap in leaf
- Store in cool dry location
- Eat as travel food or dissolve in
  water for quick energy drink
