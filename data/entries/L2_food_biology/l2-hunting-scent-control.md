# Scent Control Hunting

## Overview

- Deer smell 1,000x better than humans
- Minor wind toward deer = instant
  alarm and departure
- Wind management is primary skill
- Scent masking is secondary support
- Cannot eliminate scent -- manage it

## Wind Rules

- Always approach from downwind
- Hunt with wind in face
- Check wind: toss light grass or thread
- Wind swirls in valleys and hollows
- Hunt ridges where wind is consistent

## Natural Masking Scents

- Roll in or rub on: pine needles, mud
- Crushed spruce or fir boughs
- Dried earth, wood ash on clothing
- Avoid: food odors, petroleum, soap
- Urinating on scrape during rut works

## Approach Strategy

- Walk slowly into wind to stand
- Use terrain to stay below skyline
- Avoid brushing against vegetation
- Enter from opposite side of expected
  deer approach route
- Plan exit route with safe wind angle

## Clothing Management

- Store hunting clothes in bag with
  pine needles, earth, cedar
- Do not wear hunting clothes in camp
- Rinse clothes in creek before hunt
- Wool traps less body odor than
  synthetic fabrics

## When Wind Shifts

- Freeze and watch for deer response
- If deer stiffens and stares: freeze
- If deer flags (tail up, runs): over
- Move quietly to new wind-safe position
