# Acorn Flour Making

## Overview

- Acorns: abundant, calorie-dense,
  storable source of carbohydrate
- Must leach tannins before eating
  (bitter, cause digestive issues)
- White oak: mild tannins, less work
- Red/black oak: more tannins, more
  leaching required

## Shelling Acorns

- Crack shell with rock or knife butt
- Remove seed (the edible nut meat)
- Discard caps and shells
- Identify wormy ones: discard
- Dry shelled acorns before grinding

## Grinding to Flour

- Use two stones: flat base, round
  grinding stone
- Grind in circular motion with
  moderate pressure
- Sift through cloth to remove coarse
  bits, regrind remaining
- Goal: fine cornmeal texture

## Leaching Tannins

- Cold water leach: submerge flour in
  container of cold water 12-24 hrs,
  change water 4-6 times
- Running water: bundle in cloth,
  submerge in clean stream 1-2 days
- Hot water leach: boil 15 min,
  drain, repeat 3-4 times (faster)
- Taste test: no bitterness = done

## Nutrition

- High carbohydrate: 60-70%
- Moderate fat: 15-30%
- Some protein: 5-8%
- Rich in minerals once leached
- Calorie dense: approx 350 cal/100g

## Uses

- Mix with water for flatbread dough
- Thicken soups and stews
- Mix with other flours to stretch
- Mush: simmer in water, eat warm
