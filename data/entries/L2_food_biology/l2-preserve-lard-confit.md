# Lard Confit Method

## Overview

- Confit: cook meat in its own fat,
  store submerged under fat layer
- Fat excludes oxygen: prevents aerobic
  bacterial growth
- Traditional French and Indigenous
  preservation method
- Works with duck, rabbit, pork, fish

## Requirements

- Large amount of rendered fat:
  enough to fully submerge meat
- Clean, sealed container
- Cool storage location
- Fully cooked meat throughout

## Cooking the Meat

- Salt meat 12-24 hours before
- Place in pot with rendered fat
- Cook on very low heat: fat should
  barely shimmer (200-225F)
- Cook until meat is very tender:
  2-4 hours for most cuts
- Meat falls from bone when done

## Packing and Sealing

- Place cooked meat in clean container
- Pour hot fat over meat: fully submerge
- Allow fat to harden on top: 1/2 in
  fat cap = proper seal
- Tap container to remove air bubbles
- Allow to cool completely before
  sealing and storing

## Storage

- Keep cool: below 60F ideal
- Below 40F: shelf life 1-3 months
- 40-60F: 2-4 weeks
- Fat cap must always cover meat
  -- if exposed, re-melt and re-seal

## Serving

- Scoop out fat carefully
- Remove meat from below
- Re-melt remaining fat, re-pour
  to seal remaining meat
