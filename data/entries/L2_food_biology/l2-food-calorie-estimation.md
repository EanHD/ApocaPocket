# Calorie Estimation

## Overview

- Survival minimum: 600-800 cal/day
  (sedentary)
- Active survival: 2,000-3,500 cal/day
- First 3 days: body uses fat reserves
- Beyond 3 days: prioritize calories
- Fat has 2x calories of protein/carbs

## Calorie Density Guide

- Fat/oils: 900 cal per 100g (highest)
- Nuts: 550-700 cal per 100g
- Dried meat/pemmican: 450-600 cal/100g
- Fresh meat: 150-250 cal per 100g
- Roots/tubers: 60-100 cal per 100g
- Leafy greens: 20-50 cal per 100g

## Estimating Portions

- Fist-sized chunk of meat: approx
  200-300 calories (fresh)
- Palm-sized nut meal: approx 400 cal
- Cup of cooked roots: 100-150 cal
- Tablespoon rendered fat: 120 cal
- Cup berries: 60-80 cal

## Rabbit Starvation

- Pure lean meat diet = protein toxicity
- Symptoms: headache, nausea, fatigue
  after 1-2 weeks lean-only diet
- Solution: add fat sources urgently
- Nuts, organ fat, bone marrow fix this
- Never eat only lean meat long-term

## Priority Foods

- Priority 1: fat sources (nuts, marrow)
- Priority 2: starchy roots, tubers
- Priority 3: meat and fish
- Priority 4: greens and berries
- Mix all types for best outcome

## 3-Day Rule

- Days 1-3: focus on water and shelter
- Day 3+: food becomes critical
- Each day without food: weaker,
  harder to forage -- start early
