# Cold Storage Methods

## Overview

- Cold slows bacterial growth:
  below 40F = safe for days to weeks
- No technology required -- use what
  nature provides
- Water, caves, north slopes, snow
  all provide natural cold

## Spring Storage

- Submerge sealed container in cold
  spring or running stream
- Anchor firmly to prevent loss
- Weight with rocks on top
- Cold running water holds 40-55F
  even in summer
- Check twice daily

## North-Facing Slopes

- In sun: north slopes get less heat
- Dig into north-facing hillside
- Cache in shade under dense canopy
- Temperature 10-20F cooler than
  ambient in summer

## Snow and Ice Cache

- Pack food in snow: 32F -- excellent
- Dig snow pit 2+ feet deep
- Pack snow around food package
- Mark location with stick or branch
  above snow line

## Root Cellar Principle

- Dig 3-4 ft into ground in shaded
  area -- ground temp stable 50-60F
- Line with rocks or bark
- Cover with flat rock or log lid
- Temperature stable year-round
  below frost line

## Evaporative Cooling

- Wet a cloth thoroughly
- Wrap around container
- Set in breeze in shade
- Evaporation drops temp 10-20F
- Re-wet cloth every 2-4 hours
