# Deadfall Traps

## Overview

- Kills by crushing: reliable, silent
- No wire required -- all natural wood
- Best for: mice, squirrel, rabbit,
  marten, raccoon
- Weight rule: rock/log = 3x animal
- Bait essential for best results

## Figure-4 Deadfall

- Three sticks with specific notches:
  1. Vertical post (upright)
  2. Diagonal brace
  3. Horizontal bait stick
- Notches interlock under weight
- Bait on tip of horizontal stick
- Animal tugs, sticks collapse
- Practice until release is sensitive

## Paiute Deadfall

- Simpler and more sensitive
- Uses cord loop on trigger stick
- Vertical stick, angled support,
  bait stick, short toggle, cord
- Toggle slips when bait is pulled
- Better for small animals: mice,
  chipmunks, squirrels

## Weight Ratios

- Squirrel: 2-3 lb rock
- Rabbit: 8-12 lb rock
- Raccoon: 20-30 lb flat rock or log
- Use flat rocks for even crush

## Bait Selection

- Peanut butter, nut meats, berries
- Dried fruit, grain, cheese
- Fresh meat if no other bait
- Change bait every 24 hours
- Bait should require tugging to move

## Placement

- Near burrow entrances and runways
- Under edge of log or rock overhang
- Level ground only -- uneven misfires
- Clear debris under trigger area
- Mark locations -- easy to forget
