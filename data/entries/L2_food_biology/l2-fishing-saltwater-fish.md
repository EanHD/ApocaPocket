# Saltwater Fishing

## Overview

- Coastal areas offer abundant fish year
  round in tidal zones
- No special gear needed beyond line,
  hook, and weight
- Tides control fish movement --
  fish incoming tide for best results
- Most coastal species are edible

## Tide Timing

- Incoming tide: best fishing window
- Fish move into shallows to feed
- 2 hours before high tide = peak
- Outgoing tide: fish in channels and
  deeper water
- Slack tide: fish move little, slowest

## Surf Casting

- Find channel or cut through sandbars
- Cast beyond breaking surf line
- Heavy weight (2-4 oz rock) to hold
  bottom in current
- Natural bait: sand crabs, mussels,
  cut fish, worms
- Let bait sit, check every 10-15 min

## Target Species

- Striped bass: channels, structure
- Redfish: shallow flats, estuaries
- Flounder: flat sandy bottom
- Perch/surf perch: entire surf zone
- Bluefish: open water, voracious

## Rock Fishing

- Rocky points and headlands hold fish
- Cast into white water and surge zones
- Work crevices and edges
- Watch for rogue waves: never turn
  back to ocean on rocks

## Fish to Avoid

- Puffer fish: internal organs toxic
- Unusual spines or coloration: caution
- Rule: common coastal fish are safe,
  odd-shaped fish need identification
