# Sealed Fat Storage

## Overview

- Hardened fat layer over food creates
  oxygen barrier: no air = no aerobic
  bacteria
- Works on cooked meat, stews, organs
- Simple technique with rendered fat
- Traditional preservation: native
  cultures worldwide used this method

## Principle

- Aerobic bacteria (most spoilage)
  need oxygen to grow
- Rendered fat hardens at room temp
- Fat cap seals food beneath from
  all oxygen contact
- Anaerobic bacteria stopped by
  cooking + salt before sealing

## Preparing the Food

- Cook thoroughly: all pathogens killed
- Salt during cooking (improves safety)
- Cool slightly -- not completely cold
- Pack into clean container
- Food should be dense, not loose

## Creating the Fat Cap

- Melt rendered fat to fully liquid
- Pour over cooked food
- Amount: at least 1/2 inch layer
  covering all surface completely
- Tap container: remove air bubbles
- Allow to harden fully before
  sealing container

## Inspection

- Check fat cap weekly
- Any crack or hole: re-melt fat,
  pour additional layer to re-seal
- Surface should be smooth, solid,
  no color changes
- Sour smell when opened: discard

## Shelf Life

- Room temp (below 70F): 1-2 weeks
- Below 50F: 3-4 weeks
- Below 40F: 2+ months
- Combination of salt + fat cap +
  cool storage = best results
