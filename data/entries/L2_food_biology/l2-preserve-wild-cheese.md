# Improvised Cheese

## Overview

- Cheese requires milk: useful if
  near abandoned farmstead or
  captured domestic animals
- Simple acid-set cheese: fastest
  and easiest field method
- Result: fresh soft cheese (paneer/
  cottage cheese type)
- High protein and fat

## Milk Sources

- Domestic cattle, goats, sheep
- May find abandoned or feral herds
- Wild mammals: not practical to milk
- Fresh raw milk: must use quickly
  or pasteurize first

## Pasteurizing Raw Milk

- Heat to just below boiling
  (180F, small bubbles forming)
- Hold at temperature 2-3 minutes
- Cool quickly before making cheese

## Acid-Set Method

- Heat milk to 180F
- Add acid while stirring:
  -- Vinegar: 1 tbsp per quart
  -- Lemon juice: 2 tbsp per quart
  -- Acidic plant juice if no other
- Curds form and separate from whey
- Stop heating, let stand 10 min

## Pressing the Cheese

- Line basket or bark container
  with clean cloth
- Pour curd and whey into cloth
- Twist cloth, allow whey to drain
  30-60 minutes
- Press with rock: 1-4 hours
  for firmer texture
- Salt exterior: 1 tsp per cup of curd

## Storage

- Fresh soft cheese: eat within 2 days
  at room temperature
- Below 40F: 1 week
- Aged with more salt: longer but
  needs controlled environment
