# Bird Trapping

## Overview

- Target: quail, grouse, pheasant,
  pigeons, doves (ground feeders)
- Trapping more reliable than pursuit
- Best set near water, food, dust baths
- Bait greatly increases effectiveness

## Wire Loop Snare

- 24-gauge wire, 4-6 in diameter loop
- Height: 1-2 in above ground
- Set on bird trail or feeding area
- Anchor firmly to stake or root
- Place 3-5 snares in cluster
- Bait with grain if available

## Figure-4 Deadfall

- Three notched sticks:
  1. Upright vertical post
  2. Diagonal brace
  3. Horizontal bait stick
- Rock or log 2-3x bird's weight
- Bait triggers collapse when pulled
- Practice until assembly is smooth

## Funnel Trap

- Drive stakes in V-shape to 6-8 in gap
- Weave brush through stakes for walls
- Place bait trail into funnel
- Bird walks in, cannot turn around
- Check 2-3 times daily

## Best Locations

- Near water sources at dawn/dusk
- Under fruit or berry-producing trees
- Dust bath areas (bare soil patches)
- Along brushy field edges

## Processing

- See Bird Field Dressing entry
- Cook thoroughly -- birds carry parasites
