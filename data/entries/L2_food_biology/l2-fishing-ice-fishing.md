# Ice Fishing Basics

## Overview

- Winter fishing can be vital survival
  calorie source
- Fish are slower, concentrated deep
- Need ice pick/chisel or axe for hole
- Most effective near structure under ice
- Check ice thickness before every step

## Ice Safety

- 2 in or less: stay off
- 3 in: foot travel only, risky
- 4 in: safe for one person
- 6 in: small group safe
- Test with ice pick or pole as you go
- Listen: solid thunk = good ice
  hollow creak = dangerous, retreat

## Cutting the Hole

- 8-10 in diameter works for most fish
- Chip with axe, knife butt, sharp rock
- Remove ice chips from hole
- Keep hole clear of re-freezing with
  small stick over top
- Chisel in circular motion is fastest

## Rigging for Ice

- Short line 3-4 ft is sufficient
- Weight to reach bottom, back off 1 ft
- Set at mid-depth for suspended fish
- Jig up and down 2-3 inches every
  30 seconds to attract fish
- Tip-up: stick over hole with line,
  flags when fish bites

## Finding Fish

- Near inlet/outlet of lake
- Over submerged structure: rock piles,
  weed beds, submerged timber
- Depth: perch/pike 10-20 ft,
  walleye 15-30 ft, trout deep or inlet
- Try multiple holes if no bites in
  20-30 minutes

## In a Blizzard

- Mark hole location before any storm
- Keep gear simple: can't work cold
  complicated tackle with cold hands
- Wind protection: crouch behind pack
