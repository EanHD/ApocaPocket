# Reading Water for Fish

## Overview

- Fish are in predictable locations
  based on three needs: food, oxygen,
  protection from current
- Read the water before casting
- Best water = current edge next to
  slow water with nearby structure

## Key Structures

- Riffles: aerated, shallow, fast --
  fish feed here morning and evening
- Pools: deep, slow -- fish rest here
- Runs: mid-depth, steady current --
  fish travel corridors
- Eddies: slow current behind rocks
  or bends -- excellent fish holding
- Undercut banks: shade, protection

## Current Seams

- Where fast and slow water meet
- Fish face upstream, feed on anything
  passing in the current
- Cast above seam, drift through it
- Most productive water in any stream

## Temperature Factors

- Cold water fish (trout): seek cold
  inlets, springs, deep shade
- Warm water fish (bass, catfish):
  seek warm shallow flats at dawn/dusk
- Summer: fish go deep during day
- Winter/fall: fish concentrate deep

## Lake Structure

- Points: underwater ridges attract fish
- Drop-offs: edge of depth change
- Weed beds: edge, not middle
- Inlet streams: always fish holding
- Dam face: deep, often productive

## Reading Ripples

- Dimples on surface = insects hatching
  or fish feeding -- fish right there
- Boil: fish chasing bait fish near
  surface -- aggressive feeding
- Slick calm pool: fish resting below
