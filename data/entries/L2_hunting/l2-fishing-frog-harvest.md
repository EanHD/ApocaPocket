# Frog Harvest

## Overview

- Bullfrog legs: excellent mild meat
- Active at night along water edges
- Easy to locate by croaking sound
- Gig (multi-tined spear) most effective
- Can also be grabbed by hand in shallows

## Species to Target

- Bullfrog: largest, most common target
  -- both green and brown variants
- Green frog: smaller but abundant
- Avoid: brightly colored frogs
  (red, yellow, orange = toxic warning)
- Toads: avoid (skin secretions)
- Rule: dull colored, near water = OK

## Gigging Method

- Work at night with light source
- Walk slowly along water edge
- Frogs freeze in light (eye shine)
- Approach slowly to 3-4 ft distance
- Gig or throw net quickly
- Pin frog to bottom, grab behind legs

## Hand Capture

- Wade very slowly into shallows
- Frogs hold still in light
- Cup hands over frog from above
- Grab firmly around body just behind
  front legs -- do not let jump
- Works best in still, shallow water

## Preparation

- Remove hind legs at hip joint
- Discard body
- Skin legs: grab skin, pull down
  like removing a glove
- Rinse in clean water
- Grill, fry in rendered fat, or boil

## Cooking

- Cook thoroughly to 165F
- Fry in hot fat 3-4 min each side
- Boil in water or broth 10-15 min
- Meat is delicate and mild
- Stew with wild greens and roots
