# Bird Field Dressing

## Overview

- Two methods: plucking or skinning
- Plucking: keeps fat and skin,
  better for roasting
- Skinning: faster, removes fat,
  better for stewing
- Small birds (quail, dove): skin
- Large birds (turkey, goose): pluck

## Plucking Method

- Begin while bird still warm: feathers
  release easier
- Pull feathers in direction of growth
  (toward tail), not against
- Work in small bunches
- Singe pin feathers with flame
- Takes 10-30 min depending on size

## Skinning Method

- Insert fingers under skin at breast
- Tear skin back toward wings and legs
- Cut off wings at second joint
- Cut off feet at lower joint
- Skin pulls off like a glove

## Gutting

- Make small incision below breastbone
- Insert two fingers, scoop out organs
- Remove crop (bulge in neck/chest):
  contains undigested food material
- Wipe cavity clean
- Save: heart, liver, gizzard (edible)

## Gizzard Cleaning

- Slice gizzard open through muscle
- Turn inside out, peel inner lining
- Rinse well -- grit inside is normal
- Tough muscle: slice thin, cook long

## Cooling Small Birds

- Cavity open to air for first hour
- Do not bag warm birds
- Cook same day if above 60F
- String through neck to hang in shade
