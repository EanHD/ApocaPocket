# Fish Trap Construction

## Overview

- Passive fishing: works while you rest
- Funnel trap most effective design
- Willow, split bamboo, or wire mesh
- Place in current with opening facing
  upstream -- fish swim in, cannot exit
- Bait increases effectiveness

## Willow Funnel Trap

- Weave green willow wands into
  cylinder 12-18 in diam, 3 ft long
- Weave second smaller funnel cone
  to fit inside one end, pointing in
- Funnel opening: 2-3 in diam
- Lash funnel inside cylinder firmly
- Leave opposite end as removable plug

## Bottle Trap

- Cut plastic bottle 1/3 from top
- Invert top piece into bottle body
- Secure with cordage or tape
- Fill partway with bait
- Submerge in shallow water near bank

## Placement Rules

- Face opening upstream in current
- Submerge fully below surface
- Anchor with rocks on top and sides
- Fish hold near structure: rocks,
  logs, undercut banks -- place there
- Check morning and evening

## Baiting the Trap

- Crushed mussels or crayfish inside
- Bread or dough ball in center
- Worms wrapped in grass bundle
- Bait draws fish in -- no bait means
  only fish passing directly through

## Removing Fish

- Pull trap out slowly
- Open end quickly over container
- Re-bait and reset immediately
- Multiple traps = much better yield
