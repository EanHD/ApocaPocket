# Night Fishing

## Overview

- Catfish, bass, walleye most active
  after dark
- Set passive lines before dark,
  check at intervals
- Best passive method: trotline
  or multiple set lines
- Moon phase: darker nights produce
  better catfishing

## Night Feeding Species

- Catfish: primary night feeder,
  move onto flats to feed
- Walleye: feed aggressively at night
- Largemouth bass: hunt in shallows
- Carp: feed in shallow weedy areas
- Bullhead: always at night in mud

## Set Line Strategy

- Set 5-10 baited lines before dark
- Mark each with white cloth or stick
- Bait with strong-smelling items:
  chicken liver, cut fish, worms
- Check lines every 2 hours
- Reset and rebait any empty hooks

## Light Attraction

- Small fire or torch near water
  attracts insects, which attract fish
- Insects flutter on surface = fish rise
- Position light over water edge
- Fish rise to feed in lit area
- Dip net or spear for gigging

## Catfish Set Locations

- Outside bend of river: deep hole
- Bottom of rapids: fish rest here
- Near warm water outflows
- Submerged timber and log jams
- Under cut banks along river edge

## Safety at Night

- Mark path to water at camp
- Do not wade in unfamiliar water
- Carry light source for checking lines
- Tell someone where you are fishing
