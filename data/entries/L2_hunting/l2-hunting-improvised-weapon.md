# Improvised Hunting Tools

## Overview

- When no firearm: traps are primary
- Improvised weapons for opportunity
  shots and finishing trapped animals
- Effective range is short -- move close
- Practice builds accuracy quickly

## Throwing Stick

- Heavy hardwood, 18-24 inches, 1-2 lbs
- Throw horizontally at knee height
  into flock of ground birds
- Effective to 15-20 feet
- Kills or stuns birds in group
- Retrieval easy -- no ammunition cost

## Sling (Slingshot)

- Y-shaped fork + rubber or cordage
- Leather or cloth pouch
- Projectile: smooth round stones
- Effective range: 20-30 yards
- Requires 1-2 hours practice
- Aim just above target to compensate

## Bola

- Three stones, approx 3 oz each
- Cords of equal length: 18-24 inches
- Tied together at center
- Throw horizontally to entangle legs
- Works best on low-flying birds

## Survival Spear

- Straight pole 6-8 feet, 1-2 in diam
- Sharpen tip to long point
- Fire-harden: hold tip in coals,
  rotate until sizzling stops
- Fish gig: split tip 4 ways, spread
  with small crosspiece
- Use for finishing trapped animals

## Priority Rule

- Traps first -- always
- Best weapon use: finishing animals
  and very close range (under 10 ft)
