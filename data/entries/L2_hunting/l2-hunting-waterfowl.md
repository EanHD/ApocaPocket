# Waterfowl Hunting

## Overview

- Ducks and geese: high calorie meat
  and fat, especially fall/winter
- Concentrate near ponds, marshes,
  rivers, flooded fields
- Most active at dawn and dusk
- Migration concentrates birds
  in fall (Sept-Nov) and spring

## Finding Birds

- Look for roost water: calm ponds,
  backwaters, flooded timber
- Feeding: shallow marsh, grain fields
- Fresh droppings and feathers on bank
- Listen at dawn for calling

## Improvised Decoys

- Carve rough duck shape from wood
- Paint or char dark for body color
- Anchor with rock and cord
- 6-12 decoys in J or C pattern
- Leave landing zone open to wind

## Calling

- Mallard hen: quack series descending
  in volume
- Feeding chuckle: soft rapid notes
- Canada goose: deep honk honk
- Call when birds circle, not randomly
- Less calling in cold still conditions

## Harvest Method

- Concealment essential: crouch in reeds
- Wait until birds commit and descend
- Take at 20-30 yards maximum
- Target head and neck for clean harvest
- Mark downed birds immediately

## Setup Tips

- Wind in face so birds land toward you
- Sun at your back when possible
- Blend into natural vegetation
- Stay still until birds are in range
