# Night Hunting

## Overview

- Many animals most active at night:
  raccoon, opossum, rabbit, deer
- Passive trapping beats active hunting
  at night in most situations
- Best active method: wait at water
  source or bait site
- Moonlit nights best for still hunting

## Nocturnal Species

- Raccoon: excellent meat, follow creeks
- Opossum: slow, easy harvest
- Rabbit: active dusk through dawn
- Deer: move through darkness into dawn
- Feral pig: primarily nocturnal

## Passive Night Methods

- Set and check snares at last light
- Funnel traps work all night
- Deadfalls reset before dark
- Trotlines in water (see fishing)
- Check all passive gear at first light

## Active Night Hunting

- Still-hunt near water at moonrise
- Listen for movement and splashing
- Frogs and crawfish best by light
  in shallow water
- Fire at water hole draws game but
  also alerts all other animals

## Safety Rules

- Never move quickly in darkness
- Mark your route back to camp
- Carry fire-starting material
- Never shoot toward sound alone
- Positively identify the target

## Low-Light Aids

- Full moon: near-daylight visibility
- Birch bark torch: 15-20 ft range
- Fire at ambush point: 30 ft range
- Pine pitch torch burns 30-60 min
