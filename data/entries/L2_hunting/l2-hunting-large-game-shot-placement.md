# Large Game Shot Placement

**GOAL**: One shot, instant death, no suffering, no lost game.

---

## Vital Zone Priority

**1. Heart/Lung combo (recommended)**
- Location: behind front shoulder, lower 1/3 of body.
- Target size: 8-12 inch circle.
- Death: 10-30 seconds. Tracking: 0-50 yards.

**2. Double lung**
- Location: behind shoulder, center chest cavity.
- Target size: 10-15 inch oval.
- Death: 30-60 seconds. Tracking: 25-100 yards.

**3. Spine (high risk)**
- Location: centerline of back, directly above heart/lung line.
- Target size: 2-3 inches wide.
- Drop instantly if hit. No effect if missed by an inch.

**4. Brain (expert only, close range <50 yds)**
- Target size: 3-5 inches. Instant death if hit.

---

## Species Reference

**Moose** (800-1200 lbs):
- Vital zone: 12-15 inch circle.
- Broadside: aim 1/3 up from chest bottom, center of shoulder or just behind.
- May travel 50-100 yards even with perfect shot (momentum).

**Caribou** (200-400 lbs):
- Vital zone: 8-10 inch circle.
- Often moving  -  wait for a pause.

**Deer** (100-250 lbs):
- Vital zone: 8 inch circle.
- Lighter animal  -  drops faster with good shot.

---

## Shot Angles

**Broadside (best)**:
- Full vital zone exposed. Aim crease behind shoulder, 1/3 up from bottom of chest.

**Quartering away (good)**:
- Aim for far shoulder. Bullet travels diagonally through chest.
- Don't shoot too far back  -  risk gut shot.

**Quartering toward (wait)**:
- Shoulder bone blocks vitals. Narrow exposure. Take the shot only if animal about to leave and no other opportunity.

**Head-on or rear (never)**:
- Head-on: bone blocks heart and lungs.
- Rear: gut shot. Animal travels miles. Illegal in most jurisdictions.

---

## Caliber Minimums

- Deer: .243 Win / 130-165 grain expanding.
- Caribou: .270 Win or .30-06 / 130-180 grain.
- Moose: .30-06 minimum / 180-220 grain. .300 Win Mag preferred.

**Bullet type**: soft point or bonded expanding only. Full metal jacket is not for hunting.

**Minimum energy at impact**: deer 1000 ft-lbs, caribou 1200 ft-lbs, moose 2000 ft-lbs.

---

## After the Shot

**Immediately**:
1. Note exactly where animal was standing.
2. Observe reaction  -  hunched, tail down = good hit. Jumping/kicking = questionable.
3. **Wait 30-60 minutes. Do not push the animal.**
4. Find the arrow or mark entry point.

**Blood trail reading**:
- Bright red, frothy = lung (good, 30-80 yds typical).
- Dark red = heart or liver (good, 40-100 yds).
- Watery, foul smell = gut (wait 4-6 hours, animal 200+ yds).

**Tracking**:
- Mark every blood spot with flagging tape.
- If trail stops: circle 20-30 yard radius from last blood.
- Check thick cover and water sources  -  wounded animals seek both.

---

## Pre-Shot Checklist

- [ ] Vital zone is clearly visible (not obscured by brush)
- [ ] Range is within my skill limit
- [ ] Animal is broadside or quartering away
- [ ] Stable shooting position (rest, bipod, or sling)
- [ ] Safe backstop  -  know what is beyond the animal
- [ ] Breathing steady, crosshairs on aiming point (1/3 up, behind shoulder)

**Don't shoot if you are not 90%+ confident in the hit.**

---

## Common Mistakes

- Shooting too far: overestimate skill or underestimate wind. Know your limit.
- Rushing the shot: animal about to move, panic fires. Let it go if needed.
- Same aim point on quartering animal as broadside: visualize bullet path to far-side vitals.
- Shooting at moving animal: wait for it to stop.
- Pushing too fast after shot: track quietly after 30-60 min wait, not immediately.
