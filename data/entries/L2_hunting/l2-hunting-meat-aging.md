# Field Meat Aging

## Overview

- Aging tenderizes muscle through enzyme
  action (autolysis)
- Only safe when temp is stable 34-40F
- Do not age in warm or variable temps
- Deer: 3-7 days optimal
- Small game: 1-2 days maximum

## Conditions Required

- Air temp stable at 34-40F
- Air circulation around carcass
- Skin on: protects from flies/drying
- Shade at all times
- Night temps reliably below 40F

## Hanging Method

- Hang by head or hind legs in shade
- 6+ feet off ground
- Allow air to circulate all around
- Do not wrap in plastic
- Thin cotton cloth over exposed areas
  to keep insects off

## Signs of Proper Aging

- Surface dries, darkens slightly
- Dry crust forms on outer surface
- Smell: clean, slightly nutty/gamey
  -- not sour or ammonia-like
- Surface layer trimmed off before
  butchering

## When to Stop Aging

- 7 days maximum even in ideal temps
- Any sour or ammonia smell: butcher
  immediately and use quickly
- Slime or green color: trim deeply
  or discard affected sections

## After Aging

- Trim dry outer layer off
- Cut out discolored areas
- Process into cuts immediately
- Do not re-hang cut meat
