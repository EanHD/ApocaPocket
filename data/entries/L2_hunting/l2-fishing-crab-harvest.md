# Crab Trapping

## Overview

- Crabs: excellent protein, available
  on most coastlines
- Blue crab (East), Dungeness (West),
  Rock crabs (both coasts)
- Tidal areas, estuaries, rocky shores
- Simple baited line most effective
  improvised method

## Finding Crabs

- Rocky intertidal zones at low tide
- Under rocks and in crevices
- Muddy tidal flats: blue crab burrows
- Shallow water at night: active
- Shallow pools left by falling tide

## Bait Line Method

- Tie fish carcass or meat to cordage
- Lower into water from dock, rock,
  or bank
- When crab seizes bait: pull slowly
  straight up without jerking
- Net or container under crab as it
  nears surface -- drop into container

## Ring Net (Improvised)

- Weave wire or cordage into ring
  with mesh bottom
- Attach 3 cords to ring edge evenly
- Attach bait to center of mesh
- Lower flat, wait 5-10 min
- Pull straight up fast

## Timing

- Incoming tide: crabs moving shoreward
- Low tide: hidden under rocks
- Night: most active in shallows
- Check trap every 10-15 min

## Cooking Crabs

- Boil live in salted water
- 10-15 min for large crab
- Done: shell turns bright orange-red
- Break shell, remove gills (gray
  feathery parts -- not edible)
- Eat all white meat inside
