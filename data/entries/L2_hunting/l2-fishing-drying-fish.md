# Fish Drying

## Overview

- Simplest preservation: sun and wind
- Removes water, prevents bacterial growth
- Properly dried fish: weeks to months
- Works for any size fish
- Best in dry, breezy, sunny conditions

## Preparation

- Scale, gut, head off
- Butterfly: cut down spine from inside,
  spread flat -- single piece with skin
- Small fish: whole butterfly
- Large fish: fillet, cut shallow
  X-pattern in flesh for faster drying
- Rinse in clean water, pat dry

## Salting

- Salt both sides generously
- Rub into all cuts and surfaces
- Salt draws moisture out, inhibits
  bacteria, improves preservation
- No salt: still works but shorter
  shelf life and more spoilage risk

## Drying Methods

- Hang on line or rack in sun and wind
- Drape over clean branches
- Weave onto drying rack of sticks
- Cover with light cloth to keep
  insects off -- not plastic

## Drying Time

- Small fish (under 1 lb): 2-3 days
- Medium fish (1-3 lbs): 4-7 days
- Large fish: 1-2 weeks
- Turn once daily
- Condition: dry, breezy, sunny best

## Done Test

- Flesh is hard and dry throughout
- Bend test: cracks but does not break
- Color: golden to dark brown
- No soft or moist spots
- Store in breathable bag or basket
