# Freshwater Fish ID

## Overview

- All North American freshwater fish
  are edible -- no toxic freshwater fish
  in NA (unlike marine species)
- Focus on high-calorie target species
- Larger fish = more calories per effort
- Match technique to species

## Trout and Salmon

- Streamlined body, forked or square tail
- Speckled pattern on sides
- Adipose fin (small fin behind dorsal)
- Cold, clear, fast-moving water
- Excellent flavor, high fat content

## Bass Species

- Largemouth: large mouth extends past
  eye, dark lateral stripe on side
- Smallmouth: bronze color, vertical
  bars, small mouth
- Structure-oriented: near logs, rocks
- Aggressive and easy to catch

## Catfish Species

- No scales -- smooth skin
- Four barbels (whiskers) around mouth
- Bottom dwellers in rivers and lakes
- Night and murky water feeders
- Flathead: largest, excellent eating

## Carp and Suckers

- Large scales, bottom orientation
- Carp: large bronze/gold scales,
  barbels at mouth corner
- Suckers: downward-facing mouth
- Both edible -- strong flavor
- Common in slow muddy water

## Panfish Species

- Small, round, deep-bodied fish
- Bluegill: orange breast, blue cheeks
- Crappie: black or white spotted
- Shallow water, near vegetation
- Easiest to catch, good eating
