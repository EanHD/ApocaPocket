# Reading Deer Sign

## Overview

- Deer sign shows when and where deer
  move, feed, and bed
- Fresh sign: warm/moist, sharp edges
- Old sign: dry, crumbled, faded
- Read multiple sign types together

## Tracks

- Two teardrop-shaped toes, pointed end
  faces direction of travel
- Doe: 2 inches long, narrow shape
- Buck: 2.5-3.5 in, wider, splayed
- Walking: tracks nearly in a line
- Running: grouped in sets of four

## Rubs

- Bark scraped off sapling or tree
- Made by buck antlers in fall
- Fresh rub: white/tan wood exposed,
  sap still wet, shavings on ground
- Rub lines indicate travel routes
- Larger diameter = larger buck

## Scrapes

- Pawed bare earth patch, 1-3 ft wide
- Licking branch overhead (broken tips)
- Made by bucks during pre-rut, rut
- Fresh scrape: dark moist soil,
  strong musky odor
- Check scrapes near rub lines

## Beds

- Oval depression in leaves or grass
- Deer bed with back to wind
- Doe beds: scattered, smaller ovals
- Buck bed: single, often high ground
- Warm bed = deer left recently

## Droppings

- Pellet clusters: 50-100 pellets
- Fresh: moist, dark, shiny
- Old: dry, gray, crumbled
- Clumped pellets = feeding area
- Scattered = deer moving through
