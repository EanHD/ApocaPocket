# Cooling Meat in Field

## Overview

- Bacteria grow exponentially above 40F
- Core temp of deer: 102F at harvest
- Goal: drop core below 40F within
  2-4 hours in warm weather
- Cold weather: nature does the work
- Warm weather: every minute matters

## Immediate Steps

- Field dress immediately after harvest
- Open cavity wide with stick spreader
- Hang carcass off ground if possible
- Shade from direct sunlight
- Keep away from heat sources

## Cold Water Cooling

- Submerge quartered meat in cold
  stream or spring -- very effective
- Weight down with rocks
- Running water below 50F ideal
- Check every 2-4 hours
- Secure against predators overnight

## Air Cooling Methods

- Hang in tree in shade
- Elevation: wind increases cooling
- Pack cavity with dry grass: absorbs
  moisture, allows airflow
- Replace grass every few hours
  as it gets wet

## Temperature Guide

- Below 32F: freezes, safe long-term
- 32-40F: safe for 5-7 days
- 40-50F: use within 24-48 hours
- Above 50F: process within 12 hours
- Above 70F: process same day

## Warning Signs

- Sweet-sour odor from meat
- Slimy texture on cut surface
- Green or gray discoloration
- Unusual warmth in cavity
- Discard -- cooking does not fix spoiled
