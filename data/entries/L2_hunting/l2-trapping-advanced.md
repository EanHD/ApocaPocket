# Advanced Trapping

**GOAL**: Passive hunting. Set traps, check daily, harvest small-to-medium game.

---

## Figure-4 Trigger

**Parts**: vertical stick (12"), horizontal bait stick (8"), diagonal (10"), weight (20-80 lbs).

**Assembly**:
1. Cut 1/4" notches  -  vertical near bottom, horizontal at both ends, diagonal at both ends.
2. Lock horizontal notch over vertical notch.
3. Insert diagonal to form "4" shape.
4. Balance weight on vertical stick. Attach bait to horizontal.

**Weight by target**: squirrel 15-25 lbs, rabbit 30-50 lbs, raccoon 50-80 lbs.

---

## Paiute Trigger

**More sensitive than Figure-4.**

**Assembly**:
1. Drive vertical post into ground (6" deep).
2. Tie cordage from bait stick to weight.
3. Prop weight at angle resting on post.
4. Thread cordage under weight, over post.
5. Insert short toggle stick through cordage loop.

Animal pulls bait -> releases toggle -> weight falls.

---

## Conibear Traps

**WARNING**: Can break fingers. Use setting tongs ($20-30). Required.

**Sizes**: 110 (squirrel/mink), 220 (rabbit/raccoon), 330 (beaver).

**Setting steps**:
1. Compress springs with tongs.
2. Lock open with safety clips.
3. Place in trail or run, chain to stake.
4. Remove safety clips.
5. Test trigger with a stick.

**Ground set**: span trail fully, funnel with sticks.
**Water set**: place in underwater run, stake in bottom.
**Tree set**: lean against trunk, bait above trap.

---

## Wire Snares

**Material**: 22-24 gauge brass wire, 3-4 feet per snare.

**Simple loop**:
1. Make fixed loop (1/2") at one end.
2. Thread other end through it (slip knot).
3. Set loop size  -  squirrel: 3-4", rabbit: 5-6".
4. Attach to stake or spring pole.

**Spring snare**: bent sapling lifts animal after catch  -  prevents escape and predator theft.

---

## Scent Control

Human scent cuts success 50-80%.

- Wear gloves when handling traps.
- Boil traps in bark-water or smoke over fire.
- Never touch bait directly  -  use sticks.
- Approach trail from downwind.

---

## Trap Check Protocol

**Legal**: check every 24 hours (most jurisdictions).

**Morning check steps**:
1. Approach quietly (animal may be alive).
2. Dispatch alive animals immediately  -  blunt strike to base of skull.
3. Reset or relocate if no catch after 3 days.
4. Clean trap, re-smoke or re-boil after each catch.

---

## Trapline Layout

- Scout 1-2 days first. Find trails, burrows, water.
- Set 10-20 traps along a 2-3 hour walking loop.
- Space traps 50-100 yards apart.
- Expect 1 catch per 2-3 days on a 10-trap line.

---

## Daily Check Checklist

- [ ] Quiet approach
- [ ] Dispatch method ready (stick)
- [ ] Game bags packed
- [ ] Notes: which traps caught, which didn't
- [ ] Relocate no-catch traps after 3 days
- [ ] Change bait and re-boil trap if needed

---

## Minimum Trap Kit

- 6x 110 Conibear
- 6x 220 Conibear
- 50 ft 22-gauge brass wire
- Setting tongs
- 20 ft light chain
- Small lure bottle (anise or fish oil)
- Heavy gloves, game bags, flagging tape

**Weight**: 8-12 lbs. **Cost**: $150-250. **Lifespan**: 10-20 years maintained.

---

## Ethics

- Deadfall/Conibear: kill in 1-2 minutes. Snares: 3-5 minutes.
- Use correct trap size  -  avoid non-target animals.
- Check every 24 hours  -  no exceptions.
- Use all meat, hide, bone. Bury remains.
- Survival emergency overrides regulation  -  document circumstances.
