# Bolas (Boleadoras)

**Function**: Tangles and disables prey at 30–50 yards. Rotating balls wrap around wings, legs, or neck. Works when arrows, traps, or slings fail.

## Construction

- **2-ball basic**: two 6–10 oz weights joined by 6–8 ft cord split at center
- **3-ball** (better): two long cords 3–4 ft + one short cord 1–2 ft, all joined at center (Y-shape); long cords have 6–10 oz weights, short cord has 4–6 oz weight
- Weights: stones wrapped in 4×4 inch leather squares, gathered at top and tied; or clay balls, cast lead, fishing sinkers
- Cord: braided rawhide, sinew, or paracord — must be 50+ lb test, minimal stretch
- Join: braid all three cords together 2–3 inches at center; wrap junction with leather for grip
- Test: swing gently — all balls should orbit smoothly with no wobble; adjust cord lengths if needed

## Throwing

- **Overhead spin** (max range 40–50 yds): hold center knot; 2–3 overhead rotations; release toward target; aim above (bolas drops in arc)
- **Sidearm** (20–30 yds, ground targets): hold short-cord weight as handle; spin horizontal; release when facing target; aim at legs
- **Underhand** (10–20 yds, quick): hold center; pendulum swing; snap wrist upward at release

## Targets

- **Birds in flight**: geese, ducks, cranes; throw 30–40 yds when bird descends; wraps wings; retrieve quickly (may untangle)
- **Rabbits/small game**: throw when running away; wraps hind legs; non-lethal — can keep alive for later
- **Medium game**: fox, coyote; aim front legs; immobilizes temporarily; requires quick follow-up

## Advantages

- Reusable (no projectile loss); larger strike zone than sling; non-lethal capture option
- Build in 2 hours from rocks and cordage; works when bow string breaks or ammo is gone
- Gauchos captured 1000 lb cattle at full gallop; Inuit hunters achieved 50%+ on flying birds

## Safety

- 50 ft clearance on all sides; spectators at risk from separating balls
- Inspect all knots before every use; frayed cord = replace immediately
- Self-entanglement risk: check cords for tangles before each throw; premature release wraps thrower

## Troubleshooting

- Does not wrap: spin faster, lengthen cords to 4 ft, heavier weights (8–10 oz)
- Wraps thrower: release earlier in rotation; untangle cords before each throw
- Weights separate mid-flight: weak knots or frayed cord — replace cord immediately
