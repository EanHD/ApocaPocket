# Freshwater Turtle

## Overview

- Snapping turtles: most meat, dangerous
- Box turtle: smaller, safer to handle
- Found in ponds, streams, rivers
- Set line bait fishing works well
- High protein, 7 types of meat

## Finding Turtles

- Bask on logs and banks at midday
- Snapper: submerged in muddy shallows,
  rarely basks -- find by wading
- Box turtle: near water in forest
- Early morning: on banks near water
- Check submerged debris and log piles

## Harvesting

- Snapper: approach from behind
- Use stick to turn onto back first
- Long neck: can extend to bite
- Carry by tail, away from body
- Box turtle: safe to handle, slower

## Set Hook Method

- Heavy line anchored firmly
- Bait hook with meat or worm
- Set at bottom of pond/slow pool
- Check every few hours
- Snapper will not let go of hook

## Processing

- Cut head off: still bites for hours
  after separation, use caution
- Turn upside down
- Cut between shell and body on sides
- Remove bottom shell (plastron)
- Remove neck, legs, tail meat
- Boil for 20-30 min before further cook

## Cooking

- Parboil until meat pulls from bone
- Stew with roots and greens
- Seven types of meat: each tastes
  slightly different
- Nutritious broth from boiling bones
