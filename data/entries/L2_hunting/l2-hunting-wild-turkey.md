# Wild Turkey Hunting

## Overview

- Large bird: 8-25 lbs, 5,000+ calories
- Found in hardwood and mixed forests
- Roost in trees at night -- vocal
  at dawn
- Spring (gobbling season) easiest
- Fall: use scatter and reassemble

## Finding Turkeys

- Large three-toed tracks, J-shaped
- Droppings: J-shape (tom), round (hen)
- Scratched leaf litter: foraging sign
- Dust baths: bare areas in dry soil
- Feathers, wing drag marks in dirt
- Listen at dawn: gobbling from roost

## Spring Calling

- Set up before first light near roost
- Owl hoot or crow call to locate roost
- Hen yelp: 3-5 notes, descending pitch
- Cluck: single note, feeding sound
- Purr: soft contentment sound
- Call softly -- let tom come to you

## Fall Hunting Method

- Find and scatter a flock
- Set up where flock scattered from
- Use lost yelp (10-15 rapid notes)
- Flock tries to regroup -- comes to call
- Most effective 30-60 min after scatter

## Approach and Setup

- Sit with solid tree behind you wider
  than your shoulders
- Face direction turkey likely comes
- Stay still -- eyesight is excellent
- Do not move until shot is clear

## Field Notes

- Field dress immediately: large bird
  holds heat
- See Bird Field Dressing entry
- 15-20 lbs bird = 5-7 lbs meat
