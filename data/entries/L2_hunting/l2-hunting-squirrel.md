# Squirrel Hunting

## Overview

- Most common small game in eastern
  North America
- Best hunting: early morning, mast
  season (Sept-Nov)
- Gray and fox squirrel most common
- Reliable calorie source in hardwood
  forest zones

## Finding Squirrels

- Cuttings: nut husks piled under trees
  or at stumps -- fresh = moist/green
- Nests: large leaf balls in tree crotch
  30+ feet up
- Oaks, hickories, beech = prime areas
- Squirrels move lower before rain

## Still Hunting Method

- Enter woods quietly at dawn
- Find active nut tree with cuttings
- Sit against tree trunk, downwind
- Wait 10-15 min for woods to settle
- Watch canopy movement and listen
  for chewing or cutting sounds

## Stalk Method

- Move slowly through mast trees
- Pause 1-2 min every 20 paces
- Scratch bark with stick to mimic
  squirrel feeding -- draws attention
- Target: head or neck for clean harvest

## Trapping Squirrels

- Box trap baited with corn or nut
- Pole trap: snare on leaning sapling
- Check every 4-6 hours

## Field Notes

- Clean immediately -- gut fast
- Meat spoils quickly in warm weather
- Parboil tough squirrels 20 min first
- Bushy tail: indicator of good health
