# River Survival

## River Hazards
- Strainer: debris over current, lethal
- Undercut rock: current pulls under
- Hydraulic: recirculating hole below drop
- Sweeper: downed tree at surface
- Flash flood: rapid rise without warning

## Swimming in Current
- Defensive position: feet downstream
- Back floating: feet up, toes pointing out
- Angle to shore: don't fight current
- Work to the inside of river bends
- Grab shore features: rocks and branches

## River Crossing
- Unbuckle pack straps before crossing
- Cross at widest shallowest point
- Diagonal downstream angle
- Use pole: third point of contact
- Knee depth max for solo crossing
- Group cross: hands on shoulders

## Hypothermia in River
- Cold water: 10 min functional limit
- Swim hard in first 60 seconds
- HELP position: arms and legs together
- Huddle with others if in group
- Get out of water: priority one

## Self Rescue
- Roll to swim position at strainer
- Push up and over, not under
- Hydraulic: swim down and out
- Bank grab: slow approach, firm hold
- Whistle three times: distress signal

## River Navigation
- Inside bends: shallow and slow
- Outside bends: deep and fast
- V-point downstream: smooth, rideable
- V-point upstream: rocks: avoid
- Eddies: rest and plan next move
