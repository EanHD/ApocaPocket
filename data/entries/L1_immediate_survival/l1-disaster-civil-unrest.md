# Civil Unrest Protocol

## Stay vs Go Decision
- Unrest far away: shelter at home
- Fire or direct threat: evacuate now
- Supplies for 2 weeks: stay viable
- No supplies and no safety: go
- Have both plans ready before crisis

## Home Security
- Reinforce doors: solid core and bar
- Do not advertise supplies or generator
- Lights: appear occupied but not rich
- Know neighbors: safety in community
- Low profile: do not invite attention

## Avoiding Crowds
- Monitor scanner and radio for routes
- Move at night if safer
- Do not walk into crowds to observe
- Secure vehicle in covered location
- Travel in groups of 2 minimum

## Supply Cache Strategy
- Secondary location: family or rural
- Small caches along your route
- Cache: water, food, first aid, fuel
- Mark locations only in your memory
- Never cache in abandoned building

## Communication
- Code phrases with family for safety
- Avoid discussing plans on phone
- GMRS or FRS radio: family network
- Check-in schedule: twice daily
- Fallback meet point if comms down

## De-escalation
- Do not display weapons in public
- Avoid confrontation: walk away
- Comply if not in immediate danger
- Document incidents when safe to do so
- Protect your family first
