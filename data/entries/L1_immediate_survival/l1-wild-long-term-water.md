# Long-Term Water Plan

## Source Inventory
- Map all water within 1 mile of camp
- Rank by quality: spring > stream > pond
- Note seasonal variation of each source
- Identify backup if primary dries up
- Track contamination risks upstream

## Storage System
- 3-day water reserve minimum
- Food-grade containers only
- Wide-mouth containers: easier to fill
- Label purified vs unpurified
- Store in shade: slows algae growth

## Purification Rotation
- Primary: boiling if fuel available
- Secondary: filter pump or straw
- Tertiary: chemical treatment tabs
- Mix methods: belt and suspenders
- Replace filter cartridges on schedule

## Water Quality
- Clear running water: better than still
- Aquatic insects: sign of healthy water
- Absence of all life: serious concern
- Foam or oily sheen: upstream problem
- Animal deaths near source: danger sign

## Collection Methods
- Rain catchment: cleanest available
- Dew from leaves: morning collection
- Dig uphill of surface water: seep
- Plant transpiration bag: slow method
- Follow animal trails at dawn or dusk

## Conservation
- 1 gallon minimum per person per day
- Reduce sweating: work in cool hours
- Cook food that retains water (stew)
- Track intake and urine color daily
- Dark urine: drink more immediately
