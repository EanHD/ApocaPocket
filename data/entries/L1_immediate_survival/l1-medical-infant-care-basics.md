# Infant Emergency Care

## Feeding Alternatives

Breastfeed if at all possible. No clean water needed. Provides immunity.

Feed schedule by age:
- Newborn (0-4 weeks): every 2-3 hours
- 1-3 months: every 3-4 hours
- 3-6 months: every 4-5 hours
- 6-12 months: every 4-6 hours, add solids

Under 6 wet diapers per day after day 5: increase feeding now.

If breastfeeding is unavailable:
- Emergency substitute for infants over 6 months ONLY:
  13 oz evaporated milk + 19 oz boiled water + 2 tbsp sugar
- Use less than 48 hours. Find infant formula as soon as possible.
- Never use regular cow milk under 12 months.
- Never use plant milks: insufficient for infant nutrition.

## Diapering Hygiene

Change every 2-4 hours. Never leave soiled more than 10 minutes.

Protocol:
- Wipe front to back, always, especially for girls
- Clean all skin folds thoroughly
- Pat completely dry before new diaper
- Apply zinc oxide or petroleum jelly as barrier

Improvised diaper: folded cotton fabric, safety pins,
waterproof plastic layer over top. Boil cloth diapers
5 minutes to disinfect. Sun-dry when possible.

Diaper rash: 15 minutes naked time three times daily.
Apply thick zinc oxide every change. If satellite spots
appear around the rash border: add antifungal cream (clotrimazole).

## Fever Recognition

Any fever over 100.4F (38C) in infant under 3 months:
MEDICAL EMERGENCY. Evacuate immediately.

Temperature methods:
- Rectal: most accurate, insert 0.5 inch, hold 2 minutes
- Armpit: add 1F to reading
- Forehead strip: rough guide only

Danger signs alongside fever:
- Will not wake or is limp
- High-pitched or weak cry
- Stiff neck or bulging soft spot
- Refuses to feed for one full feeding cycle

Do not use aspirin. No cold baths. No alcohol rubs.

## Dehydration Signs

Check diaper count daily:
- Mild: dry mouth, 4-6 wet diapers, increase feeding now
- Moderate: sunken soft spot, sticky saliva, 2-4 diapers,
  give oral rehydration solution between feeds (Pedialyte)
- Severe: no tears crying, sunken eyes, no wet diaper 8+
  hours, limp or unresponsive, evacuate immediately

## Safe Sleep

Peak SIDS risk is 2-4 months. These rules cut risk by 70 percent:
- Always place on back. Never stomach or side.
- Firm flat surface only. No soft mattresses.
- Nothing in sleep space: no pillows, blankets, stuffed toys.
- Room temperature 68-72F (20-22C).
- Same room as adult caregiver. Not same bed or couch.
- No smoking anywhere in shared living space.

Emergency crib: sturdy cardboard box, thin blanket, on floor.
