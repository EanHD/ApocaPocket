# Water Contamination

## Boil Order Response
- Do not drink tap water unboiled
- Full rolling boil: 1 minute minimum
- High altitude above 6500 ft: 3 minutes
- Let cool before drinking
- Boiled water safe 24 hours covered

## Uses Requiring Boiling
- Drinking water: always boil
- Brushing teeth: always boil
- Ice cubes: use boiled water
- Baby formula: always boil water
- Food prep water: always boil

## What Tap Water is
- Flushing toilet: no boiling needed
- Washing dishes: sanitize after
- Laundry: generally safe
- Bathing adults: use caution, no ingestion
- Watering garden: generally safe

## Alternate Sources
- Stored bottled water: safest option
- Swimming pool: filter and treat
- Water heater tank: drain from valve
- Frozen ice cubes made before event
- Neighborhood water distribution point

## Purification Methods
- Boiling: most reliable, no chemicals
- Bleach: 8 drops per gallon, 30 min wait
- Iodine tablets: per label directions
- Ceramic filter: removes most bacteria
- UV pen: kills bacteria and viruses

## Chemical Contamination
- Boiling does NOT remove chemicals
- Activated carbon filter: may help
- Distillation: removes most contaminants
- Bottled water only if chemical event
- Wait for official clearance
