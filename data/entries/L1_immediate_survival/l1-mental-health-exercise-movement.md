# Exercise & Movement

## Why Move

Your body evolved for 10-15 miles of movement daily. Sedentary life is the anomaly. Movement is medicine.

- Exercise as effective as SSRIs for mild-moderate depression
- 30 min cardio 5x/week = 47% reduction in depression symptoms
- 60% reduction in panic symptoms with regular exercise
- Regular exercise delays dementia onset 5-7 years
- Sedentary humans are sick humans. No exceptions.

**What exercise does neurochemically:**
- Endorphins: natural pain relief and euphoria
- BDNF: increases 300%, promotes new brain cell growth
- Dopamine: motivation, pleasure, reward processing
- Serotonin: mood stabilization, depression prevention

## The Big 5

No equipment needed. Scalable. Anywhere.

**Push-ups (chest, shoulders, triceps, core):**
- Hands shoulder-width, body straight, lower to ground, push up
- Beginner: wall or knee push-ups. Advanced: decline or one-arm.
- Target: 3 sets x 10-25 reps

**Squats (legs, glutes, core):**
- Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, hips back, thighs parallel
- Beginner: chair squats. Advanced: pistol squats.
- Target: 3 sets x 15-30 reps

**Pull-ups (back, biceps, grip):**
- Hang from bar or branch, pull until chin over bar
- Beginner: jumping pull-ups, lower slow. No bar: inverted rows under a table.
- Target: 3 sets x 5-12 reps

**Planks (core stability):**
- Forearms on ground, body straight head to heels, squeeze everything
- Beginner: knee plank. Advanced: side planks, plank with limb lift.
- Target: 3 sets x 30-90 seconds

**Burpees (full-body cardio):**
- Stand, squat to ground, jump back to plank, push-up, jump forward, jump up
- Beginner: step instead of jump, skip push-up
- Target: 3 sets x 10-20 reps

## Daily Circuit

Warm-up 5 min: jumping jacks, arm circles, leg swings, light jog in place.

Main circuit, repeat 3-5 rounds:
- Push-ups: 10-20 reps
- Squats: 15-25 reps
- Pull-ups or rows: 5-10 reps
- Plank: 30-60 seconds
- Burpees: 10-15 reps
- Rest 60-90 seconds between rounds

Cool-down 5 min: slow walk, stretch hamstrings, quads, chest, shoulders.

Frequency: 3-5x per week. Any movement beats no movement. Consistency beats intensity.

## Functional Fitness

Survival work IS exercise. Count it.

- Chopping wood: full-body cardio, 400-600 cal/hour, primal satisfaction
- Carrying water: grip, shoulders, core, loaded walking
- Digging: full-body sustained effort, 350-500 cal/hour
- Hauling and dragging: legs, back, core, intense bursts
- Building: variable muscle groups, 300-500 cal/hour

**Balance your week:** include push tasks, pull tasks, leg-dominant work, and sustained cardio.

Work parties combine purpose + exercise. Dual benefit, no wasted time.

## Stretch and Mobility

**Daily 10 minutes prevents injury and reduces pain.**

Morning (wake up body):
- Cat-cow: 10 reps, spine mobility
- Forward fold: 30 sec, hamstrings and back
- Hip circles: 10 each direction
- Arm circles: 10 each direction

Evening (wind down):
- Child's pose: 60 sec, back and hips
- Pigeon pose: 60 sec each side, hips
- Seated forward fold: 60 sec, calm nervous system
- Supine twist: 60 sec each side

## Group Movement

**Dancing:** synchronized movement releases oxytocin. Reduces depression 47%. No equipment. All ages.

**Martial arts:** shadow boxing, kata, partner drills. Builds discipline, confidence, focus, and respect.

**Games:** tag variations, capture the flag, kickball. Fun removes the feeling of exercise. Natural interval training.

## MH Protocols

**Depression prescription:**
- 30 min moderate cardio, 5x/week
- Intensity: can talk but not sing (60-70% max heart rate)
- Type: any sustained movement (walk, jog, circuit)
- Timeline: 2-4 weeks for noticeable effect, 8-12 weeks for full benefit

**Anxiety protocol:**
- Type: rhythmic, repetitive (walking, jogging, swimming)
- Duration: 20-30 min
- Frequency: daily or as needed during anxiety spike
- Mechanism: depletes cortisol and adrenaline, activates calming response

**Sleep improvement:**
- Exercise in morning or afternoon, not within 2 hours of bed
- 30+ minutes, consistent
- Fall asleep 15-30 min faster, deeper sleep, wake less

**PTSD:** start gentle (yoga, walking), build gradually, group format ideal.

## Build the Culture

**Daily rhythm:**
- Morning: 10-min stretch, then light walk to activate circadian rhythm
- Afternoon: functional work or structured circuit
- Evening: gentle stretching or social walk

**Community integration:**
- Group morning workout: accountability, competition, skill sharing
- Work parties: rotate tasks, labor as social time
- Dance nights weekly, sports tournaments monthly

## Mission

1. Start today: 10-min walk is a victory
2. Learn the Big 5: push, pull, squat, plank, burpee
3. Build habit: consistency over intensity
4. Integrate functional work: count it
5. Create group movement culture: dance, games, workouts together

**Movement is not optional. Movement is medicine. Dose daily.**
