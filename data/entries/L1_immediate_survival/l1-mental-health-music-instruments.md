# Music & Instruments

## Why Music

Every human culture in history has made music. No exceptions. This is not coincidence.

- Music reduces anxiety 65%
- Singing in groups releases oxytocin equal to physical touch
- PTSD symptoms reduced 43% with music therapy
- Music therapy as effective as talk therapy for mild-moderate depression
- Music activates the entire brain simultaneously. No other activity does this.

A community without music is dying.

## Wind Instruments

**Bamboo flute (6-hole pentatonic):**

Materials: bamboo section 30-40 cm, 2-2.5 cm diameter, knife, drill or hot metal rod.

Construction:
1. Select section between nodes (hollow chamber)
2. Cut to length, sand smooth
3. Drill blow hole 2 cm from stopped end, 1-1.5 cm diameter, angled 45 degrees
4. Drill 6 finger holes spaced 1.8-2.2 cm apart down the tube
5. Start holes small and enlarge by testing pitch until in tune
6. Pentatonic scale: cover all holes = lowest note, uncover one at a time going toward blow end

Playing: angle 30-45 degrees downward from lips, small firm opening, steady breath. 5-10 min daily for 2 weeks to get clear tone.

Pentatonic sounds good with any combination. No wrong notes. Found in every culture globally.

**Pan pipes (easier than flute):**
- Cut tubes to lengths: 20, 18, 16, 14.5, 13 cm
- Stop one end of each with wax or cork
- Bind side-by-side, tune by adjusting wax depth
- Blow straight across the open end

**Willow whistle (15 minutes to make):**
1. Cut green willow branch 10-15 cm, thumb thickness (spring only, when sap flows)
2. Tap bark to loosen and slide off intact
3. Cut notch in wood core, hollow air channel
4. Slide bark back on and test

## Percussion

**Frame drum:**
- Bend green wood into 30-50 cm circle, lash ends, or carve a ring
- Soak animal skin 2-4 hours, stretch over frame while wet, lace around perimeter
- Dry in warm place until skin shrinks tight
- Play with palm slap, fingertip roll, or carved leather-wrapped beater

20+ minutes sustained drumming induces theta waves: stress reduction, emotional processing.

**Shakers (5 minutes to make):**
- Container: dried gourd, bamboo tube, hollow wood, tin can
- Fill with seeds, pebbles, or dried beans
- Seal and attach a handle

**Rhythm sticks:** two hardwood sticks, 20 cm x 2.5 cm diameter, sanded smooth. Strike together for sharp click.

## String Instruments

**Diddley bow (one-string):**
- Wood plank 60-90 cm, wire or fishing line strung end to end over two raised bridges
- Place can or bottle under string for resonance
- Tune by adjusting tension with end nails or screws
- Pluck or slide with glass bottle neck (blues style)
- Build time: 30-60 minutes

**Mouth bow:**
- Flex a 60-90 cm branch into arc, tie string taut
- Hold one end near or in mouth, pluck string
- Mouth shape changes resonance to create different notes
- Meditative, soothing, excellent for anxiety

## Group Music

**Singing circles:**
- Sit in circle, leader starts simple melody, others join
- Rotate leadership each session
- Song types: work songs for coordinating labor, call-and-response, rounds, hymns or spirituals
- Synchronized breathing creates calm. No equipment needed.

**Call-and-response pattern:**
- Leader sings line, group repeats or answers
- Low pressure, easy to participate, creates unity
- Can encode instructions or knowledge in lyrics

**Drum circle:**
- Start simple 4/4 beat, layer complexity gradually
- No wrong notes, everyone contributes
- 20+ minutes = trance state, measurable stress reduction, improved immune function

## Music Therapy

**Anxiety:** 60 BPM rhythm, predictable pattern, group participation, 20-30 min minimum.

**Depression:** 100-120 BPM upbeat tempo, major key, movement (dance, clap, sway), group setting essential.

**PTSD:** rhythmic and predictable, start soft, never force participation, allow emotional release.

**Grief:** minor key matches emotional state (validates it), mournful songs, collective mourning, permission to cry.

## Teach & Preserve

**No written notation needed:**
- Repetition: sing or play phrase 10-20 times
- Call-response: teacher and student alternate
- Body movement: clap and dance (kinesthetic memory)
- 10 min daily beats 1 hour weekly

**Preserve songs:**
- Interview elders for family and community songs before they are gone
- Write down lyrics even without notation
- Teach widely: multiple people knowing a song = song preserved
- Perform regularly. Use it or lose it.

**Warning signs:** when music stops completely, it signals community depression or trauma shutdown. Start small: humming, simple rhythm, gentle invitation.

## Mission

1. Make 1 wind, 1 percussion, and 1 string instrument
2. Memorize 20 songs: work, celebration, mourning, children's
3. Establish weekly music nights now
4. Teach children: they carry it forward
5. Treat silence as a warning sign and respond to it

**Music is not entertainment. Music is medicine. Dose daily.**
