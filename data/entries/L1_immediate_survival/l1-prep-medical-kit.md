# Field Medical Kit

## Bleeding Control
- CAT tourniquet or SOFTT-W: 2 each
- Compressed gauze: 3 rolls
- Israeli bandage: 2 units
- Hemostatic gauze (QuikClot): 2 packs
- Chest seal: 2 vented (for chest wounds)

## Wound Care
- Nitrile gloves: 4 pairs minimum
- Saline flush: 2 x 250ml bottles
- Irrigation syringe: 20ml
- Sterile gauze pads: 4x4 in, 10 pack
- Medical tape: 2 rolls
- Steri-strips or closure strips

## Splinting and Mobility
- SAM splint: 2 sizes
- Elastic bandage: 3 in width, 4 rolls
- Triangular bandage: 2 units
- Traction splint: optional add-on
- Improvise with sticks and clothing

## Medications
- Ibuprofen and acetaminophen
- Diphenhydramine: allergic reactions
- Loperamide: diarrhea control
- Oral rehydration salts
- Prescription antibiotics: if available

## Tools and Diagnostics
- Medical scissors and tweezers
- Digital thermometer
- Blood pressure cuff: optional
- SAM chest seal applicator
- Permanent marker: write times on skin

## Organization
- Color-coded pouches by category
- Inventory card inside kit
- Expiration dates checked every 6 months
- Keep dry and away from extreme heat
- Practice using every item before needed
