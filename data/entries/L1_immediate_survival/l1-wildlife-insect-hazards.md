# Insect Hazards Field

## Why It Matters

- Insects are the top disease vector
- Tick-borne illness disables survivors
- Bee swarms can deliver fatal dose
- Mosquitoes transmit malaria, West Nile
- Fire ants cause serious infection
- Prevention is simple and effective

## Bees and Wasps

- Do not disturb nests or hive areas
- Buzzing from ground = yellow jacket nest
- Run from swarm, do not swat
- Cover face while running from swarm
- Jump into water if available
- EpiPen required if allergic, carry it

## Ticks: Prevention

- Treat clothing with permethrin
- Tuck pants into socks in tick country
- Check body twice daily head to toe
- Focus: scalp, groin, behind knees
- Remove promptly with fine tweezers
- Pull straight out, no twisting

## Ticks: Removal

- Grasp tick close to skin surface
- Pull upward steadily without twisting
- Do not crush or squeeze tick body
- Clean bite site with alcohol
- Record date and save tick in bag
- Watch for rash or fever 7-14 days

## Mosquitoes

- Repellent with DEET 30% most effective
- Cover skin at dawn and dusk most
- Sleep under net in high-malaria zones
- Long sleeves and pants reduce contact
- Standing water is breeding ground: avoid
- Smoke from fire deters in camp

## What Can Go Wrong

- Tick not fully removed, head remains
- Bee sting triggers unsuspected allergy
- Tick check misses hairline or groin
- Permethrin worn off and not reapplied

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Full tick check in under
  5 minutes post-day in tick country
- Sign of mastery: Zero embedded
  tick past 24 hours on any trip
