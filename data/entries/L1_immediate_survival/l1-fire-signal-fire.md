# Signal Fire

## Why It Matters

- Aircraft spot fires from miles away
- Smoke visible 5-10 miles in daylight
- Flame visible 10+ miles at night
- Pre-built signal fire saves time
- Rescuers have narrow search windows
- You need to respond in under 1 min

## Site Selection

- Choose highest clear ground
- Open meadow or ridgeline best
- Away from overhead canopy
- Visible from multiple directions
- Near your shelter, not far away
- Pre-clear a 10-foot circle

## Build for Visibility

- Build large base fuel pile
- Add green boughs for white smoke
- Add rubber/plastic for black smoke
- Day: white smoke shows best
- Night: bright flame shows best
- Prep 3 fires in triangle pattern

## Smoke Color Control

- White smoke: green leaves, moss
- Black smoke: rubber, plastic, oil
- Use opposite of background
- White on dark forested hills
- Black on snow or light sky
- Wet leaves create dense white

## Triggering the Signal

- Keep dry tinder ready at base
- Light quickly when aircraft heard
- Fan to generate immediate smoke
- Wave bright cloth to direct eye
- Use mirror flash to complement
- Maintain for full aircraft pass

## What Can Go Wrong

- Fire burns before rescue arrives
- Wrong smoke color lost in sky
- Wind disperses smoke horizontally
- Fuel pile too small for impact

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Full smoke within 60 sec
- Sign of mastery: Visible plume
  rising 100 ft in 2 minutes
