# Transpiration Bag

## Why It Matters

- Works with no digging required
- Clear bag is the only tool needed
- Can collect 250-500 ml per day
- Works passively while you rest
- Set multiple bags to multiply yield
- No fire or purification needed

## Plant Selection

- Choose leafy non-toxic branch
- Green leaves work, dry leaves don't
- Broad leaf trees yield more
- Cottonwood, maple, willow are good
- Avoid toxic species (check entries)
- Full sun exposure is critical

## Setup Steps

- Select south-facing branch tips
- Place clear bag over leaf cluster
- Seal bag opening tight to branch
- Weight or tie low corner of bag
- Water pools in low corner
- Set by 9am for best production

## Harvest and Reset

- Collect by afternoon peak heat
- Untie seal, drain into container
- Shake leaves back into position
- Reseal for second collection
- Replace to new branch if output low
- Mark bag position with flagging

## What Can Go Wrong

- Bag is not fully sealed - leaks
- Cloudy day cuts yield by 80%
- Branch chosen too small/few leaves
- Bag melts or degrades in heat

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: 5 bags producing 1.5 L/day
- Sign of mastery: Setup under
  3 minutes per bag with good seal
