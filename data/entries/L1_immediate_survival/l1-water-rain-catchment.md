# Rain Water Catchment

## Why It Matters

- Rain is often the cleanest source
- No contamination if caught fresh
- Works at night and while sleeping
- Setup takes under 5 minutes
- Even light rain yields liters/hr
- Often overlooked in planning

## Surface Area

- 1 sq meter catches 1 L per mm rain
- Larger tarp = dramatically more water
- Angle surface to single drain point
- Flat tarp loses water on surface
- Poncho, bivy, emergency blanket work
- Multiple collectors multiply yield

## Quick Field Setup

- Tie tarp at 4 corners, one low point
- Weight low corner or funnel to bag
- Angle 20-30 degrees minimum
- Use rocks or sticks to hold shape
- Channel center line to drain point
- Catch start of rain after rinsing

## Improvised Collectors

- Rain poncho draped over branches
- Hole in log or rock catches
- Bark bowl leaned to channel
- Large leaf funnel to container
- Upturned lid or hubcap from vehicle
- Parachute cord taut line drips

## Storage

- Filter through cloth before storage
- Cover storage to prevent recontam.
- Purify if collecting from canopy
- Direct sky-fall water is cleanest
- Fill water bags/bottles immediately
- Mark water collection time

## What Can Go Wrong

- Tarp sags in center - pools too deep
- Rain too light, evaporates in sun
- Collection vessel not large enough
- Wind flips unsecured collection

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Rig collection catching
  rain within 90 seconds of start
- Sign of mastery: 5 L collected
  in a moderate 30-min rain
