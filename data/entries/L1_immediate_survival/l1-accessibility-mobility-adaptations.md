# Mobility Adapt for

## Evacuation Planning

Plan routes before you need them.
- Identify 2 routes from every location: primary and backup.
- Know the bottlenecks: stairs, narrow passages, rough terrain.
- Pre-assign a buddy to each mobility-limited person.
- Mark their go-bag: first to load, nearest to the door.
- Walk and practice the route — never improvise under real stress.

## Terrain Assessment

Before moving a person with limited mobility:
- Test ground firmness with a stick or foot before committing.
- Avoid slopes steeper than 5 degrees without a second person.
- Mud and gravel: slow, steady, one person stabilizes while other guides.
- Wet grass slips; paved curbs tip wheelchairs — identify both.
- Stairs require two people minimum; descend back-first.

## Improvised Aids

Crutch: sturdy branch at shoulder height, padded top with cloth.
Carry litter: two poles (branches) with a jacket threaded through sleeves.
- Tie jacket sleeves to poles. Test weight before moving.
Drag sheet: blanket under person, drag from head end only.
Wheelchair on stairs: tilt back 30 degrees, two helpers, one step at a time.

## One-Person Carry

Use only when no assistance is available:
- Assist walk: person's arm over your shoulder, your arm around waist.
- Fireman carry: person across shoulders, hold wrist and thigh.
- Drag carry: grip under armpits, drag toward safety. Short distances only.
- Cradle carry: arms under knees and back (small or light persons only).
Stop if you feel back strain. Set down, rest, then continue.
