# Ice Storm Survival

## Ice Accumulation Risk
- 0.25 in ice: power lines down risk
- 0.5 in ice: branches fall on roads
- 1.0 in ice: major tree fall and outage
- Stay indoors during active ice storm
- Do not park under trees or power lines

## Driving in Ice
- Do not drive unless necessary
- Speed: 10 mph max on ice
- Braking distance 10x longer on ice
- Bridges ice before road surfaces
- Black ice: no visible warning signs
- If sliding: steer into the slide gently

## Power Outage Response
- Generator: use only outdoors
- Never burn charcoal indoors: CO
- Food: fridge safe 4 hours, freezer 48
- Pipes: open cabinet under sinks
- Check on elderly and neighbors

## Tree and Roof Hazards
- Do not stand under loaded branches
- Cracking sounds: move away fast
- Ice on roof: collapse risk with snow
- Never attempt to remove ice manually
- Wait for thaw to clear safely

## Heating Alternatives
- Insulate one small interior room
- Body heat from multiple people
- Wool and down: best insulation
- Hand warmers: reserve for extremities
- Leave for warming shelter if needed

## After the Storm
- Check for downed lines before going out
- Roads may remain icy 24-48 hours
- Clear ice from walkways carefully
- Inspect roof for sagging sections
- Report hazards to utility company
