# Star Fire Lay

## Why It Matters

- Requires no cutting or splitting of wood
- Burns longer than a small pile
- Easy to regulate heat by push/pull
- Ideal when energy conservation is key
- Used globally by indigenous cultures
- Works with large green or dry logs

## Star Layout Setup

- Find 5 logs, each 6-10 ft long
- Arrange like spokes of a wheel
- Tips meet at center point
- Start small fire at the hub
- Push logs in as ends burn down
- Keep a 6-inch gap between logs

## Regulating the Fire

- Push logs in for more heat/flame
- Pull logs back to slow burning
- Rotate logs to burn evenly
- Add a 6th log if more heat needed
- Use wet log as a windbreak spoke
- Do not let logs overlap at center

## Ash and Coal Care

- Let ash accumulate under center
- Ash bed insulates and holds heat
- Rake ash outward if fire smothers
- Coals in center stay hot for hours
- Ideal for banking overnight fire
- Place tinder on coals to relight

## What Can Go Wrong

- Fire dies if logs spread too far
- Uneven log burn leaves hot stub
- Wet logs cause excess smoke
- Wind shifts spread sparks outward

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Sustained fire 4+ hrs, 1 log
- Sign of mastery: No tending needed
  for 30+ min intervals
