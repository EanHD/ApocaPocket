# Power Outage Protocol

## First 30 Minutes
- Check breakers before calling utility
- Report outage to utility company
- Unplug sensitive electronics
- Locate flashlights and batteries
- Fill containers with water now

## Food Safety Timeline
- Refrigerator: safe 4 hours if closed
- Freezer full: safe 48 hours if closed
- Freezer half-full: safe 24 hours
- When in doubt: throw it out
- Use coolers with ice to extend time

## Heating Without Power
- Interior room: warmest and smallest
- Seal drafts with towels and tape
- Layer all available clothing and blankets
- Candles: provide real heat but fire risk
- Propane heater: outdoor use only

## Generator Safety
- Generator outdoors only: always
- Keep 20 ft from windows and doors
- CO detector required in home
- Never refuel while running
- Ground fault protection: use outside

## Medical Equipment
- Oxygen concentrators: call provider
- CPAP: battery backup or DC adapter
- Insulin: cooler with ice water
- Medical alert: notify local fire station
- Know which conditions need evacuation

## Lighting Priorities
- Headlamps: hands-free and efficient
- LED candles: safe for children nearby
- Chemical light sticks: long duration
- Avoid open flame near gas lines
- Conserve batteries: use only as needed
