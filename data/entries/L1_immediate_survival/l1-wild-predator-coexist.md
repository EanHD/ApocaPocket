# Predator Coexistence

## Bear Protocols
- Make noise while traveling in brush
- Never approach a bear: give space
- Stand ground if bear approaches
- Black bear: fight back if attacked
- Grizzly bear: play dead if contact made
- Never run: triggers pursuit instinct

## Grizzly vs Black Bear
- Grizzly: shoulder hump, dished face
- Black bear: no hump, Roman nose
- Color alone does not identify species
- Grizzly: claws 3-4 in, black bear 1-2 in
- Response differs: know the species

## Cougar Protocols
- Make yourself look large: raise arms
- Maintain eye contact always
- Back away slowly: never turn back
- Throw rocks or sticks if it advances
- Fight back aggressively if attacked
- Go for eyes and nose

## Wolf Protocols
- Lone wolf near humans: unusual, danger
- Pack wolves: back away with group
- Do not run or crouch down
- Make noise and appear large
- Protect children: lift if possible
- Report wolf habituation to rangers

## Camp Predator Prevent
- Proper food cache: primary defense
- Cook and eat away from sleeping
- Never leave scraps or garbage in camp
- Sweep camp area before dark
- Light deterrent: red blinking light

## Bear Spray Use
- Effective within 30-60 ft range
- Deploy when bear is charging
- Aim at face, 2-second burst
- Keep accessible: not in pack bottom
- Check expiration date regularly
