# Long-Term Camp Setup

## Site Selection
- Elevated dry ground above flood line
- Within 200 yards of water source
- Natural windbreak on north side
- Access to firewood within 100 yards
- Defensible and low-visibility position

## Camp Layout Zones
- Sleeping zone: upwind from cooking
- Cooking zone: separate and downwind
- Food cache: 200 ft from sleeping
- Latrine: 200 ft from water and camp
- Work area: between sleep and cook

## Latrine Construction
- Cat hole: 6-8 in deep for 1-2 day use
- Long-term trench: 12 in deep, 3 ft long
- Downhill from camp and water source
- Cover each use with dirt immediately
- Mark location clearly for all users

## Food Cache
- Bear hang: 10 ft high, 4 ft from trunk
- Smell-proof bag inside hang bag
- Separate cache for cooking supplies
- No food in sleeping area: ever
- Clean cooking area after every meal

## Drainage and Water
- Trench around shelter on uphill side
- Channel water away from sleeping area
- Raised sleeping platform if possible
- Keep entrance covered from rain
- Check drainage after each heavy rain

## Camp Maintenance
- Daily: tend fire, check cache, water
- Weekly: inspect structure, dig new latrine
- Ongoing: expand firewood supply
- Rotate work duties if group present
- Secure loose items before storms
