# Special Needs Prep

## Medical Device
- CPAP: DC adapter or battery backup
- Oxygen concentrator: call provider now
- Ventilator: register with utility company
- Dialysis: identify alternate center
- Insulin pump: have manual injection plan

## Medication Management
- 7-day supply in go-bag always
- 30-day backup at secondary location
- Refrigerated meds: cold pack rotation
- Prescription list with dosage: paper copy
- Pharmacy contacts in multiple states

## Mobility Planning
- Wheelchair evacuation plan: practice
- Accessible vehicle: confirm ahead
- Manual backup for power wheelchair
- Route survey: identify ramp access
- Evacuation assist: pre-arrange neighbor

## Communication Needs
- Hearing aids: extra batteries in kit
- Backup communication device: AAC
- Written cards for non-verbal emergencies
- Sign language basics for family
- Contact card with medical info

## Shelter Requirements
- Accessible shelter location identified
- Call ahead to confirm access
- Bring own cot or wedge if needed
- Special dietary needs: bring own food
- Caregiver plan: who goes with person

## Registry and
- Local emergency management registry
- Utility company medical baseline program
- Neighbor notification: written form
- Hospital and care team emergency contacts
- Update registry annually
