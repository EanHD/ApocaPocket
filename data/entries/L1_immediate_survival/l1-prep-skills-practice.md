# Practice Schedule

## Monthly Drills
- January: fire evacuation and rally
- February: medical kit inventory and refresh
- March: water filter test and rotation
- April: communication check-in exercise
- May: bug-out bag pack and test walk
- June: outdoor fire starting practice

## Monthly Drills (cont.)
- July: heat response and cooling plan
- August: wildfire evacuation drill
- September: generator start and load test
- October: food inventory and rotation
- November: cold weather shelter setup
- December: winter vehicle kit check

## Skill Priority
- Quarter 1: medical skills (CPR, bleeding)
- Quarter 2: navigation and land skills
- Quarter 3: food and water skills
- Quarter 4: communications and planning
- Year 2: repeat with increased difficulty

## Family Involvement
- Include all family members over age 8
- Children: age-appropriate simple tasks
- Assign roles per person per scenario
- Debrief after each drill: what worked
- Adjust plans based on drill results

## Knowledge Testing
- Quiz each other on emergency contacts
- Locate water shutoff without looking
- Find go-bag contents in 2 minutes
- Change a tire in under 15 minutes
- Light fire without matches

## Record Keeping
- Log each drill with date and result
- Note gaps and assign follow-up
- Track equipment expiration dates
- Annual full review: March each year
- Document lessons learned each event
