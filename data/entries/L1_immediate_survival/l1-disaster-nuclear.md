# Nuclear Event Basics

## Immediate Blast
- Get inside immediately: any building
- Go to center of building, lower floor
- Stay away from all windows
- Do not look toward the blast flash
- Flash blindness: turn away from light

## Fallout Timeline
- Fallout arrives 15-60 min after blast
- Greatest danger: first 24-48 hours
- Radiation decreases rapidly with time
- Rule of 7: 7x time = 10x reduction
- Stay sheltered minimum 24 hours

## Shelter in Place
- Brick and concrete: best shielding
- Basement: best location in any building
- Seal doors and windows with tape
- Turn off all HVAC and fans
- Do not open doors for others after event

## Fallout Contamination
- Remove outer clothing: 80% reduction
- Shower with soap and water: critical
- Do not conditioner: traps particles
- Blow nose, wipe eyes and ears
- Seal contaminated clothes in bag

## Radiation Dose
- KI tablets: protect thyroid only
- Take KI only if directed by authorities
- Do not take KI for nuclear plant accident
  unless directed: different exposure type
- Food and water: cover all supplies

## Post-Event Actions
- Monitor EAS for all-clear guidance
- Do not consume outdoor food or water
- Pets outdoors: wipe down before entry
- Radiation fades: re-evaluate at 24 hr
- Follow official decontamination orders
