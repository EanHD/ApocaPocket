# Seasonal Prep Check

## Spring Check
- Inspect all outdoor structures for damage
- Test generator after winter storage
- Replace smoke and CO detector batteries
- Check go-bag: rotate expired food
- Update contact and document list

## Spring Kit Updates
- Remove winter gear from vehicle kit
- Add allergy medication if needed
- Check water filter cartridge life
- Add rain poncho and light layers
- Review flood and tornado risk plans

## Summer Check
- Heat hazard plan reviewed with family
- Extra water stored: heat increases need
- Sunscreen and electrolyte tabs in kit
- Vehicle kit: extra water added
- Fire extinguisher inspected

## Fall Check
- Add cold weather gear to all kits
- Fuel storage: check quality and amount
- Heating system serviced
- First aid kit restocked
- Freeze-dried food inventory check

## Winter Check
- Vehicle kit: winter additions confirmed
- Generator fuel: fresh and stabilized
- Home heating backup: confirmed ready
- Pipes: insulated for freeze protection
- Ice storm and blizzard plan reviewed

## Year-Round Tasks
- Prescription meds: 7-day supply always
- Phone contacts: backup printed copy
- Bug-out bag: accessible and stocked
- Family rally points: all members know
- Annual full drill: scheduled date set
