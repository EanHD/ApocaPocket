# Food Cache System

## Bear Hang Method
- Use PCT method: two-point hang
- Line: 50 ft paracord minimum
- Height: 10 ft off ground
- Distance from trunk: 4+ ft minimum
- All scented items: toothpaste, lotion

## Underground Cache
- Depth: 2-3 ft minimum
- Line with rocks to block small rodents
- Food-grade container with lid
- Pack in dry leaves or grass for cold
- Mark with natural landmark, not pile

## Dry Cache Construction
- Rock crevice: natural cold storage
- Waterproof bag inside container
- Desiccant packet extends shelf life
- Elevated on dry rock bed inside
- Check weekly for moisture intrusion

## Food Rotation
- FIFO: first in, first out rule
- Consume most perishable items first
- Smoke and dry before caching protein
- Calendar system: scratch marks on wood
- Inventory after each resupply event

## Cache Location Rules
- 200 ft from all sleeping areas
- Multiple small caches vs one large
- Never cache at same spot twice
- Natural landmark only as marker
- Remember locations: no written map

## Odor Management
- Double-bag all food in smell-proof bags
- Hang cooking clothes with food cache
- Never eat in sleeping area
- Wipe face and hands before sleeping
- Cook meals 100+ ft from sleep area
