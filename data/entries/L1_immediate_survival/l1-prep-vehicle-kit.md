# Vehicle Emergency Kit

## Flat and Mechanical
- Full-size or compact spare tire
- Jack and lug wrench: know how to use
- Jumper cables or jump starter pack
- Tire inflator and pressure gauge
- Basic tool kit: wrenches, pliers

## Weather Emergencies
- Emergency Mylar blanket: 2 per person
- Ice scraper and brush (winter)
- Sand or traction mats
- Shovel: collapsible for winter
- Rain poncho: 2 per person

## Water and Food
- 2 liters water per person minimum
- Replace every 6 months (heat degrades)
- Emergency food bars: 3 day supply
- Restock after any use immediately

## Navigation and
- State paper road map: never outdated
- Compass
- Reflective triangle or flares (3)
- LED road flares: safer than pyro
- Whistle and bright colored cloth

## First Aid and Safety
- Full first aid kit: 100-piece minimum
- Fire extinguisher: car rated
- Seatbelt cutter and window breaker tool
- Phone charger: 12V car adapter
- Flashlight and extra batteries

## Seasonal Vehicle Prep
- Summer: check tire pressure hot weather
- Winter: antifreeze and wiper fluid
- Spring: inspect undercarriage for rust
- Fall: replace worn wiper blades
- Monthly: check spare tire pressure
