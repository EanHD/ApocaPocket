# Tornado Survival Detail

## Warning Signs
- Dark greenish sky
- Large hail with no rain
- Loud roar: freight train sound
- Funnel cloud visible or not
- Debris cloud at ground level
- Sudden calm after storm front

## Shelter Priorities
- Lowest floor, interior room
- Bathroom or closet under stairs
- No windows in safe room
- Mobile home: leave for ditch
- Highway overpass: NOT safe shelter
- Ditch lower than road surface

## In a Vehicle
- Do not try to outrun large tornado
- Drive perpendicular to tornado path
- Abandon car for lower ground
- Lie flat in ditch, hands over neck
- Never shelter under an overpass

## In Open Field
- Lie flat in lowest available area
- Cover head with arms and hands
- Face down toward the ground
- Move away from trees and cars
- Stay away from potential projectiles

## After the Tornado
- Check for injuries first
- Watch for downed power lines
- Use flashlight: no open flame
- Structural damage may not be visible
- Wear sturdy shoes: debris everywhere

## Recovery Actions
- Document damage before cleanup
- Do not enter damaged structure
- Beware of natural gas leaks
- Check on neighbors nearby
- Monitor EAS for further warnings
