# Long-Term Food Plan

## Caloric Math
- Adult base need: 2000-2500 cal/day
- Hard labor in cold: up to 5000 cal
- Starvation begins below 1200 cal/day
- Fat: 9 cal/gram (most dense source)
- Protein and carb: 4 cal/gram each

## Foraging vs Hunting
- Foraging: reliable but low calorie
- Hunting: high calorie but unreliable
- Trapping: passive and efficient
- Fish: most reliable animal protein
- Insects: highly efficient cal/effort

## Priority Food
- Fat: nuts, animal fat, bone marrow
- Carbs: roots, tubers, seeds
- Protein: fish, game, insects, legumes
- Vitamins: greens, berries prevent deficiency
- Salt: critical for muscle function

## Preservation Methods
- Smoking: dries and preserves protein
- Drying: roots, berries, mushrooms
- Fat rendering: stores for months
- Fermentation: adds nutrition and shelf life
- Cold storage: stream cache in container

## Foraging Calendar
- Spring: greens, fiddleheads, ramps
- Summer: berries, roots, flowers
- Fall: nuts, seeds, late berries
- Winter: bark cambium, dried berries, roots
- Year-round: insects under logs and bark

## Effort vs Return
- Low return: gathering small items
- Medium return: fishing with line
- High return: snare and trap lines
- Best overall: multiple passive methods
- Hunting large game: high reward, high effort
