# Field Weather Forecast

## Cloud Types and
- Cirrus (high, wispy): weather in 24-48hr
- Cumulonimbus: storm within hours
- Alto-stratus (grey sheet): rain in 12-24hr
- Nimbostratus: steady rain now or soon
- Cumulus flat base, growing tall: storm

## Pressure Indicators
- Campfire smoke rises: high pressure, fair
- Campfire smoke falls: low pressure, storm
- Wood smoke smell strong: moist, rain coming
- Sounds carry farther: low pressure, rain
- Joints aching: anecdotal, low pressure sign

## Wind Reading
- Wind from south: warm, moisture coming
- Wind from north: cold, clear or snow
- Wind shifting counterclockwise: storm
- Wind shifting clockwise: clearing
- Gusty erratic wind: front approaching fast

## 24-Hour Prediction
- Clear sky at sunset: fair next morning
- Red sky at morning: storm possible
- Moon halo: ice crystals, moisture coming
- Dew on grass: clear night, fair morning
- No dew: clouds or wind, watch weather

## 48-Hour Prediction
- Cirrus then alto-stratus: storm in 2 days
- Barometric fall over 12 hours: storm likely
- Animal feeding frenzy: pressure dropping
- Insect swarms low: rain coming soon
- Persistent south wind: major change coming

## Mountain Specific
- Lenticular cloud on peak: high wind aloft
- Cap cloud building: storm by afternoon
- Cloud base lowering: precipitation imminent
- Clear morning in mountains: deceptive
- Afternoon rule: be off ridge by 2pm
