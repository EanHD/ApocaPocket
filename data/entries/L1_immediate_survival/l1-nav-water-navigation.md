# Follow Water Out

## Why It Matters

- Water flows to lowest point
- Settlements always near water
- Streams become rivers become roads
- Provides hydration during travel
- Well-worn animal trails along banks
- One decision eliminates all others

## When to Follow Water

- No known direction to civilization
- Map and compass not available
- Rescue not expected soon
- Terrain prevents other movement
- Water is running, not dry bed
- No impassable canyon downstream

## Following Safely

- Stay on the bank, not in water
- Cross only at shallow wide points
- Never cross moving water at night
- Rest at confluences and reassess
- Note stream getting larger = progress
- Sound of louder water = waterfall ahead

## Canyon Hazards

- Stop 100 m before audible waterfall
- Scout downstream before committing
- Canyon walls may trap you inside
- Look for bypass route on high bank
- Mark high point before descending
- Do not enter canyon without exit plan

## Civilization Signs

- Cut stumps or cleared areas
- Fence lines crossing stream
- Concrete or plastic debris
- Noise of machinery or vehicles
- Domestic animal tracks
- Cleared paths becoming maintained

## What Can Go Wrong

- Desert stream ends in sand
- Canyon narrows to unpassable slot
- Moving too fast past trail junctions
- Commitment to wrong drainage

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Follow drainage 5 km to
  first sign of human activity
- Sign of mastery: Identify canyon
  hazard before entering it
