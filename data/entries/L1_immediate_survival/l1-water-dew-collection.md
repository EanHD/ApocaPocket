# Dew Collection

## Why It Matters

- Dew forms every clear calm night
- No special equipment needed
- 1 liter per hour possible in dense
  dew conditions
- Works in desert and temperate zones
- Supplement to other water methods
- Best collected before sunrise

## Best Conditions

- Clear sky overnight (no cloud cover)
- Light to no wind at dawn
- Temperature drop below dew point
- Heavy on grass, leaves, surfaces
- Fog and dew can combine for more
- Spring and fall most productive

## Collection Methods

- Tie absorbent cloth to lower legs
- Walk through tall grass at dawn
- Wring cloth into container
- Wipe large leaves with cloth
- Collect from metal surfaces
- Tilt tarp or poncho to drain low pt

## Maximizing Yield

- Use multiple cloths simultaneously
- Work quickly before sun evaporates
- Cover large area in grid pattern
- Metal/plastic surfaces hold more
- Smooth surfaces yield best runoff
- Collect into wide-mouth container

## What Can Go Wrong

- Wind disperses dew before collection
- Clouds overnight = no dew forms
- Toxic plant contact on cloth
- Not enough volume in dry climate

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Collect 500 ml by sunrise
  in moderate dew conditions
- Sign of mastery: Efficient route
  yielding 1 L per 45 minutes
