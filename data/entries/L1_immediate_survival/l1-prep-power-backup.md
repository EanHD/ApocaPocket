# Backup Power Basics

## Power Bank Options
- 10,000 mAh: 2-3 phone charges
- 20,000 mAh: 4-6 phone charges
- Keep charged above 80% always
- Charge medical devices first priority
- High-quality lithium: longer lifespan

## Generator Safety
- Outdoor use only: 20 ft from windows
- Never run in garage even door open
- CO detector in home: always required
- Transfer switch: prevents backfeed
- Fuel: store in approved container only

## Generator Fuel Storage
- 5 gal approved metal or plastic cans
- Add fuel stabilizer for storage
- Rotate fuel every 6 months
- Propane stores indefinitely: preferred
- Diesel: stores longer than gasoline

## Solar Power Options
- 100W panel charges 12V battery daily
- 12V battery runs LED lights for weeks
- Charge controller: required with panel
- Inverter: converts 12V to 120V AC
- Portable solar: folding panel and bank

## Critical Load Priority
- Medical devices: CPAP, O2, insulin
- Communications: phone, radio
- Light: LED uses minimal power
- Water pump if on well system
- Refrigerator: medical meds only

## Power Calculation
- LED light: 10 watts = 240 watt-hr/day
- Phone charge: 20-30 watt-hours each
- CPAP: 30-60 watt-hours per night
- Mini fridge: 1 kWh per day
- Plan for 3-day minimum storage
