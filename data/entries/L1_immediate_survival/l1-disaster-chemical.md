# Chemical Hazard

## Initial Response
- Get inside immediately
- Close all windows and doors
- Turn off HVAC, fans, fireplace
- Move to highest floor if heavy gas
- Move to lowest floor if lighter gas

## Shelter Sealing
- Seal gaps with duct tape and plastic
- Cover electrical outlets and vents
- Wet towels at door bottoms
- One room: seal tightly, smaller better
- Turn off whole house ventilation

## Heavy vs Light Gas
- Heavy gas (chlorine): go up stairs
- Light gas (ammonia): go to basement
- Hazmat alert: follow official guidance
- When in doubt: interior room, mid-level

## Improvised Respirator
- Wet cloth over nose and mouth
- Multiple layers of fabric help
- Commercial P100 mask: best available
- N95: partial protection only
- Activated carbon cloth: best DIY

## Decontamination
- Remove clothing: step out of it
- Shower with soap and water
- Do not rub eyes: flush with water
- Flush eyes 15 min with clean water
- Bag contaminated clothes outside

## When to Evacuate
- Only evacuate on official direction
- Going through contaminated air: last resort
- If evacuation: drive fast, windows up
- Drive perpendicular to wind direction
- Do not stop until reaching clean air zone
