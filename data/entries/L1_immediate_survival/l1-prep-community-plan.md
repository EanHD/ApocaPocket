# Community Prep Plan

## Neighbor Assessment
- Know your 5 nearest neighbors
- Identify: skills, medical needs, tools
- Elderly, infants, disabled: know first
- Share basic contact information
- Annual check-in to update info

## Shared Resources
- Shared generator: rotation agreement
- Water storage: community tank plan
- Tool share: chainsaw, roto-tiller
- Large vehicle for evacuation help
- Medical skills inventory: who has what

## Communication Network
- Block captain system: one per 10 homes
- Phone tree: assign backups for each
- GMRS channel: assign one per group
- Community board: central post location
- Regular meeting: quarterly minimum

## Skill Sharing
- First aid: certify at least 2 per block
- Ham radio: at least 1 per neighborhood
- Medical: identify nurses and EMTs
- Mechanical: locate mechanics nearby
- Food skills: identify canners, farmers

## Mutual Aid Protocol
- Labor exchange for storm cleanup
- Food share if supply chain disrupted
- Child care rotation during emergency
- Security rotation for extended events
- Emotional support coordination

## Vetting and Trust
- Start small: immediate neighbors only
- Build trust over time
- Do not advertise full supply inventory
- Shared prep meetings: low key
- Focus on relationships first
