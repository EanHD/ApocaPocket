# Altitude Detail

## Why It Matters

- AMS affects 25% above 8,000 ft
- HACE and HAPE can be fatal fast
- Early recognition prevents death
- Descent is the only real treatment
- Medication buys time, not a cure
- Know which condition before acting

## AMS: Altitude Sickness

- Headache + one other symptom
- Nausea, fatigue, dizziness, poor sleep
- Onset 6-12 hours after ascent
- Stop ascending immediately
- Descend 1,000-2,000 ft if no improve
- Ibuprofen 600 mg for headache
- Acetazolamide (Diamox) helps prevention

## HACE: Cerebral Edema

- Severe headache unresponsive to meds
- Confusion, disorientation, ataxia
- Ataxia test: walk heel-to-toe line
- Cannot do it = HACE, descend now
- Hallucinations in late stage
- Dexamethasone 8 mg IM if available
- Descend minimum 1,000 ft immediately

## HAPE: Pulmonary Edema

- Dry cough becoming productive
- Reduced exercise tolerance first
- Crackles in lungs (gurgling sound)
- Bluish lips or fingernails (cyanosis)
- Resting respiratory rate over 30
- Most fatal form of altitude illness
- Descend immediately, do not wait

## Descent Decision Tree

- Any HACE sign: descend now
- Any HAPE sign: descend now
- AMS not improving in 24 hrs: descend
- Descend until symptoms resolve
- Re-ascend only after 24 hrs symptom-free
- Portable hyperbaric bag buys time

## What Can Go Wrong

- AMS ignored and continuing ascent
- HACE confusion prevents self-help
- HAPE blamed on fitness level
- Waiting for helicopter instead of descending

- Goal: Correctly triage AMS vs HACE

- Sign of mastery: Descend decision
  made before symptoms become severe
