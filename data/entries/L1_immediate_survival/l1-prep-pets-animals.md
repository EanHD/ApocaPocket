# Pet Emergency Plan

## Basic Supplies
- 3-day food supply in waterproof bag
- Water: 1 qt per dog per day
- Collapsible food and water bowls
- Vaccination records in go-bag
- Photo of pet with owner: for ID

## Carriers and
- Hard carrier: safer in disaster debris
- Soft carrier: lighter for evacuation
- Extra leash and collar with ID tag
- Microchip: most reliable ID method
- Harness: better control under stress

## Medication and Medical
- All medications in labeled bags
- Vet contact number on paper
- First aid kit: pet-specific additions
- Cone or muzzle: injured pets may bite
- Heartworm and flea prevention stash

## Evacuation with Pets
- Practice loading pets into carrier
- Pet-friendly hotels: know 3 options
- Pet-friendly shelter: identify before event
- Boarding facility: emergency contact
- Friend or family: pre-arranged plan

## Large Animals and
- Trailer: maintained and ready
- Arrange destination before disaster
- Load and unload practice: monthly
- Identification: brands or ear tags
- Feed: 3-day supply per animal

## Leaving Animals Behind
- If forced to leave: never tie outside
- Leave loose inside with food and water
- Note left on door: "pets inside"
- Return ASAP or call rescue group
- Report to animal control when safe
