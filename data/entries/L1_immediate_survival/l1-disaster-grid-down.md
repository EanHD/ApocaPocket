# Extended Grid-Down

## Hours 0-24
- Verify outage duration estimate
- Secure medications needing cold storage
- Inventory food: eat perishables first
- Fill all containers with tap water now
- Charge all devices if power partial

## Hours 24-72
- Fridge unsafe: move to cooler or discard
- Water pressure may drop: store more
- Establish communications with family
- Reduce physical activity to conserve food
- Check on neighbors with medical needs

## Days 3-7
- Fuel consumption increases in cold
- Sewage system may back up
- Hand pump or gravity filter for water
- Reduce to 2 meals per day if needed
- Community contact: share information

## Days 7-30
- Heating and cooling become critical
- Food stores need formal rationing
- Water: 1 gallon per person per day
- Security: establish neighborhood watch
- Medical: prioritize chronic conditions

## Resource Priorities
- Water: 1 gal/person/day minimum
- Food: 2000 cal/person/day
- Heat or cooling: shelter priority
- Medications: life-sustaining first
- Communication: maintain contact

## Reintegration
- Boil water until system tested
- Do not assume power is stable
- Discard all questionable food
- Check well water if on private well
- Document all resource consumption
