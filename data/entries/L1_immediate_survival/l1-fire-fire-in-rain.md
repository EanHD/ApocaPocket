# Fire in Rain

## Why It Matters

- Rain kills established fires fast
- Surviving rain is about defense
- Hot coal bed resists brief rain
- This covers MAINTAINING fire
- For finding dry materials see
  l1-fire-wet-weather entry

## Overhead Cover First

- Rig tarp before rain if possible
- Angle tarp to shed water away
- Keep tarp 4+ ft above flame
- Large bark sheet works as roof
- Lean rock slab to windward side
- Protect ignition point most

## Rain-Resistant Fire

- Dig shallow pit to shield wind
- Build thick coal base first
- Hardwood coals last under rain
- Small hot fire beats large cold one
- Use star lay to push dry ends in
- Keep reserve dry tinder covered

## Protecting Coals

- Layer hardwood coals 2-3 inches
- Cover briefly with ash if raining
- Ash insulates from water splash
- Lift ash layer to blow back alive
- Never let coal bed flood with water
- Fan coals between rain bursts

## What Can Go Wrong

- Tarp too low causes fire spread
- Steam from wet wood smothers fire
- Ash turns to paste in heavy rain
- Running out of dry reserve tinder

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Keep fire alive through
  10 min of simulated rain
- Sign of mastery: Relight from
  coals with 2 or fewer attempts
