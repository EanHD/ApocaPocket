# Wickiup Dome Shelter

## Why a Wickiup
- Warmer than a lean-to (fully enclosed)
- Faster than a log cabin (2-4 hours)
- No tools required to build
- Sleeps 4-6 people, safe for fire inside
- Lasts 6-12 months with minimal upkeep
- Apache and Paiute used as primary homes

## How It Works
- Dome = strongest shape, sheds wind/snow
- Bent saplings flex in wind (do not break)
- Multiple covering layers trap air = warmth
- Central fire heats entire space evenly
- Smoke hole at apex vents combustion gas

## Materials
- Frame: 20-30 saplings, 8-12 ft long
  (1-2 inch dia, fresh-cut, flexible wood:
  willow, alder, young maple are best)
- Covering: 150-300 sq ft total (bark,
  grass bundles, cattails, pine boughs)
- Lashing: bark strips, paracord, rope

## Step 1 — Frame the Dome
- Mark a circle 8-12 feet across on ground
- Plant 20-30 saplings around perimeter,
  12-18 inches apart, 6-12 inches deep
- Bend opposite pairs inward and tie
  at apex (4-5 feet high)
- Weave 3-5 horizontal hoops for rigidity
- Leave door opening on southeast side

## Step 2 — Cover It
- Start at ground level, work upward
- Bark: white side out, overlap 6+ inches
- Grass bundles: overlap each row by 2/3
- Minimum 3-4 layers total (more = warmer)
- Lash covering to horizontal hoops
- Leave 12-18 inch smoke hole at apex

## Step 3 — Seal and Set Up
- Bank 6-12 inches of soil around base
  (blocks ground-level drafts)
- Interior fire pit: center, ringed with stones
- Sleeping: perimeter, feet toward fire
- Ground insulation: 6-12 inches dry material

## Cold Weather Upgrades
- Double wall: inner dome 2-3 feet inside
  outer (air gap boosts R-value greatly)
- 5-6 covering layers instead of 3-4
- Small door size reduces heat loss
- Adjustable smoke hole cover (close
  when no fire, open when fire is lit)

## Troubleshooting
- Frame collapses: saplings too thin or dry
  — use 1.5-2 inch fresh-cut wood, plant 12in
- Smoke fills interior: enlarge smoke hole
  to 18-24 inches, use only dry wood
- Rain leaks through: add more layers,
  check overlap is correct (shingle style)
- Cold drafts at floor: bank more soil
  at base, extend covering to ground
