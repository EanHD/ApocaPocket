# Solar Still

## Why It Matters

- Extracts moisture from dry soil
- Works in desert with no surface water
- Produces distilled (pure) water
- Can distill contaminated water
- Requires only plastic and a container
- Best in hot sunny conditions

## Materials Needed

- Clear plastic sheet 6x6 ft minimum
- Collection container or cup
- Drinking tube (optional, keeps seal)
- Rocks or dirt to seal edges
- Green vegetation (improves yield)

## Digging and Setup

- Dig cone-shaped pit 3 ft wide
- Dig to 2 ft depth minimum
- Place collection cup at pit center
- Add green vegetation around cup
- Lay plastic over pit, seal all edges
- Weight center down over cup

## Optimizing the Still

- Deeper pit in moist ground is better
- Add urine or contaminated water
- Juicy cacti flesh boosts yield
- Orient to maximize sun exposure
- Replace vegetation daily if possible
- Use drinking tube to avoid breaking seal

## What Can Go Wrong

- Plastic tears or lifts in wind
- Soil too dry for meaningful yield
- Poor seal lets condensation escape
- Collection cup tips over inside pit

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Working still producing
  visible condensation in 2 hrs
- Sign of mastery: 500 ml by end
  of a full sunny day
