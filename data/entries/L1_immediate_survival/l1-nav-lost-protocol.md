# Lost Protocol

## Why It Matters

- Panic is the leading cause of death
- First decisions are usually worst
- STOP buys time for good thinking
- Most people are found within 3 miles
- Stay vs move decision is critical
- Rescuers search last known point

## STOP Method

- S: Stop moving immediately
- T: Think - what do you know?
- O: Observe - what can you see?
- P: Plan - what is your best action?
- STOP as soon as doubt appears
- Do not wait until fully lost

## Stay vs Move Decision

- Stay if: others know your plan
- Stay if: last known point is near
- Stay if: no clear navigation fix
- Move if: weather or water forces it
- Move if: zero search expected
- Move if: known direction to safety

## If You Stay

- Stay at last known location
- Make shelter before dark
- Create visible signals (3 fires)
- Blow whistle 3 times every 30 min
- Conserve phone for emergency only
- Document situation in writing

## If You Move

- Move toward civilization indicators
- Downstream toward lower ground
- Move during daylight only
- Leave trail markers as you go
- Note start point before moving
- Aim for high ground to see out

## What Can Go Wrong

- Moving in circles due to panic
- Waiting too long before STOP
- Leaving last known point with no plan
- Splitting group in confusion

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Run full STOP drill in under
  5 minutes when deliberately lost
- Sign of mastery: Correct stay/move
  decision in 3 of 3 scenarios
