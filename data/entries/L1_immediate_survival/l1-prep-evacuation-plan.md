# Evacuation Planning

## Route Planning
- Primary route: fastest to safety
- Secondary route: different direction
- Tertiary route: on foot if needed
- All routes: walk them annually
- Note fuel stops along each route

## Rally Points
- Near-home rally: 200 ft from home
- Neighborhood rally: 2 blocks away
- Out-of-area rally: 50+ miles away
- Each person knows all three points
- Rally point timing: 30 minutes max wait

## Trigger Points
- Define when you leave without waiting
- Wildfire: smoke visible from home
- Flood: water within 1 mile and rising
- Hurricane: Zone A = mandatory go
- Earthquake: structural damage = go

## Vehicle Prep
- Always above half tank in fire season
- Emergency kit in vehicle always
- Know alternate road exits from town
- Paper map: do not rely on GPS alone
- Fuel cans if rural or remote area

## Pets and Livestock
- Carriers and leashes ready to grab
- Vaccination records in go-bag
- Pre-arranged pet-friendly shelter
- Large animals: arrange trailer early
- Do not leave pets: plan ahead

## Signals
- Predetermined signal at home door
- Chalk mark or flag: I left safely
- Note inside mailbox with destination
- Text to out-of-area contact first
- Social media check-in: low priority
