# Long-Term Fire Strategy

## Fuel Assessment
- Identify dead standing wood first
- Hardwood: longer burn, better coals
- Softwood: fast heat, less coal
- Plan 3-day supply in advance
- Wet wood: dry near fire 24 hours

## Coal Banking
- Rake coals to ash-insulated pile
- Cover with 2 in layer of dry ash
- Top with thin layer of dry soil
- Preserved 8-12 hours or more
- Always keep one good coal reserve

## Fuel Rotation System
- Collect fuel in tiers: fine/medium/large
- Restock smallest tier most frequently
- Carry coal in clay-lined container
- Maintain fire crew duty rotation
- Never let fire completely die

## Fire Location Planning
- Downwind from camp: smoke control
- Near fuel source: reduce carry work
- Near water: safety and cooking needs
- Pit fire: reduces visibility and sparks
- Reflector wall: doubles heat to camp

## Fire for Signaling
- Maintain green bough pile for signal
- Three fires in triangle: distress sign
- Smoke column visible for 10+ miles
- Oil-soaked rags: black smoke signal
- Night fire: visible 20+ miles

## Fire Safety Long-Term
- Clear 10 ft radius of flammable debris
- Never sleep with fire burning in shelter
- Ember watch: assign before sleep
- Extinguish fully before long absence
- Rain: build ember shelter in advance
