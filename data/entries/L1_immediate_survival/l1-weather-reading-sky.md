# Reading Sky/Weather

## Why It Matters

- Weather changes can be lethal
- 6-hour forecast saves shelter time
- Clouds tell a consistent story
- Wind shifts precede major changes
- Mountain weather especially deadly
- Pattern recognition saves lives

## Rain Clouds

- Cirrus (wispy high streaks): 12-24 hr
- Cirrostratus (halo around sun/moon):
  rain within 12-18 hours
- Altocumulus (rippled mid-level):
  thunderstorm risk within 12 hours
- Nimbostratus (dark low gray blanket):
  rain is already imminent
- Cumulonimbus (towering anvil top):
  severe storm now or very soon

## Fair Weather Clouds

- Cumulus flat base not growing: stable
- Cirrus without increasing cover: fine
- Stratus burning off by midmorning:
  warm dry day likely
- Scattered puffy clouds no growth:
  no significant change expected

## Wind Indicators

- Wind backing (CCW shift) = storm coming
- Wind veering (CW shift) = clearing
- Sustained wind increase = change
- Calms before storms are real
- Two-direction conflict = instability
- Wind from south often brings moisture

## No-Tool Pressure Signs

- Earache or joint pain increasing = low
- Camp smoke rises straight = high
- Campfire embers drift more = low
- Animals sheltering = pressure drop
- Spiders retreating = storm soon
- Sudden silence of birds = severe wx

## What Can Go Wrong

- Single sign misread without context
- Mountains amplify all predictions
- Fog confused with stable weather
- Sea breeze masks ocean storm approach

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Predict next 6 hrs weather
  from cloud observation alone
- Sign of mastery: 80% accuracy
  over 7 consecutive days observed
