# Drought Survival

## Water Conservation
- 1 gallon per day: minimum survival
- Fix all leaks immediately
- Reuse gray water for toilet flushing
- No lawn watering in drought stage 3+
- Collect all cooking and rinse water

## Alternate Water
- Rainwater catchment: install gutters
- Neighbor wells: map alternatives
- Municipal water: connect even if costly
- Water delivery: arrange early
- Hauled water: food-grade containers only

## Well Management
- Monitor well water level daily
- Reduce pump draw rate if level drops
- Deeper well drilling: last resort
- Hand pump: install before crisis
- Bacteria risk rises in low wells: test

## Food Adjustments
- Shift to low-water crops: beans, grains
- Dehydrated and canned food: priority
- Reduce food that requires water to cook
- Drought-resistant seeds: store ahead
- Local food sources may also fail: plan

## Fire Risk Increase
- Drought creates extreme fire conditions
- Clear brush from around structures
- Keep propane and fuel stored safely
- Water pressure may drop: prepare hoses
- Evacuation route: plan two options

## Community Response
- Share well and cistern resources
- Coordinate water delivery schedules
- Alert authorities to failing systems
- Pool resources for large containers
- Monitor county drought stage updates
