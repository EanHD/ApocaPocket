# Carrying Live Coals

## Why It Matters

- Relighting in wet conditions is hard
- Carrying fire saves ignition attempts
- Indigenous method used globally
- Horse hoof fungus burns for hours
- Coal bundle needs no tending in bag
- Allows fire camp-to-camp relocation

## Best Bundle Materials

- Horse hoof fungus (Fomes fomentarius)
- Bracket fungi inner dry layer
- Dry punky rotten wood (amadou)
- Dried cattail fluff packed tight
- Tightly packed dry leaf bundle
- Bark tube from birch or cedar

## Building the Bundle

- Collect large coal or ember
- Nest coal in dry tinder bundle
- Pack loosely so air can circulate
- Wrap in green leaves to slow burn
- Tie with green cordage or grass
- Leave small opening for airflow

## Safe Carrying Method

- Hold bundle away from body
- Gently blow every 10-15 min
- Do not seal in airtight container
- Carry in hand or hang from pack
- Check for bundle overheating
- Keep away from dry material loads

## What Can Go Wrong

- Bundle burns through too fast
- Coal dies from lack of oxygen
- Bundle catches carried gear on fire
- Fungus too dry and burns to ash

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Transport live coal 1 mile
  without losing or starting fire
- Sign of mastery: Bundle stays
  viable for 2+ hours of travel
