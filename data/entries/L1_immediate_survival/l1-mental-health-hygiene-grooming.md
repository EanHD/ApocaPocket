## Hair Care

**Washing without shampoo:**
- Lye soap: wood ash + water + animal fat, boiled = soap
- Clay (rhassoul or bentonite): absorbs oils, ancient method
- Egg yolk: proteins clean and condition, rinse with cool water
- Frequency: every 3-7 days, adjust for activity level

**Combing:**
- Carved wood or bone comb (wide teeth for detangling, fine for grooming)
- Start at ends, work to roots
- Daily combing distributes oils, stimulates scalp, detects lice early

**Trimming:** Keep hair short or tied back for work. Less maintenance, cooler, less parasite habitat.

## Dental Care

Toothache is debilitating. Dental abscess can kill. No dentists means prevention is the only option.

**Chew sticks (5,000-year track record):**
1. Cut green twig (willow, oak, or walnut), 15-20 cm, pencil-width
2. Chew one end until fibers separate into a brush
3. Scrub teeth with gentle motion
4. Discard after 2-3 days

**Toothpaste alternatives:**
- Baking soda: mild abrasive, neutralizes acids, best option if available
- Salt: abrasive, antimicrobial, mix with water to paste
- Charcoal powder: abrasive whitening, do not overuse

**Salt water rinse:** dissolve salt in warm water, swish 30-60 seconds after meals.

**Prevent disaster:**
- Limit sugar, eat fibrous foods, get calcium (bones, greens)
- Never use teeth as tools, never chew hard objects
- Clove oil for toothache (natural anesthetic)
- Dental abscess requires antibiotics. Treat as urgent.

## Bathing

**When water is scarce, priority order:**
1. Drinking and cooking: never compromise
2. Handwashing: disease prevention, critical
3. Face, armpits, groin: the PTA minimum
4. Full body: when water allows

**Minimal water bath:**
1. Heat 1-2 liters of water
2. Wet cloth, wring out
3. Soap and wipe face, underarms, groin, feet
4. Rinse with clean wet cloth
5. Dry

**Frequency:** PTA areas daily. Full body weekly when possible.

**Solar shower (build it):**
- Black container (5-10 gallons) suspended from tree or post
- Attach hose or spout with valve
- Sun heats water to 86-104 F in 3-4 hours
- Add tarp enclosure for privacy

**Sauna:** deep clean, stress relief, immune boost, social bonding. Weekly minimum. See sauna construction in related entries.

## Shaving

**Tools:** straight razor (best, lasts lifetime with care), or a sharp knife.

**Technique:**
1. Wet face with warm water
2. Lather with lye soap
3. Shave with grain on first pass
4. Rinse with cold water to close pores
5. Apply oil or balm to prevent razor burn

**Strop razor on leather** to maintain edge between uses.

## Cloth Care

**Washing:**
1. Heat water, add soap
2. Submerge clothes, agitate, beat on rocks or stir
3. Soak 15-30 minutes for heavy soil
4. Rinse thoroughly (soap residue causes skin irritation)
5. Wring out and hang to dry

**Frequency:** undergarments every 1-3 days, outer clothes weekly, bedding monthly.

**Mending:** torn clothes lose insulation. Patch immediately with needle, sinew, and running stitch.

## Hygiene and Depression

**Watch for these signs in yourself and others:**
- Person who was clean suddenly stops bathing
- Strong body odor, unkempt hair, clothes not mended
- Avoiding others due to shame about appearance

## Community Standards

- Communal bathing area and soap supply reduce the barrier for everyone
- Morning routine: wash face, comb hair, dress in clean clothes
- Weekly: full bath, laundry day, hair washing
- Group laundry = work party + social time
