# Urban Flood Survival

## Immediate Threats
- Moving water: 6 in knocks adult down
- 12 in water moves small vehicle
- 2 ft water floats most vehicles
- Electrical hazards in standing water
- Sewage contamination in all floods
- Submerged manholes: fatal hazard

## Vehicle in Water
- Unbuckle seatbelt immediately
- Open door or window to escape
- Window jammed: wait for pressure
- If locked: use headrest post as tool
- Escape before water reaches door top
- Swim toward current on your back

## Electrical Hazards
- Do not enter flooded basement
- Downed power lines: stay 30 ft away
- Turn off main breaker if flood enters
- Never operate electrical near water

## Sewage Contamination
- Assume all floodwater has sewage
- No skin exposure without protection
- Wash hands constantly after contact
- Do not eat food touched by water
- Wounds: clean immediately

## Shelter and Stay
- Move to second floor or higher
- Signal from window or roof
- Do not walk in rushing water
- Ration food and water while waiting
- Mark location for rescuers

## Post-Flood Safety
- Do not return until declared safe
- Wear rubber boots in cleanup
- Disinfect everything flood touched
- Document damage for insurance
- Test well water before drinking
