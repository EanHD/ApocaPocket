# Heat Wave Survival

## Early Heat Illness
- Heat cramps: muscle pain with sweat
- Heat exhaustion: pale, sweating, weak
- Heat stroke: red, dry, no sweating
- Confusion is a red flag emergency
- High body temp: above 103F dangerous

## Cooling Strategy
- Air conditioning: seek it early
- Cooling centers: libraries, malls, shelters
- Wet cloth on neck, armpits, wrists
- Fan + mist: effective cooling method
- Cold bath or shower: most effective

## Night Routine
- Open windows after sunset
- Close before sun hits facade at dawn
- Hang wet sheets in open windows
- Sleep on lowest floor of home
- Wet bandana on head while sleeping

## Hydration
- Drink before you feel thirsty
- 32 oz per hour in high heat activity
- Avoid alcohol: dehydrates fast
- Avoid caffeine in extreme heat
- Sports drink: replace electrolytes

## Vulnerable People
- Check elderly every 2-4 hours
- Children overheat faster than adults
- Pets: paw pads burn on hot pavement
- Infants: never leave in car
- Outdoor workers: rest in shade hourly

## Shelter-in-Place
- Keep blinds closed on sunny side
- Curtains block 20-30% of heat gain
- Cook outside or eat no-cook foods
- Minimize appliance use: adds heat
- Ground floor is coolest in home
