# Natural Calendar

## Spring Indicators
- Sap rising: woodpecker activity peaks
- Skunk cabbage flowers: very early spring
- Ramp and fiddlehead emergence
- Frog song begins at ponds
- Migratory birds: swallows and warblers
- Last frost: lilac bloom is reliable sign

## Summer Peak Signs
- Summer solstice: longest shadow at noon
- Milkweed flowers: June-July indicator
- Black bear activity peaks: berry season
- Thunderstorm frequency peaks
- Day length begins shortening after June 21

## Fall Indicators
- Goldenrod bloom: reliable fall marker
- Acorn drop: squirrel cache activity
- Migratory birds moving south
- Night temps drop below 50F: frost soon
- Deciduous leaves changing color

## Winter Depth Signs
- Day length shortest at winter solstice
- Chickadee: stays year-round, cold sign
- Ice thickness: measurable by probing
- Animal track patterns: hunt timing
- Lichen and bark: emergency food sign

## Moon-Based Timing
- Full moon: 29.5-day reliable cycle
- Mark lunar months for time tracking
- First frost: 6 weeks after first fall moon
- Planting by moon: root crops dark phase
- Tide prediction if near coast

## Plant Phenology
- Same-species bloom within 3 days yearly
- Dandelion first bloom: safe to plant
- Cottonwood release: late spring marker
- Mushroom flush after first fall rain
- Berry set timing: consistent by region
