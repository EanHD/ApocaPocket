# Coil Pottery

## Overview

- Prepare clay: remove grit and add temper
- Roll coils; stack and blend for wall building
- Smooth inside and outside surfaces
- Dry slowly; fire in pit or kiln

## Clay Preparation

- Remove rocks and roots from raw clay
- Wedge (knead) to remove air bubbles
- Add temper: sand, grit, or ground shell
- Temper ratio: 20-30% prevents cracking when fired

## Building Coils

- Roll clay into coils 1-2cm diameter
- Keep consistent diameter throughout coil
- Work on flat smooth base (leaf or cloth)
- Coil diameter determines wall thickness

## Stacking and Blending

- Lay first coil in circle; press firmly to base
- Stack second coil on top of first
- Blend inside joint by pressing downward
- Smooth outside with damp fingers or tool

## Shaping the Form

- Support outside with curved clay paddle
- Paddle and anvil technique for thinning
- Curve inward: tilt coils inward when stacking
- Curve outward: angle coils outward slightly

## Drying and Pre-fire

- Allow to dry slowly in shade for 2-7 days
- Do not rush with heat; cracks will form
- Pre-heat in warm area before firing
- Dry until no cool patches remain on surface
