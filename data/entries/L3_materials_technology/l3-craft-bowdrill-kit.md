# Bow Drill Kit Making

## Overview

- Four components: hearth, spindle, handhold, bow
- Hearth and spindle must be matched hardness
- Friction produces heat; heat produces coal
- Kit fully dry; any moisture prevents coal

## Best Wood Combinations

- Willow spindle on willow hearth: excellent
- Cottonwood on cottonwood: very reliable
- Elm spindle on elm hearth: good
- Cedar on cedar: classic desert kit
- Avoid: pine, oak (too hard), wet wood

## Spindle Dimensions

- Length: 40-50cm; diameter: 12-18mm
- Straight-grained; no knots in working length
- Top end: slightly rounded for handhold
- Bottom end: short taper; matches socket size

## Hearth Board

- Width: 5-7cm; thickness: 12-15mm
- Drill socket: diameter matches spindle tip
- Notch: V-cut to edge of socket to collect dust
- Notch size: 1/8 of socket diameter; not wider

## Handhold

- Dense smooth hardwood or stone socket
- Small depression to accept spindle top
- Must not create friction; stays cool
- Lubricate with fat or ear wax to reduce heat

## Bow and Cordage

- Bow: curved branch 50-60cm; slight flex
- String: paracord or twisted rawhide works
- Single loose loop around spindle
- Loose enough to slip when spindle stops
