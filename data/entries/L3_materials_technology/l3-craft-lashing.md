# Lashing Techniques

## Overview

- Lashing joins poles or sticks without hardware
- Different lashes for different joint orientations
- Tighten with frapping turns between poles
- Wet cordage can be re-tensioned when dry

## Square Lashing

- Start clove hitch on vertical pole
- Wrap around both poles alternating direction
- Three complete cycles around both poles
- Frap (wrap between poles) two full turns
- Finish with clove hitch on second pole

## Diagonal Lashing

- Start with timber hitch on crossing point
- Wrap three turns on each diagonal
- Frap between poles both directions
- Clove hitch to finish; pulls joint tight

## Shear Lashing

- Bind two poles side-by-side; loose wrap
- Three full turns around both; frap between
- Open poles apart for A-frame structure
- Spread angle adjustable before final tightening

## Tripod Lashing

- Lay three poles parallel; wrap loosely
- Six-eight passes around all three poles
- Frap between each adjacent pole pair
- Open and splay poles outward into tripod

## Tension and Care

- Pull cordage tight at each wrap pass
- Frapping turns are key for final stiffness
- Natural cordage: re-tension after first rain
- Inspect lashings weekly in use structures
