# Generator Maintenance

## Oil Change Interval

Break-in: change after first 5-8 hours of use. Then every 50-100 hours or annually, whichever is first.
Use SAE 10W-30 unless manual specifies otherwise. Typical capacity: 0.6 qt.
Check level before every use. Overfill seizes engine. Low oil triggers auto-shutoff on most units.

## Air Filter

Foam filter: wash in soapy water, rinse, dry fully, lightly oil with motor oil, reinstall.
Paper filter: tap gently to remove dust. Replace if torn, wet, or heavily clogged.
Inspect every 25 hours. Replace foam annually, paper every 50-100 hours.
Clogged filter: hard start, rough running, black exhaust smoke.

## Fuel Stabilizer

Add stabilizer if storing more than 30 days. Stale fuel is the number one failure cause.
Dose: 1 oz per 2.5 gallons. Run 10 minutes after adding so stabilizer reaches carburetor.
With stabilizer: safe storage up to 12-18 months.
Without stabilizer: drain tank and carburetor completely for storage over 60 days.

## Spark Plug Check

Remove with correct socket (typically 5/8 or 3/4 inch). Inspect color:
- Tan or gray: normal.
- Black and sooty: rich mixture or fouled. Clean or replace.
- White or blistered: lean or overheating. Fix cause before running again.

Gap: 0.028-0.030 inch. Use feeler gauge. Replace if electrode worn or ceramic cracked.
Replace every 100 hours or annually.

## Storage Procedure

1. Run until tank empty, or add stabilizer and run 10 minutes.
2. Change oil while warm.
3. Remove spark plug. Squirt 1 tsp motor oil into cylinder. Pull starter 2-3 times.
4. Reinstall plug. Do not fire engine.
5. Clean exterior. Store in dry location away from fuel sources.
6. Disconnect battery on electric-start units.

## Won't Start

No fuel: check petcock is open, tank is not empty.
Choke: cold start needs full choke. Warm or hot engine needs no choke.
Spark: remove plug, ground against engine, pull cord. Must spark bright blue.
Compression: pull rope should feel strong. Weak resistance = valve or ring problem.
Carb gummed: spray carburetor cleaner into air intake briefly, retry start.
Low oil: check oil sensor light. Many units will not start with low oil level.

Diagnosis order: Fuel > Choke > Spark > Compression > Carburetor.
