# Coiled Basketry

## Overview

- Foundation coil wrapped with binding material
- Each row stitches into the row below
- Builds up walls with continuous coiling
- Traditional style; strong and watertight if tight

## Materials Needed

- Foundation: bundled grass, rush, or pine needles
- Binding: thin split willow, yucca leaf, cordage
- Bone awl or sharpened stick for stitching
- Water to keep materials supple while working

## Starting the Base

- Coil foundation tightly into small circle
- Bind with wrap-and-stitch through center
- Continue spiraling outward from center
- Keep tight; loose center causes floppy base

## Building the Walls

- Angle foundation slightly upward after base
- Each stitch: wrap foundation, pierce prior row
- Maintain consistent spacing between stitches
- Add foundation material continuously as needed

## Common Stitch Types

- Figure-eight: most common; passes through gap
- Lazy squaw: wraps multiple times before stitch
- Mariposa: split prior coil for decorative look
- Any consistent method works; stay uniform

## Finishing

- Taper foundation at end to thin coil finish
- Wrap final coil with extra tight binding
- Trim any protruding ends with sharp knife
- Seal interior with pine pitch for waterproofing
