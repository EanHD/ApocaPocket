# Wool Spinning

## Overview

- Fiber must be drafted (thinned) then twisted
- Twist locks fibers; creates yarn strength
- Drop spindle: weighted disk on stick
- Plying: twist two singles together for strength

## Spindle Construction

- Whorl: flat circular disk of wood or clay
- Spindle: straight hardwood stick 30cm long
- Whorl 8-12cm diameter; 100-200g weight
- Notch at top end to hold yarn loop

## Fiber Preparation

- Wash raw wool; remove lanolin and debris
- Comb or card to align fibers parallel
- Teased wool is ready for spinning directly
- Prepare large amount before starting session

## Drafting Technique

- Attach leader yarn to spindle
- Attach raw fiber to leader end
- Pinch and pull (draft) small section thin
- Allow spindle to twist drafted section

## Spinning Mechanics

- Flick spindle to spin clockwise (Z-twist)
- Draft fiber ahead of pinch zone
- Release pinch; twist travels into fiber
- Wind onto spindle when yarn gets long

## Plying for Strength

- Spin two bobbins of singles yarn
- Ply by twisting two together counter-clockwise
- Plied yarn much stronger than singles
- Wind off plied yarn into ball for storage
