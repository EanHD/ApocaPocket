# Generator Basics

CO is deadly. Never run indoors, in garages, or near open windows. Outdoors only, 20+ feet from structures.

## Generator Types

Portable (1-12 kW):
- Direct gasoline tank, air-cooled, 3,600 RPM.
- Pull-start or electric start. Loud (65-75 dB).
- Best for temporary power via extension cords.

Inverter (1-7 kW):
- Clean power (<3% THD). Safe for sensitive electronics.
- Quiet (48-57 dB), fuel-efficient (variable RPM).
- 1.5-2x cost of conventional portable.

Standby (7-50 kW):
- Permanent mount, automatic transfer switch, utility gas or propane.
- Starts automatically in 10-30 seconds. Professional installation required.

## Load Sizing

Running watts = continuous load. Starting watts = 2-4x running for motors.

Example (home essentials):
- Refrigerator: 700W run, 2,100W start.
- Furnace blower: 800W run, 2,400W start.
- Sump pump: 800W run, 2,400W start.
- LED lights and electronics: 250W run.
- Running total: 2,550W. Highest surge above running: +1,600W.
- Minimum generator: 4,150W. Recommended: 5,000-6,000W.

Target 50-75% of rated capacity during operation. Loses ~15% power at 5,000 ft altitude.

## Fuel

Gasoline:
- Shelf life: 30 days untreated, 12-24 months with stabilizer.
- Add stabilizer immediately. Rotate stock every 6-12 months.
- Approved containers, cool dark location, max 25 gallons residential.
- Consumption: 7,500W at 50% load = ~0.55 gal/hr.

Propane:
- Indefinite shelf life. Cleaner combustion. No gumming in carburetor.
- ~10% less power than gasoline.
- 20 lb tank = ~9 hours at 5 kW, 50% load.
- Below 0 F: low pressure reduces flow. Use larger tank or manifold two tanks.

Diesel:
- 30-50% more fuel-efficient than gasoline. Longer engine life.
- Harder to cold-start. Add biocide for storage over 6 months.

## Transfer Switches

Never backfeed utility lines. Connecting a generator without a transfer switch
energizes lines and can electrocute utility workers. Criminal liability applies.

Manual: flip from UTILITY to GENERATOR during outage.
Automatic (ATS): detects outage, starts generator, transfers load in 10-30 sec.
Interlock kit: mechanical device on main panel. Lowest cost (00-750 installed).

## Safety Rules

Carbon monoxide: 800 PPM = dizziness in 45 min. 1,600 PPM = death in 20 min.
- Exhaust downwind, 20+ feet from all structures and openings.
- Battery-powered CO detectors in all living areas. Test monthly.

Refueling:
- Shut down and cool 5+ minutes before adding fuel.
- No smoking near fuel. Wipe all spills before restarting.

Extension cords:
- 0-50 ft: 12 AWG (20A max). 50-100 ft: 10 AWG.
- Outdoor-rated (SJTW) only. Inspect for damage before each use.

Fire extinguisher: 10 lb ABC within 10 feet. 3-foot clearance from combustibles.

## Maintenance

- Oil change every 50-100 hours or annually.
- Monthly exercise run: 30 minutes under load.
- Spark plugs: annually or per manufacturer schedule.
- Air filter: check every 50 hours, replace annually.
- Run carburetor dry or add stabilizer before storage over 30 days.

## Fuel Planning Example

7,500W generator at 50% load = 0.55 gal/hr.
Run 12 hr/day x 3 days = 36 hours total.
36 x 0.55 = 19.8 gallons. Stage 20+ gallons minimum.
