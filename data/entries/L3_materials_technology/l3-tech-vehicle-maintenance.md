# Vehicle Maint Basics

## Pre-Trip Checklist

Do before any trip over 50 miles or after sitting 1 week.

- Oil: dipstick between MIN and MAX. Black and gritty = overdue change.
- Coolant: reservoir between LOW and FULL. Check cold engine only.
- Brake fluid: reservoir between MIN and MAX lines.
- Tire pressure: check cold. See door-jamb sticker, not tire sidewall. Typical 32-36 PSI.
- Tires: no sidewall cracks or bulges. Penny test: Lincoln head must disappear in tread.
- Battery terminals: no white or blue corrosion. Hold-down secure.
- Belts: no cracks, fraying, glazing. Squealing on startup = inspect now.
- Lights: walk-around check. Brake lights require a helper or wall reflection.

## Fluids and Pressure

Oil change: 3,000 miles conventional oil, 5,000-7,500 miles synthetic.
Coolant flush: every 2 years or 30,000 miles.
Tire rotation: every 5,000-7,500 miles.
Target pressure is on the door-jamb sticker. Tire sidewall shows maximum, not target.

## Jump-Starting

Order matters. Wrong order risks battery explosion.
Connect: red to dead positive, red to good positive, black to good negative, black to unpainted metal on dead car.
Start good car. Run 2 minutes. Start dead car. No start: wait 5 more minutes, retry.
Disconnect in reverse: black unpainted metal first, then black negative, red positive, red positive.
Do not jump a cracked, swollen, or leaking battery. Replace it first.

## Tire Change

1. Loosen lug nuts before jacking. One-quarter turn each, star pattern.
2. Jack under frame or pinch weld per owner manual. Never under plastic body panels.
3. Remove lugs, pull wheel, mount spare.
4. Hand-tighten lugs in star pattern. Lower car. Torque to 80-100 ft-lbs.
5. Donut spare: 50 MPH max, 50-70 miles max. Check spare pressure monthly (60 PSI).

## Belt Warning Signs

Serpentine belt: squealing at startup, AC or power-steering or battery warning light, overheating. Replace immediately.
Timing belt: interval 60,000-100,000 miles. Check owner manual. Failure destroys engine.
Replace if: cracking, fraying, glazed surface, or chunks missing.

## Emergency Kit

- Jumper cables or jump pack (800+ CCA)
- Tire inflator (12V compressor)
- Spare tire, jack, lug wrench
- Flashlight with extra batteries
- Screwdrivers, pliers, zip ties, tape
- Motor oil (1 qt, correct grade)
- Coolant (1 qt, pre-mixed)
- First aid kit
- Water (1 gallon)
- Rain poncho and gloves
- Warning triangles or road flares
- Phone charger (12V adapter)
