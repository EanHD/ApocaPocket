# Gourd Vessels

## Overview

- Hard-shelled gourds (Lagenaria) are best
- Curing hardens the shell for durable use
- Cut to shape after curing; clean inside
- Lightweight watertight natural container

## Curing Process

- Harvest when stem is dry and brown
- Hang in dry airy location for 3-6 months
- Shake periodically; contents dry inside
- Cured when very light and hard when knocked

## Cutting and Shaping

- Cut with saw or sharp knife when fully cured
- Bowl: cut across widest diameter
- Bottle: cut small opening at narrow neck
- Spoon: cut handle section from curved area

## Cleaning the Interior

- Shake out dried seeds and pulp
- Rinse with water; scrub with sand and gravel
- Re-rinse until water runs clear
- Dry completely before use

## Waterproofing

- Rub interior with tallow or beeswax
- Pine pitch seals any hairline cracks
- Oil with linseed or other drying oil
- Reapply seasoning after prolonged water use

## Uses by Shape

- Round bottom: water storage and fermentation
- Flat bottom: bowl, eating vessel, plate
- Long neck: scoop and ladle for liquids
- Small gourd: carrying container for medicines
