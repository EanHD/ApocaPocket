# Twined Basketry

## Overview

- Warp: rigid upright stakes forming structure
- Weft: flexible strands woven around warps
- Two weft strands twisted around each warp
- Produces strong, open or closed weave forms

## Materials Needed

- Warp: willow, reed, or split wood stakes
- Weft: thin willow withies, split root, cordage
- Sharpening knife to taper stake ends
- Water for keeping all material supple

## Setting Up the Base

- Cross 6-8 stakes; pair and separate 3 and 3
- Tie crossing with two weft strands
- Twine around pairs outward to lock cross
- Splay stakes evenly; maintain even spacing

## Twining Technique

- Hold two weft strands at work point
- Pass front strand behind next stake
- Bring rear strand to front position
- Continue; strands twist as they cross

## Building Walls

- Bend stakes upward when base is right size
- Tie stakes to hoop to hold upright form
- Continue twining rows up walls
- Pack rows tight for watertight weave

## Shapes and Forms

- Open weave: wide spacing between rows
- Closed weave: rows packed tightly together
- Round base: most common and practical
- Rectangular: easier to store and stack
