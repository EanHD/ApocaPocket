# Pinch Pot Method

## Overview

- Simplest pottery technique; minimal tools
- One ball of clay forms base and walls
- Good for small cups, bowls, and lamp bases
- Best first pottery project for beginners

## Clay Preparation

- Wedge clay to remove air pockets
- Add 20-25% sand or grit temper to clay
- Ball should be tennis-ball size for small bowl
- Keep clay covered; do not let it dry out

## Pinching Technique

- Press thumb into center of clay ball
- Keep bottom thick (5-8mm); walls 5-6mm
- Rotate in hand; pinch walls between thumb
  and fingers to raise and thin
- Work in spiral upward from base

## Achieving Even Walls

- Check thickness constantly with fingertips
- Thin spots: add small pieces and blend
- Thick spots: pinch and thin evenly
- Consistent walls prevent cracking in fire

## Surface Smoothing

- Use damp finger to smooth inside and out
- Scrape with flat stone or shell when stiff
- Burnish (polish with smooth pebble) when dry
- Burnished surface smooths and seals slightly

## Drying Before Firing

- Allow to dry completely in shade (2-5 days)
- Leather-hard stage: shape feet or add handles
- Bone-dry stage: ready for fire
- Test: no cool spot when held to cheek
