# Pit Kiln Firing

## Overview

- Pit firing reaches 800-1000C with hardwood
- Faster fire = better temperature reached
- Blackware results from smoky reducing fire
- Simple and effective; no kiln structure needed

## Preparation

- Ensure all pottery is bone-dry (no cool spots)
- Pre-heat pots near fire for 1-2 hours first
- Dig pit 30-40cm deep; 60cm wide
- Line base with dry hardwood fuel

## Loading the Pit

- Place pre-heated pottery in center of fuel
- Pack hardwood around and above pieces
- Separate pots with pot shards or stones
- Larger pieces on bottom; small on top

## Firing Process

- Light fuel at base; maintain feeding top
- Build to maximum heat in first 30 minutes
- Maintain temperature for 45-60 minutes
- Avoid cold water or rain during firing

## Cooling

- Allow pit to cool naturally; minimum 2 hours
- Remove pots when cool enough to touch
- Check for cracks; tap gently (clear ring=good)
- Cloudy thud = crack inside; handle carefully

## Blackware Technique

- Cover pit with organic material while hot
- Leaves, grass, sawdust create reducing smoke
- Carbon deposits produce black color overall
- Purely decorative; not stronger than standard
