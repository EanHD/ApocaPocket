# Rope Making

## Overview

- Rope = multiple strands twisted together
- Each strand twisted opposite to overall rope
- Twist locks strands under load (self-tightening)
- Three-strand most common and practical

## Materials Needed

- Prepared plant fiber cordage (three bundles)
- Post or stake to anchor work end
- Smooth stick as ply-twisting tool (optional)
- Water to keep fibers supple while working

## Three-Strand Twist

- Anchor three cordage bundles to fixed point
- Hold all three; twist two together clockwise
- Wrap the twisted pair counter-clockwise
  around the third strand
- Repeat: twist right pair CW, wrap CCW

## Even Tension

- Keep tension on anchor end while working
- Work strands should all be equal length
- Add new fiber by overlap splice (15cm overlap)
- Taper splice zone for smooth join

## Diameter and Strength

- 6mm rope: carry 25-30 kg safely
- 10mm rope: carry 60-80 kg safely
- 15mm rope: carry 120-150 kg safely
- Triple minimum for body-weight life safety

## Finishing

- Whip rope ends with fine cordage to prevent fray
- Or melt synthetic ends; tie overhand to secure
- Coil and store dry; rot sets in when stored wet
- Test each batch before trusting critical loads
