# Eye Injury Field Care

## Why It Matters

- Eye injuries cause permanent damage
- Delay in treatment worsens outcome
- Field care is often highly effective
- Wrong treatment causes more damage
- Chemical exposure = flush first, ask later
- Shield properly to prevent further harm

## Foreign Body in Eye

- Do not rub the eye
- Irrigate with clean water, inside corner
- Upper lid: pull over lower lash
- Lower lid: pull down, look for particle
- Cotton swab can lift loose particle
- Flush thoroughly if found or unfound
- Patch if cornea is scratched

## Corneal Abrasion

- Pain and light sensitivity
- Tearing and blinking spasm
- Blurred vision in affected eye
- Feels like something still in eye
- Treatment: patch, rest, low light
- Antibiotic eye drops if available

## Chemical Eye Exposure

- Flush immediately, do not wait
- 20 minutes minimum irrigation
- Use any clean water available
- Hold eye open during irrigation
- Flush from inner corner outward
- Acid and alkali both need 20 min flush
- Alkali burns penetrate deeper

## Blunt Eye Trauma

- Apply cold compress over closed lid
- No pressure on eyeball
- Check vision in both eyes
- Hyphema (blood in iris): eye rest
- Limit eye movement in severe blunt
- Do not patch tightly over trauma

## What Can Go Wrong

- Rubbing foreign body deeper
- Insufficient flush for chemicals
- Pressure on globe during treatment
- Penetrating object removed in field

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Correct eye irrigation
  technique for 20 minutes
- Sign of mastery: Identify 4 eye
  injuries and give correct first action
