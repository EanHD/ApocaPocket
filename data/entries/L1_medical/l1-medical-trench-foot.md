# Trench Foot Prevention

## Why It Matters

- Wet cold above freezing causes injury
- Trench foot occurred at 50F in rain
- No frost needed for serious damage
- WWI and modern cases are identical
- Prevention requires only dry socks
- Permanent nerve damage in 48 hours

## Risk Conditions

- Feet wet more than 12 hours
- Temperature below 60F / 15C
- Movement restricted (sleep, in hole)
- No dry sock change available
- Rain, rivers, flooded terrain
- Sweaty boots trapped against feet

## Signs and Stages

- Early: numbness, pale, prickly feeling
- Intermediate: red or blue skin
  swelling begins, walking difficult
- Late: blisters, tissue breakdown
  severe pain on rewarming
- Gangrene in untreated severe cases

## Prevention Protocol

- Change to dry socks 1-2x per day
- Carry minimum 3 pairs of socks
- Wring out wet socks, wear inside
  shirt against skin to dry overnight
- Remove boots to sleep when possible
- Inspect feet daily in wet conditions

## Treatment Protocol

- Get out of wet environment first
- Remove footwear and dry feet gently
- Air dry completely before re-covering
- Rewarm slowly at room temperature
- Do not rub or massage
- Elevate feet, reduce walking
- Ibuprofen for pain and inflammation

## What Can Go Wrong

- No sock changes for multiple days
- Rubbing feet during rewarming
- Continuing to walk on affected feet
- Mistaking for normal cold foot

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Daily foot inspection
  protocol from memory
- Sign of mastery: Zero trench foot
  signs after 3-day wet conditions
