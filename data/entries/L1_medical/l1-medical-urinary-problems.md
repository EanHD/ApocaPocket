# Urinary Problems

## Why It Matters

- UTIs are common in field conditions
- Kidney stones cause severe pain
- Both worsen without treatment
- UTI can ascend to kidney infection
- Dehydration worsens both conditions
- Early treatment prevents evacuation

## UTI Signs

- Burning or pain on urination
- Urgency and frequency of urination
- Cloudy, dark, or foul-smelling urine
- Lower abdominal pressure or pain
- No fever in uncomplicated UTI
- More common in women

## UTI Field Treatment

- Increase fluids immediately: 3+ L/day
- Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole if available:
  1 double-strength tablet BID x 3 days
  or ciprofloxacin 500 mg BID x 3 days
- Ibuprofen for pain
- Cranberry concentrate may help mildly
- Monitor for fever - sign of kidney spread
- Evacuate if fever develops

## Kidney Stones

- Sudden severe flank or back pain
- Pain radiates to groin or testicle
- Colicky: comes in waves
- Nausea, vomiting with pain
- Blood in urine possible
- Pain may be worst of your life

## Kidney Stone Care

- Ibuprofen 600 mg for pain (best)
- Drink large volumes of fluid
- Alpha-blockers (tamsulosin) help passage
- Movement may help stone move
- Fever with stone = pyelonephritis
- Evacuate if fever or obstruction

## What Can Go Wrong

- UTI ascending to kidney unnoticed
- Dehydration worsening stone
- Painkiller masking worsening signs
- Stone causing complete obstruction

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Correctly stage UTI vs
  kidney stone from symptoms
- Sign of mastery: Correct antibiotic
  choice and dose from memory
