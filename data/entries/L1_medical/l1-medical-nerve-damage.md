# Nerve Damage Signs

## Why It Matters

- Nerve damage changes fracture treatment
- Splints can cause nerve compression
- Tourniquets damage nerves over time
- Spinal injury presents as nerve signs
- Early detection prevents permanent loss
- CSM check is a core field skill

## CSM Check

- Circulation: pulse distal to injury?
- Sensation: can they feel touch there?
- Motion: can they move fingers or toes?
- Perform before and after splinting
- Recheck every 30 minutes
- Change in any = adjust treatment

## Peripheral Nerve Signs

- Numbness in specific area = nerve
- Weakness in specific movement = nerve
- Tingling or burning = nerve pressure
- Dropped wrist after humeral fracture
- Foot drop after fibula fracture
- These are diagnostic patterns

## Compartment Syndrome

- Pain out of proportion to injury
- Tense, hard compartment (forearm, calf)
- Worsening pain with passive stretch
- Numbness, weakness, pale limb
- Medical emergency: needs surgery
- Evacuate immediately, do not wait

## Spinal Cord Signs

- Numbness below injury level
- Weakness or paralysis below injury
- Loss of bladder or bowel control
- Priapism in males after injury
- Hypotension with bradycardia
  (neurogenic shock pattern)
- Spinal precautions immediately

## Tourniquet Nerves

- Check sensation below tourniquet
- Numb below within 30-60 min is normal
- Document time of application always
- Loosen at 2 hours if possible safely
- Note any new weakness after removal
- Time-dependent nerve injury risk

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Complete CSM check on
  simulated fracture in 60 seconds
- Sign of mastery: Identify compartment
  syndrome signs in case description
