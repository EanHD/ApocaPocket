# Blister Prevention Care

## Why It Matters

- Blisters end wilderness trips
- Untreated blisters become infected
- Prevention is far better than treatment
- Hot spot detection is a learnable skill
- Early care takes 30 sec, late care hours
- Foot care is a survival skill

## Prevention Protocol

- Break in boots before long trips
- Wear moisture-wicking inner socks
- Add wool or thick sock as outer layer
- Stop at first hint of hot spot
- Address immediately, not at camp
- Lubricants (Body Glide) reduce friction

## Hot Spot Treatment

- Stop when you feel a hot spot
- Clean area and dry thoroughly
- Apply moleskin donut around hot spot
- Donut keeps pressure off center
- Cover with medical tape over all
- Continue if no more discomfort

## To Drain or Not

- Drain if: painful and interfering
- Drain if: over joint that must bend
- Do not drain: blood blister
- Do not drain: small, painless blister
- Do not drain: top is already torn

## Draining Protocol

- Sterilize needle with flame or alcohol
- Pierce base of blister (not top)
- Express fluid gently from top
- Do not remove blister roof
- Apply antibiotic ointment over it
- Cover with non-stick dressing

## What Can Go Wrong

- Hot spot ignored until blister forms
- Roof removed and raw skin exposed
- Blood blister drained and infected
- Dressing bunches and creates new friction

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Apply moleskin donut for
  hot spot in under 2 minutes
- Sign of mastery: Complete foot
  check in under 5 minutes per foot
