# Wound Infection Signs

## Why It Matters

- All field wounds have infection risk
- Early signs are easy to treat
- Late signs can be life-threatening
- Monitoring schedule prevents surprise
- Antibiotics most effective if early
- Missing early signs wastes treatment

## Normal Appearance

- Pink edges for first 24 hours
- Mild warmth is normal
- Clear or slightly yellow fluid ok
- Swelling proportional to injury size
- Tenderness decreasing over 48 hrs
- No fever associated with wound

## Signs at 24 Hours

- Increased redness beyond wound edge
- Warmth spreading beyond wound
- Swelling increasing not decreasing
- Wound edges separating
- Any pus: cloudy, yellow, or green
- Fever: temperature above 101F

## Signs at 48 Hours

- Redness spreading in all directions
- Visible pus with odor
- Wound edges look necrotic or dark
- Fever persisting or increasing
- Increased pain despite rest
- Regional lymph nodes swollen

## Signs at 72 Hours

- Red streak spreading up limb
- High fever over 103F
- Rigors (shaking chills)
- Confusion or altered mental status
- Wound smells foul
- These signs = evacuate immediately

## Treatment Actions

- Mild 24 hr: re-irrigate, new dressing
- Early infection: open wound, irrigate
- Antibiotics if available: amoxicillin
  500 mg TID or doxycycline 100 mg BID
- Do not close infected wounds
- Red streak = evacuate, no field treatment

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Correctly stage 4 wound
  infection photos shown to you
- Sign of mastery: 72-hr check
  protocol done from memory
