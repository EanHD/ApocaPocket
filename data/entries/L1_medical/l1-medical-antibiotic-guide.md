# Antibiotic Guide

## Bacterial vs Viral

- Antibiotics do NOTHING for: cold, flu, COVID, most bronchitis
- Bacterial signs: fever >10 days, pus present, spreading, not improving
- Viral signs: clears in 5-7 days, cough, clear runny nose
- If medical care possible in 24-48h: wait for diagnosis

## When to Use

- Strep throat: pus on tonsils, no cough, fever, tender neck nodes
- Bacterial pneumonia: colored sputum, fever, chest pain, short of breath
- UTI: burning urination, cloudy foul-smelling urine, pelvic pain
- Cellulitis: spreading red warm skin, possible pus, red streaks
- Infected wound: pus, red streaks, increasing pain after injury
- Sinusitis: >10 days no improvement, or sudden worsening after improving
- Animal or human bites: start antibiotics within 8 hours

## First-Line Treatment

- Strep throat: Amoxicillin 500mg 3x/day x10 days
- Pneumonia: Amoxicillin 500mg 3x/day x5-7 days
- UTI (women): TMP-SMX DS 2x/day x3 days
- Skin/cellulitis: Cephalexin 500mg 4x/day x7-10 days
- MRSA/tick illness: Doxycycline 100mg 2x/day x7-10 days
- Animal bite: Augmentin 875/125mg 2x/day x7-10 days
- Traveler's diarrhea: Ciprofloxacin 500mg 2x/day x1-3 days

## Key Antibiotics

- Amoxicillin: strep, ear, pneumonia — safe in pregnancy
- Augmentin: bites, sinusitis, resistant infections
- Cephalexin: skin, UTI — 5-10% cross-react if penicillin allergy
- Doxycycline: MRSA, Lyme, tick disease, atypical pneumonia
- Azithromycin: atypical pneumonia, penicillin allergy backup
- Ciprofloxacin: UTI, diarrhea — last resort
- TMP-SMX (Bactrim): MRSA skin infections, UTI
- Metronidazole: abscess, anaerobic, Giardia — NO alcohol

## Pediatric Dosing

- Amoxicillin: 45-90 mg/kg/day divided 2-3x daily
- Cephalexin: 25-50 mg/kg/day divided 4x daily
- Azithromycin: 10 mg/kg day 1, then 5 mg/kg days 2-5
- TMP-SMX: 8-10 mg/kg/day (TMP component) divided 2x daily
- Doxycycline: age 8+ only, 2-4 mg/kg/day divided twice daily
- Ciprofloxacin: avoid in children unless no alternative

## Contraindications

- Doxycycline: NOT in pregnancy, NOT breastfeeding, NOT under age 8
- Ciprofloxacin: avoid in pregnancy and children (cartilage risk)
- TMP-SMX: avoid with sulfa allergy or first trimester pregnancy
- Metronidazole: no alcohol during treatment + 3 days after
- Severe penicillin allergy (anaphylaxis): avoid cephalosporins
- Penicillin allergy alternatives: azithromycin or doxycycline

## Stop: Danger Signs

- Anaphylaxis: throat swelling, hives, breathing difficulty
- Give epinephrine 0.3mg IM immediately for anaphylaxis
- C. diff: severe watery diarrhea during/after — stop antibiotic
- Tendon pain with ciprofloxacin: stop immediately, rupture risk
- Rash + mouth sores + fever (Stevens-Johnson): stop immediately

## Finish Course / Help

- Complete full course even if feeling better — prevents resistance
- Expect improvement in 48-72 hours
- No improvement in 72h: wrong drug or wrong diagnosis
- Sepsis signs: fever + confusion + rapid breathing + fast pulse
- Get help: sepsis present, infection spreading, no improvement
