# Field Childbirth

## Why It Matters

- Unplanned delivery occurs in field
- Correct support prevents deaths
- Most normal deliveries need minimal
  intervention from assistant
- Wrong intervention causes harm
- Cord complications are life-threatening

## Imminent Delivery

- Contractions every 2-3 minutes
- Urge to push uncontrollable
- Crowning visible at birth canal
- Water already broken
- Mother says it is coming now
- At this point: prepare to assist

## Preparation

- Warm clean environment if possible
- Clean hands, gloves if available
- Clean cloths for infant
- Tie material for cord if needed
- Suction bulb if available
- Do not leave mother alone

## Delivery Support

- Do not pull on infant
- Support head gently as it emerges
- Check for cord around neck immediately
- Cord around neck: loop over head gently
  or clamp and cut if tight
- Let shoulders deliver with next push
- Support body, do not pull

## Newborn Care

- Dry and warm infant immediately
- Stimulate by rubbing back if not crying
- Clear mouth and nose gently
- Place skin-to-skin on mother chest
- Tie cord at 2 locations, cut between
- Watch for breathing within 60 seconds

## Placenta Care

- Placenta delivers in 5-30 minutes
- Do not pull on cord to hurry it
- Inspect placenta is whole
- Massage uterus if heavy bleeding
- Keep both warm, monitor mother
- Evacuate as soon as possible

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: State 6 steps of delivery
  support in correct order
- Sign of mastery: Correctly handle
  cord complication scenario
