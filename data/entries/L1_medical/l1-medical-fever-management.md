# Fever Management

## Why It Matters

- Fever is a sign, not a disease
- Low fever is immune system working
- High fever causes brain damage
- Field cooling is highly effective
- Fever + stiff neck = meningitis
- Fever + wound = infected wound

## Temperature Reference

- Normal: 98.6F / 37C
- Low fever: 99-101F / 37.2-38.3C
- Moderate: 101-103F / 38.3-39.4C
- High fever: 103-105F / 39.4-40.5C
- Danger: above 104F adults, 103F child
- Critical: above 106F is emergency

## When to Treat Fever

- Under 101F: monitor only, fluids
- 101-103F: ibuprofen or acetaminophen
- Over 103F: medications AND cooling
- Any fever with stiff neck: evacuate
- Any fever with rash: evacuate
- Any fever over 104F: active cooling

## Medication Options

- Ibuprofen: 400-600 mg every 6-8 hrs
- Acetaminophen: 650-1000 mg every 6 hrs
- Do not exceed labeled daily limits
- Alternate them for stubborn fever
- No aspirin in children under 18
- Give with food to prevent nausea

## Cooling Methods

- Wet cloth on forehead, neck, armpits
- Cool water sponge bath to skin
- Fan to promote evaporation
- Cool (not cold) water orally
- Shade and ventilation critical
- Do not use alcohol rubs

## What Can Go Wrong

- Fever masked by meds, hides worsening
- Dehydration from fever uncompensated
- Febrile seizure in child unrecognized
- Aspirin given to child

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Estimate temperature using
  touch and behavioral signs
- Sign of mastery: Cooling fever
  from 104F to below 103F in 1 hr
