# Pain Management Field

## Why It Matters

- Uncontrolled pain prevents recovery
- Pain impairs judgment and will
- Field pharmacology is limited
- Combinations work better than one
- Position and immobilization reduce pain
- Herbal options are real last resort

## OTC Medications

- Ibuprofen: 400-600 mg every 6-8 hrs
  Best for: muscle, joint, dental, fracture
  Anti-inflammatory effect is valuable
- Acetaminophen: 650-1000 mg every 6 hrs
  Best for: fever, headache, mild pain
  Combine with ibuprofen for more effect
- Max daily: ibuprofen 2400 mg,
  acetaminophen 3000 mg (field context)

## Rx Medications

- Ibuprofen 800 mg (prescription dose)
  Not more effective than 600 mg alone
- Tramadol: 50-100 mg every 6-8 hrs
  More effective but impairs judgment
- Oxycodone: use only when immobile
  Strong pain relief, strong impairment
- Ketorolac (Toradol): 15-30 mg IM/IV

## Positional Relief

- Fracture: splint in position of comfort
- Shoulder: sling and swathe reduces pain
- Rib injury: semi-reclined reduces pain
- Abdominal: knees to chest reduces pain
- Headache: head elevated 30 degrees
- Eye injury: both eyes patched (resting)

## Herbal Options

- Willow bark tea: salicin (aspirin-like)
- Clove oil on dental pain (topical)
- Cold water or ice on acute injury
- Heat after 48 hrs for muscle pain
- Elevation reduces swelling and pain
- Distraction and task focus help

## What Can Go Wrong

- Pain masked before serious condition known
- Dosing exceeded from combined meds
- Acetaminophen + alcohol liver damage
- Narcotics given before evacuation decision

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Determine correct pain
  regimen for 3 different injuries
- Sign of mastery: Achieve VAS pain
  reduction from 8 to below 5 in field
