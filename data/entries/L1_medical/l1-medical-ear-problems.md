# Ear Problems Field

## Why It Matters

- Ear infections are common in field
- Water immersion accelerates them
- Pain is incapacitating if severe
- Early treatment prevents worsening
- Misidentification leads to wrong care
- Very common in river and paddling trips

## Swimmer's Ear

- Pain on moving the outer ear
- Itching, discharge, reduced hearing
- Caused by water and bacteria in canal
- Outer ear canal red and swollen
- Do not put anything deep in canal
- Prevention: dry ear after immersion

## Swimmer Ear Care

- White vinegar drops (2-3) acidify canal
- Dilute 50/50 white vinegar and water
- Tilt head, hold drops 1-2 minutes
- Repeat 3x daily for 3-5 days
- Keep ear dry during treatment
- Ibuprofen for pain relief

## Middle Ear Infection

- Deep internal ear pain
- Hearing muffled or reduced
- Fever often present
- Moving outer ear causes no pain
- Fullness sensation deep in ear
- Requires antibiotics: amoxicillin

## Ear Wax Impaction

- Gradual hearing reduction
- No pain typically
- Sensation of fullness
- Warm water irrigation if no perforation
- Tilt head, gently flood warm water
- Do not use sharp objects in ear
- Debrox or oil drops soften first

## What Can Go Wrong

- Irrigating perforated eardrum
- Vinegar used full strength
- Sharp object pushed into canal
- Outer vs middle ear confused

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Correctly distinguish
  outer vs middle ear infection
- Sign of mastery: Vinegar drops
  properly applied for swimmer ear
