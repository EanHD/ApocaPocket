# Wheel and Axle

## Principle

- Wheel: large radius turning device
- Axle: small radius shaft through wheel center
- Both rotate together
- Mechanical advantage = wheel radius / axle radius
- Large wheel + small axle = high MA
- Example: 50cm wheel, 5cm axle = MA of 10

## Windlass (Well Crank)

- Horizontal drum (axle) with crank handle
- Crank handle = wheel radius (lever arm)
- Rope winds onto drum as you crank
- MA = handle arm length / drum radius
- Typical windlass MA: 4-8x
- Common use: lifting water from wells

## Capstan

- Vertical rotating drum
- Bars inserted in holes at top
  (like wheel spokes = wheel radius)
- Men walk in circle, pushing bars
- Rope winds on drum, pulling load
- MA = bar length / drum radius
- Powered by multiple people for very heavy loads

## Wheel Construction

- Hub: largest load-bearing component
- Spokes: compression elements
- Felloes: curved sections of rim
- Iron tire: steel band holding rim in tension
- Spoke count: 8-12 for typical wheel
- Mortise-and-tenon: spokes into hub and felloe

## Bearing Friction

- Axle on fixed support: friction is MA enemy
- Wood on wood: high friction, short life
- Wood on greased wood: reduced friction
- Iron bushing in wood hub: better
- Iron on iron (greased): best available
- Grease: animal fat, plant oils

## Applications

- Wheelbarrow wheel
- Cart and wagon wheels
- Water-lifting wheels (chain of pots)
- Steering mechanisms
- Potter's wheel (heavy flywheel)
- Lathe drive wheel

## Project Calculation

- Need to lift 200kg with 50kg effort: MA=4
- Drum radius 10cm: handle arm = 40cm
- Bar from center: 50cm reaches 40cm from drum
- Wheel diameter 100cm on 10cm axle: MA=10
- Adjust dimensions to suit available materials
