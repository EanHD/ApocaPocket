# Screw Press

## Operating Principle

- Screw thread is inclined plane on cylinder
- Each turn of screw: tiny downward movement
  with multiplied force
- MA of typical screw: 50-500:1
- Lever arm on screw head adds more MA
- Combined: very large forces from human power

## Press Force

- MA = (2 x pi x lever length) / thread pitch
- Lever 60cm, pitch 3cm: MA = 125
- Person applies 50kg on lever:
  Press delivers 6,250 kg of force
- Enough for olive oil, cider, cheese, etc.
- More threads per cm = higher MA, slower

## Types of Screw Presses

- Cider press: apples crushed, juice extracted
- Oil press: olives, sunflower, flaxseed
- Cheese press: whey expelled from curd
- Book press: traditional print binding
- Linen press: flatten woven cloth
- All use same basic mechanism

## Wood Screw Build

- Screw: large wooden post with carved thread
- Nut: wooden block with matching thread
- Thread carved with saw and chisel
- Or: use metal threaded rod (easier)
- Frame: four corner posts, top plate, bottom
- Pressing plate: fits between frame posts

## Carving a Wooden Screw

- Select: straight-grained dense hardwood
- Mark thread pitch around diameter
- Saw kerfs at pitch marks around cylinder
- Chisel out between kerfs to form thread form
- File thread smooth
- Nut: drill hole, carve inside thread to match
- Test fit: should turn smoothly with some force

## Metal Rod Option

- Large all-thread rod (jack screw size)
- Weld or pin to pressing plate
- Thread nut welded to top frame
- Simpler but requires steel
- Car jack screw mechanism: same principle

## Press Maintenance

- Wooden screws: oil with linseed oil annually
- Check frame joints before each heavy pressing
- Press plate: replace if cracked
- Do not over-tighten: wood can split frame
