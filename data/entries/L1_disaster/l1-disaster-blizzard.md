# Blizzard Survival

## Before the Storm
- Never travel without supplies in winter
- Full tank of fuel before storm arrives
- Extra blankets, food, water in vehicle
- Inform someone of your travel route
- Charge all devices fully

## Vehicle Stranded
- Stay with vehicle: easier to find
- Run engine 10 min per hour for heat
- Check exhaust pipe for snow blockage
- Crack window to prevent CO buildup
- Tie bright cloth to antenna or mirror

## Whiteout Navigation
- Stop moving if disoriented: stay put
- No landmarks: use compass or sun
- Walk into wind to find shelter
- Rope yourself to partner if moving
- Building to building: rope technique

## Building Shelter
- Snow trench: insulates well
- Quinzhee: 1-2 hour build
- Never sleep without insulation layer
- Body warmth: conserve by curling up
- Signal: stomp SOS in snow field

## Frostbite Watch
- Extremities first: fingers, toes, nose
- White or gray skin: danger sign
- Do not rub frostbitten tissue
- Rewarm only if no refreeze risk
- Wet socks: change immediately

## Indoor Survival
- Seal drafts with towels and cloth
- Interior room is warmest in home
- Layer clothing even indoors
- Candle: significant heat source
- Carbon monoxide: never burn inside
