# Earthquake Survival

## During the Shaking
- Drop to hands and knees immediately
- Cover: get under sturdy desk or table
- Hold on until shaking fully stops
- Face away from windows always
- Do not run outside during shaking
- If in bed: stay, cover head with pillow

## If No Cover Available
- Move to interior wall, low stance
- Away from windows, bookcases, TVs
- Never use elevator during quake
- If driving: pull over, away from trees
- If outside: away from buildings
- Open field is safest outdoor spot

## Immediately After
- Check for injuries: treat bleeding first
- Check for gas smell: evacuate if yes
- Do not use open flame if gas suspected
- Check for structural damage before entry
- Expect aftershocks: be ready to drop

## Gas and Fire Hazards
- Smell gas: leave building immediately
- Open windows on way out
- Do not flip any switches
- Shut off gas at meter if trained
- Call from outside and away from building

## Utility Shutdown
- Gas: quarter-turn valve at meter
- Water: main shutoff at meter
- Electric: main breaker in panel
- Know locations before disaster
- Keep wrench near gas meter

## After the Event
- Do not enter red-tagged buildings
- Use text not calls: networks congested
- Check on neighbors when safe
- Follow official evacuation orders
