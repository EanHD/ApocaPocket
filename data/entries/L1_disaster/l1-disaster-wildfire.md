# Wildfire Survival

## Evacuation Decision
- Leave at first warning: do not wait
- Watch signs: ash, orange sky, smoke
- Once fire is visible: may be too late
- Take: IDs, meds, water, phone, cash
- Go now; come back later

## If Trapped by Fire
- Call 911 and give exact location
- Stay in house: do not flee on foot
- Close all vents, windows, and doors
- Shut off propane and gas at tank
- Fill tubs and sinks with water

## Shelter in Building
- Stay in room away from fire side
- Wet towels under all doors
- Doors closed slow smoke and heat
- Watch for embers through windows
- Signal from window for rescuers

## If Caught in Open
- Lie face down in low area
- Cover with non-synthetic clothing
- Avoid hilltops: fire moves up fast
- Move into already-burned area
- Rock outcrops offer some protection

## Vehicle Survival
- Drive away from fire path
- If stopped: engine off, windows up
- Lie on floor under windows
- Cover with wool blanket if available
- Stay put: do not run from vehicle

## Home Preparation
- Clear 30 ft defensible space
- Metal mesh vents and gutters
- Non-flammable roofing material
- Hose and water access near home
