# Disasters: Start Here

## Use this when:
- A natural disaster is imminent or actively occurring
- You need to identify threat type and act in under 60 seconds
- You are pre-planning response protocols by disaster category

## Threat Recognition
- Tornado: rotating funnel, greenish sky, freight-train roar
- Wildfire: smoke columns, ember shower, orange horizon glow
- Earthquake: ground shaking, deep rumble, no warning
- Flood: rising water level, heavy upstream rain, dam failure alerts
- Blizzard: whiteout visibility, temp drop, wind over 35 mph

## Immediate Action
- Tornado: go underground or interior room lowest floor, away from windows
- Wildfire: evacuate early, do not wait for order; close all vents/doors if sheltering
- Earthquake: drop/cover/hold; after shaking stops, exit and check for hazards
- Flood: move to high ground immediately; never walk or drive through floodwater
- Blizzard: stay inside, insulate, conserve heat, signal for help if stranded outside

## Don't Miss:
- Secondary threats follow primary events (fire after quake, flood after storm)
- Pre-position: water, food, documents, go-bag before season peaks
- Know your local alert system and evacuation routes before you need them
