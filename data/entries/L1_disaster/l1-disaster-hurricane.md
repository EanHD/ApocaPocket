# Hurricane Survival

## Before Landfall
- Evacuate if ordered: do not wait
- Board windows or use hurricane film
- Fill bathtub with water now
- Charge all devices and power banks
- Fuel all vehicles before crowds form
- 3-day minimum water and food supply

## Storm Surge Risk
- Storm surge is deadlier than wind
- Know your surge zone (A, B, C, D)
- Zone A: evacuate without question
- 20 ft surge possible in Cat 4-5
- Inland 30+ miles can still surge

## During the Storm
- Stay in interior rooms, no windows
- Do not go outside during eye passage
- The eye wall is the most violent part
- Do not open windows to equalize
- If flooding: move to upper floors

## Eye Passage Warning
- Calm does not mean storm is over
- The back eye wall follows: deadly
- Do not go outside during the eye
- Wait for official all-clear only

## After the Storm
- Do not wade in floodwater: current
- Electrical hazards in standing water
- Report gas leaks from a distance
- Do not run generator indoors: CO risk
- Wait for roads cleared before travel

## Recovery Priorities
- Water first: pipes may be contaminated
- Refrigerator safe only 4 hours max
- Check on elderly neighbors first
- Document all damage before cleanup
