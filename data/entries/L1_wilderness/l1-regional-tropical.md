# Tropical Survival

## Key Hazards
- Water everywhere but all contaminated
- Parasites in all water and soil
- Venomous spiders and centipedes
- Dense jungle: navigation near zero
- Infection in cuts within hours
- Heat and humidity never relents

## Water Sources
- Collect only direct rainwater
- Bamboo sections hold clean water
- Banana tree: cut stem, collect sap
- Vine water: cut, hold end down
- Never drink from ground sources raw
- Solar still inefficient in cloud cover

## Food Sources
- Coconut: food and liquid source
- Banana and plantain: starch source
- Heart of palm: edible inner core
- Jungle fruit: run edibility test first
- Fish in streams: hook and line
- Large insects: calorie-dense option

## Shelter Priorities
- Get off ground: insects and snakes
- Hammock with rain fly: ideal setup
- Palm fronds: effective roof thatch
- Build high: flooding from rain fast
- Mosquito and insect net: critical

## Navigation
- Dense canopy blocks sun completely
- Water flows to coast: follow rivers
- Ridge tops have better visibility
- Mark return route as you travel
- Listen for ocean, vehicles, aircraft

## Evacuation Routes
- Rivers lead to coastal settlements
- Move to coast for rescue visibility
- Avoid river rapids and waterfalls
- Open clearing: use for signal fire
