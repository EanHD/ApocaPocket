# PNW Hazards

## Wildlife Threats
- Black bear: common; use bear hang
- Cougar: rare; do not run if seen
- Coyotes: rarely attack adults
- Rattlesnakes: east of Cascades only
- Ticks: Lyme disease risk spring-fall

## Volcanic Hazards
- Mt. Rainier, St. Helens, Hood active
- Lahar: mud flow follows river valleys
- Ash cloud: cover mouth and nose
- Move perpendicular to river valleys
- Ashfall: collapse risk on flat roofs

## Seismic Risk
- Cascadia subduction: M9+ possible
- Liquefaction risk in lowland soils
- Tsunami risk within 1 mile of coast
- Drop, cover, hold during shaking
- After shaking: move inland fast

## Landslide Risk
- Steep slopes unstable after rain
- Avoid gullies and debris fans
- Warning signs: cracking soil, tilt
- Run uphill and perpendicular
- Listen for rumbling sound upslope

## Tsunami Response
- Ground shaking is your warning
- Head inland and uphill immediately
- 100 ft elevation minimum target
- Do not return until official all-clear
- First wave may not be the largest

## Flood Hazard
- Atmospheric rivers cause rapid rise
- Glacial outburst floods in summer
- Never cross water above your knee
- Monitor NWS river gauge alerts
