# Universal Edibility

## When to Use
- No reference material available
- Unknown plant, no prior knowledge
- Hungry and must eat something
- Takes 24+ hours per plant to complete
- Only test one plant at a time

## Test Limitations
- Does NOT apply to mushrooms
- Does NOT apply to aquatic plants
- Does NOT detect all toxins
- Does NOT apply to berries in bulk
- Partial test is not safe

## Test Procedure: Steps
- Step 1: Separate into parts (leaf, stem,
  root, flower, seed) - test one part only
- Step 2: Smell for strong chemicals
- Step 3: Rub on inner wrist: wait 15 min
- Step 4: Touch to lips: wait 3 min

## Test Procedure: Steps
- Step 5: Touch to tongue tip: wait 15 min
- Step 6: Chew small piece: do not swallow
  Hold in mouth 15 minutes
- Step 7: Swallow small amount, wait 8 hrs
- Step 8: If no reaction: eat 1/4 cup wait 8hr
- Reaction at any step: discard that part

## Reaction Signs
- Burning, numbing: discard immediately
- Nausea or vomiting: discard
- Rash or irritation: discard
- No reaction through all steps: likely safe
- Safe does not mean nutritious

## Automatically Reject
- Milky sap that is not white
- Spines and fine hairs on surface
- Bean or pea shape seeds in pod
- Bitter almond smell: cyanide risk
- Three-leaf pattern like poison ivy
