# Island Survival

## Water Priority
- Drinking seawater accelerates death
- Rain catchment: first priority
- Coconut water: good electrolytes
- Solar still: sheet over vegetation
- Dig inland above tide line for seep
- Morning dew: lick rocks and leaves

## Coconut Use
- Green coconut: best liquid content
- Mature coconut: meat for calories
- Husk: rope fiber and tinder
- Shell: container and tool
- Fronds: roofing and weaving material
- Not all palms have edible coconut

## Coastal Food Sources
- Coral reef: fish, sea urchin, shells
- Tidal pools: crabs, snails, limpets
- Seaweed: most edible, boil first
- Sea birds: eggs on rocky shores
- Seagrass flats: fish aggregators
- Shellfish: cook thoroughly always

## Shelter
- Inland from storm surge
- Use palm fronds for roof thatch
- Orientate entrance away from wind
- Raised sleeping platform: insects
- Coconut husk: good insulation layer

## Signaling
- Beach: large SOS in stones or sand
- Signal fire on highest point
- Mirror or reflective surface: aircraft
- Three fires: international distress
- Contrast: rock on white sand

## Navigation
- Stars: south is Southern Cross (S hemi)
- North star: Polaris if northern
- Ocean swells: consistent direction
- Bird flight at dusk: toward land
- Floating debris indicates current
