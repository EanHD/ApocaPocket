# Water Ecology

## Biological Quality
- Stonefly and mayfly larvae: clean water
- Caddisfly cases on rocks: healthy stream
- No invertebrates: contaminated water
- Abundant aquatic insects: positive sign
- Algae only: enriched, possible issues

## Visual Water Quality
- Clear cold water: generally good quality
- Brown tannin color: safe if acidic
- Blue-green algae bloom: toxic, avoid
- Oily surface sheen: petroleum warning
- White foam that persists: sewage risk

## Fish Location
- Trout: cold well-oxygenated riffles
- Bass: warm slower pools and structure
- Catfish: bottom, slow deep water
- Creek bends: fish holding on inside
- Below falls: fish wait for food

## Stream Reading
- Riffle: shallow fast, high oxygen
- Pool: deep slow, fish rest here
- Run: smooth, moderate depth, feeding
- Eddy: circular behind obstruction
- Best fishing: riffle-pool transitions

## Upstream Contamination
- Dead fish floating downstream
- Foam and discoloration from upstream
- Mining operations on map: acid drain
- Agricultural areas: nitrate risk
- Town or road upstream: chemical risk

## Wetland Water Quality
- Pitcher plant bog: acidic, filter more
- Cattail marsh: filter and boil always
- Beaver ponds: giardia risk very high
- Seeps from spring: often best quality
- Slow moving water: always treat
