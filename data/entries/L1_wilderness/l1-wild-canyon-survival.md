# Canyon Survival

## Flash Flood Awareness
- Sound of distant roar: move high now
- Check sky upstream, not just overhead
- Dark sky 50 miles away = flood risk
- Water stain lines show historical height
- Move to high ledge: 20 ft minimum

## Water in Canyons
- Potholes (tinaja): check shaded shelves
- Seeps from sandstone at layer breaks
- Stream pools: purify and treat always
- Follow slot to wider sections: pools
- Track animal trails toward water

## Heat and Shade
- Shade moves: track it through the day
- Canyon walls: morning and afternoon shade
- Deep narrows: stay cool even in summer
- High canyon walls: radiate heat at night
- Move in shaded section of canyon

## Echo Navigation
- Clap or whistle: hear canyon walls
- Wider echoes: canyon opening ahead
- No echo: wall directly ahead
- Sound of water: direction clue
- Listen for traffic: civilization nearby

## Escape Routes
- Scout up for ledge systems
- Look for natural ramp or talus slope
- Check both walls for easiest route up
- Never climb without secure handholds
- Down-climb is harder than up: plan return

## Canyon Hazards
- Rockfall: avoid base of active walls
- Undercuts: do not stand below them
- Loose scree: move quickly across
- Narrow slots: test width before entering
- Cottonwood trees: mark canyon water
