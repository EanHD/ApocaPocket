# Natural Medicine Field

## Willow (Salix spp.)
- Contains salicin: aspirin precursor
- Chew inner bark for pain and fever
- Tea: boil bark 10 min, cool before drink
- Anti-inflammatory for joint pain
- Thin branches: flexible splint material
- Identify: long narrow leaves, near water

## Plantain (Plantago
- Broad or narrow leaf: both work
- Wound poultice: chew and apply
- Reduces swelling and draws infection
- Apply to bee stings and insect bites
- Identified by parallel veins on leaf
- Ubiquitous in disturbed soil and lawns

## Yarrow (Achillea
- Wound: pack directly to slow bleeding
- Feathery leaf: distinctive identification
- White flat-top flower clusters
- Mild antimicrobial properties
- Tea: mild fever reduction
- Dry and carry as wound dressing

## Raw Honey
- Apply directly to infected wounds
- Osmotic: draws fluid and bacteria out
- Manuka most potent, any honey helps
- Cover with bandage to hold in place
- Change dressing every 12-24 hours
- Effective for burns and abrasions

## Charcoal
- Activated charcoal: adsorbs toxins
- Crush char to fine powder
- Mix with water: drink for ingestion
- Not effective for all poisons
- Do not use for acid or lye ingestion

## Garlic
- Broad-spectrum antimicrobial
- Crush and apply to wound surface
- May cause skin irritation with prolonged use
- Tea or raw: internal immune support
- Identify by smell: distinctive
