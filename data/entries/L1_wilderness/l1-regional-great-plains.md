# Regional Survival

## Key Hazards
- Tornadoes: spring and summer peak
- Blizzard: whiteout with no shelter
- Extreme heat: humidity amplifies risk
- Open terrain: no natural shelter
- Lightning: no trees for miles
- Flash flooding in draws and ravines

## Water Sources
- Stock tanks: common on ranches
- Rivers: Missouri, Platte, Arkansas
- Windmill tanks mark water wells
- Surface water: filter and treat
- Cattails mark water in flatland
- Morning dew: collect off tall grass

## Food Sources
- Prairie turnip: edible root
- Wild plums: thickets along creeks
- Sunflower seeds: late summer
- Rabbit and ground squirrel: common
- Deer along river corridors
- Grasshoppers: calorie-dense protein

## Shelter Priorities
- Lowest ground for tornado safety
- Creek banks cut into soil: shelter
- Culverts: last resort; flood risk
- Build windbreak from packed grass
- Abandoned farmsteads: common here

## Navigation
- Section-line roads: 1-mile grid
- Grain elevators visible for miles
- Water towers mark every town
- Prevailing wind blows from SW
- Powerlines and roads run N-S/E-W

## Evacuation Routes
- I-70, I-80, I-90: E-W interstates
- US-83 runs N-S through center
- Follow rail lines to towns
- Any paved road leads to a town
