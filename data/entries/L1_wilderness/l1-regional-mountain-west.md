# Regional Survival

## Key Hazards
- Altitude sickness above 8,000 ft
- Lightning: afternoon storms daily
- Rockslide and scree field falls
- Rapid weather change: snow any month
- Hypothermia at elevation year-round
- Dehydration hides in cool dry air

## Water Sources
- Snowmelt streams: abundant in summer
- Springs at base of rock outcrops
- Alpine lakes: filter and treat
- Never drink from mining area streams
- Heavy metals contaminate mine drains

## Food Sources
- Whitebark pine nuts: high elevation
- Wild strawberries: forest edges
- Elk and deer: most drainages
- Grouse and ptarmigan at treeline
- Trout in clear cold streams
- Serviceberries: late summer, abundant

## Shelter Priorities
- Treeline: wind breaks and material
- Stay below ridgeline by 200 ft
- Rock windbreaks build fast
- Snow shelter if above treeline
- Avoid avalanche runout zones

## Navigation
- Continental Divide: water flows E/W
- Peaks and passes are key landmarks
- Jeep roads network most ranges
- Compass: declination 10-15 degrees E
- Trail signs common in national parks

## Evacuation Routes
- Descend drainage to lower road
- US-40, US-24, I-70: mountain passes
- Forest Service roads are numbered
- Follow powerlines to civilization
