# Swamp Survival

## Quicksand Reality
- True quicksand: rarely deep enough to die
- Thrashing makes it worse: stay calm
- Lean back slowly to distribute weight
- Work legs out with slow movements
- Spread weight on surface to escape
- Never fight it: slow extraction only

## Water Quality
- Standing swamp water: always treat
- Tannin-black water: acidic, not toxic
- Blue-green algae: DO NOT use or filter
- Moving water always better than still
- Raincollection: cleanest option here

## Navigation
- Hammock (tree island): dry, elevated
- Cypress knees: mark water depth
- Water flows toward lower elevation
- Follow waterfowl to open water
- Compass: essential in dense swamp

## Edible Plants
- Cattail: year-round all parts edible
- Pickerelweed: young leaves edible
- Arrowhead (wapato): starchy tubers
- Duck potato: similar to wapato
- Elderberry: ripe dark fruit edible

## Wildlife Hazards
- Alligator: avoid water edges at night
- Cottonmouth: aggressive near water
- Leeches: not dangerous, remove with salt
- Mosquitoes: cover all skin, dusk-dawn
- Snapping turtle: powerful bite in water

## Travel
- Move on root systems and hammocks
- Probe ahead with long stick
- Avoid wading in water above knee
- Move slowly to avoid noise and sink
- Night travel: extremely dangerous here
