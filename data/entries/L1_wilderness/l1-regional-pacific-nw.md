# Regional Survival

## Key Hazards
- Hypothermia: rain and cold year-round
- Flooding rivers after heavy rain
- Dense forest limits visibility
- Fog and low clouds hide landmarks
- Wet clothing kills; stay dry first

## Water Sources
- Rainfall catchment: use tarps/leaves
- Rivers and streams: filter and boil
- Avoid standing water: parasites high
- Snowmelt in spring: safe after filter
- Dew collection on moss possible

## Food Sources
- Blackberries, huckleberries: summer
- Cattail roots: year-round near water
- Salmon in rivers: Aug-Oct migration
- Fiddlehead ferns: early spring
- Chanterelles: fall harvest
- Nettles: spring, boil before eating

## Shelter Priorities
- Stay dry: highest priority always
- Debris hut: thick leaf walls work
- Conifer boughs shed rain well
- Avoid canyon bottoms: flood risk
- Elevated ground near tree line

## Navigation Landmarks
- Rivers flow west toward Pacific
- Cascades ridge runs N-S inland
- Moss grows heaviest on north side
- Listen for ocean surf near coast
- Powerlines cross mountain passes

## Evacuation Routes
- Follow rivers downstream to towns
- Main roads parallel river valleys
- I-5 corridor: main N-S artery
- US-2 and US-12: E-W cross routes
