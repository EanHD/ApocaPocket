# Lightning Safety Detail

## Why It Matters

- 2,000 lightning deaths per year globally
- Most are preventable with positioning
- Strikes can come before audible thunder
- Ground current injures more than strikes
- Suspension of activity is mandatory
- Most people are hit in open terrain

## 30-30 Rule

- See flash: count to thunder in seconds
- Divide by 5 = miles to strike
- Under 30 sec count = seek shelter now
- Stay sheltered 30 min after last thunder
- Do not return to open until 30 min clear
- Reset 30 min timer on each new thunder

## Danger Zones to Avoid

- Open meadows, hilltops, ridgelines
- Lone or tallest trees in area
- Open water bodies
- Metal structures, fences, bleachers
- Cave entrances (ground current risk)
- Cliff overhangs (same ground current)

## Safe Position

- Move away from high points
- Get into dense uniform tree cover
- Crouch low if no shelter available
- Feet together, on balls of feet
- Hands over ears, head down
- Do not lie flat, ground current risk
- Separate group members by 100 ft

## In a Vehicle

- Hard-top vehicle is safe shelter
- Pull over, stay inside
- Avoid touching metal interior
- Windows up, antennas stowed
- Do not shelter under trees with car

## What Can Go Wrong

- 30-30 rule ignored in afternoon
- Sheltering under isolated large tree
- Group clusters together in open
- Lying flat extends ground current risk

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Identify and reach safe
  position within 60 seconds
- Sign of mastery: Correct response
  to 5 different scenario questions
