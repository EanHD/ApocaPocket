# Wilderness: Start Here

## Use this when:
- You are lost, injured, or stranded in a non-urban environment
- You must survive in a specific biome (arctic, desert, jungle, forest)
- You need to signal for rescue or manage wildlife threats

## First 5 Minutes
- Stop moving immediately; panic leads to deeper disorientation
- Breathe; scan for landmarks, water, sound of roads or aircraft
- Blow whistle (3 blasts = distress); if no whistle, use voice or mirror
- Stay put if rescuers know your last location  -  movement hides you
- If you must move, follow water downhill toward civilization

## Biome Risks Compared:
- Arctic: hypothermia is primary killer; shelter and fire before all else
- Desert: dehydration and heat stroke; move at night, shelter in day
- Jungle: infection, insects, disorientation; stay near water, signal up
- Forest/Temperate: most forgiving; fire, water, and shelter within reach

## Wildlife Notes:
- Bears: make noise while moving; carry bear spray; know play-dead vs fight-back
- Snakes: watch foot placement; don't reach under rocks or logs
- Big cats: maintain eye contact; back away slowly; appear large

## Weather Awareness:
- Build shelter before weather turns; clouds and wind direction signal change
- Lightning: avoid ridgelines, lone trees, water; seek low dense brush
