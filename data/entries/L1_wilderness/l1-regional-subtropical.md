# Subtropical Survival

## Key Hazards
- Humidity accelerates infection fast
- Venomous snakes: cottonmouth, coral
- Alligators and crocodiles in water
- Mosquito-borne illness risk is high
- Flash flooding from tropical rain
- Heat exhaustion and heat stroke

## Water Sources
- Rainfall heavy but contaminates fast
- Collect rain before it hits ground
- Treat all standing water: parasites
- Springs and artesian wells: cleanest
- Avoid stagnant swamp water

## Food Sources
- Freshwater fish: abundant
- Frogs: common near water
- Wild berries: identify carefully
- Cattails: throughout wetlands
- Palmetto hearts: emergency use
- Crabbing and clamming: tidal zones

## Shelter Priorities
- Insect net is non-negotiable
- Elevated sleeping above ground
- Avoid areas with standing water
- Shade reduces heat illness risk
- Concrete buildings: hurricane safe

## Medical Priorities
- Keep all wounds clean and dry
- Change wound dressings frequently
- Hydrate constantly in humidity
- Check for ticks and leeches daily
- Foot care: trench foot risk here

## Evacuation Routes
- Move during the cooler parts of day
- Stay on high ground along rivers
- Major highways above flood line
- Monitor tropical storm tracks
