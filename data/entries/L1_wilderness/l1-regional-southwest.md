# Regional Survival

## Key Hazards
- Heat stroke: core temp over 104F
- Dehydration: 1 gal per day minimum
- Flash floods in slot canyons
- Venomous snakes and scorpions
- Sun exposure and high UV radiation
- Hypothermia at night in winter

## Water Finding
- Dry riverbeds: dig inside the bend
- Follow animal trails toward water
- Rock potholes after rain: check shaded
- Morning dew on rocks and plants
- Barrel cactus: moist pulp, not drink
- Cottonwood trees mark water table

## Food Sources
- Prickly pear: pads and fruit edible
- Mesquite pods: grind into flour
- Agave heart: roast for calories
- Lizards and snakes: high protein
- Grasshoppers: abundant in summer
- Desert chia seeds: found near streams

## Shelter Priorities
- Shade is survival: rest 10am-4pm
- Rock overhangs block sun and wind
- Dig into sandy soil for coolness
- Mylar reflects heat effectively
- Build shelter before dark in winter

## Navigation
- Sun rises ESE, sets WSW here
- Highway signs visible from far off
- Power lines lead to settlements
- Dry lake beds visible on all horizons
- Mountains are key fixed landmarks

## Evacuation Routes
- Move at night: cooler and safer
- Interstate 10, 40, 8: main routes
- Follow power lines or pipelines
- Seek cattle tanks for water stops
