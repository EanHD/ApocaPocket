# Soil Ecology

## Fertility Indicators
- Earthworms: healthy soil with high organics
- Millipedes: decomposing matter present
- Dark rich color: high carbon, fertile
- Pale sandy: low nutrients, low yield
- Reddish soil: iron-rich, check for acidity

## Plant Indicators of
- Nettles: high nitrogen, fertile location
- Lamb's quarters: good garden potential
- Chickweed: moist, rich, workable soil
- Bracken fern: acidic, lower fertility
- Cattail: saturated or wet soil nearby

## Root Crop Soil
- Loamy loose soil: deep root crops
- Heavy clay: smaller, harder to dig
- Sandy soil: dig deep for moisture
- Rocky: surface crops only viable
- Best roots: moist loamy slopes

## Contamination
- Plants absent or stunted: check why
- Mine tailings: orange-stained soil
- Petroleum: rainbow oil sheen on wet soil
- Heavy metals: nothing grows at all
- Old orchard soil: arsenic risk from lead

## Improving Soil for
- Charcoal and ash: raise pH and nutrients
- Green material mulch: break down fast
- Leaf litter mix into top layer
- Urine: diluted, nitrogen source
- Bone and blood meal: slow release N

## Earthworm Harvest
- Dig moist soil after rain
- Edible: high protein, clean gut before eat
- Soak in clean water 2 hours to purge
- Roast or boil to prepare
- Indicator of safe soil for food growing
