# Flash Flood Survival

## Why It Matters

- Floods are the top US weather hazard
- Canyon floods arrive with no warning
- 12 inches of moving water = vehicle swept
- Desert canyons concentrate water fast
- Rain 50 miles away causes local flood
- Escape window may be under 2 minutes

## Canyon Warning Signs

- Distant rumbling or roaring sound
- Smell of fresh soil or vegetation
- Water level rising in creek bed
- Turbid or muddy water appearing
- Debris (logs, foam) moving downstream
- Ground shaking from upstream

## Escape Protocol

- Move perpendicular to water flow
- Reach high ground immediately
- Go up canyon walls, not downstream
- High ground means above flood line
- Flood debris line shows past levels
- Move to 20 ft above creek bottom

## Canyon Camp Rules

- Never camp in canyon bottom
- Check flood stain lines on walls
- Note escape routes before sleep
- Camp above highest stain line
- Stake gear above flood line too
- Weather upstream determines risk

## If Swept Away

- Last resort only
- Lie on back, feet downstream
- Feet absorb impacts first
- Arms out for steering and balance
- Aim for bank angles not walls
- Avoid getting pushed under debris

## What Can Go Wrong

- Hesitating while water rises
- Moving downstream (wrong direction)
- Camp placed in canyon below walls
- Flood debris line misread as rock

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Identify escape route in
  any canyon within 30 seconds
- Sign of mastery: Camp placement
  above flood line on every outing
