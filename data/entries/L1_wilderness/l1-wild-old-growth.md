# Old Growth Forest

## Navigation
- Canopy closes: sun rarely visible
- Compass: essential navigation tool
- Moss: grows N, heaviest on N side
- Old growth: very few straight lines
- Ridgelines: best travel in old growth

## Fallen Log Resources
- Nurse log: grows mushrooms year-round
- Soft interior: dry tinder inside
- Cambium layer: emergency food source
- Habitat for insects: food source
- Hollow logs: emergency shelter option

## Water Sources
- Epiphyte (hanging moss): squeeze for water
- Rain catchment easier in open gaps
- Streams and seeps: common and clean
- Dew on large leaves at dawn
- Old growth: usually near water

## Food Sources
- Chanterelle, lobster, hen-of-woods
- Oxalis (sorrel): sour edible greens
- Sword fern fiddleheads: early spring
- Berries in canopy gaps
- Tree cambium: slippery elm and others
- Ground insects under bark and logs

## Shelter in Old Growth
- Massive root balls: windbreak and cave
- Old growth canopy blocks most rain
- Dense duff floor: insulating layer
- Fallen log lean-to: quick roof base
- Windfall hollows: natural shelter base

## Old Growth Traits
- Multi-age canopy: 200-1000 year trees
- Abundant dead wood: 30%+ of biomass
- Structural complexity: 5+ canopy layers
- High biodiversity: medicines and food
- Rare and protected: leave no trace
