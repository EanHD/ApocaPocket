# Snake Avoidance

## Why It Matters

- 7,000+ venomous bites per year in US
- Most bites are entirely preventable
- Envenomation can be fatal without care
- Antivenom may be hours away
- Avoidance beats treatment always
- Simple habits prevent nearly all bites

## High-Risk Habitats

- Rocky outcrops and crevices
- Log piles and dense brush
- Leaf litter on forest floor
- Stream and pond edges
- Tall grass and field edges
- Under debris, tarps, gear left out

## Safe Movement Habits

- Watch every step in snake country
- Use trekking poles to probe ahead
- Step ON logs before stepping over
- Use flashlight at night always
- Never reach into unseen spaces
- Wear ankle-high boots and gaiters

## Camp Safety Rules

- Shake out boots before wearing
- Check sleeping bag before entering
- Clear 10 ft circle around camp
- Do not sit on unseen log edges
- Keep camp neat, no hiding spots
- Avoid sitting on ground in tall grass

## Snake Encounter

- Stop movement immediately
- Wait for it to move away
- Back away slowly if it does not move
- Give it 10 feet of space minimum
- Do not attempt identification while
  snake is present and aware of you

## What Can Go Wrong

- Reaching into rock crevice for water
- Hiking at night without flashlight
- Handling gear left in tall grass
- Trying to photograph too close

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Identify snake habitat at
  a campsite and modify setup
- Sign of mastery: Zero near-contact
  incidents in 5 days snake country
