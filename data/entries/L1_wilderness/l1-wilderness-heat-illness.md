# Heat Illness Spectrum

## Why It Matters

- Heat illness progresses fast
- Stroke can follow exhaustion quickly
- Early signs are easy to miss
- Wrong response makes it worse
- Cooling is the primary treatment
- Recognition speed determines outcome

## Heat Cramps

- Painful muscle spasms, usually legs
- Occurs during or after exertion
- Caused by sodium and fluid loss
- Rest in shade immediately
- Drink electrolyte solution slowly
- Stretch and massage affected muscles

## Heat Exhaustion

- Heavy sweating, pale, cool, clammy
- Weakness, fatigue, nausea
- Headache, dizziness, fainting possible
- Core temp below 104F
- Move to cool shade, loosen clothing
- Oral fluids if conscious and tolerating
- Rest 2-4 hours before activity

## Heat Stroke Signs

- Core temp above 104F
- Hot, red skin (may be dry or sweaty)
- Altered mental status - confusion
- Slurred speech, combative behavior
- Rapid heart rate, rapid breathing
- Loss of consciousness in late stage

## Heat Stroke: Treatment

- Cool immediately, question later
- Immerse in cold water if possible
- Ice packs to neck, armpits, groin
- Fan wet skin continuously
- Stop cooling at 102F
- Transport urgently, do not delay
- No aspirin or ibuprofen

## What Can Go Wrong

- Exhaustion becomes stroke unnoticed
- Oral fluids given to confused patient
- Cooling stopped too early
- Aspirin given (worsens clotting)

- Goal: Correctly stage 5 heat
  illness scenarios presented
- Sign of mastery: First cooling
