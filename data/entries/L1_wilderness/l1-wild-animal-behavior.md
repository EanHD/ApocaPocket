# Animal Behavior Field

## Pre-Storm Behavior
- Birds roost early: storm within hours
- Animals feeding heavily: cold snap coming
- Ants building high mounds: rain coming
- Fish jump frequently: pressure dropping
- Cows lying down: anecdotal rain sign

## Territorial Signs
- Claw marks on trees: bear height = size
- Scrapes and rubs: deer breeding season
- Urine marking on stumps and rocks
- Territorial howls: wolf pack boundary
- Scat piles in same location: territory

## Seasonal Patterns
- Spring: mating calls and territory fights
- Summer: feeding maximum, predictable
- Fall: fattening phase, more active
- Winter: reduced or absent from area
- Migration: predictable by species

## Using Animals to Find
- Bees: fly direct to water source
- Flies: water or carrion within 1 mile
- Birds: converge at water at dawn/dusk
- Game trails: lead to water source
- Watching flight direction at dawn

## Prey Animal Behavior
- Deer bed in cover during midday
- Dawn and dusk: feeding movement peaks
- Moon phase affects deer movement
- Wind: deer move crosswind while feeding
- Approaching storm: feeding intensifies

## Predator Avoidance
- Stay downwind of animal you approach
- Move slowly and avoid direct eye contact
  (except cougar: maintain eye contact)
- Never approach mother with young
- Do not approach a feeding animal
- Back away slowly and give wide berth
