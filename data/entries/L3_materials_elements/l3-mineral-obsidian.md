# Obsidian

## Overview

- Volcanic glass; amorphous silica structure
- No crystal structure = sharpest fracture edge
- Edge one molecule thick; sharper than steel
- Found near volcanic vents and lava flows

## Identification

- Jet black; sometimes brown, red, or banded
- Glassy smooth surface when unworked
- Shiny like glass; highly reflective
- Conchoidal fracture; very predictable

## Where Found

- Near volcanic regions and calderas
- Yellowstone, Cascades, Central America
- Mediterranean: Sardinia, Melos, Lipari
- Africa: Rift Valley volcanic zones

## Knapping Properties

- Easier to work than flint; more predictable
- Very thin flakes possible; minimal force
- Produces fine edge for surgery or cutting
- Brittle; snaps if flexed or dropped

## Uses

- Cutting tools: knife blades, scrapers
- Arrow and spear tips (fine points)
- Surgical blades (field emergency)
- Razor-sharp flake for initial cuts in butchering

## Limitations

- Brittle; edge chips from bone contact
- Not for prying or heavy duty chopping
- Found only near volcanic areas
- Highly valued as trade good historically
