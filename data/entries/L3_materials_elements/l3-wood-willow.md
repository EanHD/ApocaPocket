# Willow Properties

## Overview

- Fast-growing riparian tree or shrub
- Multiple species share similar properties
- Flexible young shoots ideal for basketry
- Bark contains salicin (aspirin precursor)

## Physical Properties

- Density: 25-30 lbs per cubic foot
- Light, soft wood; not structural grade
- Pale white to creamy color throughout
- Diffuse-porous; easily worked by hand

## Coppicing and Regrowth

- Coppice (cut to stump) for annual shoot harvest
- Regrows 6-8 feet of whips per season
- Most sustainable basketry wood available
- Harvest in late winter for most flexible shoots

## Medicinal Properties

- Boil inner bark for fever and pain relief
- Contains salicin; converts to salicylic acid
- 1-2 cups bark tea for mild pain relief
- Anti-inflammatory; use for joint pain

## Basket Making

- Harvest whips; soak dried ones before use
- Base, stakes, and weavers all from willow
- Most forgiving weaving material available
- Traditional basket material worldwide

## Other Uses

- Stakes for garden; roots readily in water
- Weave as hurdle fencing panels
- Long shoots as lashing cordage material
- Erosion control: plant cuttings in stream banks
