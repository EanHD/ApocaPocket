# Copper Ore

## Overview

- Copper was first smelted metal in history
- Identified by vivid green and blue minerals
- Smelt at much lower temp than iron (~1085C)
- Found in oxidized zone of copper deposits

## Malachite

- Vivid green banded or massive mineral
- Effervesces weakly with strong acid
- Common oxidized copper ore worldwide
- Green staining on rock surfaces indicates nearby

## Azurite

- Vivid blue color; often with malachite
- Most identifiable copper mineral by color
- Converts to malachite over time outdoors
- Both azurite and malachite yield copper

## Native Copper

- Metallic copper in natural state
- Reddish-orange metallic color; heavy
- Found in volcanic basalt regions
- Superior Lakes region: famous occurrence

## Chrysocolla

- Blue-green to turquoise; glassy or earthy
- Lower copper content than malachite
- Smelts with difficulty; avoid for ore
- Often found with better ore nearby

## Smelting Basics

- Grind ore; mix with charcoal in clay crucible
- Heat to 1085C+ in forced-draft charcoal fire
- Copper settles at bottom; slag floats on top
- Pour liquid copper into clay or sand mold
