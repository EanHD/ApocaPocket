# Slate

## Overview

- Fine-grained metamorphic rock from shale
- Splits cleanly along cleavage planes
- Produces thin, flat, durable sheets
- Natural choice for roofing, writing, trays

## Identification

- Grey, blue-grey, or dark green color
- Smooth to touch; finely layered structure
- Splits cleanly with chisel along cleavage
- Makes musical ring when tapped (good quality)

## Splitting Technique

- Insert chisel or flat wedge at edge
- Tap lightly; slate peels along cleavage
- Successive splits can make very thin sheets
- Work from edge inward for large pieces

## Roofing Use

- Overlap slate tiles like fish scales
- Drill holes with hardened point; use wooden pegs
- Durable 50-200 years when properly installed
- No maintenance if stone is sound quality

## Writing Surface

- Soft chalk or soapstone writes on slate
- Smooth one face for writing use
- Portable field note-taking in rough conditions
- Scratch marks also visible; use sharp point

## Container and Cooking

- Two flat pieces form simple cooking griddle
- Small slate trays hold liquids briefly
- Boil water by dropping hot stones into slate dish
- Flat pieces under coals: hearth base
