# Natural Antimicrobials

## How They Work

- Plant compounds inhibit bacterial growth
- Not as reliable as pharmaceutical options
- Best for minor wounds and infections
- Not a substitute for serious infections

## Honey

- Raw honey best, not processed
- Hydrogen peroxide and pH inhibit bacteria
- Manuka honey clinically studied
- Apply directly to wounds, cover

## Garlic

- Allicin released when crushed
- Broad-spectrum antimicrobial evidence
- Apply crushed to wound or take orally
- Loses potency quickly, use fresh

## Turmeric

- Curcumin has anti-inflammatory properties
- Mix with honey for wound paste
- Also useful for digestive issues
- Better absorbed with fat

## Thyme and Oregano

- Thymol and carvacrol compounds
- Antifungal and antibacterial
- Tea for respiratory infections
- Diluted oil for wound application

## Limitations

- Allergic reactions possible
- Do not use instead of professional care
- Serious infections require medical help
- No antibiotic resistance risk (diff mechanism)
