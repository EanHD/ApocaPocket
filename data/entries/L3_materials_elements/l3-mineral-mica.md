# Mica

## Overview

- Silicate mineral; perfect cleavage into sheets
- Transparent to translucent in thin sheets
- Heat-resistant to 500C; non-conductive
- Found in granite, pegmatite, and metamorphics

## Identification

- Silvery or golden metallic sheen
- Peels into flexible transparent sheets
- Found as flakes in granite and sandy soil
- Books of mica visible in pegmatite rock

## Fire Window Use

- Split into sheets thin enough for light passage
- Mica panes in stove or lantern door
- Transmits light; resists heat (unlike glass)
- Used as window substitute historically

## Insulation Uses

- Electrical insulator; does not conduct charge
- Thermal insulator between metal surfaces
- Pack mica flakes in wall cavities
- Wrap around heated metal for handle insulation

## Lubrication

- Mica flakes as dry lubricant between surfaces
- Reduce friction on wooden bearing surfaces
- Apply to wooden axle and runner bearings
- Mix with tallow for improved lubricant paste

## Other Uses

- Ground mica as paint pigment (sparkle)
- Transparent piece as fire inspection window
- Backing behind hot coals for heat reflection
- Add to clay for improved thermal shock resist
