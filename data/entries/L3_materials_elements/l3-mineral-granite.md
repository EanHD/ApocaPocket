# Granite

## Overview

- Igneous rock; interlocked crystal structure
- Composed of quartz, feldspar, and mica
- Very hard; resists weathering and abrasion
- Safe for use with fire and cooking surfaces

## Identification

- Coarse crystalline texture; salt-and-pepper
- Pink, grey, or white base color
- Crystals visible to naked eye (>1mm)
- Heavy and very hard; resists scratching

## Fire Safety

- Does not contain moisture pockets that explode
- Safe to use as cooking stones
- Safe for fire ring construction
- Heats slowly; retains heat well

## Grinding Stone Use

- Dense quartz crystals abrade metal and bone
- Shape into saddle or flat grinding surface
- Two-stone grinding (metate and mano) for grain
- Pecking worn surface to re-create grit

## Building Uses

- Excellent foundation and base stone
- Highly durable for outdoor walls
- Too hard to dress without steel tools
- Use as-found boulders for walls (dry-stack)

## Other Uses

- Heavy weight for pressing and extraction
- Smooth surface for food preparation
- Anvil stone for metal working and flaking
- Ballast weight for fermentation weights
