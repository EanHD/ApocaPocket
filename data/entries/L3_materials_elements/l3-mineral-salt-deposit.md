# Salt Deposits

## Overview

- Halite (NaCl) is the mineral form of salt
- Forms by evaporation of ancient sea water
- Surface deposits (salt flats) widespread
- Underground mines and springs worldwide

## Visual Identification

- White to clear crystalline mineral
- Cubic crystal structure when well-formed
- Often found in white powdery surface crusts
- May have slight blue or purple tinge

## Taste Confirmation

- Dissolve small amount in clean water
- Taste solution: distinct sharp salty taste
- Do not place dry mineral directly in mouth
- Salty brine is safe for this test

## Animal Behavior Clues

- Deer, elk, cattle converge at salt licks
- Well-worn animal trails leading to rock
- Bare mineral exposure from licking
- Multiple species present at same site

## Salt Spring ID

- White mineral crust around spring opening
- Slightly saline taste in water
- Oily or slightly thick appearance to water
- Evaporate spring water to test for salt

## Harvesting Methods

- Dry flat: scrape crystals from surface
- Brine spring: collect and evaporate
- Rock salt: chip from exposed deposit
- Filter any dirt before use in food
