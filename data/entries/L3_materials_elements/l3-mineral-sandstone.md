# Sandstone Uses

## Overview

- Sedimentary rock of cemented sand grains
- Grain size and cement type varies widely
- Coarse = good grinding; fine = polishing
- Widely available; easy to recognize and work

## Identification

- Granular texture; visible sand grains
- Scratches easily with copper or steel
- Ranges from tan to red to grey
- Layered deposits; often in cliffs and beds

## Grinding and Abrading

- Natural sharpening stone for tools
- Wet grind to extend stone life and reduce dust
- Coarse sandstone: initial edge shaping
- Fine sandstone: final honing of edge

## Grain Cultivation

- Select piece with consistent grain throughout
- Flat face for grinding flat tools and blades
- Groove face used for sharpening arrows/spears
- Roughen worn surface by pecking with hard stone

## Building Uses

- Cuts and shapes easily with steel or stone
- Blocks for walls if not bearing heavy loads
- Good for fire-surround and non-structural work
- Weathers moderately; decent outdoor durability

## Limitations

- Porous; absorbs water; can spall when frozen
- Weaker than granite or quartzite
- Not for use in forge areas; may spall with heat
- Limestone better for lime production
