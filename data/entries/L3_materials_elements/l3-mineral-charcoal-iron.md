# Charcoal and Iron

## Overview

- Iron ore requires 1200C+ to smelt to metal
- Charcoal provides fuel and carbon for reduction
- Carbon from charcoal bonds with oxygen in ore
- Result: iron metal bloom (sponge iron)

## The Chemistry

- Iron oxide + carbon = iron metal + CO2
- Carbon monoxide (CO) does most of reduction
- Higher charcoal ratio = more carbon available
- Too much carbon = cast iron (brittle)

## Charcoal Requirements

- Dense hardwood charcoal for longest burn
- Must be dry; moisture wastes heat energy
- Grind ore to fist-size; charcoal similar size
- Layer: charcoal, ore, charcoal, ore, repeat

## Furnace Construction

- Clay furnace with forced-air tuyere
- Bellows or blow pipe at base level
- Preheat furnace before adding ore and charcoal
- Tall shaft allows ore to pre-heat as it falls

## Bloom Extraction

- Stop when slag fills lower furnace
- Break open furnace; extract bloom while hot
- Bloom is sponge iron mixed with slag
- Hammer while hot to expel slag inclusions

## Working the Iron

- Wrought iron: hammer repeatedly while hot
- Low carbon iron: tough but cannot be quenched
- Add more carbon by heating in charcoal bed
- Medium carbon steel can be hardened by quench
