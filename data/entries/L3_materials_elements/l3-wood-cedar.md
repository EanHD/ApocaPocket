# Cedar Properties

## Overview

- Multiple species; all aromatic and rot-resistant
- Eastern red cedar: most common in US East
- Western red cedar: largest; Pacific coast
- Natural oils deter insects and resist decay

## Physical Properties

- Density: 23-30 lbs per cubic foot (lightweight)
- Soft for a hardwood; works easily
- Reddish-brown heartwood; pale sapwood
- Distinctive sharp aromatic smell

## Rot Resistance

- Heartwood rated durable to very durable
- Excellent for fence posts and ground contact
- Used for siding without paint for centuries
- Naturally lasts 30-50 years in soil

## Insect Repelling

- Volatile oils repel clothes moths
- Use cedar shavings in storage areas
- Line storage boxes with cedar planks
- Oils effective for 2-3 years then diminish

## Craft and Building

- Shingle and shake roofing material
- Lightweight outdoor furniture
- Bark strips for cordage and basket weaving
- Bows: poor elasticity; not recommended

## Fire Use

- Burns fast and hot; good kindling
- Produces aromatic smoke for smoking fish
- Bark: excellent dry tinder material
- Not a long-burning fuel wood
