# Birch Properties

## Overview

- Distinctive white papery outer bark
- Moderate hardwood; easier to work than oak
- Bark contains betulin; highly water-resistant
- Fast-growing colonizer; regrows quickly

## Physical Properties

- Density: 40-44 lbs per cubic foot
- Creamy white to pale yellow-brown wood
- Diffuse-porous; smooth and even texture
- Good bending strength; not as dense as oak

## Bark Characteristics

- Paper-thin layers that peel easily
- Highly water-resistant; does not rot easily
- Contains oils that burn even when wet
- Inner bark edible in emergency (cambium)

## Fire Use

- Bark lights in wet conditions; contains oils
- Excellent fire-starting material year-round
- Birch bark tar: best field adhesive resin
- Wood burns fast; good for quick hot fires

## Container Uses

- Fold and sew bark into watertight containers
- Birch bark canoe construction (traditional)
- Lightweight storage boxes and trays
- Bark for ground cover and waterproofing

## Medicinal Uses

- Inner bark contains salicylate compounds
- Boil bark for pain-relief tea (weak aspirin)
- Cambium layer eaten as famine food
- Bark strips for writing and kindling
