# Pine Wood Properties

## Overview

- Most abundant softwood globally
- High resin content; preserves in dry conditions
- Non-porous conifer; distinct resin canals
- Varies widely: longleaf dense, white pine soft

## Physical Properties

- Density: 25-35 lbs per cubic foot (varies)
- Lighter than most hardwoods
- Yellowish to orange heartwood; pale sapwood
- Resin-soaked fatwood has high BTU content

## Rot Resistance

- Longleaf pine heartwood: highly rot-resistant
- White pine: not rot-resistant outdoors
- Resin content protects heartwood in soil
- Treat with tar or charring for fence posts

## Resin and By-Products

- Highest resin yield of common trees
- Collect for pine pitch adhesive production
- Distill for turpentine and rosin products
- Fatwood (resin-soaked old heartwood): fire starter

## Building Uses

- Widely used structural lumber
- Easy to work; nails and splits well
- Less durable than hardwood for ground contact
- Excellent rafters, boards, and general framing

## Fuel Properties

- High resin = burns hot and fast; much spark
- Not preferred for long-burning fires
- Good kindling and fire-starting material
- Fatwood chips: reliable fire starter in rain
