# Natural Nav Indicators

## Why It Matters

- Backup to all other nav methods
- Useful when sun and stars hidden
- Patterns consistent across a region
- Read multiple signs to cross-check
- No equipment needed
- Practiced awareness is the skill

## Moss and Lichen

- Moss grows thicker on north side
  of trees and rocks
- North side wetter and shadier
- Confirm on 3+ trees before trusting
- Exceptions in dense forest common
- More reliable in open areas
- Lichen also favors north in NH

## Tree Growth Patterns

- More branches on south-facing side
- Tree rings wider on south side
  (cut stump reveals this)
- Leaning: may indicate prevailing wind
- Record wind direction first if using
- Lone trees more reliable than groves
- Evergreens show sun-side growth well

## Snow Melt Patterns

- South-facing slopes melt faster
- North-facing snow persists longer
- Look at open hillsides for contrast
- Shadows on north sides in morning
- Ice in shaded zones = north-facing
- Patchy melt lines follow sun exposure

## Animals and Ant Hills

- Ant mounds often have south face
  (warmer for colony heat)
- Spiderwebs often face warmth (south)
- Bird nests often in tree crotches
  on south side
- Deer trails often follow ridgelines

## What Can Go Wrong

- Local conditions override patterns
- Heavy cloud cover on all references
- Southern hemisphere signs reversed
- Forest density masks sun effects

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Identify north using 3 signs
  with consistent agreement
- Sign of mastery: Navigate 500 m
  through forest using natural signs
