# Navigation: Start Here

## Use this when:
- Phone/GPS is unavailable, dead, or unreliable
- You are lost or disoriented in urban or wilderness terrain
- You need to establish direction or plot a route without tech

## No Phone/GPS Flow
- Have a compass? Use compass + known heading
- No compass, daytime? Use sun position (rises E, sets W) + shadow stick
- No compass, nighttime? Find Polaris via Big Dipper for true north
- Urban grid-down? Use street grid logic, landmarks, and address numbering
- Wilderness, no landmarks? Pace-count to track distance; use ridgelines

## Methods Compared:
- Urban Grid Navigation: fast, works in most cities, needs street awareness
- Natural Landmarks: rivers flow downhill to civilization; follow them
- Stars (Polaris): gives true north, clear night required, learn in advance
- Compass + Map: most reliable tool combo, must be pre-carried
- Pacing/Dead Reckoning: tracks distance without landmarks, error accumulates

## Key Rules:
- Stop and observe before moving; panic wastes time and energy
- Confirm direction with two independent methods before committing
- Leave trail markers or note landmarks so you can backtrack
