# Natural Tinder ID

## Why It Matters

- Tinder is the critical link in fire
- Poor tinder wastes every spark
- Knowing 10 tinders gives options
- Regional variety ensures success
- Prep determines catch rate
- Dry and fluffy = takes spark best

## Fungal and Wood Tinder

- Horse hoof fungus inner layer
- Amadou from bracket fungi
- Punky dry rotten wood (crumbled)
- Birch bark inner papery layer
- Cedar bark inner layer shredded
- Dried cattail head fluffed

## Plant Material Tinder

- Dried grass twisted into bundle
- Mullein dried leaf fluffed fine
- Thistle seed head down
- Dried goldenrod fluff
- Dead dry fern crumbled fine
- Dry dandelion seed puff

## Preparation Steps

- Dry tinder completely before use
- Keep inside clothing if damp out
- Shred or fluff all materials fine
- Form into bird-nest bundle shape
- Nest should fit two cupped hands
- Catch spark or coal in center

## Field Dryness Test

- Squeeze tinder hard in fist
- Dry: springs back, no moisture
- Damp: feels cool, packs flat
- Store dry tinder in sealed bag
- Body heat dries marginal tinder
- Carry reserve tinder always

## What Can Go Wrong

- Fungi ID wrong - some too hard
- Tinder looks dry but has moisture
- Bundle too tightly packed to catch
- Bundle too loose - burns through fast

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Catch spark and produce
  flame from 3 different tinders
- Sign of mastery: Coal to flame
  transfer in under 30 seconds
