# Fire Management

## Why It Matters

- Uncontrolled fire wastes fuel fast
- Too-large fire is exhausting to feed
- Managed fire serves all needs
- Correct size = warmth + cooking
- Banking overnight extends coals
- Fuel planning prevents running out

## Fire Sizing Guide

- Cooking: fire the size of your hat
- Warmth: fire the size of your torso
- Signaling: fire as large as possible
- Bigger is not always better
- Small hot fire beats large weak fire
- Match fuel type to purpose

## Fuel Feed Rate

- Add fuel before fire shrinks low
- Wait until new wood catches fully
- Do not smother with too much wood
- Hardwood: add every 20-30 min
- Softwood: add every 10-15 min
- Keep 30-min reserve beside fire

## Banking for Overnight

- Let fire burn to thick coal bed
- Push coals into tight mound
- Cover coals with 1 inch of ash
- Do not smother completely
- In morning: clear ash, add tinder
- Fan gently to revive coals
- Hardwood coals last 8-12 hours

## What Can Go Wrong

- Coals banked too deep - smothered
- Wind revives fire unexpectedly
- Running out of fuel at night
- Wet morning wood won't catch coals

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Maintain fire 8 hrs on
  hardwood with banking overnight
- Sign of mastery: Revive banked
  coals to flame in under 5 min
