# Fire: Start Here

## Use this when:
- You need warmth, light, or signaling in the field
- You must cook/purify water without modern gear
- You are preparing before an emergency and want a skill baseline

## Methods Compared:
- Ferro Rod: fast, reliable, works wet, needs dry tinder
- Bow Drill: no tools needed, high skill, slow, fails in wet
- Solar Ignition: zero consumables, daylight only, needs lens or mirror
- Char Cloth: extends ember life, must be pre-made, best with ferro
- Friction (hand drill): most primitive, hardest to master, very slow

## Conditions Guide:
- Wet environment: ferro rod + waterproof tinder prep is priority
- Dry environment: all methods viable; bow drill or solar if no tools
- No tools at all: bow drill or hand drill; find dry inner bark for tinder
- Emergency/speed needed: ferro rod first, solar second if sunny

## Tinder Notes:
- Dry grass, cattail fluff, inner bark shreds, char cloth, dry fungi
- Keep tinder inside clothing to stay dry
- Prepare tinder bundle before striking any ignition source
