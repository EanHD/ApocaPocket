# Camp Fire Safety

## Why It Matters

- Wildfire kills more than it warms
- Escaped campfires cause disasters
- Wind and dry conditions change risk
- Proper siting prevents accidents
- Extinguishing fully is non-optional
- Know your escape path before start

## Site Selection Rules

- 10-foot clear circle from all fuel
- Remove dry leaves and duff layer
- At least 15 ft from shelter
- Never under overhanging branches
- Upwind from camp if possible
- Use existing fire ring if available

## Wind and Terrain Risk

- Check wind direction first
- Do not build in dry grass fields
- Slope accelerates fire uphill fast
- Avoid building in saddles or draws
- Rocky or sandy ground is safer
- Determine escape route NOW

## Spark Control

- Keep fire small in wind
- Use hardwood to reduce sparks
- Add screen or rock windbreak
- Never leave unattended in wind
- Have dirt or water within reach
- Keep 2-gallon water reserve

## Extinguishing Protocol

- Drown with water until steam stops
- Stir ashes and drown again
- Touch ash with bare hand to verify
- Cold-to-touch is the only standard
- Spread and drown dispersed coals
- Never leave before fully cold

## What Can Go Wrong

- Wind picks up during sleep
- Embers roll from ring onto duff
- Fire "dead" but coals underground
- Nearby tree roots catch fire below

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Full extinguish verified by
  hand touch in under 5 minutes
- Sign of mastery: Zero ember
  migration outside cleared zone
