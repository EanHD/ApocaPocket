# Candle Making

## Overview

- Tallow (rendered fat) is most accessible
- Beeswax burns cleaner with less smoke
- Rush light: simplest form; fat-soaked pith
- Dipping builds layers; mold gives shape

## Materials Needed

- Rendered tallow or beeswax
- Twisted plant fiber or cotton wick
- Tall container for dipping or clay mold
- String to hang between dips

## Wick Preparation

- Twist plant fibers: cattail, cotton, nettle
- Braid 3 strands for even, slow burn
- Pre-treat wick in saltwater and dry fully
- Dry completely before use

## Dipped Candle Method

- Melt fat in tall narrow container
- Dip wick; remove; let cool 60 seconds
- Repeat 20-30 times building each layer
- Final candle 15-25mm diameter burns 1-2h

## Molded Candle Method

- Pour melted wax into clay or tin mold
- Thread wick through center before pour
- Cool slowly to prevent surface cracking
- Trim wick to 1cm before first burn

## Rush Light Method

- Peel bulrush leaving one strip of bark
- Soak in melted tallow 1-2 hours
- Dry on rack; support at 45 degree angle
- Burns 20-30 minutes per foot of rush
