# Activated Charcoal

## Overview

- Regular charcoal has some adsorption ability
- Activation opens pores; greatly increases area
- Used for water filtration and toxin binding
- Field activation is partial; still effective

## Chemical Activation

- Soak charcoal in 25% calcium chloride solution
- Alternative: lemon juice or zinc chloride
- Soak 24 hours; drain and rinse thoroughly
- Bake at 200C for 1 hour to drive off moisture

## Steam Activation

- Pack charcoal pieces in sealed clay tube
- Pass steam through tube from boiling water
- Collect and dry the activated charcoal
- Most effective field activation method

## Water Filtration Use

- Layer in container: grass, sand, charcoal
- Activated charcoal layer 5-10cm thick
- Water passes through; repeat if unclear
- Does not sterilize; always boil after filtering

## Emergency Medical Use

- Adult dose: 25-100g in water as thick slurry
- Adsorbs many ingested organic toxins
- Not effective for acids, alkalis, or metals
- Seek medical attention as soon as possible

## Storage and Reuse

- Store dry in sealed container
- Reactivate by heating if used for filtration
- Replace filtration charcoal every 1-2 weeks
- Used contaminated charcoal: bury or burn
