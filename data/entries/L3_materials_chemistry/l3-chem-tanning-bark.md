# Bark Tanning Hides

## Overview

- Tannin from oak, hemlock, or chestnut bark
- Binds to collagen; prevents putrefaction
- Produces firm, water-resistant leather
- Pit: months; float or vat tanning: weeks

## Materials Needed

- Scraped, de-haired hide (raw)
- Oak or hemlock inner bark (pounded)
- Large vat, barrel, or pit
- Stirring stick

## Bark Preparation

- Pound fresh inner bark to expose tannin
- Simmer bark in water 30-60 minutes
- Dark tea-colored water = tannin bath
- Prepare weak and strong baths separately

## Tanning Process

- Start hide in weak tannin bath
- Move to progressively stronger baths
- Stir or fold hide daily for even penetration
- Thin hides: 2-4 weeks; thick: months

## Finishing

- Remove hide; rinse in clean water
- Oil while damp with tallow or fish oil
- Stretch while drying to prevent stiffness
- Finished leather is firm but workable

## Signs of Completion

- Cross-section cut shows uniform brown color
- No white center remaining in thick section
- Does not rot when repeatedly wet and dried
