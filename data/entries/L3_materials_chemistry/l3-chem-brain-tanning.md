# Brain Tanning Hides

## Overview

- Every animal has enough brain to tan its hide
- Brain emulsion opens fiber structure; stays soft
- Produces softest leather (true buckskin quality)
- Must be smoked to become waterproof

## Materials Needed

- Scraped, de-haired hide
- Animal brain (same size or larger animal)
- Warm water; staking beam or frame
- Rotten wood for smoking

## Brain Emulsion Prep

- Mash brain with warm water to smooth paste
- Mix 1 brain to 1-2 quarts warm water
- Egg yolks are a usable substitute
- Keep emulsion warm but not boiling

## Working the Hide

- Wring brain solution into hide thoroughly
- Work hide by hand repeatedly while drying
- Stake over beam; work all sections evenly
- Re-wet and re-work for maximum softness

## Smoking Process

- Sew hide into cone or tube shape
- Suspend over smoldering rotten wood
- Smoke until hide turns tan-brown color
- Smoke both sides; 30-60 min each side

## Final Product Notes

- Smoked hide is washable and stays soft
- Unsmoked hide stiffens hard when wet
- Smoke color: cream to dark brown range
- Re-work while re-wetting keeps it soft
