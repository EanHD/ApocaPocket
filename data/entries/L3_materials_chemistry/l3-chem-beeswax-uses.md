# Beeswax Uses

## Overview

- Hard natural wax; melting point 63C (145F)
- Waterproof, non-toxic, long shelf life
- Versatile binding and finishing material
- Harvest from comb after honey extraction

## Harvesting

- Melt comb in water bath; wax floats on top
- Filter through cloth while still liquid
- Pour into block molds; cool for storage
- Dark wax from old comb still functional

## Waterproofing Uses

- Rub onto thread before sewing leather
- Melt into cloth for water resistance
- Coat wood bowls and containers
- Protect metal tools from rust and oxidation

## Adhesive Applications

- Add 10-20% to pine pitch glue for flex
- Lubricates saw blades and wooden plane soles
- Stiffens bowstrings for water resistance
- Mixed with pigment for encaustic painting

## Wood Finishing

- Melt into warm wood surface with rag
- Buff when cool for smooth water-resist finish
- Mix with turpentine for easier application
- Reapply annually for outdoor furniture

## Other Uses

- Coat stitching thread to prevent rot
- Mix with fat for lip balm and skin salve
- Coat sinew bowstring to shed water
- Stiffen rawhide lashings for durability
