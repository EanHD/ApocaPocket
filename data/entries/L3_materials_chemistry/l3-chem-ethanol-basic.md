# Basic Ethanol Distill

## Overview

- Ferment sugar source to 5-12% wash first
- Ethanol boiling point 78C; water is 100C
- Heat wash; collect ethanol vapors via condenser
- Fuel grade: 85-95%; drinking grade: 40-60%

## Mash Preparation

- Grain: crack corn or barley; add hot water
- Fruit: crush and allow wild yeast to start
- Potatoes: cook and mash; add yeast
- Target 10-15% fermentable sugar in wash

## Still Construction

- Heat-proof pot with tight-fitting lid
- Clay or copper tube outlet from lid hole
- Run tube through cold water bath to cool
- Collect distillate from cool tube end

## Distillation Process

- Heat mash slowly; collect from 78-100C
- Discard first 50ml per gallon (foreshots)
- Collect main run separately (hearts)
- Stop when vapor smells harsh (tails fraction)

## Safety Critical Points

- Methanol in foreshots: blindness and death
- Seal all joints; test for leaks with steam
- Keep open flame far from any vapor release
- Never seal still completely; always vent

## Fuel Grade Notes

- 70%+ burns in alcohol lamp and stoves
- 85%+ best for fuel applications
- Mix 10-20% with wood gas for engine use
- Not suitable for drinking without proper cuts
