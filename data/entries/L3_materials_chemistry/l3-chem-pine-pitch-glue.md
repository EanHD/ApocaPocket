# Pine Pitch Adhesive

## Overview

- Raw pitch is brittle; additives improve flex
- Classic ratio: 3 parts pitch, 1 charcoal
- Add 10-20% beeswax or fat for flexibility
- Hafting, waterproofing, repair applications

## Gathering Pitch

- Collect dried sap blobs from pine wounds
- Fresh resin also usable; dried is stronger
- Large branch wounds yield most material
- Harvest in warm months for liquid flow

## Mixing Process

- Melt pitch over low heat; do not boil
- Filter out bark debris with a stick
- Add powdered charcoal; stir thoroughly
- Add small amount of fat or beeswax last

## Application

- Keep warm and fluid while applying
- Apply with stick while just-molten
- Press parts together; hold until cool
- Reapply heat with ember to reposition

## Strengthening the Bond

- Wrap sinew or cordage over pitched joint
- Charcoal powder prevents brittleness
- Fat prevents shrinkage from temp change
- More charcoal = stiffer; more fat = flexible

## Primary Uses

- Hafting stone arrowheads and knife blades
- Waterproofing seams in bark containers
- Patching holes in baskets or canoes
- Securing feather fletching to arrow shafts
