# Rabbit Skin Glue

## Overview

- Lighter, lower gel than heavy cattle hide glue
- Traditional artist canvas sizing agent
- Used for sinew backing on self bows
- Same process as hide glue; shorter boil

## Materials

- Whole rabbit skin or scrap pieces
- Cold water for soaking; hot for extraction
- Pot, strainer, storage container

## Process

- Soak skin 12 hours in cold water
- Drain; add fresh water; simmer 2-4 hours
- Skim surface; never boil vigorously
- Strain; reduce to light gel consistency

## Concentration Guide

- Sizing: very thin; barely gels when cold
- Adhesive: moderate; firm gel when chilled
- Strong bond: firm gel that barely dents
- Over-concentrated: brittle and cracks dry

## Bow Backing Use

- Apply warm thin coat to dry bow back (flat side)
- Lay sinew strips while glue is still tacky
- Apply successive thin layers; alternate direction
- Allow 24 hours between layers to cure fully

## Storage

- Refrigerate liquid up to one week
- Freeze blocks for longer-term storage
- Reconstitute dried blocks in warm water
- Discard if mold or foul odor present
