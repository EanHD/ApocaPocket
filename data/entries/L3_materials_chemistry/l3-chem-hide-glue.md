# Hide Glue Making

## Overview

- Collagen from hide scraps forms strong glue
- Strongest traditional woodworking adhesive
- Reversible with moisture and heat
- Sets quickly; fully cured in 24 hours

## Materials Needed

- Hide scraps, hooves, ear cartilage, tendons
- Clean water, pot for simmering
- Strainer; shallow pan for setting
- Wooden spoon

## Extraction Process

- Soak scraps overnight in cold water
- Drain; add fresh water to cover in pot
- Simmer (never boil hard) for 4-8 hours
- Stir frequently; skim foam from surface

## Concentration Check

- Strain solids; return liquid to pot
- Reduce by simmering until syrupy texture
- Test: drop on cold surface should gel firm
- Too thick: add water; too thin: reduce more

## Setting and Storage

- Pour into shallow pan; gel overnight
- Cut into blocks or strips when cold
- Dried blocks store months in cool place
- Reheat in water bath to liquefy for use

## Uses

- Furniture and tool handle joinery
- Bow lamination: sinew backing on stave
- Sizing canvas for painting or weatherproofing
- Binding charcoal for drawing sticks
