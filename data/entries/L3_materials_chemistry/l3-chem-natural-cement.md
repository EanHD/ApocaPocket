# Natural Cement

## Overview

- Plain lime mortar needs air to carbonate
- Hydraulic cement sets even in wet conditions
- Pozzolans react with lime to form C-S-H bonds
- Romans used Pozzuoli volcanic ash; lasts today

## Pozzolan Sources

- Volcanic ash: best natural pozzolan available
- Fired clay: grind broken brick or old pottery
- Pumice: porous volcanic rock ground fine
- Ground slag from iron smelting operations

## Making the Mix

- Burn limestone; slake to lime putty first
- Grind pozzolan material to fine powder
- Mix 2 parts lime putty to 1 part pozzolan
- Add sand; mix to working consistency

## Standard Mix Ratios

- Mortar: 1 lime, 1 pozzolan, 2-3 sand parts
- Plaster: 1 lime, 0.5 pozzolan, 2 sand
- Underwater use: increase pozzolan fraction
- Add water slowly; mix to stiff paste

## Setting and Curing

- Sets in 1-7 days depending on pozzolan type
- Keep moist during first week of curing
- Full strength in 28 days or more
- Stronger than plain lime mortar over time

## Field Activity Test

- Mix small batch; allow to cure underwater
- Hard in 24-48 hours = pozzolan is active
- Regular lime mortar dissolves when submerged
- Active pozzolan withstands full water immersion
