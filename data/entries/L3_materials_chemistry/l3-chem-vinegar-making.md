# Making Vinegar

## Overview

- Stage 1: yeast ferments sugar to alcohol
- Stage 2: bacteria convert alcohol to acetic acid
- Any fruit juice or grain wash works
- Finished vinegar: 4-8% acetic acid

## Materials Needed

- Fruit juice, grain mash, or dilute alcohol
- Wild yeast (fruit skins, open air)
- Wide-mouth container with cloth cover
- Warm location 60-90F (16-32C)

## Stage 1 Alcohol

- Press fruit or make grain mash with water
- Add wild yeast or unwashed fruit skins
- Cover with cloth; ferment 1-2 weeks
- Bubbling stops when fermentation complete

## Stage 2 Acetification

- Transfer to wide-mouth open vessel
- Add mother culture if available from prior batch
- Leave cloth-covered; maximize air contact
- Stir daily; vinegar forms in 4-8 weeks

## Mother Culture

- Gelatinous mat of cellulose and bacteria
- Save from finished batch to start next
- Speeds future batches to 2-3 weeks
- Treat it gently; do not disturb once started

## Testing and Uses

- Sharp sour smell and taste; no rot odor
- Use for preservation, cleaning, mordant
- Neutralizes lye; identifies bases in testing
- Rinse for wounds and skin conditions
