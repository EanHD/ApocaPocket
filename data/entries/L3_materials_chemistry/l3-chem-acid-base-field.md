# Acids and Bases Field

## Overview

- Acids: sour taste, react with metals, pH < 7
- Bases: slippery feel, bitter taste, pH > 7
- Neutral: pure water at pH 7.0
- Neutralization: acid + base = salt + water

## Natural Indicators

- Red cabbage juice: pink=acid, green=base
- Turmeric powder: yellow=neutral, red=base
- Blackberry juice: pink=acid, green=base
- Boil red cabbage; use cooled liquid as strips

## Common Field Acids

- Vinegar: acetic acid (5-8% concentration)
- Citrus or sour berry juice: citric acid
- Fermented foods: lactic acid (weaker)
- Spoiled milk: lactic and butyric acids

## Common Field Bases

- Wood ash lye: strong potassium hydroxide
- Lime water: calcium hydroxide (moderate)
- Baking soda if available: very mild base
- Seawater: very slightly basic at pH 8.1

## Neutralization Uses

- Acid soil: add wood ash or lime powder
- Lye spill on skin: pour vinegar to neutralize
- Insect sting: apply baking soda paste
- pH test before making soap or dyeing

## pH Scale Reference

- 1-3: strong acids (battery acid level)
- 4-6: weak acids (vinegar, fruit juice)
- 7: neutral (pure distilled water)
- 8-10: weak bases (baking soda, seawater)
- 11-14: strong bases (ammonia, lye)
