# Wood Ash Uses

## Overview

- Contains potassium, calcium, phosphorus
- Hardwood ash most useful (higher potash)
- Store dry; wet ash loses potency quickly
- pH 9-11; highly alkaline when moistened

## Lye Production

- Leach with water for potassium hydroxide
- Used in soap making and hide processing
- Concentrate weak leachate by boiling down
- Hardwood ash (oak, hickory) gives most potash

## Garden Fertilizer

- Apply 1-2 cups per square meter of soil
- Raises pH; beneficial for acid soils
- Source of potassium and calcium nutrients
- Avoid on acid-preferring plants

## Cleaning and Hygiene

- Mixed with water makes alkaline wash solution
- Scours pots and utensils effectively
- Degreases leather, hides, and metal tools
- Diluted in bathwater as skin cleanser

## Pest Control Uses

- Dust on plant leaves to repel soft insects
- Mix with stored grain to repel weevils
- Coat meat before smoking to repel flies
- Sprinkle around camp to deter crawling pests

## Other Uses

- Dry abrasive cleaning powder for cookware
- Mild tooth cleaning powder
- Neutralize acid soil or acid chemical spills
- Flux in metal smelting to remove slag
