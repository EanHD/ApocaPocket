# Leather Waterproofing

## Overview

- Tanning removes natural moisture-binding oils
- Fat filling replaces oils and waterproofs fiber
- Beeswax fills pores; tallow conditions fibers
- Apply to damp leather for best absorption

## Materials

- Rendered tallow or lard
- Beeswax (optional enhancer of water resistance)
- Neatsfoot oil (from boiled cattle hooves/leg)
- Waterproof tin or clay container for mix

## Basic Tallow Treatment

- Warm tallow until just liquid; not hot
- Work into damp leather by hand firmly
- Massage into all surfaces and stitching seams
- Allow to absorb fully; repeat if leather dry

## Beeswax-Tallow Mix

- Melt 2 parts tallow to 1 part beeswax
- Stir well while liquid; pour into tin
- Apply with cloth to slightly warm leather
- Buff off excess for smooth water-resist finish

## Neatsfoot Oil

- Boil cattle hooves and lower leg bones
- Skim yellow oil from water surface
- Best single conditioner for supple leather
- Apply to dry leather; allow overnight absorb

## Application Tips

- Warm leather before applying any fat
- Work in small circular sections at a time
- Allow overnight absorption before wearing
- Repeat treatment after prolonged wet exposure
