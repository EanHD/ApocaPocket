# Lye from Wood Ash

## Overview

- Wood ash contains potassium carbonate
- Water leaches out alkaline compounds
- Result: potassium hydroxide solution
- Hardwood ash yields stronger lye

## Best Ash Sources

- Hardwoods: oak, hickory, beech preferred
- Avoid coal ash; contains sulfur compounds
- Fresh white ash best; grey reduces yield
- Mix ratio: 3 parts ash to 1 part water

## Leaching Setup

- Line barrel or bucket with straw at drain
- Pack ash; pour water slowly from top
- Collect brown lye liquid from bottom drain
- Repeat passes until liquid runs clear

## Concentration Tests

- Egg float: egg sinks slightly = too weak
- Egg float: egg floats halfway = correct
- Feather test: lye dissolves feather in minutes
- Boil down 30-50% to concentrate weak lye

## Safety and Warnings

- Lye destroys skin and eyes on contact
- Keep vinegar nearby as neutralizer
- Use wooden or clay containers; not aluminum
- Label all lye containers clearly and keep away
