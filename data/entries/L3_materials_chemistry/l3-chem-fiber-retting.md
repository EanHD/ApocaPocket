# Fiber Retting

## Overview

- Retting breaks down pectin binding fibers
- Bast fibers in stem cortex are the target
- Water retting uses microbial action
- Dew retting uses alternating wet-dry cycles

## Best Fiber Plants

- Flax: finest fiber; excellent for linen thread
- Stinging nettle: strong; widespread globally
- Hemp: coarse but very strong; rope use
- Dogbane: excellent native North American fiber

## Water Retting Process

- Harvest stalks at flowering or early seed stage
- Bundle loosely; submerge in slow-moving water
- Weight bundles to keep fully submerged
- Ret 1-3 weeks; water turns progressively brown

## Dew Retting Method

- Spread stalks on grass in thin single layers
- Turn stalks daily; natural dew provides moisture
- Takes 2-6 weeks total; minimal odor
- Produces slightly coarser fiber than water ret

## Testing Completion

- Pull stem; fibers separate easily from wood
- Stem snaps; fibers peel away from core cleanly
- Under-retted: fibers still stuck in woody core
- Over-retted: fibers become weak and tear easily

## Post-Retting Process

- Dry retted bundles completely before processing
- Break and scutch woody core away from fibers
- Hackle (comb through) to align and separate
- Spin or twist directly for cordage use
