# Alcohol as Fuel

## Overview

- Ethanol burns clean with very little soot
- 70%+ concentration required for reliable flame
- Miscible with water; low proof burns poorly
- Powers lamps, small stoves, fire-starting

## Minimum Proof

- 70% (140 proof): burns readily; good fuel
- 60% (120 proof): burns weakly; poor for stove
- Below 50%: will not sustain a flame alone
- Denatured or fuel-grade 85-95% burns best

## Simple Alcohol Lamp

- Glass or clay bottle with narrow neck
- Cotton or plant fiber wick through hole
- Fill 3/4 full with fuel; wick draws by capillary
- Trim wick to 5mm; burns 4-8 hours per fill

## Camp Stove Use

- Two flat stones as windbreak and support
- Pour alcohol into small clay or tin dish
- Light and simmer food over flame directly
- Boils 1 liter of water in about 10 minutes

## Fire-Starting Use

- Soak tinder in alcohol before sparking
- Ignites from single spark at 70%+
- Alcohol-soaked cloth stores for months
- Alcohol gel (mixed with salt): safer storage

## Safety

- Mark container clearly; never in food bottles
- Alcohol fire: smother with wet cloth
- Store in sealed container away from heat
- Evaporates quickly from open container
