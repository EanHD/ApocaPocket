# Log Cabin Construction

## Site Selection
- Level ground or slight slope (drainage)
- **South-facing** for passive solar heat
- Near water but **above flood level**
- Protected from wind by terrain or trees
- 100+ straight trees within hauling distance
- Avoid: flood plains, avalanche paths, dead-tree zones, swampy ground

## Foundation
- Large flat rocks or rot-resistant logs (cedar, locust) as ground sills
- Elevate floor **6-12 inches** above grade  -  prevents rot and moisture
- Pack **gravel bed** under foundation for drainage

## Log Selection
- Straight, **6-10 inch** diameter conifers (pine, spruce, fir)  -  light and easy to work
- 10x12 cabin: **40-50 wall logs** (14-16 ft long walls, 12-14 ft short) + **15-20** for roof/floor

## Notching Logs
**Saddle notch** is the simplest cut:
1. Scoop a **half-round notch** in the bottom of each log
2. Notch seats over the log below it
3. **Alternate butt/tip ends** each row to keep walls level
4. Chink gaps with moss, clay, or wood strips

## Wall Construction
- Build up **8-10 rounds** for ~7 ft wall height
- **Cut door AFTER walls are up**  -  easier than framing as you go
- Frame all openings with vertical boards pinned to log ends
- Leave **2" gap at top of frames** for settling

## Roof Construction
- Ridge pole on **two forked posts** at the peak
- Rafters: 4-6" poles leaning from ridge to wall at **30-45 degrees**
- Cover with split shakes, bark slabs, or thatch  -  **layer like shingles, overlap 50%**
- **Overhang walls 12-18"** to protect from rain and wall rot

## Floor & Chinking
- **Floor A:** split logs flat-side up, fitted tightly together
- **Floor B:** packed clay (faster and warmer than expected if above grade)
- **Chinking:** clay + dried grass + water to thick paste; apply 2-3" over gaps; re-chink annually

## Door Construction
- Split planks with **Z-bracing** (2 horizontal boards + 1 diagonal)
- **Hinges:** wooden pin hinges or leather strap hinges
- Fit loosely to allow settling; seal edges with clay daub in winter

## Maintenance
- **Critical:** keep roof intact  -  a leaking roof destroys walls
- Re-chink gaps every year; ensure drainage flows away from foundation
- Replace rotting ground-contact logs before rot spreads inward
- Well-built cabin lasts **30-50+ years** with basic maintenance
