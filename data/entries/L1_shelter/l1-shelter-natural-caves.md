# Cave Selection Safety

## Why It Matters

- Caves seem perfect but often are not
- Wrong cave choice can be fatal
- Bear dens look like shelter caves
- Flooding risk in rain is extreme
- Unstable ceilings collapse silently
- Proper assessment takes 10 minutes

## Initial Hazard Check

- Look for scratches on entrance rock
- Claw marks = large animal present
- Bones or scat inside = occupied
- Smell test: strong musk = wildlife
- Dark stains at entrance = flood
- Debris line inside shows flood level

## Structural Safety

- Check ceiling with torch/light
- Loose rock, cracks = avoid
- Tap ceiling with stick - hollow sound
- Rub walls: crumbling = unstable
- Narrow caves with tight fit risky
- Wide flat ceiling safest choice

## Ventilation Assessment

- Light a small fire at entrance
- Smoke should draft outward
- No draft = dangerous for fire use
- Use fire near entrance if ventilation
  is questionable, not deep inside
- Single exit is high-risk for CO

## Flooding Risk

- Check outside drainage toward cave
- River cave = avoid in rain season
- Slick mineral-stained walls = floods
- Cave should be above stream level
- Check weather before committing
- Plan escape route before sleeping

## What Can Go Wrong

- Animal returns at night
- Fire smoke has no exit path
- Flash flood with one entrance
- Hypothermia from cave dampness

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Full cave assessment in
  under 10 minutes
- Sign of mastery: Correctly reject
  3 problem caves and select 1 safe
