# Quinzhee Snow Shelter

## Why It Matters

- Interior stays near 32F in -40F out
- Any snow type works, not just powder
- Saves lives in open winter terrain
- No rigid frame or tools required
- Two people can build in 3-4 hours
- Far better than exposed bivy

## Phase 1: Pile the Snow

- Pile snow into dome, 7 ft high
- 10-12 ft wide at the base
- Use all snow types including wet
- Pile equipment inside if needed
- Do not compact pile while building
- Mark center with a stick

## Sintering: Wait Time

- Wait 2-3 hours before hollowing
- Snow crystals bond during this time
- Skipping sinter = collapse risk
- Place 12-inch sticks as depth guides
- Insert sticks radially all around
- Sticks ensure even 8-10 inch walls

## Phase 2: Hollowing

- Dig entrance tunnel from downhill
- Tunnel up into dome interior
- Remove equipment buried inside
- Follow sticks inward to stop digging
- Smooth inside ceiling (no drips)
- Dig sleeping shelf above door level

## Ventilation and Sleep

- Poke vent hole with pole at apex
- Mark vent outside with stick/ski
- Keep shovel inside in case of drift
- Cold air sinks, warm air rises in
- Sleep on insulated pad on shelf
- Candle warms interior noticeably

## What Can Go Wrong

- Sinter skipped - wall too thin
- Vent blocked - CO2 buildup
- Entrance too wide - cold floods in
- Rain or warm weather collapses dome

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Complete shelter in 4 hrs
  for 2 people with sinter included
- Sign of mastery: Interior temp
  10+ degrees warmer than outside
