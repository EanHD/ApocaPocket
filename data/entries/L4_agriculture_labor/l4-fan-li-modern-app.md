# Fan Li Modern Methods

## Overview

- Fan Li's principles need no technology
- Replace silk with any fiber crop if needed
- Replace carp with locally available species
- Replace rice with any grain suited to flooding
- Core loops work regardless of species used
- Adaptability was built into the system

## Minimal Starting Kit

- One small pond (even 50 sq m works)
- 20-30 fish fingerlings (any carp family)
- Seeds for one grain or vegetable crop
- 5-10 laying birds (ducks or chickens)
- Compost pile to cycle organic matter
- That is all needed to begin

## Temperate Adaptations

- Replace rice with millet or water-loving crops
- Common carp + trout (separate cold pond)
- Muscovy ducks tolerate cold well
- Willow instead of mulberry on pond banks
- Willow leaves also edible by fish
- Same nutrient cycling principles apply

## Arid Adaptations

- Use lined or clay ponds to reduce seepage
- Smaller ponds lose less to evaporation
- Cover ponds with shade structure if needed
- Tilapia tolerates higher temperatures
- Duckweed as supplemental fish feed
- Harvest water from roof into pond

## Post-Grid Inputs

- No electricity needed for small ponds
- Aeration via small waterfall or paddle
- Water lifting via hand pump or gravity
- Netting hand-made from plant fiber
- Seine net from paracord and sticks
- All record-keeping on paper or device

## Integration

- Pond mud to garden beds (phosphorus)
- Grain chaff and bran as fish supplement
- Livestock manure to pond (diluted)
- Biochar added to pond mud compost
- Cover crops grown on pond banks
- Root vegetables grown in pond mud plots

## Failure Modes to Avoid

- Starting too large before learning system
- Neglecting daily observation
- Ignoring early signs of oxygen stress
- Mixing incompatible species
- Failing to save breeding stock
- Abandoning system during hard year 1-2
