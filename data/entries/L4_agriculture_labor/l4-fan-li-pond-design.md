# Polyculture Pond Design

## Site Selection

- Clay subsoil preferred to hold water
- Test: dig 30cm, wet soil, squeeze - holds shape?
- Avoid sandy or gravelly subsoil
- Gentle slope allows gravity drainage
- Near water source for refilling
- Downhill from garden beds for runoff use
- Minimum 6 hours direct sunlight daily

## Pond Dimensions

- Fan Li recommended 6 mu (~4000 sq m) max
- Small ponds (200-500 sq m) easier to manage
- Rectangular shape simplifies netting/harvest
- Depth: shallow end 0.5m, deep end 1.8-2m
- Depth gradient supports multiple species
- Length:width ratio 3:2 ideal for water flow

## Bank Construction

- Slope: 3 horizontal to 1 vertical (3:1)
- Compact banks in 15cm lifts
- Top bank width minimum 1 meter
- Raise banks 30cm above max water level
- Plant grass on banks immediately
- Mulberry or other trees on outer bank edge

## Water Zones

- Shallow zone (0-0.5m): ducks, frogs, wading
- Mid zone (0.5-1m): grass carp, crucian
- Deep zone (1-2m): common carp overwinter
- Surface: bighead/silver carp feed on plankton
- Bottom: mud carp, catfish, crawfish
- Each zone harvests different food sources

## Inlet and Outlet

- Inlet: screened, brings fresh water in
- Outlet: screened standpipe at deep end
- Screen mesh 1cm for fish containment
- Allow 10-20% water exchange per month
- After rain, overflow outlet prevents flooding
- Gravity drain for harvest: slope is key

## Productivity Features

- One large rock or log on bottom for refuge
- Aquatic plants in shallow zone (20% coverage)
- Submerged plants oxygenate water at night
- Floating plants (water hyacinth) filter nutrients
- Do not exceed 30% surface cover
- Open water needed for fish to surface feed

## Winter Design

- Deep zone must be below frost line
- Fish cluster in deep zone in cold weather
- Do not break ice vigorously - stresses fish
- Small hole in ice allows gas exchange
- Reduce feeding when water below 10C
