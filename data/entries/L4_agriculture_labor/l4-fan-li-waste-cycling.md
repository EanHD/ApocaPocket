# Waste Cycling System

## Core Principle

- Every output in the system is an input
- Fan Li: "the skilled farmer wastes nothing"
- Waste = food out of place
- Goal: zero organic matter leaves the system
- Nutrient flows in loops, not lines
- Longer the loop, more productive the system

## Animal Waste Flows

- Duck waste drops into paddy water
- Dissolved nutrients feed algae and plankton
- Plankton feeds filter-feeding fish
- Fish waste settles as bottom mud
- Mud dredged onto field and bank edges
- Crops grow, feed animals, cycle repeats

## Household Waste

- All food scraps composted or to pig/poultry
- Pig manure slurried into pond (diluted)
- Human urine (dilute 10:1) for fertilizer
- Wood ash to pond or garden for potash
- Bones boiled for broth, ash to soil
- Bathwater routed to garden beds

## Crop Residue Cycling

- Rice straw: mulch, animal bedding, compost
- Straw soaked in water as silage for fish
- Vegetable tops to rabbits or chickens
- Chicken manure to compost in 2 weeks
- Green manure crops tilled into paddy
- Aquatic weeds removed, fed to grass carp

## The Silkworm Loop

- Mulberry leaf to silkworm
- Silkworm frass to fish pond
- Spent pupae fed to fish in pond
- Pond mud applied to mulberry bank
- Mulberry grows vigorously, cycle continues
- Each pass increases total fertility

## Measuring Efficiency

- Good system: no nutrient export needed
- Soil improves year over year
- Fish grow larger per season over time
- Fewer inputs needed as system matures
- Surplus production appears in year 3-5
- Excess sold or preserved as food

## What Breaks the Loop

- Selling ALL harvest with no return
- Using chemicals that harm soil organisms
- Draining pond waste away vs. reusing it
- Buying in feed without return nutrients
- Monoculture breaks the cycling web
