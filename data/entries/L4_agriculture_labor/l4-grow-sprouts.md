# Sprouting Seeds

## Why Sprout

- Protein in 2-5 days with no equipment
- Increases protein digestibility vs dry seed
- Produces vitamin C from stored seeds
- Reduces antinutrients (phytates, lectins)
- No soil, no light, minimal space required
- Works from food storage seeds

## Seeds Safe to Sprout

- Lentils: fastest (2-3 days), most reliable
- Mung beans: classic, mild flavor
- Chickpeas: 3-4 days, nutty flavor
- Radish seed: spicy, 4-5 days
- Wheat and rye: sweet wheatgrass in 5 days
- Sunflower seeds (hulled)
- Peas: large, 3-4 days

## Basic Jar Method

- Soak 2 tablespoons seeds in water 8-12 hours
- Drain water, rinse well
- Place jar at 45-degree angle
  (drainage position, holes in lid)
- Rinse with fresh water twice per day
- Keep in warm location (18-25C)
- Harvest when 2-5cm long

## Rinsing is Critical

- Twice-daily rinsing prevents bacterial growth
- Morning and evening routine works well
- Use clean water each time
- Drain completely after each rinse
- Sitting in water causes rot and mold
- If mold appears: discard and restart

## Harvest and Eating

- Harvest when sprout tail is 2-5x seed size
- Final rinse, drain well
- Eat raw or lightly stir-fried
- Refrigerate and use within 3-5 days
- High moisture: do not store long
- Lentil sprouts can be cooked normally

## Scaling Up

- Use any container with drainage holes
- Basket lined with cloth
- Stacked trays with cloth layers
- Large batch: use salad spinner for rinsing
- 200g dry lentils = 600g sprouts (3 days)

## Nutritional Impact

- Vitamin C: increases 10-30x during sprouting
- B vitamins increase significantly
- Protein more digestible after sprouting
- Enzyme activity increases: aids digestion
- Iron and zinc more bioavailable
- Essential supplement to stored-grain diet
