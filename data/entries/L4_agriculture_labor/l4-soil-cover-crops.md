# Cover Crops

## Purpose of Cover Crops

- Protect bare soil from rain compaction
- Fix nitrogen if legume species used
- Suppress weed growth with dense canopy
- Build organic matter as green manure
- Roots improve soil structure
- Provide wildlife and pollinator habitat

## Fall/Winter Crops

- Winter rye: most cold-hardy, any climate
  Sow 6 weeks before frost
- Hairy vetch: nitrogen fixer, winter-hardy
  Sow with rye for combined benefit
- Crimson clover: beautiful, good N fixer
  Sow 4-6 weeks before frost
- Oats: winter-kills, easy spring cleanup
  Mulch-in-place when frozen

## Spring/Summer Crops

- Buckwheat: fast (6 weeks), smothers weeds
  Excellent between spring and fall crops
- Phacelia: ornamental, extreme bee attractor
  Till under before seed set
- Sunn hemp: tropical, high biomass
  Warm season, fixes nitrogen well
- Cowpea: drought-tolerant nitrogen fixer

## Tillage Radish

- Large taproot penetrates compacted soil
- Winter-kills in most climates
- Frozen root rots in place leaving channel
- Plant in late summer, harvest in spring
- Excellent pre-tillage alternative
- Following crops benefit from open channels

## When to Incorporate

- Till or cut at bud/flowering stage
- Maximum nitrogen before seed is set
- Allow 2-3 weeks to decompose before planting
- No-till: roll-crimp at flowering stage
- Chop and drop on surface also works
- Roots decompose underground releasing N

## Seeding Methods

- Broadcast seed by hand over tilled bed
- Rake in lightly, no deep burial
- Overseed into standing crops at
  last cultivation (interseeding)
- Use seed drill for large areas
- Target seed-to-soil contact for germination

## Seed Rate Guide

- 1 kg rye seed covers ~100 sq m
- 500g clover covers ~100 sq m
- 500g buckwheat covers ~50 sq m
- These amounts are easy to save from
  your own garden year to year
