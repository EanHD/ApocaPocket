# Duck Integration

## Ducks vs Chickens

- Tolerates wet conditions better than chickens
- Resistant to most poultry diseases
- Superior pest control in wet gardens
- Excellent egg producers: 200-300 eggs/yr
- Eat slugs, snails, mosquito larvae
- Integrate with pond and rice systems

## Best Breeds

- Khaki Campbell: most productive layer
  (300+ eggs per year)
- Indian Runner: excellent forager, comical
- Muscovy: quiet, good meat, tolerate cold
- Welsh Harlequin: dual-purpose, calm
- Pekin: fast-growing meat duck
- For integration with ponds: any breed

## Housing Requirements

- Simple three-sided shelter acceptable
- 0.5 sq m per duck minimum
- No roosting bars needed (sleep on ground)
- Nest boxes ground level
- Access to water for swimming preferred
- Small plastic tub works if no pond

## Feeding

- Duck pellets or crumble 100-150g per day
- Foraging reduces feed cost significantly
- Supplement: greens, grains, aquatic plants
- No grit needed if foraging (find own)
- Ducks need to submerge heads in water
  to clear nostrils - provide deep bucket

## Pest Control Use

- Release into garden in late afternoon
- Ducks patrol for slugs, snails, cutworms
- Retrieve before nightfall
- Do not release among small seedlings
  (they trample and eat)
- Safe around established plants over 30cm

## Pond Integration

- Ducks on pond eat: mosquito larvae, algae
- Duck waste fertilizes fish pond
- Collect eggs from shelter each morning
- 4-6 ducks per 100 sq m pond maximum
- More ducks: too much waste, algae bloom
- System self-balances at correct density

## Duck vs Chicken

- Eggs: duck eggs larger (80g vs 60g)
- Egg flavor: richer, preferred for baking
- Disease resistance: ducks far superior
- Noise: drakes quieter than roosters
- Wet climate: ducks preferred
- Dry/hot climate: chickens preferred
