# Sandy Soil Fixes

## Identifying Sandy Soil

- Gritty texture, falls apart when squeezed
- Water drains within minutes of irrigation
- Dries out quickly between rains
- Low nutrient holding capacity
- Light colored (pale tan or grey)
- Jar test: all material in bottom layer

## Organic Matter Fix

- Organic matter is water sponge of soil
- 1% organic matter holds 170L water/ton soil
- Apply compost 10cm thick per season
- Work into top 30cm for best results
- Repeat every season until soil darkens
- Target soil color: rich brown, not tan

## Biochar Benefits

- Biochar holds both water and nutrients
- Does not decompose like compost
- Single application lasts centuries
- Apply 5-10% by volume into top 20cm
- Charge with compost or urine first
- One-time investment with lasting return

## Mulching is Critical

- Bare sandy soil dries in hours
- Apply 10-15cm organic mulch always
- Straw, wood chips, leaf mold all work
- Replenish as mulch decomposes
- Mulch doubles effective rainfall use
- Wood chip mulch becomes compost in place

## Sandy Cover Crops

- Buckwheat is ideal sand-tolerant cover
- Clover tolerates dry, sandy conditions
- Vetch adds nitrogen and bulk
- Their roots improve soil structure
- Organic matter from roots stays underground
- Roots leave channels improving water hold

## Irrigation Strategy

- Sandy soil: water more often, less at once
- Deep watering pulls nutrients past roots
- Drip irrigation most efficient for sand
- Mulch reduces irrigation need by 50%+
- Water in morning to reduce evaporation

## Sandy Soil Crops

- Carrots, parsnips prefer sandy loam
- Potatoes do well in light soils
- Asparagus, garlic, herbs tolerate sand
- Avoid waterlogged crops: celery, rice
- As soil improves, expand crop selection
