# Fan Li's Fish Classic

## Overview

- Written c. 460 BC by Fan Li (Taozhu Gong)
- Full title: Taozhu Gong Yangyu Jing
- Oldest known aquaculture manual on Earth
- Describes carp pond construction and stocking
- Covers breeding ratios, harvest timing
- Still valid after 2,500 years of practice
- Transmitted orally then written by disciples

## Key Teachings

- Use 6 male carp to 1 female for breeding
- Stock pond in spring after water warms
- Ideal pond: 6 mu (~4000 sq m) or less
- Include large stones in pond for shelter
- Natural vegetation provides fish habitat
- Harvest after 3 years for best yield

## Pond Specifications

- Depth: 1-2 meters ideal
- Include shallow margins for spawning
- Muddy bottom preferred for carp
- Islands or rocks for refuge from birds
- Inlet and outlet channels needed
- Slope banks at 3:1 for stability

## Breeding Guidance

- Begin with 20 mature breeders
- 6:1 male to female ratio for carp
- Spring spawning when water > 18C
- Separate fry once 5cm to prevent predation
- Move juveniles to grow-out pond at 10cm
- Harvest mature fish, keep best breeders

## Harvest Principles

- Partial harvest, not complete pond drain
- Remove largest fish, leave medium ones
- Harvest in autumn before cold sets in
- Stagger harvests across multiple ponds
- Smoke or dry surplus immediately
- Return fish scraps to pond as feed

## Legacy

- Text preserved through Tang Dynasty copies
- Influenced all subsequent Chinese aquaculture
- Cited by Li Shizhen's Bencao Gangmu (1596)
- Modern integrated rice-fish traces to this
- FAO documented lineage to Fan Li methods

## Why It Matters

- Demonstrates 2,500 yr food security model
- No external inputs, fully self-contained
- Works at family scale to village scale
- Protein source that improves the land
- Blueprint still deployable post-collapse
