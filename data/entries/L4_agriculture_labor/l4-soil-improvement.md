# Soil Improvement

## The Problem

- Most degraded soils lack organic matter
- Organic matter feeds soil biology
- Soil biology makes nutrients available
- Nutrients feed plants
- Fix organic matter first, fertilize second
- Each 1% organic matter increase holds
  170,000 liters water per hectare more

## Compost Application

- Apply 5-10cm compost to surface
- Work in gently or leave as mulch
- Apply in fall for spring planting
- Finished compost: dark, crumbly, earthy smell
- 1 cubic meter covers 20 sq m at 5cm
- Annual application improves soil each year

## Cover Cropping

- Sow legumes in fall after harvest
- Crimson clover, vetch, winter peas
- Legumes fix nitrogen from air into soil
- Till under before flowering (green manure)
- Cereal rye holds soil through winter
- Buckwheat smothers weeds in summer fallow

## Mulch for Soil

- Apply 10-15cm straw, wood chips, leaves
- Worms and soil organisms consume from below
- Keeps soil moist and temperature stable
- Weed suppression as bonus benefit
- Wood chip mulch takes 2 years to improve soil
- Straw mulch breaks down in one season

## Biochar Addition

- Biochar holds nutrients and water long-term
- Charge biochar first: mix with compost
  or soak in urine/compost tea before use
- Apply 1-5% by volume of top 15cm soil
- One-time application lasts centuries
- Do not over-apply: 10% degrades yields

## Drainage Improvement

- Sandy soil: add clay and organic matter
- Clay soil: add gypsum (2kg per sq m)
- Clay soil: add coarse sand + compost
- Never add fine sand to clay (makes cement)
- Raised beds bypass poor drainage entirely
- Swales catch and hold water in landscape

## Signs of Success

- Earthworms increase in count
- Soil crumbles easily when dry
- Plants show deeper green color
- Water infiltrates faster each season
- Fewer weeds as crop canopy thickens
