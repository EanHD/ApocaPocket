# Transplanting

## Transplant vs Sow

- Get head start on short seasons
- Concentrate seedling care in small space
- Transplants suppress weeds faster
- Some crops do better transplanted
  (tomato, pepper, celery, brassicas)
- Crops that dislike transplanting:
  carrot, parsnip, pea, bean, corn

## Starting Transplants

- Use small pots or cell trays
- Seed-starting mix (not garden soil)
- Sow 2-3 seeds per cell, thin to one
- Keep moist but not waterlogged
- Temperature: 18-24C for germination
- Light: 14-16 hours per day ideal

## Hardening Off

- Indoor seedlings are soft and sun-sensitive
- Hardening = gradual outdoor exposure
- Day 1-2: 1 hour in shade outdoors
- Day 3-4: 2-3 hours shade/dappled light
- Day 5-6: half day partial sun
- Day 7+: full day sun exposure
- Do not rush: burned leaves set plants back

## Technique

- Choose overcast day or late afternoon
- Water seedlings well 1 hour before
- Dig hole larger than root ball
- Place plant at same depth as in pot
- Tomatoes exception: bury stem deep
- Firm soil gently, no air pockets

## Post-Transplant Water

- Water in well immediately after planting
- Use dilute seaweed solution if available
- Water daily for first week
- Look for wilting in afternoon heat
- Afternoon wilt recovers by morning: OK
- All-day wilt: needs more water immediately

## Root Shock Symptoms

- Yellowing lower leaves for 1-2 weeks
- Temporary growth stall (normal)
- Recovery usually complete within 2 weeks
- Severe wilt: shade plant for 2-3 days
- If bare-root transplanting: prune 1/3
  of top growth to balance with roots

## Transplant Calendar

- 8 weeks before last frost: peppers, tomatoes
- 6 weeks: brassicas, celery
- 4 weeks: lettuce, herbs
- 2 weeks: squash, cucumbers (frost-tender)
