# Grain Growing Basics

## Selecting Grain Types

- Winter wheat: sow fall, harvest summer
  most calorie-dense per sq m
- Spring wheat: sow spring, harvest summer
  shorter season option
- Rye: most cold-hardy, tolerates poor soil
  excellent for degraded land
- Oats: tolerates wet, cool conditions
- Buckwheat: not a true grain, fast (10 weeks)
  frost-sensitive but extremely productive

## Site Requirements

- Full sun essential for grain filling
- Well-drained soil prevents root rot
- pH 6.0-7.0 for wheat/oats
- Rye tolerates pH 5.0-7.0
- Clear site of perennial weeds first
- Grain competes poorly with weeds early

## Planting

- Broadcast seed by hand over prepared bed
- Rake in lightly to 2-3cm depth
- Winter wheat: sow 6 weeks before frost
- Spring wheat: sow as early as soil allows
- Seed rate: 10-15g per sq meter
- One sq m = ~200g grain at harvest

## Growing Season Care

- Thin broadcast if very dense (>30 stems/sqm)
- Weeding: critical in first 6 weeks
- Once grain canopy closes: weeds suppressed
- No irrigation needed if rainfall >400mm/yr
- Watch for rust (orange powder on stems)
  Rust = harvest early if severe

## Harvest Timing

- Harvest when grain is hard and golden
- Bend stalk: grain should not dent thumb
- Cut with scythe or sickle at base
- Bundle into sheaves, stand to dry
- Dry 1-2 weeks before threshing
- Damp grain will mold in storage

## Threshing

- Thresh: beat bundles against barrel or board
- Grain falls out of heads onto sheet
- Winnow: pour grain in wind (or fan)
- Chaff blows away, heavy grain falls
- Simple wooden flail: two sticks tied with cord
- Repeat twice for clean grain

## Yield Expectations

- Garden scale: 200-400g grain per sq m
- 10 sq m = 2-4 kg grain
- 100 sq m = 20-40 kg grain
- A family needs ~200 kg grain/person/year
- 1000 sq m minimum for food security
