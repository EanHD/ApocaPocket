# Microgreens

## What are Microgreens

- Seedlings harvested at 7-14 days old
- Cut just above soil line when 5-8cm tall
- Higher nutritional density than mature plant
- Up to 40x nutrients vs mature equivalent
- Grow indoors with minimal space and light
- Any seeds work: sunflower, pea, radish, kale

## What You Need

- Shallow tray (5-8cm deep)
- Growing medium: potting soil or coconut coir
- Seeds: any vegetable or herb seed
- Light: window or lamp (6+ hours)
- Water spray bottle
- Harvesting knife or scissors

## Planting Method

- Fill tray 3-5cm with moist growing medium
- Broadcast seeds densely across surface
  (touching each other is fine)
- Press seeds gently into medium
- Cover tray with another tray for blackout
- Or cover with damp cloth
- Check daily for germination

## Growing Phase

- Day 1-4: germination under cover
- Day 4-5: remove cover when 2-3cm tall
- Move to light source immediately
- Water from below if possible (less mold)
- Keep medium moist, not saturated
- Day 7-14: ready to harvest

## Harvest Technique

- Cut with sharp knife at soil level
- Harvest just before eating (freshest)
- Or harvest whole tray and refrigerate
- Lasts 3-5 days refrigerated
- Do not wash until ready to eat
- Second cut rarely successful: start new tray

## Best Seeds

- Sunflower: mild, meaty texture, popular
- Peas: sweet, substantial, high protein
- Radish: spicy, fast (5-7 days)
- Brassica mix: kale, broccoli, cabbage
- Amaranth: mild, colorful
- Cilantro: slow but distinctive flavor

## Nutrition Gaps

- Vitamin C (especially pea and sunflower)
- Vitamin K (all brassica microgreens)
- Iron (sunflower, amaranth)
- Folate (pea, brassica)
- Provides critical micronutrients in winter
