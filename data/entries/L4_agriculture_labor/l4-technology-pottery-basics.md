# Pottery Basics

## Clay Preparation

- Remove stones, grit, organic matter
- Wedge (knead) to remove air bubbles
- Air pockets cause explosions when fired
- Add grog for strength

## Hand Building  -  Pinch

- Start with ball of clay
- Push thumb in center
- Pinch walls thin and even
- Rotate as you pinch

## Hand Building  -  Coil

- Roll clay into ropes 1-2cm diameter
- Stack coils and blend inside wall
- Smooth outside gradually
- Build walls to desired height

## Drying

- Air dry slowly, 1-2 weeks
- Cover with plastic to slow drying
- Bone-dry before any firing
- Greenware is fragile, handle minimally

## Pit Firing

- Dig pit 50cm deep
- Layer: wood, pots, wood, cover
- Burn 3-4 hours, let cool overnight
- Low-fire for basic utility ware

## Basic Glazing

- Slip (liquid clay) for decoration
- Wood ash glaze: ash plus clay
- Apply to bone-dry or bisque ware
- Second firing at higher temp
