# Chicken Care

## Getting Started

- Minimum flock: 4-6 laying hens
- No rooster needed for eggs
- Rooster needed for fertile eggs and chicks
- Start with pullets (young hens) 16-20 weeks
- Or incubate fertilized eggs from neighbor
- Day-old chicks need heat lamp (35C) for 4 weeks

## Housing Requirements

- 0.3 sq m per bird inside coop minimum
- 1 sq m per bird in outdoor run
- Roosting bar: 20-30cm per bird
- Nest boxes: 1 per 4 hens (30x30cm)
- Ventilation critical: ammonia buildup harmful
- Hardware cloth, not chicken wire for security

## Feeding

- Layer pellets: 100-120g per hen per day
- Free-range reduces feed cost by 30-50%
- Calcium supplement (oyster shell) for strong eggs
- Greens, table scraps supplement feed
- Grit required for grinding food in gizzard
- Water: always fresh, 500ml per hen per day

## Egg Production

- Production begins at 20-24 weeks old
- Peak production: 5-6 eggs per hen per week
- Drops after year 2-3 significantly
- Stops during molt (feather replacement)
- Short days reduce production (less than 14hr light)
- Good winter production needs supplemental light

## Managing the Flock

- Deep litter method: 15-30cm bedding
  Turn occasionally, compost when full
- Collect eggs daily to prevent brooding
- Watch for: mites, lice (check under wings)
- Respiratory illness: isolate sick birds
- Annual molt: lower feed for 6-8 weeks

## Predator Management

- Close coop every night without exception
- Hardware cloth on all openings (not wire)
- Bury wire 30cm to deter diggers
- Motion lights, noise deterrents help
- Cover run with netting against aerial attack
- Common predators: fox, raccoon, hawk, weasel

## Meat Production

- Dual-purpose breeds (Plymouth Rock, Sussex)
- Cockerels and spent hens for meat
- Process at 16-24 weeks for table birds
- Specialized meat breeds: Cornish Cross
  (process at 8 weeks, poor layers)
