# Duck-Rice-Fish System

## Overview

- Three-tier integration: ducks, rice, fish
- Ducks eat insects, weeds, snails in paddy
- Duck waste feeds fish and fertilizes rice
- Fish aerate soil, eat duck feces, pests
- Rice provides cover and food for ducks/fish
- Aigamo method: Japan refined this c. 1980s
- Fan Li documented the core principle c. 460 BC

## Species Selection

- Muscovy or Khaki Campbell ducks best
- Small breeds preferred (less rice trampling)
- Use 15-30 ducklings per 0.1 hectare
- Common carp or crucian carp for fish layer
- Rice: tall traditional varieties withstand
  duck activity better than dwarf varieties

## Setup Sequence

- Week 1: Transplant rice seedlings
- Week 2-3: Flood paddy to 10-15cm
- Week 3: Stock fish fingerlings
- Week 4: Release 2-week-old ducklings
- Week 8: Remove ducks when rice heads
- Week 12+: Harvest fish, then rice

## Duck Management

- Release ducklings in morning, shelter at night
- Simple floating shelter on water works
- Ducks need no supplemental feed if
  paddy is well-stocked with insects
- Guard against predators at night
- Do NOT leave ducks in after heading -
  they will eat the grain

## Nutrient Flow

- One duck produces ~50g waste per day
- Duck waste = nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium
- Fish metabolize waste into plant-available form
- Rice roots absorb directly from water column
- System is self-fertilizing when balanced
- Eliminates need for external fertilizer

## Pest Control Benefits

- Ducks consume: brown planthoppers,
  stem borers, leaf rollers, snails, weeds
- Estimated 80-90% reduction in pesticide need
- Fish eat submerged weeds and aquatic pests
- Duck feet disturb soil, improving aeration
- Combined: near-complete IPM without chemicals

## Yields

- Rice yields equal or exceed conventional
- Duck yield: 2-3 kg meat per bird (harvest fall)
- Eggs if laying breeds used
- Fish yield 200-300 kg/hectare additional
- Total system caloric output far exceeds
  single-crop rice production
