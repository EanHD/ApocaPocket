# Chicken Husb

**Priority: VERY HIGH** | Easy | Reliable protein + eggs + manure. First animal for any settlement.

---

## Why Chickens First

- 1 hen = 200-300 eggs/year.
- 10 hens = 2000+ eggs/year plus manure for composting.
- 25 broilers (12 weeks) = 75-100 lbs meat.
- Setup: $200-500. Feed: $15-30/month for 6 hens.
- Fast reproduction  -  flock doubles in 6 months.

---

## Housing

**Coop (indoor)**:
- 4 sq ft per bird minimum.
- Roosts: 2x4 boards on edge, 2-4 ft high, 8-10" per bird.
- Nesting boxes: 1 per 3-4 hens, 12x12x12", filled with straw, slightly dark.
- Ventilation: vents near roof (1 sq ft per 10 sq ft floor). Draft-free at bird level.
- Pop door: 12x12" opening to run. Closeable at night.

**Run (outdoor)**:
- 10 sq ft per bird minimum.
- Fencing: 1/2" hardware cloth (not chicken wire  -  weasels bite through it).
- Bury fence 12" underground or lay 18" apron to stop digging.
- Roof or net to stop hawk attacks.

**Predator protection**:
- Use complex latches (raccoons open simple hooks).
- Close pop door every night  -  most predators are nocturnal.
- Hardware cloth over all openings including vents.

---

## Feeding

**Commercial feed**:
- Chicks 0-6 weeks: starter (18-20% protein).
- Pullets 6-18 weeks: grower (16-18% protein).
- Laying hens 18+ weeks: layer feed (16-18% protein + calcium).
- Amount: 1/4 lb per bird per day.

**Safe kitchen scraps**: vegetable and fruit scraps, cooked grains, bread (moderation), cooked meat.

**Avoid**: raw potato peels, avocado skin and pit, chocolate, onions (large amounts), dried or undercooked beans, moldy food.

**Supplements**: oyster shell (free-choice calcium for strong eggshells). Grit if no dirt access.

**Self-sufficient feeding**:
- Grow corn, wheat, field peas, sunflowers for supplemental grain.
- Mealworm farm for protein in winter.
- Free-range reduces feed 25-50% in season.

---

## Egg Production

**Timeline**: 18-22 weeks to first egg. Peak production 6 months - 2 years. Declines ~10%/year after age 2.

**Best laying breeds**: Leghorn (280-320 eggs/yr), Rhode Island Red (250-300), Australorp (250-300), Plymouth Rock (200-280).

**Factors affecting production**:
- Light: need 14-16 hours/day (add artificial light in winter to maintain laying).
- Stress from predators, moves, or changes reduces laying.
- Annual molt: 6-8 weeks of no eggs.

**Egg collection**: 1-2x daily prevents egg-eating habit, freezing, and cracking.

**Storage**: unwashed eggs last 2-3 weeks at room temp (natural bloom protects). Refrigerated 4-5 weeks.

---

## Breeding

**Flock ratio**: 1 rooster per 8-12 hens. More roosters = fighting and over-mating.

**Natural incubation**: Broody hen sits on up to 12 fertile eggs for 21 days. Raises chicks herself.

**Artificial incubation**:
1. Incubator at 99.5 degF, 50-55% humidity, days 1-18.
2. Turn eggs 3-5x daily (or automatic turner).
3. Day 18 lockdown: stop turning, raise humidity to 65-70%.
4. Day 21: chicks hatch. Leave 12-24 hours to dry.
5. Move to brooder: 95 degF week 1, drop 5 degF/week until room temp.

**Best dual-purpose breeds**: Rhode Island Red, Plymouth Rock, Sussex, Orpington, Wyandotte.

---

## Health Basics

**Daily**: fresh water, check for lethargy or injury.
**Weekly**: clean droppings from roosts.
**Monthly**: deep clean coop, check for mites under wings and at vent, inspect feet for bumblefoot.

**Common issues**:
- Coccidiosis: bloody droppings  -  treat with Amprolium (Corid).
- Respiratory: sneezing, gurgling  -  improve ventilation, treat with antibiotics if severe.
- Mites/lice: feather loss, pale comb  -  treat with permethrin spray, provide dust baths.

**Biosecurity**: quarantine new birds 30 days before introducing to flock. Remove dead birds immediately.

---

## Butchering Overview

**Age**: 8-12 weeks (broilers), 16-20 weeks (dual-purpose).
**Process**: fast 12-24 hours > cut jugular > bleed 2-3 min > scald 140-160 degF 30-60 sec > pluck > eviscerate > cold water rinse > age refrigerated 24-48 hours.
**Yield**: 65-75% of live weight.
**Time**: 15-30 minutes per bird once practiced.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Weak predator fencing: use hardware cloth, buried apron, complex latches.
- Overcrowding: 4 sq ft indoor + 10 sq ft outdoor minimum per bird.
- Poor ventilation: high vents, draft-free at bird level.
- Dirty water: clean drinkers daily, fresh water always.
- Expecting eggs immediately: 18-22 weeks to first egg.
