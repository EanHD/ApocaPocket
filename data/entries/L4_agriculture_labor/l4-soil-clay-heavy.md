# Clay Soil Fixes

## Identifying Clay Soil

- Sticky and plastic when wet
- Forms long ribbon in ribbon test
- Cracks and hardens when dry
- Puddles form and drain slowly
- Roots struggle to penetrate
- Often waterlogged in spring

## Gypsum Treatment

- Gypsum (calcium sulfate) breaks clay bonds
- Apply 2-3 kg per sq meter
- Work into top 15cm if possible
- Results visible within 2-3 rain cycles
- Safe for all plants, no pH change
- Unlike lime, gypsum does not raise pH

## Organic Matter Fix

- Each shovelful of compost helps
- Target: raise organic matter to 5%+
- This takes 3-5 years of consistent input
- Compost improves both drainage and aeration
- Leaf mold is excellent and often free
- Green manure crops are self-generating

## What NOT to Add

- Fine sand + clay = pottery-like mix
- Coarse grit (>3mm) can help in very
  small amounts but is not reliable fix
- Peat is acidic and unsustainable
- Use compost as primary amendment always

## Raised Beds as Bypass

- Build raised beds above clay subsoil
- Fill with 50% topsoil, 50% compost
- 30-45cm depth sufficient for most crops
- Excellent for vegetables and herbs
- Still plant perennials in ground
- Use paths to avoid compacting beds

## Bio-Drill Crops

- Daikon radish penetrates 60cm into clay
- Tap roots leave channels when they rot
- Till under before seed head forms
- Plant one season of daikon before crops
- Comfrey roots break compaction too
- Repeat annually in persistent problem areas

## Long-Term Success Plan

- Year 1: gypsum + compost + cover crop
- Year 2: green manure + deep mulch
- Year 3: soil noticeably improved
- Year 5: workable for most crops
- Never let soil dry bare in summer
- Permanent mulch layer protects structure
