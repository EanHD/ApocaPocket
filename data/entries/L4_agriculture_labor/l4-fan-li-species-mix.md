# Polyculture Species Mix

## Overview

- Fan Li stocked four carp by water layer
- Each species exploits a different food niche
- No competition = maximum combined yield
- Known as "Four Major Chinese Carps" system
- Still dominant aquaculture method in China
- Applicable globally with local species

## Four Classic Species

- Silver carp (Hypophthalmichthys molitrix)
- Feeds on phytoplankton, surface layer
- Fast growing, 1-2 kg per season
- Bighead carp (H. nobilis)
- Feeds on zooplankton, upper-mid layer
- Complements silver carp exactly
- Grass carp (Ctenopharyngodon idella)
- Feeds on aquatic plants, mid layer
- Also accepts cut land plants, mulberry
- Common carp (Cyprinus carpio)
- Bottom feeder, roots through mud
- Aerates pond bottom while feeding

## Stocking Ratios

- Silver carp: 40% of total stock
- Bighead carp: 20% of total stock
- Grass carp: 30% of total stock
- Common carp: 10% of total stock
- Total: 3-5 fish per 10 sq meters
- Adjust based on natural food availability

## Regional Substitutions

- No silver/bighead? Use tilapia (surface)
- Temperate only: rainbow trout (cold water)
- North America: bluegill + catfish + bass
- Avoid mixing bass with small carp fingerlings
- Crucian carp substitutes for common carp
- Catfish as bottom layer replacement

## Feeding the Layers

- Surface plankton: boosted by duck/animal waste
- Mid layer: cut grasses, vegetable trimmings
- Bottom: earthworms, kitchen scraps, manure
- Supplement only if natural food is scarce
- Rice bran broadcast on surface is universal
- Fermented plant slurry boosts plankton

## Harvest Rotation

- Harvest largest fish each season
- Replenish with fingerlings from hatchery pond
- Keep a breeding pond separate from grow-out
- Small separate pond for fry production
- Net harvest: long seine net across pond
- Leave 20-30% of stock each harvest

## Signs of Good Balance

- Fish visible and active at dawn
- Water clear to 30cm depth (Secchi disk)
- No surface gasping (good oxygen)
- No dead fish on banks
- Steady growth visible each month
