# FoodForests

**Priority: HIGH** | Plant once, harvest for decades. Less work every year as system matures.

---

## The 7 Layers

Stack productive plants vertically to maximize space:

- **Layer 1 — Canopy (30-100 ft)**: Walnut, oak, chestnut, pecan. Mature 10-30 yrs. Space 30-50 ft apart.
- **Layer 2 — Understory (15-30 ft)**: Apple, pear, plum, cherry. Mature 3-7 yrs. Space 15-20 ft apart.
- **Layer 3 — Shrubs (3-15 ft)**: Blueberry, currant, gooseberry, hazelnut. Mature 2-4 yrs. Space 4-8 ft apart.
- **Layer 4 — Herbaceous (1-3 ft)**: Asparagus, rhubarb, comfrey, sorrel, perennial onions. Mature 1-3 yrs.
- **Layer 5 — Ground cover (<1 ft)**: Strawberry, clover, thyme. Suppress weeds and fix nitrogen.
- **Layer 6 — Rhizosphere (underground)**: Potatoes, Jerusalem artichoke, garlic, ginger. Annual harvest of perennials.
- **Layer 7 — Vertical (climbers)**: Grapes, kiwi, hops, climbing beans. Support on trees and trellises.

---

## Nitrogen Fixers

Plant 1 nitrogen fixer per 3-4 regular plants.

- Clover: ground cover, easy, zone 3-10.
- Siberian pea shrub: shrub, very high output, zone 2-7.
- Black locust: tree, very high output, fast-growing, zone 4-8.
- Alder: tree, very high output, wet sites, zone 3-7.

**Method**: Cut nitrogen fixer leaves 2-3x/year and drop as mulch around neighboring plants.

---

## Perennial Staples

**Nut trees (calorie priority)**:
- Chestnut: 40,000-60,000 cal/tree/year. Mature 3-5 yrs (grafted). Stores 6+ months.
- Walnut: 30,000-50,000 cal/tree/year. Mature 7-10 yrs. Stores 12+ months.
- Hazelnut: 10,000-15,000 cal/tree/year. Mature 3-4 yrs. Compact and reliable.

**One mature chestnut = 2,000 sq ft of wheat without annual planting.**

**Berry shrubs**:
- Currant and gooseberry: 8-12 lbs/bush/year. Hardy to -40°F. Mature in 2-3 yrs.
- Elderberry: 12-20 lbs/bush/year. Hardy to -30°F. Also medicinal.

**Perennial vegetables**:
- Asparagus: half lb/plant/year. 20+ year lifespan once established.
- Rhubarb: 2-3 lbs/plant/year. 20+ year lifespan. Leaves are toxic — stalks only.
- Walking onions: continuous harvest, indefinite lifespan.

## Apple Tree Guild

Plant around a dwarf apple tree (15 ft spacing):
1. Comfrey (3-4 plants, 3 ft from trunk): mines deep minerals, mulch 3-4x/year.
2. Clover (ground cover): fixes nitrogen, suppresses weeds.
3. Chives (ring at drip line): repels aphids and borers.
4. Nasturtium (outside ring): aphid trap crop, edible flowers.
5. Daffodils (scattered): deters rodents and deer.

**Result**: Tree grows faster with fewer pests and no fertilizer needed.

## Establishment Timeline

- **Year 1**: Sheet mulch (cardboard + 6" wood chips). Plant nitrogen fixers, ground covers, bare-root trees. 40-60 hours work.
- **Year 2**: Add herbaceous layer and berry shrubs. First greens and strawberries. 20-30 hours.
- **Year 3**: Berries and limited apples. Asparagus begins. 15-20 hours.
- **Years 4-5**: Full berry production, regular fruit harvests. 10-15 hours/year.
- **Years 10+**: Nut trees producing. <10 hours/year. System largely self-maintaining.

## Water Management

**Swales** (level trenches on contour):
1. Mark contour with A-frame level.
2. Dig trench 1-2 ft deep, 2-4 ft wide. Pile soil on downhill side as berm.
3. Plant trees on berm (roots access stored water).
4. Space swales 20-50 ft apart. Effect: 50-70% less runoff.

**Mulch**: 4-6" wood chips. Retain moisture, suppress weeds, build soil. Renew 2-3" annually. Keep 2-3" away from tree trunks.

---

## Maintenance Rules

- Annual pruning (winter, dormant season).
- Harvest.
- Do NOT till (damages perennial roots).
- Do NOT fertilize (nitrogen fixers plus mulch handle it).
- Do NOT weed (ground cover and mulch suppress).

---

## Common Mistakes

- Planting too densely: space for mature size. Trees need 15-30 ft.
- No nitrogen fixers: minimum 1 per 3-4 plants.
- Skipping mulch: 4-6" wood chips, renewed annually.
- Expecting immediate yields: substantial harvest starts Year 3-5.
- Sun-lovers planted in shade: match light needs to forest layer.
