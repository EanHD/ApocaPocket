# Rabbit Breeding

## Why Rabbits

- Gestation: 28-31 days (very fast cycle)
- Litter size: 6-10 kits (young)
- Weaning: 4-6 weeks
- Market weight: 8-12 weeks (2kg live)
- Feed conversion: 3:1 (excellent)
- One doe produces 50-80 kits per year

## Meat Breeds

- New Zealand White: most common meat breed
- California: excellent meat-to-bone ratio
- Flemish Giant: large, slower maturing
- Rex: fine fur + good meat, dual purpose
- Any large breed works for survival use
- Local landrace breeds have adapted immunity

## Housing Requirements

- Wire floor cages suspend over drop pans
- Minimum 60x90cm per adult rabbit
- Doe needs nest box before kindling
- Temperature: 10-25C ideal
- Below 0C: provide bedding, shelter
- Above 30C: serious heat stress risk

## Breeding Management

- Buck to doe ratio: 1 buck to 5-10 does
- Bring doe to buck cage, never reverse
- Mate 2-3 times in 10 minutes
- Return doe to her cage
- Rebreed 6-8 weeks after kindling
- Does cycle year-round

## Nest Box and Kindling

- Provide nest box 27 days after mating
- Wooden box 30x40cm with low entrance
- Fill with straw, doe adds own fur
- Do not disturb nest for first 2 days
- Check on day 3: count kits, remove dead
- Does kindle at night, usually

## Feeding

- Hay: 70% of diet (unlimited)
- Pellets: 120g per adult per day
- Fresh greens: kale, comfrey, dandelion
- Water: unlimited, critical in hot weather
- Rabbits also eat their own cecotropes
  (soft pellets, normal and necessary)

## Harvest Weight

- 8-10 weeks: 1.8-2.3kg live weight
- Dress out at 50-55% of live weight
- One kit = ~1 kg dressed meat
- 50 kits/doe/year = 50 kg meat per doe
- Manure: 1 kg/day per adult rabbit
  Excellent direct garden fertilizer
