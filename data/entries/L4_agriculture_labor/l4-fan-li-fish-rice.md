# Fish-Rice Paddy System

## Overview

- Integrate fish into flooded rice paddies
- Fish eat weeds, insects, and snails
- Fish waste directly fertilizes rice roots
- Rice provides shade and cover for fish
- Dual harvest: rice grain + fish protein
- Practiced 2,000+ years across Asia
- Requires no chemical inputs

## Paddy Preparation

- Dig fish refuge trench along paddy edge
- Trench 30-50cm deeper than paddy floor
- Width 50cm minimum for fish movement
- Install inlet screen to keep fish in
- Raise paddy bunds (walls) to 40cm min
- Bunds must hold 15-20cm water depth

## Fish Species Selection

- Common carp: best bottom feeder
- Crucian carp: cold tolerant, hardy
- Tilapia: warm climates only (>20C)
- Grass carp: weed control specialist
- Avoid carnivorous species (eat each other)
- Stock 2-4 fingerlings per sq meter

## Stocking Timing

- Add fish 1-2 weeks after transplanting rice
- Rice needs to be established first
- Water depth 10-15cm at stocking
- Gradually raise to 15-20cm as rice grows
- Drain to refuge trench at harvest time
- Never drain completely until fish removed

## Managing the System

- Check screens weekly for damage
- Remove excess aquatic weeds by hand
- Supplement with rice bran if needed
- Monitor water clarity - should see fish
- Cloudy brown water = healthy pond
- Green algae bloom = too much sun

## Harvest Sequence

- Drain paddy slowly to refuge trench
- Fish concentrate in trench for easy catch
- Harvest fish before cutting rice
- Small fish return to pond for next season
- Use fish offal as fertilizer immediately
- Rotate paddies to rest soil every 3rd year

## Yields and Benefits

- Rice yield up 8-15% vs no-fish paddies
- Fish provide 200-400 kg/hectare protein
- Methane emissions reduced vs plain paddy
- Pesticide use drops to near zero
- Weed pressure reduced significantly
- Soil biology improves year over year
