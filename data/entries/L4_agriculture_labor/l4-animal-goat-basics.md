# Goat Basics

## Browse vs Graze

- Goats: natural browsers (shrubs, weeds)
- Cattle/sheep: grazers (grass)
- Goats prefer: blackberry, brush, tree bark
- Will eat grass but do poorly on grass alone
- Excellent for clearing overgrown land
- Supplemental hay always needed

## Best Breeds

- Nigerian Dwarf: small, high milk fat, easy
- Nubian: large, high production, long ears
- Boer: meat breed, large, fast growing
- Alpine: hardy, good milk, European origin
- For beginners: Nigerian Dwarf or Alpine
- Dual-purpose: Nubian or Kinder cross

## Housing

- Simple three-sided shelter from rain and wind
- Minimum 2 sq m per goat inside
- 10 sq m per goat outside (browsing area)
- Raised sleeping platform preferred
- Goats hate getting wet
- Good ventilation but no drafts

## Feeding

- Hay: primary diet (1-2 kg per day)
- Grain: milking does and growing kids only
- Browse: allows foraging reduces cost
- Loose minerals always available
- Fresh water daily (they refuse stale water)
- Do not feed: nightshade, azalea, laurel

## Milking

- Does freshen (give milk) after kidding
- Milking twice daily: maximum production
- Once daily: acceptable, less yield
- Production: 1-3 liters per day per doe
- Milk contains: 3-4% fat, high protein
- Goat milk easier to digest than cow milk

## Breeding Timing

- Does cycle Oct-Feb (short day breeders)
- Gestation: 150 days (5 months)
- Litter: 1-3 kids typical
- Weigh and tag kids at birth
- Buck needed for breeding or AI
- Keep buck separate except breeding season

## Health Essentials

- Hoof trimming every 6-8 weeks
- Deworming: fecal egg count method
- CDT vaccine annually
- FAMACHA eye scoring for anemia (parasites)
- Selenium deficiency common in many areas
