# Distilling Seawater

## Why It Matters

- Ocean water kills from salt load
- Distillation removes salt completely
- Fire still is reliable and fast
- Solar method needs no fuel
- Coastal survival depends on this
- Two methods cover different scenarios

## Fire Still Setup

- Fill pot with seawater
- Stretch cloth tightly across top
- Weight cloth center down slightly
- Steam hits cloth, drips to center
- Place cup under cloth center
- Boil gently, collect condensation

## Tubing Still Method

- Seal pot lid with clay or cloth
- Route steam through tube or hose
- Tube runs through cold water
- Steam condenses to fresh water
- Collect at tube exit end
- Cold water bath increases yield

## Coastal Solar Still

- Fill dark container with seawater
- Cover with sloped clear plastic
- Weight plastic center down
- Condensation runs to low point
- Collect at low corner daily
- Yield: 500 ml per day in full sun

## Output Rates

- Fire still: 250 ml per 30 min
- Solar still: 300-500 ml per day
- Large fire setup scales upward
- Prioritize fuel-efficient approach
- Multiple solar stills compound yield

## What Can Go Wrong

- Steam escapes before condensing
- Collected water recontacted by steam
- Solar still clouds over - zero yield
- Burning pot dry destroys equipment

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: 500 ml fresh from seawater
  using fire method in 2 hours
- Sign of mastery: Taste test
  confirms no salt in product
