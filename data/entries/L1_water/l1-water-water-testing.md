# Testing Water Safety

## Why It Matters

- Purification kills biology only
- Chemical contamination is untreatable
- Source assessment changes method
- Bad decisions here are irreversible
- Know what risk you are accepting
- All field water carries some risk

## Source Risk Hierarchy

- Moving mountain springs: lowest risk
- Running streams above human activity
- Still ponds and lakes: moderate
- Downstream from settlement: high
- Industrial or mine runoff: extreme
- Floodwater: assume contaminated

## Visual Assessment

- Clear is better but not proof safe
- Brownish tannin water is filterable
- Green algae - filter and purify
- Oily rainbow sheen = contamination
- Foam downstream of froth = chemical
- Dead animals in/near water = avoid

## Odor Assessment

- No odor is not a safety guarantee
- Sulfur smell (eggs) = minerals OK
- Sweet or fruity = chemical contamination
- Petroleum smell = do not drink
- Sewage/decay smell = avoid
- Algae bloom smell = blue-green risk

## Biological Risk Clues

- No aquatic life = possible toxin
- Dead fish = serious contamination
- Animal carcasses upstream = risky
- Healthy insects/frogs = good sign
- Mine tailings in watershed = avoid
- Industrial discharge upstream: skip

## What Can Go Wrong

- Clarity deceives - Giardia invisible
- Mountain spring may be surface fed
- Chemical contamination has no smell
- Over-confidence kills more than doubt

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Source risk assessment
  complete in under 60 seconds
- Sign of mastery: Correct risk
  tier for 5 different sources shown
