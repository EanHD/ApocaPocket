# Water Safety Overview

All wild and untreated water can carry pathogens.
Boiling is most reliable. Bleach/iodine when no fire.
SODIS when no chemicals. Always filter turbid
water first, then treat.

## Method Ranking

**Most reliable -> field expedient:**

1. **Boiling**  -  kills everything, needs fire
2. **Bleach**  -  kills bacteria/viruses, fast
3. **Iodine tabs**  -  kills bacteria/viruses, fast
4. **SODIS**  -  kills most, needs sun + 6hrs
5. **Filtration**  -  removes particles/some bugs
6. **Solar still**  -  last resort, very slow

## Pre-Treatment

If water is cloudy or turbid:
- Filter through cotton cloth or T-shirt first
- Let settle 1 hour, then decant from top
- Add 1 extra drop/liter if using bleach
- Clear water = SODIS and UV can work

Skipping pre-treatment with chemical or UV
methods reduces effectiveness by 50%+.

## Decision Guide

**Have fire?** Boil. Rolling boil 1 min
(3 min above 6,500 ft). Most reliable.

**No fire, have bleach?** 2 drops/liter
(5-6%), wait 30 min. See dosing entry.

**Have iodine tablets?** 1-2 tabs/liter,
30 min. Not for long-term or pregnancy.

**No chemicals, sunny day?** SODIS.
Clear PET bottle, 6hrs direct sun.

**No heat, chemicals, or sun?** Filter
+ let it settle. Still treat before drinking.

**Need a permanent source?** Develop a
spring or dig a well. See those entries.

## Water Sources Ranked

**Safest to most risky:**
1. Commercial bottled water
2. Developed spring (properly protected)
3. Rainwater (collected clean)
4. Deep well water
5. Flowing stream (uphill from humans)
6. Still pond or lake
7. Puddles / floodwater (highest risk)

Any source at #2 or below must be treated.
