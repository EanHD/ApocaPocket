# Water from Cacti

## Why It Matters

- Common belief is wrong and deadly
- Most cacti do NOT have safe water
- Two safe species are identifiable
- Wrong choice = severe dehydration
- Know before you cut - not after
- This knowledge is life or death

## Barrel Cactus

- Ferocactus genus is safest option
- Fishhook barrel common SW deserts
- Top cut off, mash interior flesh
- Squeeze flesh through cloth
- Yields bitter but safe liquid
- Use as last resort emergency only

## Prickly Pear

- Opuntia genus, flat paddle shape
- Red/purple fruits in season
- Peel fruit, eat or squeeze fluid
- Pads also contain moisture
- Roast pad to access gel inside
- Widely available, easily identified

## Dangerous Species

- Saguaro: vomiting and diarrhea
- Any tall columnar cactus
- Euphorbia (spurge) - toxic white sap
- Agave is NOT a cactus - avoid fluid
- Rule: if sap is milky white - toxic
- If unsure, do not consume

## Preparation Method

- Use cloth or bandana as filter
- Squeeze mashed flesh through it
- Do not consume seeds or skin
- Drink small amount, wait 30 min
- Watch for nausea before drinking more

## What Can Go Wrong

- Misidentifying toxic species
- Consuming seeds or alkaloid skin
- Spending energy for minimal yield
- Spines in hands prevent work

## Practice Benchmark

- Goal: Identify safe vs unsafe cactus
  at 10 feet in your region
- Sign of mastery: Extract 200 ml
  from barrel cactus without tools
