# Hand Well Construction

A hand-dug well reaches the water table for a reliable
water source. Wells 10-30 ft deep can be dug with hand
tools in 1-4 weeks. A properly constructed well provides
clean water for decades.

## Site Selection

Choose location carefully  -  wrong site = contaminated well:
- **100 ft minimum** from latrines, animal pens,
  or any waste area
- Downhill from clean areas, uphill from contamination
- Look for shallow water table indicators:
  lush vegetation, low-lying areas, existing seeps,
  neighbors with successful wells
- **Not** in a flood plain  -  floodwater will
  contaminate the well

## Digging

Standard diameter: **3-5 feet** (round is strongest):
- Use pick to loosen soil; shovel into bucket
- Haul bucket up with rope and pulley
- **Line walls as you go**  -  stone, brick,
  concrete rings, or tightly fitted timber
- Install lining before digging further down
- Never work below un-shored walls (cave-in kills)

## Reaching Water Table

Signs you've reached it: hole begins filling with water:
- Dig **3-5 ft below** the water table for reservoir
- Bail water as you dig (bucket on rope)
- This is the hardest part  -  digging in water

**Ventilation required below 10 ft:**
- CO2 and methane accumulate in deep holes
- Lower a candle on string  -  if it goes out,
  air is dangerous. Ventilate before entering.

## Well Lining and Head

**Lining zones:**
- Upper 10 ft: impermeable (concrete/stone+mortar/clay)
   -  prevents surface contamination from entering
- Below water table: permeable (dry-stacked stone/gravel)
   -  allows water to enter

**Well head construction:**
- Extend 1-2 ft above ground level
- Build concrete/stone apron sloping away,
  3 ft radius minimum
- Install a **sealed cover**  -  keeps out animals,
  debris, and surface water

## Pumping Options

- **Rope-and-bucket**  -  simple but sanitary risk
  if bucket touches contaminated surfaces
- **Hand pump**  -  best option, most sanitary
- **DIY bailer**  -  tube with check valve on bottom,
  lowered on rope; more sanitary than open bucket

## Disinfect and Maintain

**New well disinfection (before first use):**
1. Pour 1 cup bleach per 100 gallons of well water
2. Let sit 24 hours
3. Pump/bail until chlorine smell is gone
4. Boil water for the first week

**Monthly maintenance:**
- Check cover integrity
- Test water clarity and taste
- Turbidity after rain = surface contamination
  entering  -  improve well head seal and drainage
